    About {{SITE}}

    Updated: November 18, 2026

    GTA6Box is an independent guide to Grand Theft Auto VI: mission walkthroughs, the map of Leonida, characters, vehicles, weapons and buying advice.

    How we work

    • Confirmed facts first. Every guide shows a status: confirmed, partly confirmed or not confirmed yet. We say where information comes from.
    • No leaks as fact. We don't present leaked or rumoured details as confirmed, and we don't invent mission steps, cheat codes or statistics.
    • Updated daily. Guides are revised as the game is explored and patched. Each page shows when it was last updated.
    • Original writing. Our guides are written for this site. We research with the game itself, Rockstar's official announcements and reputable public sources, and we use AI tools to help draft and update pages, with every fact checked against those sources.

    Corrections

    Spotted a mistake or something out of date? Email contact@gta6box.com and we will fix it.

    Independence

    GTA6Box is an unofficial fan site. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto, GTA and related names are trademarks of their respective owners. We don't host official artwork, screenshots or game files.
