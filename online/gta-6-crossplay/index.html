    GTA 6 Crossplay: PS5 and Xbox Explained

    GTA 6 has no crossplay at launch because it launches as a single-player game. Here is what is known about cross-platform play and cross-progression.

    Status
    Not confirmed yet
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 launches as a single-player game, so there is no crossplay at launch.
    • Rockstar has not said whether a future online mode will support crossplay between PS5 and Xbox.
    • Cross-progression between platforms is also unconfirmed.
    On this page 3
    1. Will GTA 6 online have crossplay later?
    2. Cross-progression and cross-save
    3. What to do if you and your friends are on different consoles

    Short answer: GTA 6 does not have crossplay at launch. The game releases on November 19, 2026 as a single-player experience, so there is no multiplayer mode for PS5 and Xbox players to share.

    Will GTA 6 online have crossplay later?

    Rockstar has not said. It has not yet detailed an online mode for GTA 6 at all; see GTA 6 online. For reference, GTA Online on GTA 5 never added crossplay between PlayStation and Xbox.

    Cross-progression and cross-save

    Nothing is confirmed about moving a save or an online character between PS5 and Xbox. Story saves on consoles are normally tied to the platform you play on.

    What to do if you and your friends are on different consoles

    For the story this does not matter, since it is single-player. If playing together later is important to you, the safe choice is to buy on the same platform as your friends.

    We will update this page as soon as Rockstar announces online details.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is GTA 6 crossplay?

    Not at launch. GTA 6 launches as a single-player experience, and Rockstar has not announced crossplay for any future online mode.

    Can PS5 and Xbox players play GTA 6 together?

    Not at launch, because there is no multiplayer mode yet.

    Does GTA Online have crossplay?

    GTA Online on GTA 5 does not support crossplay between PlayStation and Xbox.
