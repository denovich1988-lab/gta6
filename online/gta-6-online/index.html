    GTA 6 Online: Is There Multiplayer at Launch?

    GTA 6 launches as a single-player game. Here is what Rockstar has confirmed about online and multiplayer, and what is still unknown.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar describes GTA 6 at launch as a single-player experience.
    • A new online mode has not been dated or detailed.
    • Nothing is confirmed about transferring GTA Online characters or progress.
    On this page 4
    1. What that means
    2. When is GTA 6 online coming?
    3. What about GTA Online?
    4. Crossplay

    When Rockstar revealed the price of GTA 6 in June 2026, it also said the game would be a single-player experience at launch.

    What that means

    • On November 19, 2026 you get the story mode and the open world of Leonida.
    • There is no online multiplayer mode on day one.
    • You do not need a console subscription to play the story.

    When is GTA 6 online coming?

    Rockstar has not announced a date, a name or features for a new online mode. GTA 5 added GTA Online two weeks after launch in 2013, but that tells us nothing certain about GTA 6.

    What about GTA Online?

    The existing GTA Online, built on GTA 5, continues to run. Whether characters, money or purchases will move to a future GTA 6 online mode is not confirmed.

    Crossplay

    With no multiplayer at launch, there is no crossplay at launch either. Details are in our GTA 6 crossplay guide.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

    Not at launch. Rockstar has described the launch game as a single-player experience and has not given a date for an online mode.

    Will GTA Online progress carry over to GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not said. Do not buy anything on the assumption that it will transfer.

    Do I need PS Plus or Game Pass to play GTA 6?

    No subscription is needed to play the single-player story.
