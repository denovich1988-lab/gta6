    GTA 6 PC Requirements: No Official Specs Yet

    GTA 6 PC requirements have not been published because there is no PC version yet. What Take-Two has said, what the consoles tell us and how to prepare.

    Not confirmed yet
    Updated

    Quick answer

    There are no official GTA 6 PC requirements. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar Games has not announced a PC version, so it has not published minimum or recommended specs. This page separates what is confirmed from what is guesswork and suggests how to plan a PC without relying on invented numbers.

    GTA 6 system requirements at a glance

    Question Answer Status
    Official PC requirements None published Confirmed
    PC version announced No Confirmed
    Platforms at launch PS5, Xbox Series X and Series S Confirmed by Rockstar
    Console storage Up to 212 GB Rockstar's figure
    Console frame rate 30 FPS target Reported
    PC release window Early 2027, according to one report Unconfirmed

    Why there are no PC specs

    System requirements are published when a PC version is announced, usually on the store page. GTA 6 has no Steam page, no Epic Games Store page and no PC listing on Rockstar's site, which shows two platforms. Rockstar's launch post on November 19 says the game is available for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    What Take-Two has said about PC

    Rockstar's parent company has been asked about PC many times. Its chief executive, Strauss Zelnick, has answered in general terms:

    When What he said Reported by
    February 2025 Rockstar has historically started on some platforms and moved to others later Endgame Tools, Gaming Route
    May 2026 Rockstar "always starts on console"; PC can be 45 to 50% of sales for a big game Endgame Tools, Gaming Route
    November 9, 2026 GTA VI is on two consoles at launch, and news about other versions will come from Rockstar Endgame Tools, Output Lag, Hone

    None of these is an announcement, and none mentions hardware.

    The spec lists you see online

    Search results are full of "GTA 6 minimum requirements" tables with specific processors and graphics cards. They are predictions. Nobody outside Rockstar has seen a PC build's requirements, and every guide we checked that is careful about sourcing says the same. We do not publish a predicted table, because a guess presented in a table looks like a fact.

    What the console version tells us

    The consoles are the only real evidence of how demanding the game is:

    • Storage. Rockstar says the download needs up to 212 GB depending on the device. See GTA 6 file size.
    • Frame rate. The game targets 30 FPS on every console, with no 60 FPS mode. See does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.
    • Hardware floor. It runs on the Xbox Series S, the least powerful of the supported consoles. See GTA 6 platforms.

    These facts suggest a heavy game that needs a fast SSD with a lot of free space. They do not translate into a specific graphics card.

    How long Rockstar's PC versions have taken

    Game Console release PC release Gap
    GTA 5 September 17, 2013 April 14, 2015 About 19 months
    Red Dead Redemption 2 October 26, 2018 November 5, 2019 About 12 months

    In both cases the requirements came with the PC announcement, not at console launch. Counting the same gaps from November 19, 2026 gives late November 2027 to mid-June 2028. That is arithmetic, not a forecast.

    One report points earlier: GamesBeat wrote on October 13, 2026, citing unnamed sources, that a PC version is planned for the first three months of 2027. Rockstar did not comment, and it remains unconfirmed. Our GTA 6 PC release date page follows this.

    How to prepare a PC without guessing

    1. Do not buy parts for GTA 6 yet. Hardware gets cheaper and better while you wait, and the real requirements may be a year or more away.
    2. If you are building now anyway, leave room for a large game: at least 250 GB free on an NVMe SSD is a reasonable plan given the console size.
    3. Ignore "GTA 6 ready" labels on pre-built PCs. No such certification exists.
    4. Do not buy PC keys. Any site selling a GTA 6 PC key is not selling a real product.

    Can you play GTA 6 on a PC today?

    Only through a console you own. Rockstar's support pages say the game supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox, so you can stream it from your own PS5 or Xbox to a PC screen. Xbox Cloud Gaming is listed for Xbox consoles, not for Windows or browsers.

    What is not confirmed

    • That a PC version is in development.
    • Any PC release date or window.
    • Any minimum or recommended specification, storage size or price for PC.
    • Whether console saves would carry over.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the GTA 6 PC requirements?

    None have been published. Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6, so there are no minimum or recommended specs. Lists you see online are estimates.

    What are the GTA 6 system requirements on console?

    A PlayStation 5 or an Xbox Series X or S, and up to 212 GB of free storage according to Rockstar. On PS5 the latest system software is also required.

    How much storage will GTA 6 need on PC?

    Unknown. The console version needs up to 212 GB, so planning for at least that much free SSD space is sensible, but it is not an official PC figure.

    Can I play GTA 6 on my PC now?

    Only by streaming from a console you own with Remote Play. There is no Steam, Epic or Rockstar Games Launcher version.
