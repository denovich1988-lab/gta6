    GTA 6 PC Release Date: What Is Confirmed

    GTA 6 is not on PC at launch. Here is what Rockstar and Take-Two have said, how long earlier PC ports took, and what to expect for system requirements.

    Status
    Not confirmed yet
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or date.
    • GTA 5 reached PC about 19 months after consoles and Red Dead Redemption 2 about a year after.
    • There are no official PC system requirements, so any you see online are guesses.

    GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version at launch, and Rockstar has not announced one.

    What Rockstar has said

    Rockstar's announcements list only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The publisher, Take-Two, has been asked about PC repeatedly and has not given a date.

    How long did earlier Rockstar PC ports take?

    Game Console release PC release Gap
    GTA 4 April 2008 December 2008 about 8 months
    GTA 5 September 2013 April 2015 about 19 months
    Red Dead Redemption 2 October 2018 November 2019 about 12 months

    If GTA 6 follows the same pattern, a PC version would arrive somewhere between late 2027 and 2028. That is an estimate from history, not an announcement.

    GTA 6 system requirements

    There are no official requirements. Lists of "minimum" and "recommended" specs online are speculation. We will publish the real ones the day Rockstar announces them.

    What PC players can do now

    • Don't buy keys. Any site selling a GTA 6 PC key today is not selling a real product.
    • Play on console if you don't want to wait; see price and editions.
    • Follow this page. We update it as soon as there is official news.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will GTA 6 be on PC?

    Rockstar has not announced a PC version. Its recent games did come to PC later, so a PC release is widely expected, but it is not confirmed.

    When is the GTA 6 PC release date?

    There is no PC release date. Based on GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, ports have arrived roughly one to two years after console launch.

    What are the GTA 6 system requirements?

    None have been published, because no PC version has been announced.

    Can I pre-order GTA 6 on Steam?

    No. There is no Steam or Epic Games Store page for GTA 6.
