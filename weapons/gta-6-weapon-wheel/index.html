    GTA 6 Weapon Wheel: Controls, Loadout Limit and Storage

    How the GTA 6 weapon wheel works: open it with L1 or LB, switch between Weapons and Items, how many guns you carry and where the rest are stored.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) to open the GTA 6 weapon wheel; press R1 or RB to switch between the Weapons and Items tabs.
    • You carry two sidearms and two long guns, one melee weapon and explosives; everything else goes in your car's trunk or a weapon locker.
    • Healing items sit in the bottom-left corner of the wheel: Square and Triangle on PS5, X and Y on Xbox.

    The GTA 6 weapon wheel opens when you hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox, and it now shows only what you are carrying, not your whole arsenal. GTA 6 limits you to two sidearms and two long guns at a time, and the wheel doubles as your gear menu, with a second tab for clothing and quick slots for healing items. This page covers the controls, the loadout limit, where spare guns are stored and how to set up a loadout before a mission.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    Weapon wheel controls

    Action PS5 Xbox
    Open the wheel Hold L1 Hold LB
    Highlight a slot Right stick Right stick
    Equip the highlighted item Release L1 Release LB
    Switch between Weapons and Items R1 (while holding L1) RB (while holding LB)
    Use a healing pill Square X
    Drink Triangle Y

    Tip: the healing slots work on either tab, so you can heal mid-fight without picking a weapon first.

    What the wheel shows

    The Weapons tab has the slots for everything you carry: sidearms, long guns, melee, explosives and equipment such as binoculars.

    The Items tab holds what you wear: hats, glasses, masks, bags and outfits. Each item is marked on or off. Highlight one to take it off or change its style.

    Why it matters: clothes and gear live in the same menu as your guns, so you rarely need to open the phone or a pause menu during a job.

    How many guns you can carry

    Slot Limit
    Sidearms (pistols, revolvers, sidearm SMGs) 2
    Long guns (rifles, shotguns, SMGs, snipers) 2
    Melee 1

    One long gun is slung on your back while the other is in your hands. Explosives and throwables are carried on top of that. Rockstar experimented with alternatives for years before keeping the wheel, Dexerto reports, but the limit itself is the real change from GTA 5.

    Where your other guns go

    1. Your car's trunk. Open the trunk of your vehicle to store guns and swap them into your loadout. Keep your favourites in a registered car, since unregistered cars disappear (see GTA 6 cars).
    2. Ammu-Nation weapon lockers. Some stores have a locker that holds your full collection. Lucia's optional Chapter 2 task Pick Up a Gun lets you take a gun from the green locker at Gellhorn Outfitters instead of buying one.

    Tip: before a mission, park your car close to the start and set your loadout from the trunk. Two long guns for range and close quarters, such as an assault rifle and a shotgun, cover most fights. Prices are in GTA 6 guns.

    Binoculars and other equipment

    Binoculars sit on the wheel like a weapon: open the wheel with L1 or LB and pick them from their slot. The game teaches them in Gone Fishing, where you use them to watch a meeting on the docks.

    What is not confirmed yet

    • Upgrades to carry space. Some guides say bigger bags appear later in the story, but they do not agree on what changes.
    • PC controls, until the PC version is out.
    • What happens to your loadout when you are arrested or wasted.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you open the weapon wheel in GTA 6?

    Hold L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox, tilt the right stick to a slot and let go to equip it. Press R1 or RB while the wheel is open to switch to the Items tab.

    How many weapons can you carry in GTA 6?

    Two sidearms and two long guns, with one long gun on your back and one in your hands, plus one melee weapon and explosives.

    Where do extra guns go in GTA 6?

    In the trunk of your vehicle or the weapon locker at some Ammu-Nation stores. You swap guns there.

    Is there a weapon wheel in GTA 6?

    Yes. It keeps the radial wheel from earlier games with a new look, but it only shows what you carry, not every gun you own.
