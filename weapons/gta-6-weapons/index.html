    GTA 6 Weapons: What Is Confirmed

    What is known about GTA 6 weapons and guns before launch, and how our verified weapons list will work.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar has not published an official GTA 6 weapons list.
    • Trailers and screenshots show pistols, rifles, shotguns and melee weapons.
    • We will list each weapon with its in-game name, stats and location once verified.
    On this page 3
    1. Weapon types seen in official footage
    2. What we don't know yet
    3. What our weapons list will include

    Rockstar has not released a GTA 6 weapons list. Before launch, the only reliable information comes from official trailers and screenshots.

    Weapon types seen in official footage

    • Handguns: pistols and revolvers
    • Long guns: assault rifles, hunting rifles and shotguns
    • Melee: bats, knives and improvised weapons

    What we don't know yet

    • The full list and in-game names
    • How many weapons you can carry and how you switch between them
    • Where weapons are sold and what they cost
    • Upgrades and attachments

    Details reported from leaks are not confirmed, and we do not publish them as fact.

    What our weapons list will include

    For each verified weapon:

    1. In-game name and class
    2. Damage, range and rate of fire
    3. Where to buy or find it, and the price
    4. The best missions and situations to use it in

    See also: GTA 6 cheats for weapon cheats once they are verified.

    Frequently asked questions

    What guns are in GTA 6?

    There is no official list yet. Official footage shows handguns, rifles, shotguns and melee weapons, and we will name each weapon once verified in the game.

    Is there a weapon wheel in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not detailed the weapon selection system. We will explain how the loadout works after launch.

    Where do you buy guns in GTA 6?

    Earlier GTA games used Ammu-Nation stores. We will confirm the GTA 6 shops and their locations after launch.
