    GTA 6 Guns: Every Weapon, Price and Where to Buy

    Every GTA 6 gun by class with Ammu-Nation prices and stats, the best weapon in each class, the carry limit and all 24 Ammu-Nation gun stores.

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 guns come in pistols, revolvers, SMGs, shotguns, assault rifles, tactical rifles, sniper rifles, a machine gun and a launcher, with prices from $550 to $12,000.
    • You carry two sidearms and two long guns at once; the rest go in your car's trunk or a weapon locker.
    • Leonida has 24 Ammu-Nation gun stores; Expanded stores stock the heavy weapons.

    GTA 6 guns are sold at 24 Ammu-Nation stores across Leonida and cost from $550 for a cheap revolver to $12,000 for the launcher. This page lists every gun by class with its price and stats, picks the best weapon in each class, explains the new carry limit and lists every gun store. Guides count the total differently, from 34 standard guns to more than 50 when variants are included, and Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides. Prices and stats come from Mobalytics; Tracker.gg shows the same prices for every gun we cross-checked. Stats use the in-game 0–10 scale.

    How many guns you can carry

    GTA 6 drops the bottomless pockets of GTA 5. Guides agree you carry two sidearms and two long guns, with one long gun on your back and one in your hands, plus one melee weapon and explosives. Guns that do not fit go in your car's trunk, and some Ammu-Nation stores have a weapon locker for the rest. The full controls are in GTA 6 weapon wheel.

    All 24 Ammu-Nation gun stores

    Every store sells weapons, ammo, body armour and parachutes, and each one has at least one unique weapon variant. Expanded stores carry the most, including the machine gun, sniper rifles and launchers. Store names match on PowerPyx and GTA Wiki; tiers are as PowerPyx lists them.

    Stores without a tier in brackets are Standard. Lucia's optional Chapter 2 task Pick Up a Gun sends you to Gellhorn Outfitters in Port Gellhorn, where you can buy a gun or take one from the weapon locker.

    The Ultimate Edition adds personalised versions of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17, and a pair of engraved Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers. See GTA 6 Ultimate Edition.

    Frequently asked questions

    What guns are in GTA 6?

    Pistols such as the Girardi ES9 and Klose K17, revolvers such as the Duke Mustang .357, SMGs such as the Vom Feuer VF5, shotguns such as the Moreland 850, assault rifles such as the Duke 556, sniper rifles such as the Fairview FF-50, the ADB M350 machine gun and the Hillinger Boomer launcher.

    What is the best gun in GTA 6?

    For most fights an assault rifle: GameSpot calls the Duke 556 the most consistent and easiest to control. The Fairview FF-50 hits hardest among sniper rifles, and the Hillinger Boomer launcher has the highest damage rating of all.

    How many guns can you carry in GTA 6?

    Two sidearms and two long guns, plus a melee weapon and explosives. Extra guns go in your car's trunk or the weapon locker at some Ammu-Nation stores.

    Where do you buy guns in GTA 6?

    At Ammu-Nation. There are 24 gun stores across Leonida; Expanded stores such as Big Shots, Woodhatch and Phil's Ammu-Nation carry the most weapons.
