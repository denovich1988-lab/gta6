    GTA 6 Characters: Every Confirmed Name

    ConfirmedUpdated November 18, 20261 min readChecked against 1 source

    Short answer: All GTA 6 characters confirmed by Rockstar: protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, plus Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Raul Bautista and more.

    Key facts

    • GTA 6 has two playable protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
    • Rockstar has named six supporting characters or groups: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder.
    • Rockstar has not officially announced the voice cast.

    Rockstar has introduced the main cast of GTA 6 on its official site. Here is everyone confirmed so far.

    Protagonists

    Character Who they are
    Jason Duval A former soldier now working for drug runners in the Leonida Keys
    Lucia Caminos Fresh out of prison and determined to build a better life with Jason

    Supporting characters

    Character Who they are
    Cal Hampton Jason's friend, a paranoid conspiracy fan who is happiest at home
    Boobie Ike A Vice City local legend turned businessman, with interests in property, a strip club and a recording studio
    Dre'Quan Priest A music hustler and Boobie's partner in the label Only Raw Records
    Real Dimez Rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records
    Raul Bautista A veteran bank robber who is always looking for talent for the next score
    Brian Heder An old-school drug runner in the Keys who lets Jason live rent-free in return for help

    How the two protagonists work

    Rockstar has confirmed that you play as both Jason and Lucia. Exactly how and when you switch between them is something we will document from the game after launch, on the mission list and in each walkthrough.

    Voice actors

    Rockstar traditionally does not announce the cast before release, and it has not done so for GTA 6. We will add the cast from the game's credits.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who is the main character in GTA 6?

    There are two main characters: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Both are playable.

    Is Lucia the first female protagonist in GTA?

    Lucia is the first female lead protagonist in a mainline 3D Grand Theft Auto game.

    Who are the GTA 6 voice actors?

    Rockstar has not officially announced the voice cast. Names circulating online are unconfirmed until Rockstar or the credits confirm them.

    Are Michael, Franklin or Trevor in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not confirmed any returning GTA 5 protagonists.
