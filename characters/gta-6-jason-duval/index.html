    Jason Duval in GTA 6: Everything Confirmed

    Who Jason Duval is in GTA 6: his Army past, his work for drug runners in the Leonida Keys, his friends and his relationship with Lucia.

    Quick answer

    • Jason Duval is one of GTA 6's two playable protagonists.
    • He grew up around grifters, joined the Army to escape that life, and now works for drug runners in the Leonida Keys.
    • He lives rent-free on a property owned by Brian Heder in exchange for helping with shakedowns.

    Jason Duval is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6. He was introduced in the first trailer and is the focus of the second.

    Jason's backstory

    According to Rockstar's official character description:

    • He grew up around grifters and crooks.
    • He joined the Army to get away from that life.
    • After his service he ended up in the Leonida Keys, doing what he knows best: working for local drug runners.
    • He wants an easy life, but things keep getting harder.

    Where Jason lives and who he works with

    • Brian Heder, a veteran drug runner, lets Jason live rent-free at one of his properties in the Keys as long as Jason helps with local shakedowns.
    • Cal Hampton is Jason's friend: a homebody with a taste for conspiracy theories.

    See the full cast on the GTA 6 characters page.

    Jason and Lucia

    Jason is in a relationship with Lucia Caminos. Rockstar suggests that meeting her could be the best or the worst thing that ever happened to him. The story follows the pair after a simple score goes wrong.

    Playing as Jason

    You play as both Jason and Lucia. We will cover his missions and any character-specific abilities in the missions section after launch.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who is Jason in GTA 6?

    Jason Duval is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6, alongside his partner Lucia Caminos.

    Was Jason in the military?

    Yes. Rockstar's description says he spent time in the Army trying to shake off a troubled youth.

    Who plays Jason in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not officially announced Jason's actor. Treat names online as unconfirmed.
