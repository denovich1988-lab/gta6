    GTA 6 Voice Actors: Who Plays Lucia and Jason

    GTA 6 voice actors confirmed so far: Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. Here is what is known about Jason Duval and the rest of the cast.

    Status

    Updated

    Quick answer

    • Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos, confirmed by her Game Awards 2026 nomination.
    • Jason Duval's actor has not been officially confirmed.
    • Rockstar does not announce its cast before release; the credits will settle the rest.

    Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos in GTA 6. That is the one lead role confirmed before launch. It became official on November 17, 2026, when The Game Awards nominated her for her performance in the game.

    GTA 6 cast: confirmed and unconfirmed

    Character Actor Status
    Lucia Caminos Manni L. Perez Confirmed (The Game Awards nomination)
    Jason Duval Not announced Unconfirmed
    Supporting cast Not announced To be confirmed from the credits

    Who plays Jason?

    Rockstar has not said, and no actor has confirmed the role. One name has circulated among fans for a long time, based on voice comparisons, but it remains a guess. We do not list unconfirmed names as fact.

    Other actors

    Actor Stephen Root has said he is in the game, without Rockstar confirming a role. Other castings shared online come from fan matching of voices in the trailers.

    Why so little is official

    Rockstar uses full performance capture and usually casts lesser-known actors for its leads. It keeps the cast private until the game is out, then the credits and the actors themselves fill in the list.

    We will update this page from the in-game credits. For who the characters are, see GTA 6 characters.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who plays Lucia in GTA 6?

    Manni L. Perez. The Game Awards confirmed it on November 17, 2026 by nominating her for her performance in Grand Theft Auto VI.

    Who voices Jason in GTA 6?

    It has not been officially confirmed. Names shared by fans are guesses until the credits or the actor confirm it.

    Why does Rockstar not announce the GTA 6 cast?

    Rockstar traditionally keeps its performers private until release. The same happened with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2.
