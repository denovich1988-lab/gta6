    GTA 6 Police and Gangs: Every Force, Every Crew, Where They Are

    GTA 6 police and gangs explained: the law enforcement agencies of Leonida, how cops respond, the eight named gangs and where each one operates.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    6 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Leonida has no single police force: city departments, county sheriffs, a highway patrol and state agencies each carry their own name and livery.
    • Eight named gangs plus a group of independent criminals run 39 illegal operations across the map.
    • Police no longer show on the minimap; you find them with Focus, and they keep a description of you after the chase.

    GTA 6 police are not one force but a patchwork of city departments, county sheriffs and state agencies, and the criminal side is just as split: eight named gangs hold their own corners of Leonida. This page covers who they all are, how the cops behave, where each gang operates and how both affect missions and free roam.

    It does not repeat how stars and heat work; that is in our GTA 6 wanted level guide. Everything below is confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two guides that agree, and single-source details are named as such.

    GTA 6 police forces at a glance

    Agency names appear on vehicles, uniforms and badges. These are the ones at least two sources list:

    Agency Type Where its name points
    Vice City Police Department (VCPD) City Vice City
    Vice Beach Police Department City Vice Beach
    Vice-Dale Police Department County Vice-Dale County, around Vice City
    Key Lento Police Department City Key Lento, Leonida Keys
    Port Gellhorn Police Department City Port Gellhorn
    Leonard County Sheriff's Office County Leonard County
    Mariana County Sheriff's Office County Mariana County
    Leonida Highway Patrol State Highways
    Leonida Department of Corrections State Prisons
    POACH (Protection of Animals & Controlled Hunting) State Wildlife enforcement
    United States Coast Guard Federal Coastal waters

    Why it matters: the force you meet changes with the region, but no guide reports that one agency chases differently from another.

    How GTA 6 cops respond

    • They have to be called. GTABase lists three triggers: a witness, a robbery alarm, or a stolen vehicle with an advanced tracker. Officers then travel to the scene rather than appearing next to you.
    • They are not on the minimap. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told press that police "show up when you use Focus" instead of on the radar.
    • They remember. Nelson said the team wanted police to have "a bit more persistent memory", so a description of you outlasts the chase.
    • Weapons in the open draw attention. Carrying a visible gun gets a reaction from cops and civilians before you fire.
    • Helicopters and K-9 units join in. A police helicopter is overhead from the start of the escape in Bricked Up. K-9 units were shown in Edge magazine's hands-on, and GTABase says a dog keeps tracking you once it is called in.
    • Violence changes the response. GTABase says killing officers makes the pursuit more aggressive and moves your Criminal Profile toward Violent. Shooting out tyres slows a chase without that cost.

    Two details come from one source each. Edge's hands-on, as reported by Beebom, describes spike strips that are hard to see at night. GTABase says a helicopter can pull back if you shoot at it.

    Tip: tap Focus before you leave a building during a chase. It is the only reliable way to see where officers are.

    Getting arrested

    You can surrender at lower wanted levels. Guides agree on two costs of an arrest:

    1. You lose cash.
    2. You lose weapons you picked up; weapons you own are kept.

    Guides disagree on how much cash goes and where you wake up, so we do not state either. A car left in the search area can be impounded; the Impounded Vehicle walkthrough shows how to get one back.

    Police in story missions

    The police are also characters. In Bricked Up, a pickup for Boobie Ike is raided by Captain Méndez and his dirty cops. In The Cartel Heist, cutting cell towers buys time before police arrive, and a SWAT team then breaches the bank. Mission chases are scripted, so the star level you face there is fixed.

    GTA 6 gangs: all eight and where they operate

    The Progress menu has a Gangs tab that tracks each group's illegal operations. PowerPyx and KeenGamer give the same counts:

    Gang What it is Where Operations
    Kingfishers Swamp smugglers Grassrivers 3
    Kolts & Associates Painkiller dealers Port Gellhorn 5
    M7 Street gang Hamlet 2
    La Polilla Negra Colombian cartel Site in Watson Bay 2
    Final Chapter Outlaw motorcycle club Ambrosia 4
    San4San Haitian street gang La Perle, Vice City 5
    PTT Youngin$ Street gang Southside, Vice City 2
    The Guardia Brothers Organised crime family Little Cuba, Vice City 3
    Other Criminals Independent fronts Across Leonida 13

    That makes 39. The Ultimate Edition adds one extra PTT Youngin$ robbery, which both guides say does not count toward the total.

    Why it matters: the map splits cleanly. Rural Leonida belongs to the Kingfishers, Kolts and Final Chapter; Vice City is shared by San4San, PTT Youngin$ and the Guardia Brothers.

    How gangs affect missions

    Most gangs are introduced by the story before you can rob them:

    Gang Where you meet them
    Kingfishers Gone Fishing, where they take Donnie
    Kolts & Associates Hit Kingpin Bowling, a raid on their bowling alley
    Final Chapter Clear Skies, where a deal collapses
    M7 Mami Knows Best, next door to the house Mia is selling
    San4San Bricked Up, the cocaine pickup
    The Guardia Brothers Crosstown Traffic, where their shipment is the target
    La Polilla Negra The Cartel Heist, as security inside the bank

    Cash City, the crew that protects DWNPLY in Ride or Die, is a gang in the story but has no operations of its own in the Progress menu.

    How gangs affect free roam

    Gang turf matters in free roam through illegal operations: guarded sites with a safe, valuables and often narcotics.

    1. An operation stays hidden until you enter its area. Before that, the map calls it a "Robbery Opportunity".
    2. Clearing the guards is not enough. You must collect every key valuable for it to count.
    3. A finished operation turns grey, then comes back after a cooldown and can be robbed again.
    4. One operation from each of the nine groups earns the Pest Control trophy; all 39 are needed for 100% completion.

    KeenGamer reports that police are slower to react at gang sites than at stores or banks, and that a suppressed weapon keeps a raid quiet. No second guide confirms this yet.

    Tip: rob one site per gang as the story takes you through each region. It is the cheapest route to Pest Control, and the wider robbery system is covered in GTA 6 heists.

    What is not confirmed

    • Gang wars or territory control. No guide describes taking over turf.
    • Gangs attacking you on sight in their area during free roam. Only mission-specific chases are documented.
    • Different tactics per agency, and which units arrive at which star level.
    • A military response at six stars. This comes from leaks, not from the game.
    • A Kelly County Police Department. It was named by fans from the first trailer, but we have not found it confirmed in the released game.
    • Arrest details: the exact cash penalty and the respawn point.
    • Bribes, or paying to clear a wanted level.

    We will add the per-star police response and a list of all 39 operation sites once two guides agree on them.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do GTA 6 police work?

    Officers are sent to a crime when a witness, an alarm or a vehicle tracker reports it. They do not appear on the minimap, they remember your face, clothes and vehicle, and at higher levels helicopters and K-9 units join in.

    How many GTA 6 gangs are there?

    Eight named gangs: Kingfishers, Kolts & Associates, M7, La Polilla Negra, Final Chapter, San4San, PTT Youngin$ and the Guardia Brothers. The game's Progress menu adds a ninth group, Other Criminals.

    Can you surrender to GTA 6 cops?

    Yes, at lower wanted levels. Being arrested costs you cash and any weapons you picked up rather than bought; weapons you own are kept.

    Are there gang wars or territory takeovers in GTA 6?

    No guide we have checked describes a turf war system. Gang locations are robbed as illegal operations, which come back after a cooldown instead of changing hands.
