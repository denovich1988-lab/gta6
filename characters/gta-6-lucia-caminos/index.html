    Lucia Caminos in GTA 6: Everything Confirmed

    Who Lucia Caminos is in GTA 6: her backstory, her relationship with Jason, why she was in prison and what Rockstar has confirmed about her.

    Quick answer

    • Lucia Caminos is one of GTA 6's two playable protagonists.
    • She grew up in Liberty City, learned to fight from her father and has just been released from Leonida Penitentiary.
    • Her goal is the good life her mother dreamed of, and she is planning it with Jason.

    Lucia Caminos is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6, and the first female lead in a mainline 3D Grand Theft Auto game.

    Lucia's backstory

    According to Rockstar's official character description:

    • Her father taught her to fight from a young age.
    • She grew up in Liberty City and has had to fight for her family.
    • That fight landed her in Leonida Penitentiary; the first trailer opens with her there.
    • She got out through "sheer luck" and has decided to make smarter moves from now on.

    Lucia and Jason

    Lucia is in a relationship with Jason Duval. The second trailer shows Jason picking her up on her release. Rockstar frames the story around the two of them: a simple score goes wrong, and they find themselves on the wrong side of a criminal conspiracy across Leonida, forced to rely on each other.

    What Lucia wants

    Rockstar describes her as more committed than ever to the good life her mother has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City. Rather than daydreaming, Lucia is ready to take control.

    Playing as Lucia

    You play as both Lucia and Jason. We will document her missions, abilities and any differences from Jason after launch in the missions section.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who is Lucia in GTA 6?

    Lucia Caminos is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6, alongside her partner Jason Duval.

    Why was Lucia in prison?

    Rockstar's description says she was fighting for her family and ended up in Leonida Penitentiary. The full story is told in the game.

    Who plays Lucia in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not officially announced Lucia's actor. Treat names online as unconfirmed.
