    GTA 6 Price and Editions: Standard vs Ultimate

    How much GTA 6 costs, what the Ultimate Edition adds, and what the Vintage Vice City pre-order bonus includes.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US.
    • Pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack.
    • Digital pre-orders also come with one month of GTA+.

    Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 pricing in June 2026, and pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026.

    GTA 6 editions compared

    Standard Edition Ultimate Edition
    US price $79.99 $99.99
    Full game Yes Yes
    Extra in-game items No Yes
    Vintage Vice City Pack (buy before Nov 20, 2026) Yes Yes
    One month of GTA+ (digital pre-order) Yes Yes

    Which edition should you buy?

    • Standard if you just want to play the story. Nothing in the Ultimate Edition is required to finish the game.
    • Ultimate if you want the extra in-game items from the start. Check the store listing for the exact contents in your region.

    Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

    Both editions include the Vintage Vice City Pack if you buy before November 20, 2026. It is themed around the original GTA: Vice City.

    Platforms

    GTA 6 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and no PC version has been announced; see GTA 6 on PC.

    Online purchases

    GTA 6 launches as a single-player game. See GTA 6 online for what that means.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is GTA 6?

    In the US, the Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Prices in other countries vary by store.

    Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it?

    It adds in-game items rather than story content. If you only want the game, the Standard Edition is enough, and an upgrade to Ultimate has been reported at $20 if you change your mind.

    What is the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

    The Vintage Vice City Pack, included with pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026.

    Is there a GTA 6 Collector's Edition?

    Rockstar has announced two editions: Standard and Ultimate. No physical Collector's Edition has been announced.
