    GTA 6 Controller: Limited Edition PS5 DualSense

    The GTA 6 controller is a limited edition PS5 DualSense in black or white for $84.99. Price by region, where to buy it, and whether a PS5 bundle exists.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Sony sells two limited edition GTA VI DualSense controllers: black and white.
    • Each costs $84.99, £74.99 or €84.99 and launched on November 19, 2026.
    • Sony has not announced an official PS5 console bundle with GTA 6.
    On this page 4
    1. Key facts
    2. What is different about it
    3. Is there a GTA 6 PS5 bundle?
    4. Do you need it?

    The official GTA 6 controller is a limited edition DualSense for PS5, sold in two colours. Both went on sale on November 19, 2026, the same day as the game.

    Key facts
    Models Black Limited Edition and White Limited Edition
    Price $84.99 / £74.99 / €84.99
    Release November 19, 2026
    Pre-orders opened September 10, 2026
    Where to buy White: PlayStation Direct and retailers. Black: PlayStation Direct only

    What is different about it

    The design has moulded palm tree detailing and the Roman numeral VI on the touchpad. Inside it is a standard DualSense: the same haptics, triggers and battery. It costs $15 more than a regular controller.

    Is there a GTA 6 PS5 bundle?

    No official one. Sony's own page sells the game by itself, and the only GTA-themed hardware so far is these two controllers. Some retailers put together their own console-plus-game offers, so compare the total with buying the items separately.

    Do you need it?

    No. Any PS5 controller plays the game the same way. This one is for collectors, and limited editions can sell out, so check stock at PlayStation Direct first.

    For the game itself, see GTA 6 price and editions.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is the GTA 6 controller?

    $84.99 in the US, £74.99 in the UK and €84.99 in Europe, for either colour.

    Is there a GTA 6 PS5 bundle?

    Sony has not announced an official console bundle. Some retailers sell their own console and game packages.

    Is there a GTA 6 Xbox controller?

    No official GTA 6 Xbox controller has been announced.
