    GTA 6 Platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PS4, Switch 2 and PC

    GTA 6 is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. It is not on PS4, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch 2 or PC. What each supported console offers.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
    • It is not on PS4 or Xbox One, and no Switch 2 or PC version has been announced.
    • It runs on Xbox Series S, and every console targets 30 FPS.

    GTA 6 is on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and nowhere else.

    Where you can play GTA 6

    Platform GTA 6? Notes
    PlayStation 5 Yes 30 FPS target
    PS5 Pro Yes 30 FPS target, ray-traced shadows, PSSR upscaling
    Xbox Series X Yes 30 FPS target
    Xbox Series S Yes 30 FPS target, at a lower resolution
    PlayStation 4 No Not supported
    Xbox One No No native version; Microsoft says Xbox Cloud Gaming can stream it at Series S settings
    Nintendo Switch 2 No Nothing announced
    PC No Nothing announced; see GTA 6 on PC

    Which console should you use?

    • You own a PS5 or Series X: you are set. They get the same game at the same frame rate target.
    • You own a Series S: it is supported. Expect a lower resolution, and note that the download uses a large share of a 512 GB drive.
    • You own a PS5 Pro: you get the best confirmed image quality, not a higher frame rate.
    • You own a PS4 or Xbox One: you need a newer console.

    Rockstar has not published a resolution table for each console.

    Storage on every platform

    Plan for up to 212 GB free. Details: GTA 6 file size.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will GTA 6 be on PS4?

    No. GTA 6 is only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    Will GTA 6 be on Xbox Series S?

    Yes. The Series S is supported and targets 30 FPS like the other consoles.

    Will GTA 6 be on Switch 2?

    No Nintendo Switch 2 version has been announced.

    Is GTA 6 better on PS5 Pro?

    The PS5 Pro adds ray-traced shadows and PSSR upscaling. The frame rate target is the same 30 FPS.
