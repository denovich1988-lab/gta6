    GTA 6 Ultimate Edition: What You Get for $99.99

    The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition costs $99.99 and adds 16 extra in-game items to the full game. What is included, and whether it is worth the extra $20.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • The Ultimate Edition costs $99.99, $20 more than the Standard Edition.
    • It adds an upgrade with 16 items: vehicles, weapons, clothing, locations and activities.
    • It is digital only; the boxed version is the Standard Edition.

    The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition costs $99.99 and adds 16 extra in-game items to the complete game. Nothing in it is needed to finish the story.

    Standard vs Ultimate

    Standard Ultimate
    US price $79.99 $99.99
    Full game Yes Yes

    What the upgrade contains

    Rockstar describes 16 exclusive additions in five groups:

    • Vehicles
    • Weapons
    • Clothing and outfits
    • Special locations such as garages and shops
    • In-game activities

    Check your console store listing for the exact list in your region.

    Is it worth the extra $20?

    • Buy Standard if you only want the story and the open world.
    • Buy Ultimate if you want more vehicles and gear from the first hours and do not mind a digital-only copy.

    There is no collector's edition and no deluxe edition.

    The pre-order bonus is separate and comes with both editions: see the Vintage Vice City Pack. For all prices, see GTA 6 price and editions.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

    The full game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: 16 extra items spanning vehicles, weapons, clothing, special locations and in-game activities.

    How much is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

    $99.99 in the US. The Standard Edition is $79.99.

    Is there a GTA 6 collector's edition?

    No collector's or deluxe edition has been released. There are two editions: Standard and Ultimate.
