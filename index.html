      Frequently asked questions

      What is GTA 6 and when does it come out?

      Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar Games’ open-world crime game set in the state of Leonida. It was released on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

      Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

      You play as two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple pulled into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida.

      Is GTA 6 on PC?

      Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date.

      Is GTA6Box an official Rockstar site?

      No. GTA6Box is an independent fan site. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
