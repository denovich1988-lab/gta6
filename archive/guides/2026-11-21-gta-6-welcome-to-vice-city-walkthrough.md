**This GTA 6 Welcome to Vice City walkthrough covers the first mission of Chapter 3, which introduces the city, two new contacts and your new home.** There is no combat and nothing to fail. What matters is knowing where the apartment door is and what the mission switches on afterwards.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Welcome to Vice City (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts | Automatically, on the highway into [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | Finish [Checking Out](/missions/gta-6-checking-out-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Chapter 3 apartment, Pay 'n' Spray car sales, a trophy |

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists eight on-screen steps; GTABase groups them into four.

1. Go to the Jack of Hearts.
2. Enter the Jack of Hearts.
3. Go to the back of the Jack of Hearts.
4. Throw money to the dancers.
5. Return to Dre'Quan when you are ready.
6. Go to the apartment.
7. Park at the apartment.
8. Go to the apartment.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive to the Jack of Hearts

After a short drive Jason calls Dre'Quan, who asks to meet at the Jack of Hearts, a club in Crosstown where he runs a record label. The car stops outside on its own.

### 2. Use the other entrance

Talk to the bouncer at the front door, then walk round to the other side of the building. Dre'Quan comes out there. Follow him through the club to the stairs.

### 3. Throw money, or don't

Dre'Quan offers you cash for the dancers. If you accept, walk up to a dancer, hold **L2** (LT on Xbox) to focus and press **R1** (RB) to throw. When the cash runs out, go back to Dre'Quan at the stairs.

### 4. Meet Boobie Ike

Follow Dre'Quan upstairs. His boss, Boobie Ike, lets the couple stay in an apartment he has nearby.

### 5. Move in

Return to the car and drive to the apartment. Park in the covered spot. Walk round to the front of the building, climb to the second floor and enter the first door in front of you. The mission ends there.

**Tip:** both guides note that the door opposite the parking spots can be used as a shortcut from now on.

## Where players fail

There is no fail state. The usual hold-ups are:

- **Waiting at the front door.** The bouncer will not let you in; go round the building.
- **Looking for the apartment door at the back.** For this mission you have to enter from the front.
