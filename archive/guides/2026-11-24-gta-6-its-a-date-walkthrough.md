**This GTA 6 It's a Date! walkthrough covers one of the shortest missions in the game: a drink for two at the Courtyard Bar.** It has two objectives and cannot go wrong. The only thing worth planning is when you do it, because the next story mission makes dates much harder.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | It's a Date! (Chapter 4, optional) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), together |
| Given by | Nobody; it is marked on the map |
| Starts at | Courtyard Bar, Stockyard, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | [Heavy Lifting](/missions/gta-6-heavy-lifting-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Relationship increase |

You have been here before: the Courtyard Bar is where Lucia can meet Raul to start [Crosstown Traffic](/missions/gta-6-crosstown-traffic-walkthrough/) in Chapter 3.

## Objectives

1. Go to the Courtyard Bar.
2. Buy a drink in the Courtyard Bar.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go together

The date marker appears as soon as Heavy Lifting ends. Both leads have to be there, so bring your partner along rather than arriving alone. If the two are apart, see [how to switch characters](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/) and meet up first.

### 2. Buy a drink

Walk into the bar area and buy a drink.

### 3. Dance, if you want

You can dance for a while. Both guides list it as optional.

### 4. Walk back to the street

Leave the bar area. The mission completes when you step back out onto the street.

## Why to do it now

[Making Gainz](/missions/gta-6-making-gainz-walkthrough/) unlocks a couple of in-game days after Heavy Lifting. Both guides warn that once it is done, Jason and Lucia cannot be seen together in public until the VCIA Takedown, and can only spend time together in the Y Hotel room. That makes this date, and any other couple activity, far easier to fit in beforehand.

**Tip:** treat It's a Date! as the first item on a short list. Anything that needs both characters in public, including partner goals from [GTA 6 romance](/blog/gta-6-romance/), is best done before you start Making Gainz.

## Where players fail

There is nothing to fail. Neither guide lists a fail condition or a time limit.
