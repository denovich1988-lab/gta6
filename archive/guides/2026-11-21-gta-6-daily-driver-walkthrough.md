**This GTA 6 Daily Driver walkthrough shows where to find the '82 Crawler and how to collect the full $1,750.** Moses wants the jeep taken back from clients who did not pay him. It is a short theft with armed company, and it is the best-paid job available at this point in Chapter 2. PowerPyx lists it as Steal the Crawler for Moses.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Daily Driver (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), solo |
| Given by | Moses, by text message |
| Starts at | Search area near [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) |
| Requirement | Finish [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $1,250, plus $500 for good condition |

## Objectives

1. Search the area for the '82 Crawler.
2. Deliver the '82 Crawler to Moses.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Read the text

A little while after the Moses mission, he sends a picture of the car. Opening the message marks the search area on your map.

### 2. Find the jeep

The '82 Canis Crawler is a white jeep parked in the middle of the search area, by the old Traders' Union building. VGC describes the place as a scrap yard with a fence around it.

### 3. Get to the car

Several people are standing around it and turn hostile if they see Lucia.

| Approach | How | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet | Climb the fence and sneak to the jeep | VGC |
| Loud | Shoot the guards first | VGC, PowerPyx |
| Fast | Run straight to the car and drive away | PowerPyx, GTABase |

**Tip:** the bonus depends on the car's condition, so a firefight around the jeep works against you. Sneaking in, or sprinting to the door, keeps the bodywork clean.

### 4. Drive out

VGC says to leave through the gate. Moses's garage in Port Gellhorn is close.

### 5. Deliver it

Park the Crawler in the garage to finish the mission. Drive carefully on the way: every dent counts against the $500.

## Where players fail

- **Shooting near the jeep.** Stray bullets cost you the condition bonus.
- **Crashing on the short drive back.** There is no timer in any of the guides, so take it slowly.
- **Fighting everyone.** You only need the car.
