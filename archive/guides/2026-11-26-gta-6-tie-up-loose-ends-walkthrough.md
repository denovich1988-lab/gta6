**This GTA 6 Tie Up Loose Ends walkthrough explains the quiet stretch after the airport heist, where the only objective is to wait.** There is nothing to fight and nowhere to go. What matters is knowing how to shorten the wait, and what to finish before you end it, because the mission that follows closes off free roam.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Tie Up Loose Ends (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts | Automatically, after the [VCIA Takedown](/missions/gta-6-vcia-takedown-walkthrough/) |
| Map marker | None; it appears in the story list with a pink icon as a main mission |
| Reward | None |

The guides differ on the giver (PowerPyx lists Jason and Lucia themselves, GTABase lists Raul), but they agree on everything you actually do.

| Source | Objectives |

## Step-by-step

### 1. Wait for Raul

After the heist, Raul is supposed to get in touch about selling the hard drive. He does not. GTABase is direct about it: the call never comes, no matter how long you wait.

### 2. Take Valentina's call

Valentina phones with an update on the sale and says you can call her back if you want things to move faster.

### 3. Call Valentina back

| Way to call | How |
|---|---|
| Phone | Open Jason's or Lucia's phone and dial Valentina |
| Map | Select the objective on the map; the call is placed automatically |

**Tip:** the call is optional, but both guides say it makes the next mission appear much sooner. If you skip it, the wait is simply longer.

### 4. Use the time

This is the last comfortable window in Chapter 4. Starting the next mission, [Sunset](/missions/gta-6-sunset-walkthrough/), moves the story into Chapter 5, and PowerPyx says most of Chapter 5 has to be finished before the open world is available again.

Things worth doing first:

1. Play Yasmani's optional bank job, [That's Where the Money Is…](/missions/gta-6-thats-where-the-money-is-walkthrough/). It opens during this wait and is worth about $80,000.
2. Finish any open side missions; see [Strangers and Freaks](/missions/gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/).
3. Make a manual save you will not overwrite: [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).
4. Check your progress against the [100% completion checklist](/missions/gta-6-100-completion/).

### 5. Collect the note

After more time passes, a note is left for you at the front desk of the Y Hotel. PowerPyx points out that this is the hotel's front desk, not Santino's desk. Picking it up starts Sunset and completes Tie Up Loose Ends.

## Where players fail

You cannot fail this mission, and GTABase says it is not missable: it always ends when Sunset begins. The mistake to avoid is ending it too early.

- **Picking up the note before you are ready.** Everything optional should be done first.
- **Waiting for Raul.** Nothing happens until Valentina's call is dealt with and time passes.
