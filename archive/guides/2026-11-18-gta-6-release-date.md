**GTA 6 releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## GTA 6 timeline

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| December 2023 | Trailer 1 reveals Leonida, Vice City and Lucia, with a 2025 release window |
| May 2, 2025 | Rockstar delays the game to May 26, 2026 |
| May 6, 2025 | Trailer 2 focuses on Jason and Lucia; the official site adds characters and locations |
| November 6, 2025 | Rockstar moves the release to November 19, 2026 |
| June 2026 | Price and editions confirmed; pre-orders open on June 25 |
| November 19, 2026 | Release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S |

## Platforms

- **PlayStation 5** (including PS5 Pro)
- **Xbox Series X|S**

There is no version for PS4, Xbox One or Nintendo Switch. For PC, see [GTA 6 PC release date](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/).
