**This GTA 6 Party Hard walkthrough covers Valentina's security job, which starts as a chauffeur run and turns into the biggest action mission of early Chapter 4.** You switch between Jason and Lucia several times, fight hand to hand, sneak across a hostage floor and end up flying a helicopter. PowerPyx subtitles it Work Personal Security.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Party Hard (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Valentina |
| Starts at | Vice City International Airport (VCIA), Vice-Dale County |
| Requirement | [Lawyer Up](/missions/gta-6-lawyer-up-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None listed |

The client is Andrés de León, a telenovela star arriving for the opening of the Megamundo Network headquarters. Two other names matter later: César Navarro, the network's CEO, and Petra Navarro, its COO and Andrés's wife.

**Tip:** make a manual save before you meet Valentina. The mission is long and cannot be replayed afterwards. See [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists twelve on-screen objectives; GTABase groups the mission into nine stages.

1. Follow the car.
2. Pick up Andrés' order.
3. Drive to the Megamundo offices.
4. Follow Andrés.
5. Follow Petra.
6. Check on Andrés.
7. Pick up the radio.
8. Go to the stairwell.
9. Eliminate the gunmen.
10. Rescue the Navarros.
11. Get in the helicopter.
12. Land on the helipad.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive Andrés out of the airport

Meet Valentina at VCIA, then drive Andrés out of the airport behind the lead car.

### 2. Make the detour to Little Cuba

Andrés wants a drink. Go to the marked spot in Little Cuba to collect his order, then drive on to the Megamundo offices in Downtown.

### 3. Join the party

Follow Andrés into the building. After the scene in the lobby, follow Petra and the others into the elevator. Upstairs, go to the marked conference room to check on Andrés.

### 4. The attack (Jason)

Gunmen from a group both guides call FAP storm the floor and take Andrés. One stays behind.

1. Melee him to knock the gun out of his hand, then knock him out.
2. Go into the hallway and fight through the enemies there.
3. When the floor is clear, pick up the radio from the marked body.

### 5. The stairwell (Lucia)

The game switches to Lucia. Crouch-walk along the left side of the room to the stairwell, where an attacker jumps her. PowerPyx gives the prompts:

| Moment | Input on PS5 (Xbox) |
|---|---|
| Fight back, then kick the gun away | Mash Square (X) |
| Fight from the floor | Hold R2 (RT) and mash Square (X) |
| Finish him with the shoe | Triangle (Y) when prompted |

### 6. Up through the building

- **Jason:** keep fighting while you follow the floor round to the left. More enemies come out of the offices. When it is clear, take the stairs up.
- **Lucia:** shoot the man in the elevator, climb the stairs to a hole in the wall, climb into the vent and crawl through until you drop out on the party floor.

### 7. Clear the party floor (Lucia)

This is the hard part. Lucia has a silenced pistol and no spare ammunition, and there are hostages on the lower level.

- Use Focus to highlight enemies before you move: **L3 + R3** when you are not aiming (PowerPyx).
- Take gunmen one at a time from cover. GTABase's tip is the same: being seen turns the whole floor against you.
- Firing an unsilenced weapon has the same effect as being spotted.
- Do not hit the hostages downstairs.

When every gunman is down, go to the lower floor and untie Valentina, then check the marked door. The Navarros are hiding behind it.

### 8. The helicopter (Jason)

Switch to Jason and sprint to the roof. The kidnappers' helicopter is already lifting off.

1. Press **X** (A) to jump on.
2. Mash **Square** (X) to fight the kidnappers inside.
3. Press **X** (A) when prompted to dodge.
4. Once they are kicked out, hold **R2** (RT) and mash **X** (A) to pull Andrés back in.

### 9. Land on the helipad

Take the controls, fly back to the Megamundo building and land on the rooftop helipad. That completes the mission. If you have not flown much yet, our [planes and helicopters](/vehicles/gta-6-planes-helicopters/) page lists what else you can fly.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists fail conditions. The stage that costs people time is the party floor: there is no spare ammunition, so missed shots matter, and one alert makes every remaining gunman search for you. Use Focus before each shot rather than after.
