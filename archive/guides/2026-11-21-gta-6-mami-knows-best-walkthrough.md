**This GTA 6 Mami Knows Best walkthrough covers Lucia's first mission with her mother in Vice City, which starts as yard work and ends with an excavator.** It is easy, with one moment where you have to react quickly. PowerPyx subtitles it Catch Up With Mia.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive to Hamlet

Meet Mia in Peacock Bay, get in her car and drive to the marked neighbourhood in Hamlet, where she has a house to sell.

### 2. Look around

Follow Mia inside and walk through the rooms while she inspects them. The place is in no state to sell. Go back towards the entrance and interact with Mia to help her up.

### 3. Clear the lawn

Pick up each piece of junk on the front lawn, carry it to the blue dumpster and press **R2** (RT on Xbox) to throw it in. Keep going while Mia is on the phone.

### 4. The mattress and the alligator

Follow Mia to the backyard and interact with the mattress in the pool. Hold **L2 + R2** (LT + RT) and tap **X** (A) to drag it out.

An alligator comes out with it and grabs Mia's leg. Shoot it quickly.

### 5. The neighbours

Members of the M7 gang from the house next door hold you at gunpoint, tell you to stay away and leave.

### 6. The excavator

Mia points you to an excavator across the street. Drive it to the gang's house and damage the building and the cars in the driveway. Press **R1** (RB) to close the claw; you can pick the cars up and throw them around.

### 7. Back to the office

When enough is destroyed, get back in Mia's car and drive to her office nearby to finish.

## Where players fail

- **Reacting slowly at the pool.** Have a gun ready before you touch the mattress. Alligators are one of more than 170 species in the game; see [GTA 6 animals](/map/gta-6-animals/).
- **Leaving the excavator early.** The prompt to return to the car only appears after enough damage.
