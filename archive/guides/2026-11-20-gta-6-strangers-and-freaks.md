**GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks are the optional side missions given by characters you meet around Leonida, and there are more than 35 of them spread over the first four chapters.** This page lists every one that appears on both major lists, GTABase and PowerPyx, with the giver, who can play it and the chapter. Where the two disagree, we say so. The status is "partly confirmed" because neither list gives rewards, and a few details differ.

## How Strangers and Freaks work

- **Finding them.** PowerPyx says they usually show as Optional Missions in the map's quest list or as purple side-activity markers; some are unmarked.
- **Strands.** Each character has a chain of missions. After one, do something else until the character calls or texts; the next part can take in-game days and can cross into the next chapter.
- **Characters.** Many can be played as either lead, but some are for [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) only or [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) only.
- **After the story.** Both lists say strands stay available in free roam afterwards, apart from the missable ones.
- **No replay.** See [can you replay missions in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-replay-missions/).

## Which strands count for the trophy

The Strangers and Freaks trophy asks for **8 strands**.

| Counts | Does not count | Not labelled yet |
|---|---|---|
| Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete | Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone, Eddie Acosta, Shirley | Bret, Randy, Dunk |

PowerPyx adds Cal and Moses to the "does not count" side. Only four strands are confirmed as counting so far, which is fewer than the eight required, so this table is incomplete.

**Tip:** PowerPyx suggests checking your trophy progress before and after the last mission of a strand to see whether it counted. More in the [trophy guide](/missions/gta-6-trophies/).

## Missable missions

| Mission | Why |
|---|---|
| Get Shirley's Rum | Chapter 1 only |
| Steal the Scooter | Chapter 1 only |
| Race Against Yago | Time-sensitive |

Chapter 1's free roam ends at [Tourist Trap](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/), so do Shirley's two before then. Both lists say missable missions are not required for [100% completion](/missions/gta-6-100-completion/), which PowerPyx says counts 100 optional tasks.
