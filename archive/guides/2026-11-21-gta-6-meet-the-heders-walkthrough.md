**This GTA 6 Meet the Heders walkthrough covers a dinner that turns into a car theft: Lucia breaks into a shipping container and drives a very fast car out of a moving trailer.** It also hands you a permanent tool. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Meet Brian & Lori.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Meet the Heders (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) at dinner, [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) for the job |
| Given by | Brian Heder |
| Starts at | The bag of clothes in the bedroom, Bayside apartment |
| Requirement | [Dealer's Choice](/missions/gta-6-dealers-choice-walkthrough/), Cal's visit about the hard drive, then a wait of a day or two |
| Reward | Cut-off tool |

## Objectives

1. Go to the restaurant.
2. Meet Brian at the bar.
3. Go to the van.
4. Open the van's rear doors.
5. Go to the dockyard.
6. Search the dockyard for the SCOOP containers.
7. Break into the container.
8. Steal the car from the container.
9. Break the car out of the container.
10. Lose the cops.
11. Drive to the lockup.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Dinner on the rooftop

Drive Lucia to the restaurant on the east side of Vice City and take the elevator up. At the bar you can pick up the two drinks and hand one to Lucia, then follow everyone to the table.

**Tip:** when the waiter comes, order the **Surf and Turf** that Lucia asked for. Both guides report a relationship bonus. More on that system in [GTA 6 romance](/blog/gta-6-romance/).

### 2. Talk to Brian

When the women leave, join Brian at the railing. He offers a cigar; accept or decline. When prompted, switch to Lucia: hold **Right on the D-pad**, then press **R1** (RB on Xbox).

### 3. The van

As Lucia, cross the bridge. A white van sits in the parking lot on the right. Open its rear doors. Lucia changes into a disguise and ends up in the driver's seat.

### 4. Into the dockyard

Drive south to the dockyard gate and wait a few seconds to be let in. Park nearby.

### 5. Find and cut the container

Look for the yellow SCOOP containers. The target is the one on top of the central stack. Interact with its door to use the cut-off tool, and hold **R2** (RT) so the bar stays inside the marked section, which cuts faster.

The lower containers open the same way. The first has a cigar and $600 in small boxes.

### 6. Chase the trailer

A scene shows gang members hauling the container away on a trailer. Take a nearby bike and catch up; men in the truck bed shoot at you. Get close to the back of the container and hold **Square** (X) to jump on.

### 7. Break the car out

Interact with the car inside and hold **L2** (LT) until it breaks free and reverses out.

### 8. Lose the police and deliver

The car is very fast. Both guides give the same advice: stay on the highway, do not crash and keep driving until the police give up. Then take it to the lockup in the north-west corner of the city and drive inside.

## Where players fail

- **Leaving the highway during the escape.** Side streets make a crash more likely in a car this quick. For how the search works, see [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
- **Hanging back from the trailer.** The jump prompt only appears close to the container.
- **Letting the cutting bar drift.** Keeping it in the marked zone is what speeds up the cut.
