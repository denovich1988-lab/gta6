**This GTA 6 How Do I Look? walkthrough covers the second half of Lucia's makeover: after the haircut in New Look, she needs clothes.** It is a shopping mission with a single objective and no way to fail, and it is a good moment to learn how clothing stores work.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | How Do I Look? (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Lucia (self-started) |
| Location | Bargain World, south [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) |
| Requirement | Finish [Girl's Trip](/missions/gta-6-girls-trip-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None listed |

## Objectives

1. Buy a new outfit: a top and pants.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go to Bargain World

The store is in the south of Port Gellhorn. The mission description offers a choice between shopping around and getting cheap clothes here; both guides send you to Bargain World.

### 2. Buy a top

Browse the tops and buy one. There are plenty of options and none is required.

### 3. Buy pants or shorts

Add a pair of pants or shorts. With both pieces bought, the mission completes.

**Tip:** the objective needs two separate items. A top alone will not finish it.

## If you are short of cash

PowerPyx repeats the advice from [New Look](/missions/gta-6-new-look-walkthrough/): mug someone or rob another store's register. If you have finished [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/), selling an untracked car is the lower-risk way to fund both errands.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. The usual hold-up is buying only one of the two pieces.
