**This GTA 6 Pipework walkthrough covers the seventh mission, a short indoor job where Jason has to unblock a tenant's sink before she will talk about rent.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Collect Mandy's Rent". There is no combat and no way to fail.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Pipework (mission 7, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Collect Mandy's Rent |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Mandy's house in Key Lento |
| Requirement | Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch and Unwanted Attention (PowerPyx) |
| Reward | Macizo Cigar (PowerPyx); rent money (VGC) |
| Next mission | [Hey Arnold!](/missions/gta-6-hey-arnold-walkthrough/) |

If the marker is missing, finish the four earlier missions first: [Gear Up](/missions/gta-6-gear-up-walkthrough/), [Lab Fees](/missions/gta-6-lab-fees-walkthrough/), [Just a Scratch](/missions/gta-6-just-a-scratch-walkthrough/) and [Unwanted Attention](/missions/gta-6-unwanted-attention-walkthrough/).

## Objectives

1. Follow Mandy.
2. Pick up the plunger.
3. Fix the sink.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Talk to Mandy

Drive to the house and talk to Mandy outside. She is one of Brian's tenants, and she will not pay until her sink is fixed. Follow her in. PowerPyx notes that you can let her keep talking or cut in and ask where the plunger is.

### 2. Pick up the plunger

The plunger is on the floor of the **bathroom**, across from the bedroom.

### 3. Plunge the sink

Go to the kitchen, interact with the sink and tap **R3** to work the plunger.

### 4. Clear the pipe

The second half happens under the sink. The guides word it differently:

| Source | What you do |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx | Press R2 (RT) to use a wrench on the pipe |
| VGC | Move under the sink and clear the pipes in a short minigame |

Follow the on-screen prompt. Mandy keeps flirting with Jason throughout.

### 5. The conversation afterwards

When the sink is fixed, Mandy makes a pass at Jason. You can interrupt or hear her out. PowerPyx reports the same result either way: she offers a cigar for the repair, Jason says no to the rest, and she throws the cigar box out into the street.

### 6. Leave the house

Head outside. PowerPyx says to pick up the cigar from the ground on your way out. VGC says you collect the money from Mandy and that Brian phones as you leave.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. The two things players get stuck on are:

- **Not finding the plunger.** It is in the bathroom, not the kitchen.
- **Walking past the cigar.** If you want it, pick it up before you drive off.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |

| Reward | Macizo Cigar, if you pick it up (PowerPyx) |

## Does the choice matter?

Not for romance. VGC says plainly that Jason cannot romance Mandy however you handle the visit, and that the relationship with her is still intact when you leave. The dialogue choice only changes how much of her pitch you hear. Jason's story is with [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), who returns four missions later in [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/).

After Pipework, wait a few minutes: PowerPyx reports that a call from Brian starts the next mission, [Hey Arnold!](/missions/gta-6-hey-arnold-walkthrough/). The full order is in the [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
