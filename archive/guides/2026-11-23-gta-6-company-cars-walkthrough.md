**This GTA 6 Company Cars walkthrough covers the mission where Jason and Lucia each pick a free high-end car, paid for by Valentina.** It is short and has no combat, but you keep both cars, so it helps to know what is on offer before you sit in the first driver's seat. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Speak to the Concierge.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Company Cars (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Santino, the Y Hotel concierge |
| Starts at | Y Hotel lobby, Washington Beach (Vice Beach) |
| Requirement | [Pool Day](/missions/gta-6-pool-day-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Two cars, one for each lead |

The lobby marker can show a lock right after Pool Day. Both guides say to ignore it and talk to Santino.

## Objectives

1. Speak to the salesman.
2. Follow Rob.
3. Choose a car for Jason.
4. Choose a car for Lucia.
5. Chase Lucia.
6. Race to the Y Hotel.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Talk to Santino

In the lobby, Santino asks whether you want to go to the dealership now or later. Say now, and the hotel's car service drives you straight to Status Auto Imports.

**Tip:** the car service is worth remembering. Both guides say it works as a free taxi from the front of the Y Hotel at any time. For other ways to get around, see [GTA 6 fast travel](/map/gta-6-fast-travel/).

### 2. Meet the salesman

Inside the dealership, a salesman is arguing with another man beside a red car. Talk to him, then follow Rob to the group of cars on the other side of the showroom.

**Tip:** the showroom is full of high-end cars. Scan them with the WAiNK app while you are there; both guides note that they count towards your collection. See [GTA 6 collectibles](/map/gta-6-collectibles/).

### 3. Pick two cars

Jason chooses first, then Lucia. To choose, get into the driver's seat and confirm (X on PS5, A on Xbox, per PowerPyx).

| If you want | Pick |
|---|---|
| The fastest car | Pfister Comet S2, which has the best base stats |
| A balanced pair | The truck or the SUV as the second car |

Both cars become yours. You can upgrade them, customise them or sell them later, so a pick you regret is not wasted.

### 4. Race back to the Y Hotel

Outside, follow Lucia as she drives ahead. She challenges you to a race back to the hotel. Damage does not matter because the cars are already yours, though Lucia will make fun of you for it. Reaching the hotel completes the mission.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. Take your time in the showroom: the choice is confirmed as soon as you accept the prompt in the driver's seat.
