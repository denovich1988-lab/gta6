**To change clothes in GTA 6, use the wardrobe at your safehouse; to change hair, visit a barber or stylist.** Clothing is more than cosmetic in this game, because your outfit is part of what the police use to recognise you. This page covers where to change, how saved outfits work, and what is different for Jason and Lucia.

## Clothes and hair at a glance

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Change clothes | Wardrobe at your safehouse |
| Try and change in a shop | Fitting rooms in some clothing stores (PPE.pl) |
| Quick change in the field | An outfit saved to your car trunk |
| Haircut, hair colour | Barber shops and stylists |
| Facial hair (Jason) | Barbers |
| Makeup and nails (Lucia) | Beauty parlours, lip icon on the map |

## How to change clothes

Everything you buy or collect goes to the wardrobe in your safehouse, and that is where you put outfits together. VGC points out one catch: an item you take in the world, such as a hat from someone you knocked out, is not kept until you have been back to the safehouse.

Clothing stores are the other place to change. PPE.pl describes fitting rooms at the back of some shops where you can try items and walk out wearing them.

## Saved outfits and the car trunk

You can keep more than one outfit in the trunk of [Jason's](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) or [Lucia's](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) car. Two things to know:

1. **It is not automatic.** VGC says you assign an outfit to the car from the safehouse wardrobe menu, even for clothes you have just bought.
2. **There are limits.** PPE.pl reports up to 10 named outfits in the wardrobe and up to 5 in the trunk. No second source gives those numbers yet.

**Tip:** put a plain spare outfit in the trunk before any robbery.

## Why your outfit matters to the police

The red heat icons under your wanted stars show what the police know: your face, your clothes and your vehicle. Changing clothes removes one of those. VGC calls it the quickest way to bring a wanted level down, and PPE.pl says the game itself suggests grabbing an outfit from the nearest clothing store if you are being chased with nothing to change into.

The full system is explained in [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

## How to change hair

Go to a barber shop or stylist and pay for the change. VGC lists what is on offer:

| Character | At barbers and stylists | Extra |
|---|---|---|
| Jason | Haircuts, hair colour, facial hair | - |
| Lucia | Haircuts, hair colour | Beauty parlours (lip icon) for makeup and nails |

Each lead has signature hairstyles alongside the standard ones. Not every style is sold everywhere: VGC notes that some cuts are only available at certain locations.

You get one haircut early as part of the story: a visit to the barber is one of the optional errands before [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/).

## Free outfits from the story

Some clothes come from missions. In [Day Release](/missions/gta-6-day-release-walkthrough/) Lucia picks a dress that VGC says she keeps, and in [Home Sweet Home](/missions/gta-6-home-sweet-home-walkthrough/) one escape route ends with a unique jacket.

## Pre-order and edition items

Hairstyles and outfits from the pre-order pack can be selected at the same barbers and wardrobes once you own them, according to VGC. What is in each pack is covered in [Vintage Vice City Pack](/buying/gta-6-vintage-vice-city-pack/) and [GTA 6 Ultimate Edition](/buying/gta-6-ultimate-edition/).

## What is not confirmed

- **Prices.** No guide lists what clothes or haircuts cost.
- **Tattoos.** A tattoo shop was named before launch; how tattoos work in the released game is not documented in the guides we checked.
- **Button prompts.** Only one guide gives fitting-room controls, without naming the platform.
- **Unlock timing.** No guide says exactly when each shop type opens.

We will add shop names, locations and prices when two sources agree on them.
