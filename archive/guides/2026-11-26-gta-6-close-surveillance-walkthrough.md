**This GTA 6 Close Surveillance walkthrough covers the drone mission where Jason and Karim spy on Nate and Cisco to find out who their inside man at the airport is.** There is no shooting and no driving until the last minute. Everything depends on keeping the drone close enough to listen and far enough away not to be noticed.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Close Surveillance (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) only |
| Starts at | Top floor of the marked parking garage, Ocean Beach, Vice Beach |
| You meet | Karim, at his van |
| Requirement | [Power Play](/missions/gta-6-power-play-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | No cash; Professional bonus for returning the drone |

The two guides credit a different mission giver: PowerPyx names Karim, GTABase names Raul. Either way, Karim is the person waiting for you.

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists thirteen on-screen steps; GTABase groups the same route into ten.

1. Fly the drone to The Calique.
2. Search the roof for the bodybuilders.
3. Follow Nate and Francisco.
4. Search the alley for the bodybuilders' car.
5. Follow Nate and Francisco.
6. Enter the apartment.
7. Place cameras around the apartment.
8. Listen to the interrogation.
9. Escape the apartment.
10. Fly to the van.
11. Get in the car.
12. Drop Lucia in the alley.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Karim and launch the drone

Go up to the top floor of the garage. Karim handles the microphone and Jason flies. Both guides describe the controls the same way: the drone handles much like a helicopter, holds its height by itself, and the triggers move it up and down.

### 2. Find Nate and Cisco at The Calique

Fly to the marked building, a rooftop bar. Head for the white boxes on the roof; the two men are sitting under the smaller white shelter on the north side.

### 3. Listen in

Move close enough to fill the bar at the bottom of the screen. That bar is your range for the microphone.

When they leave by the elevator, do not follow them down. Fly to the alley on the right of the elevator to find Cisco's car.

### 4. Follow the car

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Stay high above the car | Drop to street level |
| Keep the bar in the green | Let the car get out of range |

They stop once so Nate can collect something from a nearby building, then drive on to an apartment block.

### 5. Get into the apartment

Karim points out a balcony: it is the third one up, and its door is open. Fly in. GTABase gives the address as apartment 405; the man who lives there, Omar, runs baggage handling at the airport.

### 6. Place the six cameras

Omar walks around while you work. Watch the awareness indicator and stay in a different room from him.

| # | Room | Spot |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Living room | Beside the TV |
| 2 | Living room | Shelf on the opposite wall |
| 3 | Kitchen | Corner of the counter (after Omar leaves the kitchen) |
| 4 | Kitchen | Top of the fridge |
| 5 | Hall | Shelf near the front door |
| 6 | Bedroom | Shelf to the right of the bed (after Omar opens the bedroom door) |

**Tip:** if Omar walks toward the drone, GTABase's advice is to tuck it out of sight and wait for him to leave the room before you carry on.

### 7. Watch the interrogation

Nate and Cisco arrive and lean on Omar. Switch between your cameras to keep them in view while they talk.

### 8. Get the drone out

Karim broadcasts through the drone's speaker by mistake. Fly out of the apartment at once, before the men find it, and return to the van on the street, marked with a green square.

**Tip:** bring the drone all the way back. Both guides say retrieving it is what earns the Professional bonus; GTABase adds that the bonus counts toward your Criminal Profile.

### 9. Drop Lucia off

After a scene at the van about the hard drive, get in the car with [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) and drive her to the alley near Ajax Gym. The mission ends there.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a formal fail condition. These are the points they warn about:

- **Losing the car.** Dropping low to follow it costs you sight lines; height keeps the bar green.
- **Being noticed in the apartment.** Place cameras only in rooms Omar has left.
- **Leaving the drone behind.** You lose the Professional bonus if it does not make it back to the van.
