**This GTA 6 trophy guide lists all 55 trophies and achievements, what each one asks for, and the order to do them in.** The list is the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Story trophies are kept vague here to avoid spoilers. We only give a requirement when at least two guides agree on it.

## Missable GTA 6 achievements

None of the story trophies can be missed. Two guides flag one trophy as possibly missable:

- **Positive Mental Attitude.** One of the 18 meditation spots is tied to Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether it stays available later. Meditate when the game first offers it in Chapter 1 to be safe.

Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

## GTA 6 platinum roadmap

1. **Play the story.** This gives 12 trophies. Meditate in Chapter 1. Do stranger missions as they appear.
2. **Finish the stranger strands.** Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have a trophy, and 8 strands in total give Strangers & Freaks. Kenton is away for a while after Chapter 1 and comes back later.
3. **Work through the 100% checklist.** It shows up after the story. It covers most other trophies on the way, because it asks for more: 300 stunts against 250, 70 challenges against 50, 200 vehicles against 100. See [GTA 6 100% completion](/missions/gta-6-100-completion/).
4. **Clean up the one-off trophies.** The tank, the airport, the jewelry store, 4-star escape and the hidden ones.

Two trophies need more than the 100% checklist does. Positive Mental Attitude needs all 18 meditation spots, while 100% needs 15. Top Fun needs a win at every drone race location.
