**This GTA 6 Here, Kitty Kitty walkthrough covers a short tracking job for Tyrone: find a man called Charles, follow him through a camp and bring back what he was carrying.** There is no shooting if you play it as the guides suggest. PowerPyx subtitles it Speak with Tyrone.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Here, Kitty Kitty (Chapter 3) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) only |
| Given by | Tyrone, the dealer from [Dealer's Choice](/missions/gta-6-dealers-choice-walkthrough/) |
| Starts at | Apartment complex in La Perle, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | Part 1 of [Dubious Provenance](/missions/gta-6-dubious-provenance-walkthrough/) |
| Bring | $20 in cash if you want the quick route |
| Reward | Tyrone as a goods drop-off |

GTABase lists a separate entry called Tyrone just before this one, described as Jason going to see Tyrone about work; PowerPyx treats that visit as the start of this mission.

## Objectives

1. Go to Charles' apartment.
2. Find out where Charles is.
3. Go to the camp in La Perle.
4. Chase after Charles.
5. Approach Charles.
6. Search Charles' body.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Charles' apartment

Tyrone wants Charles found and says he lives in Rockridge. At the apartment you have two options.

| Option | What to do |
|---|---|
| Pay | Give the man in the doorway $20 for a tip. You need the cash on you. |
| Break in | Nobody is home. Inspect the Aline's Thrift Store receipt in the bedroom; Jason asks Tyrone, who supplies an address. |

### 2. The camp

Go to the homeless camp on the north-west edge of La Perle and break open the door on the ground floor.

### 3. The chase

Charles runs when he sees you. Follow him up the stairs on the far side of the building. If anyone attacks on the way, use your fists.

### 4. The top floor

Charles goes over the wall at the top. Take the stairs back down and search him. You pick up a bag of Kitty Litter, and the mission ends.

## Where players fail

- **Arriving with no cash.** The $20 tip is only offered if you are carrying the money. Cash in your pocket is also at risk if you are wasted, so do not carry more than you need.
- **Using guns in the camp.** Melee keeps the chase simple, and both guides advise it.
