**This GTA 6 Just Moved In walkthrough lists the eight supplies Lucia has to buy and the quickest way to finish the errand.** Jason cannot leave the motel bed, so Lucia stocks the room. It is a shopping trip with no enemies, but one item list differs between guides and there is a small catch on the way back.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Just Moved In (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Jason and Lucia |
| Starts at | Starlet Motel, Kelly County |
| Requirement | Finish [What's in the Bag?](/missions/gta-6-whats-in-the-bag-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Relationship increase |

## Objectives

1. Buy supplies.
2. Return to the motel.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Cross the road

Go to the gas station opposite the motel and enter its convenience store. PowerPyx and GTABase call it the Alpha convenience store; VGC just says the grocery store.

### 2. Collect the eight items

The items show on the minimap. PowerPyx notes that this depends on minimap markers being switched on in the interface settings.

| Item | PowerPyx and GTABase | VGC |
|---|---|---|
| Soda | Yes | Yes |
| Protein bars | Yes | Yes |
| Gauze | Yes | Yes |
| Bandana | Yes | Yes |
| Hair clippers | Yes | Yes |
| Makeup bag | Yes | Yes |
| Fruit | Fruit | Apples |
| Eighth item | Soup | Soap |

Six items match everywhere. The fruit is the same thing under two names; for the last one, simply follow the final marker.

**Tip:** VGC suggests Focus Mode (press L3 and R3 together) if you cannot spot an item on the shelves.

### 3. Walk out

Each item is charged the moment you take it. There is no need to queue at the counter.

### 4. Return to the room

Take the supplies back to your room at the motel and speak to Jason.

VGC adds a warning: if you drove and you wreck the vehicle or wander too far from it, you are sent back to the store. The shop is across the street, so walking avoids the problem.

## What happens next

VGC's guide continues the scene: the motel manager comes to talk about the rent, and Jason asks for stronger painkillers, which sends Lucia to Vicki in Room 10. PowerPyx and GTABase treat those as separate missions: [Overnight Charges](/missions/gta-6-overnight-charges-walkthrough/) and [Girl's Trip](/missions/gta-6-girls-trip-walkthrough/).

## Where players fail

- **Missing an item.** Count eight before you leave.
- **Losing the car.** Keep any vehicle intact and close, or go on foot.
- **Minimap markers off.** Turn them on if nothing appears.
