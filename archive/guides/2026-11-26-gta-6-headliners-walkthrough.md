**This GTA 6 Headliners walkthrough covers the Dimez' launch party at Club VYB, where Jason and Lucia run the door, the lights and the VIP tables before the night goes wrong.** It is the one mission in this part of the story where your choices change the payout, so the table picks are worth getting right.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Headliners (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/); either can start it |
| Given by | Dre'Quan |
| Starts at | Club VYB, Washington Beach, Vice Beach |
| Requirement | The other Chapter 4 missions before the airport heist |
| Reward | Up to around $50,000, Brass Knuckles |

The two guides word the requirement differently. PowerPyx says every other Chapter 4 main mission before the VCIA Takedown must be done; GTABase says the heist's setup jobs. It follows Dre'Quan's earlier missions [Watch It](/missions/gta-6-watch-it-walkthrough/) and [Fresh Evidence](/missions/gta-6-fresh-evidence-walkthrough/).

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists thirteen on-screen steps.

1. Test the lights.
2. Assign the VIP tables.
3. Check on Maleek Rivers.
4. Check on KD.
5. Take a picture of KD.
6. Meet up with Dre'Quan.
7. Open the car door.
8. Follow Dre'Quan.
9. Escort Sexxy Red to the dance floor.
10. Free up a table.
11. Follow Jason.
12. Grab DWNPLY.
13. Watch the show.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Tour the club and test the lights

Find Dre'Quan near the entrance and follow him around. Then, as Lucia, go to the DJ booth and try the lights and effects. Use the exit prompt when you are done.

### 2. Assign the VIP tables (Jason)

Each guest shows two numbers: an **offer**, paid up front, and a **followers** count, which can bring in more money later. You have three tables.

| Guest | Why both guides pick them |
|---|---|
| Donatello Lambrini (you deal with his assistant) | The most followers |
| The Game Developer | The highest upfront offer |
| KD | The second-most followers, and history with Dre'Quan |

Hold the confirm prompt to lock in the three.

### 3. Look after the guests (Lucia)

- **Maleek Rivers:** go to his booth. He wants the lights to pick him out. Both guides recommend agreeing, because of his following.
- **KD:** if you let him in, Dre'Quan sends you to his table. Agree to the photo and take a picture of him with his group.

### 4. Work the door (Jason)

In the lobby, a man claims he has already been inside. You can throw him out or let him stay; neither guide ties a consequence to it. Go out front to Dre'Quan and, when the cars pull up, open the door of the one on his right. Follow him back in, then lead the group down the stairs to the dance floor and into the VIP area.

### 5. Free up a table

Dre'Quan needs a table back, so one of your three groups has to move.

**Tip:** both guides say to move the Game Developer if you seated them. You keep their $50,000 offer, they bring few extra followers, and they accept the "upgrade" story most easily.

### 6. Take down DWNPLY (Lucia)

DWNPLY and his crew take over the stage. From the green room, follow Jason onto the balcony, drop to the lower level and go to the front right, directly below DWNPLY. Pull him off the stage. When his crew grabs Lucia, mash the prompt to break free and fight off the men who attack.

### 7. Fight the big guy (Jason)

| Phase | What to do |
|---|---|
| Start | Dodge and stay patient; he has brass knuckles and hits hard |
| Middle | Take the knife from the bar counter and stab him |
| End | Jason takes one of his brass knuckles and finishes the fight |

### 8. Disarm DWNPLY and watch the show

Back in the green room as Lucia, hit DWNPLY the moment the scene ends to take his gun and knock him out. Then join the dance floor: dance or watch the set. When Dre'Quan asks whether you want to stay or leave, either answer leads to the final scene.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a formal fail condition. The hard parts are these:

- **The upstairs fight.** PowerPyx calls him the strongest melee opponent in the game, and GTABase the toughest brawler. Trading punches does not work; dodging does.
- **The green room.** Be ready to strike DWNPLY as soon as you regain control.
- **The table picks.** A weak choice costs money, not the mission.
