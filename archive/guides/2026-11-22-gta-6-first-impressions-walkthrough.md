**This GTA 6 First Impressions walkthrough covers the first job with Raul in person: a getaway from a bank robbery that turns out to be a decoy.** It is mostly a chase, with Jason shooting and Lucia driving. PowerPyx subtitles it Meeting with Raul.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | First Impressions (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Raul |
| Starts at | The garage on Waxman Island, Vice Beach; go there with both characters |
| Requirement | [Car Service](/missions/gta-6-car-service-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $3,000 |

Stock up on healing items first. Jason takes fire on foot in the middle of the mission.

## Objectives

The two guides word the on-screen goals differently. This is the order both describe:

1. Open the garage and get in the car.
2. Go to the meetup and pick up Raul.
3. Hold off the cops.
4. Drive to Rialto Islands.
5. Go to the parking lot and switch to the van.
6. Drive Raul to the drop-off.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open the garage with 2404

At the Waxman Island garage, use the panel on the wall and enter **2404**. The game puts you in control of Lucia. Get in the car.

### 2. Pick up Raul

Drive to the marked spot on the south side of Ocean Beach. Raul gets in, and the police show up at once: he has just robbed a Fleeca bank.

### 3. Hold off the cops

You play Jason, shooting from the car while Lucia drives.

**Tip:** for a Professional or Hardened rating, aim at tires and engines. Shooting drivers also crashes their cars, but doing it often counts as Violent.

The car then crashes. Keep officers away from it; shoot the ones who come close in the legs. Watch Jason's health and heal from the inventory when you need to.

### 4. Reverse out and drive to Rialto Islands

Control passes to Lucia. Back the car fully onto the street, then head for Rialto Islands, the islands between the two halves of [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/). Jason and Raul keep shooting; your job is to avoid the police cars in your path.

The bridge ahead is partly raised. Jump it and the police are left behind.

### 5. Switch vehicles

Drive to the marked parking lot. Get out quickly and take the van parked nearby.

### 6. Drop off Raul

Drive to the marker in Bayside. Raul explains that the bank job was a decoy for a bigger robbery on the other side of the city, pays you and gets out. That ends the mission.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. These are the points where the mission goes wrong in practice:

- **Low health after the crash.** Jason is exposed on foot. Heal before officers close in.
- **Driving forward out of the crash.** Reverse fully onto the street first.
- **Ramming police cars on the way to Rialto Islands.** Steer around them; the bridge jump ends the chase for you.
