**The GTA 6 cover is a panel collage in the classic Grand Theft Auto style, with Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in the centre.** Rockstar Games revealed it on June 18, 2026, in the same post that announced pre-orders for June 25. This page explains what is in the panels, what is and is not known about the "cover girl", and where to find the artwork. We describe the cover rather than show it: this is a fan site and we do not host Rockstar's images.

## GTA 6 cover at a glance

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Revealed | June 18, 2026 |
| Where | Rockstar's social media, with the pre-order announcement |
| Style | Multi-panel collage, as on earlier GTA covers |
| Centre | [Jason Duval](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia Caminos](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Other panels | Alligator, helicopter, speedboat, motorbike, sports car, supporting characters |
| Official download | On Rockstar's GTA VI site |

## When and how Rockstar revealed it

Rockstar did not hold an event for it. On June 18, 2026 it posted that pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI would begin on June 25 at digital storefronts and other select retailers, and added the official cover art, also offered as downloadable artwork. Outlets noted that no new trailer came with it. The post was widely read as a sign that the November 19 date was safe; see our [release date history](/news/gta-6-release-date/).

Boobie Ike and Raul Bautista are both characters Rockstar introduced before launch; they are covered in [GTA 6 characters](/characters/gta-6-characters/). Their names on the cover come from the outlets' reading of the artwork, not from a Rockstar caption.

The alligator is no surprise. Leonida's wildlife has been part of the marketing since the first trailer; see [GTA 6 animals](/map/gta-6-animals/).

## Who is the GTA 6 cover girl?

GTA covers have long included a panel that fans call the cover girl, and GTA 6 keeps the habit: one panel shows a woman in a bikini who is not one of the named cast.

- **Rockstar has not named her.** No official source says who she is or whether she appears in the game.
- GTABoom's analysis lists her as an unnamed character.
- PC Gamer reads a tattoo near her bikini strap as "Vice Baby". That is the outlet's reading of the image.

What is different this time is that the cover's most prominent woman is a playable lead. [Lucia Caminos](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) stands in the centre with Jason.

## How it compares with earlier GTA covers

The grid of panels is the series' established look, and GTA 6 does not break from it. Shacknews describes it as the traditional picture-collage format. PC Gamer and Kotaku both compare it directly with the cover of GTA: Vice City, which fits: GTA 6 goes back to [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/).

## The GTA 6 logo

The cover carries the game's VI logo. Rockstar has not published design notes or credits for the logo or the cover illustration, and none of the reports we checked name an artist. We will add a credit here if Rockstar gives one.

## Is the cover on a real box?

Yes, but the box does not hold a disc. The boxed edition contains a download code; details are in [GTA 6 physical copy](/buying/gta-6-physical-copy/). For what each edition includes, see [price and editions](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/).

## Where to get the cover art

1. Go to Rockstar's official GTA VI site, where the cover is offered as downloadable artwork.
2. Avoid "4K cover" downloads from unofficial sites; they are often altered or fan-made.

## What is not confirmed

- The identity of the woman in the bikini panel.
- Who drew the cover.
- Whether every panel shows a scene that is in the game.
