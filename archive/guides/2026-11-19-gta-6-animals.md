**GTA 6 has more than 170 animal species**, the most in any Grand Theft Auto. Rockstar shared the number in a Game Informer cover story in September 2026: over 60 birds, more than 30 land mammals, plus fish, reptiles and marine life.

## Animals confirmed in official footage and previews

| Group | Species seen |
|---|---|
| Reptiles | Alligators, green iguanas, sea turtles |
| Land mammals | Deer, wild boar, bobcats, big cats, dogs |
| Marine life | Sharks, dolphins, manatees |
| Wetland birds | Flamingos, pelicans, spoonbills, cranes, storks, ducks |
| Other birds | Eagles, woodpeckers, pigeons, seagulls |

Alligators, flamingos, pelicans and manatees are new to Rockstar's games.

## Where to look

- **Grassrivers:** the swamp region is alligator and wading-bird country.
- **Leonida Keys and the coast:** pelicans, gulls, dolphins and sharks.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park:** deer and forest predators.
- **Vice City:** pigeons, gulls, pets, and the occasional alligator where it should not be.

See the [GTA 6 map guide](/map/gta-6-map/) for the regions.

## What is not confirmed

- A full official species list. Rockstar has given the total, not the names.
- Hunting, selling or collecting animals.
- How dangerous predators are to the player.
