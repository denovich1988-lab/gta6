**This GTA 6 Hey Arnold! walkthrough covers the eighth mission: a meeting with Brian's broker that goes wrong, the first proper car chase with shooting, and the first choice between killing and sparing someone.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Meet Brian". The choice at the end does not change the story, so play it the way you prefer.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Hey Arnold! (mission 8, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Meet Brian |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Brian, at the pool bar in the south of Key Lento |
| Requirement | Finish [Pipework](/missions/gta-6-pipework-walkthrough/), then wait for Brian's call |
| Reward | None listed |
| Next mission | [Island Dreams](/missions/gta-6-island-dreams-walkthrough/) |

Arnold is one of Brian's contacts. He has gone quiet since the trouble with the Kingfishers in [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/), and Brian wants to know why. Bring a gun with ammo: you will be shooting from the car.

## Objectives

1. Follow Brian.
2. Drive to the liquor store.
3. Find Arnold at the radar station.
4. Chase Arnold.
5. Choose what to do with Arnold.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Brian and take his truck

Talk to Brian at the bar, follow him to his vehicle, get in the driver's seat and wait for him to climb in.

### 2. Pick up Cal

Drive to the liquor store close by and wait for Cal to get in.

### 3. Find Arnold

A pink search area appears further south. It shrinks as you get closer. Arnold is at the **radar station**, standing in front of a small building. Reaching him starts a cutscene.

### 4. The shoot-out and the car chase

The meeting goes badly. Arnold's men open fire and he drives off. Follow him. Men on motorcycles shoot at you along the way.

- **L1** (LB) draws your weapon in the car, **R1** (RB) fires.
- **L3 + R3** slows time so you can aim.

The route crosses a bridge with a missing section. Keep your speed up and the ramp carries you across; PowerPyx notes the stunt jump happens automatically.

### 5. The foot chase

Arnold eventually leaves his car and runs. Chase him to the docks, where he gives up.

VGC adds a harder alternative: you can destroy his car and kill him during the driving section, before he ever gets out.

### 6. Kill or spare Arnold

At the docks you decide.

| Choice | What happens | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Spare him | He swims away and does not return. Jason tells Brian that Arnold will not be a problem, and Brian accepts it | PowerPyx |
| Kill him | The mission ends the same way. VGC saw no consequence except a change to Jason's criminal profile | VGC |

Both guides agree there is no lasting effect on the story.

## Where players fail

- **Losing Arnold in the car.** Stay close and let the bikers come to you rather than slowing to aim at them.
- **Braking before the bridge.** The gap needs speed.
- **Hanging around after a loud kill.** VGC warns that if you shoot Arnold, you should leave before the police arrive. See [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
