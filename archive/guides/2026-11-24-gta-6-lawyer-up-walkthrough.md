**This GTA 6 Lawyer Up walkthrough covers the short Chapter 4 mission in which Jason and Lucia meet the lawyer Abe Gonzalez.** It takes a few minutes and has no enemies, but two bigger missions depend on it, so it is worth doing as soon as the call comes. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Meet Abe Gonzalez.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Lawyer Up (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/); either can start it |
| Given by | Valentina |
| Starts at | Abe Gonzalez's office, Downtown [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | [Pool Day](/missions/gta-6-pool-day-walkthrough/), then Valentina's call |
| Reward | None |

Valentina promised this meeting when you moved into the Y Hotel in [Ocean Beach](/missions/gta-6-ocean-beach-walkthrough/). If the call has not come yet, both guides say it follows Pool Day; [Company Cars](/missions/gta-6-company-cars-walkthrough/) is a good way to fill the gap.

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists two on-screen objectives; GTABase splits the same route into three.

1. Go to Abe's office.
2. Go to Sandy.
3. Go to Abe.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive Downtown

After Valentina's call, the office is marked on the map in Downtown Vice City. Drive there and go inside.

**Tip:** if you are starting from the Y Hotel, both guides noted in Company Cars that the hotel's car service works as a free ride, and the RydeMe app takes you across the city from anywhere. See [GTA 6 fast travel](/map/gta-6-fast-travel/).

### 2. Talk to the receptionist, then Sandy

Speak to the receptionist at the front. Then walk to the back of the office and talk to Sandy, Abe's assistant.

### 3. Go upstairs to Abe

After Sandy, take the stairs up to Abe's office. The rest is a cutscene:

- Abe agrees to work on the couple's legal problems, in return for a favor.
- While Jason is out of the room, Lucia is told her own situation would be much easier to fix if she gave Jason up. GTABase attributes the line to Abe. She refuses.

The mission ends when the scene does.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. The only way to lose time is to wait at the hotel for a marker: the mission starts at the office, not at the Y Hotel.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cash | None |
| Unlocks | [Heavy Lifting](/missions/gta-6-heavy-lifting-walkthrough/) (from Raul) and [Party Hard](/missions/gta-6-party-hard-walkthrough/) (from Valentina) |
| 100% | GTABase marks the mission as required |

Both follow-up missions list Lawyer Up as their requirement, and the two guides put them in a different order: PowerPyx plays Heavy Lifting first, GTABase lists Party Hard first. You can take either. Abe's favor comes back later in the chapter; PowerPyx has a short entry called Pay Abe's Fee after Party Hard, which GTABase does not list, so we have not covered it yet. Full order: [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
