**Short answer: GTA 6 does not have crossplay at launch.** The game releases on November 19, 2026 as a single-player experience, so there is no multiplayer mode for PS5 and Xbox players to share.

## Will GTA 6 online have crossplay later?

Rockstar has not said. It has not yet detailed an online mode for GTA 6 at all; see [GTA 6 online](/online/gta-6-online/). For reference, GTA Online on GTA 5 never added crossplay between PlayStation and Xbox.

## Cross-progression and cross-save

Nothing is confirmed about moving a save or an online character between PS5 and Xbox. Story saves on consoles are normally tied to the platform you play on.

## What to do if you and your friends are on different consoles

For the story this does not matter, since it is single-player. If playing together later is important to you, the safe choice is to buy on the same platform as your friends.
