**This GTA 6 Breakfast Run walkthrough covers the mission where Jason gets back on his feet and the couple go straight back to work.** It is a chain of quick store robberies that teaches the hold-up system you will use for money for the rest of the game. PowerPyx subtitles it Check on Jason.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Breakfast Run (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), with Jason alongside |
| Given by | Jason and Lucia |
| Starts at | Starlet Motel, Kelly County (pink icon) |
| Requirement | Finish [Slam Dunk](/missions/gta-6-slam-dunk-walkthrough/), then wait for Jason's call |
| Reward | Trust trophy; Jason playable again |

## Objectives

1. Go to Hank's Waffles.
2. Rob the diner.
3. Rob the liquor store.
4. Rob the thrift store.
5. Get in the car.
6. Lose the cops.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Take Jason's call

The call comes roughly half a day after Slam Dunk. Both guides say finishing other open missions helps bring it on. Go back to the motel room; a scene shows time passing until Jason has healed, and the two decide to rob some stores.

### 2. Drive to Hank's Waffles

Follow the marker to the waffle house.

### 3. Rob the diner

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Threaten | Aim your gun at the staff and use the threaten prompt (Square on PS5, X on Xbox, per PowerPyx) |
| Collect | They open the register and put cash on the counter; pick it up |
| Leave | A one-minute timer runs in the bottom-right corner; walk out before it ends |

When you leave, the game moves you straight to the next store.

### 4. Liquor store and thrift store

You appear inside the liquor store. Aim at the clerk, take the money and get in the car, which takes you to the thrift store. Repeat.

Jason and Lucia then hit a gas station and another liquor store by themselves.

### 5. Lose the cops

Back at the car you have a **two-star wanted level**. Both guides recommend the small off-road trails as the easiest way out. See [how the wanted level works](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

### 6. Back at the motel

A scene returns you to the Starlet Motel for the night. In the morning Vicki comes for her laptop, and the story runs directly into [Coffee Break](/missions/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/).

## Where players fail

- **Letting the timer run down.** Threaten, grab the cash and go; do not browse.
- **Staying on main roads with two stars.** Take the dirt trails.
- **Expecting free roam afterwards.** Coffee Break starts immediately.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Trophy | Trust (both guides) |
