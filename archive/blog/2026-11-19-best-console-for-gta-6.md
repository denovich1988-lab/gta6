**The best console for GTA 6 is the PS5 Pro if you want the sharpest picture, but it is not faster: every console targets 30 FPS.** Rockstar confirmed the console differences shortly before launch, and they are smaller than many players expected. This guide compares all four versions, explains what each extra actually does, and tells you which one to pick for your budget and the console you already own.

Every fact here is confirmed by Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft, or reported by at least two independent outlets. Anything rumoured is listed separately at the end.

## Best console for GTA 6 at a glance

| | PS5 | PS5 Pro | Xbox Series X | Xbox Series S |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Native launch version | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Frame rate target | 30 FPS | 30 FPS | 30 FPS | 30 FPS |
| 60 FPS mode | No | No | No | No |
| Upscaling | Not announced | PSSR | Not announced | Not announced |
| Ray-traced shadows | No | Yes (only console) | Not announced | Not announced |
| Controller extras | DualSense haptics, adaptive triggers, speaker | Same as PS5 | Not announced | Not announced |
| 3D audio | Tempest 3D Audio | Tempest 3D Audio | Not announced | Not announced |
| Cloud option | No | No | Xbox Cloud Gaming | Xbox Cloud Gaming |
| Best for | Most players | Best image | Xbox owners | Lowest cost |

Rockstar has not published a resolution for any console, so the table leaves resolution out on purpose.

## 1. The frame rate is the same everywhere

Rockstar's target for every current console is 30 frames per second. There is no performance mode and no 60 FPS option at launch, and that includes the PS5 Pro. The Extended Look gameplay video in August was captured on a standard PS5, and outlets that watched it noted that it appeared to hold 30 FPS for most of the footage.

**Why it matters:** the most common reason to buy a more powerful console, a higher frame rate, does not apply to GTA 6 right now. You are choosing image quality, controller features and price, not smoothness. Full details: [GTA 6 frame rate on every console](/blog/gta-6-60-fps/).

## 2. PS5 Pro: the sharpest version

The PS5 Pro is the only console with two confirmed extras: PSSR (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution), Sony's machine-learning upscaler, and ray-traced shadows that Rockstar lists as exclusive to the Pro. Rockstar describes the result as higher image clarity.

**Why it matters:** on a large 4K TV, upscaling quality is where you notice the difference, especially in fine detail like palm fronds, power lines and distant traffic. On a small screen or a 1080p TV, the gap shrinks.

**Tip:** if you already own a standard PS5, the Pro is an upgrade for picture quality only. Do not buy it expecting GTA 6 to feel faster.

## 3. Standard PS5: best value for most players

The base PS5 gets the same 30 FPS target as the Pro, plus the PlayStation-specific features: DualSense haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, sounds such as police radio and phone calls played through the controller speaker, and Tempest 3D Audio. Rockstar also mentions shorter loading times on PS5. According to GIGA's report on Rockstar's announcement, Rockstar said the game plays best on PlayStation 5.

**Why it matters:** these are the only confirmed gameplay-feel differences between the PlayStation and Xbox versions. If you are buying a console just for GTA 6, the standard PS5 gets you every confirmed feature except the Pro's image upgrades.

**Tip:** the haptics can be adjusted in the accessibility settings if they feel too strong in long driving sessions. More on the pad: [GTA 6 controller guide](/buying/gta-6-controller/).

## 4. Xbox Series X: the best native Xbox version

The Series X installs and runs GTA 6 locally with the same 30 FPS target. Microsoft calls it the best native Xbox experience for the game. Rockstar has not published Xbox-specific resolution, ray tracing or visual settings, and analysts expect the standard PS5 and Series X to perform very similarly.

**Why it matters:** if your friends, your saved games and your digital library are on Xbox, there is no confirmed reason to switch. The confirmed PlayStation extras are about the controller and audio, not the frame rate.

**Tip:** check where your friends will play before you buy. Crossplay depends on GTA Online, which arrives later: [GTA 6 crossplay](/online/gta-6-crossplay/).

## 5. Xbox Series S: the cheapest way to play natively

The Series S is a full launch platform with the same 30 FPS target. Microsoft positions it as the most affordable way to play GTA 6 natively on Xbox. Rockstar has not published its internal resolution or which visual effects are reduced, but it is the least powerful of the four consoles, so expect a softer picture.

**Why it matters:** storage is the real problem. The download is about 210 GB, and the 512 GB Series S has roughly 364 GB usable, so GTA 6 alone takes more than half of it.

**Tip:** if you have a 512 GB Series S, plan to uninstall other games or add an expansion card before launch day. See [GTA 6 file size](/blog/gta-6-file-size/) for the numbers on each console.

## 6. Xbox Cloud Gaming and Xbox One

There is no native Xbox One version. Microsoft says GTA 6 can be streamed through Xbox Cloud Gaming, including on Xbox One, and that the stream uses Series S graphics settings. Picture quality and input delay depend on your internet connection.

**Why it matters:** cloud play is a way in if you do not own a current console, but it will not match a local install. A wired connection or a strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi signal makes a big difference.

**Tip:** check the cloud gaming requirements on your Xbox account before you rely on it, since availability and plans can change. The full platform list is in [GTA 6 platforms](/buying/gta-6-platforms/).

## 7. Storage on every console

Rockstar says to plan for up to 212 GB of free space, and boxed copies still need the large download. A standard PS5 or Series X has room, but a nearly full drive will not.

**Why it matters:** running out of space on launch night is one of the most common problems. Clear space before the pre-load, not after.

**Tip:** a disc does not save space. More in [GTA 6 physical copy](/buying/gta-6-physical-copy/).

## 8. Watch the launch trailer

The official launch trailer shows the look Rockstar is aiming for on current consoles. It is a short mood piece rather than a gameplay demo, so treat it as a guide to the style, not as a test of any one console.

**Why it matters:** if the trailer's image looks good on your TV, the standard version will too. The Pro's extras sharpen the same picture rather than change it.

## 9. What about PC?

Rockstar has not announced a PC version or date. If you only play on PC, you will need a console or Xbox Cloud Gaming to play at launch. We track every update in [GTA 6 on PC](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/).

## What is not confirmed

These claims circulate widely, but Rockstar has not confirmed them:

- **A 40 FPS or 120 Hz mode on PS5 Pro.** This comes from dataminers, not Rockstar.
- **Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos on Xbox.** Based on a photo of a physical Xbox case, not an official feature list.
- **Quick Resume, VRR, FSR or Auto HDR support.** Named in rumours and community discussions only.
- **Resolution for any console.** No official figures yet.

We will move any of these into the comparison table once Rockstar or two reliable outlets confirm them with the released game.

## Which console should you buy? A quick checklist

1. **You already own a PS5 or Series X:** keep it. Both get the same frame rate target and the full game.
2. **You own a Series S:** it works. Free up storage and accept a softer picture.
3. **You are buying new and want the best image:** the PS5 Pro, ideally with a 4K TV.
4. **You are buying new on a budget:** the standard PS5 gets every confirmed feature except the Pro's image upgrades.
5. **You prefer Xbox or your friends are there:** the Series X.
6. **You own only an Xbox One or a PC:** try Xbox Cloud Gaming first, and check your connection.

Before you buy, compare the editions too: [GTA 6 price and editions](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/).

## Verdict: what this means for you

The best console for GTA 6 depends less on raw power than usual. With a 30 FPS target everywhere, the PS5 Pro wins on image quality, the standard PS5 wins on value and controller features, and the Series X is the natural choice for anyone already on Xbox. The Series S runs the full game but needs careful storage planning. If you already own one of these consoles, the best console for GTA 6 is probably the one under your TV.
