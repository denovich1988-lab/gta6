**GTA 6 DLC has not been announced: as of November 26, 2026, Rockstar Games and Take-Two have not confirmed a story expansion, a season pass or any new missions for Grand Theft Auto VI.** The game has been out for a week, and the question is already one of the most searched about it. This page separates what is on the record from what is guesswork: what Take-Two's chief executive actually said, which add-ons exist today, what Rockstar did after its previous games, and what to do with a finished save in the meantime.

How we checked: statements are included only where at least two reports agree on what was said and when. History comes from Rockstar's own release record. Leaks and "insider" claims are left out.

## Has Rockstar announced GTA 6 DLC?

No. Three separate round-ups of the question reach the same answer, and nothing on Rockstar's site or in Take-Two's launch press release mentions post-launch story content. The release, dated November 20, is about sales and review scores; it names no future content at all.

**Why it matters:** any page that gives a name, date or price for GTA 6 story DLC today is inventing it. The honest answer is short, and it is "not yet, and maybe never".

## What Take-Two has actually said

The closest thing to a statement came on Take-Two's earnings call on November 9, ten days before launch. Chief executive Strauss Zelnick was asked what players would do after the campaign, and about multiplayer.

| What he was asked | What he said, in brief | What he did not say |
|---|---|---|
| What players do after the story | That there is plenty to do, and that with this series you can always keep playing | Anything about new missions |
| A multiplayer mode | No details | A date, a name or features |
| The wider franchise | That there is a great deal still to be done with Grand Theft Auto | What, or when |

Reports of the call agree on the substance: confidence in how much the game contains, and no specifics on anything that comes after. ComicBook.com adds that Zelnick indicated Rockstar would move on to its next project after GTA 6, again without detail.

**Why it matters:** these are careful remarks on an investor call. They do not rule out DLC, and they do not promise it.

## What you can add to GTA 6 today

There is paid extra content for GTA 6, but it is cosmetic and practical, not story.

| Add-on | What it is | Adds missions? |
|---|---|---|
| [Ultimate Edition](/buying/gta-6-ultimate-edition/) | 16 extra items: vehicles, weapons, clothing, locations and activities | No |
| [Vintage Vice City Pack](/buying/gta-6-vintage-vice-city-pack/) | The pre-order and early-purchase bonus | No |
| GTA+ | A subscription; one month came with digital pre-orders | No |

**Tip:** if a store listing or video calls one of these "the GTA 6 DLC", that is loose wording. None of them extends the story, and you can finish everything in the game without them.

## The Ultimate Edition does not include future DLC

Some special editions of other games bundle a season pass. GTA 6's does not. The Ultimate Edition's extras are all available now, inside the launch game. Both HappyGamer and GTA6Gang make the same point: the editions differ in launch extras, not in any claim on future content.

**Why it matters:** there is no reason to buy the more expensive edition as insurance against missing an expansion. If one is ever sold, nothing published so far ties it to an edition.

## What Rockstar did after its last four big games

Rockstar's history is the best evidence there is, and it points in two directions.

| Game | Year | Story DLC? | Where later content went |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grand Theft Auto IV | 2008 | Yes: The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony, both 2009 | Two story expansions, later sold together as Episodes from Liberty City |
| Red Dead Redemption | 2010 | Yes: Undead Nightmare | One story expansion |
| Grand Theft Auto V | 2013 | No | GTA Online |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | 2018 | No | Red Dead Online |

The older pattern is story expansions within a year or so. The newer pattern, which has now held for more than a decade, is an online mode that receives the new content while the single-player game stays as it shipped.

**Why it matters:** anyone predicting GTA 6 story DLC is betting that Rockstar returns to how it worked in 2009. Anyone predicting none is betting on how it has worked since 2013.

## Why GTA 5 never received its expansion

GTA 5 came close. A single-player expansion was in development and was dropped. Dan Houser, the Rockstar co-founder who left the company in 2020, talked about it in an interview in 2025, as reported by GamesRadar+: he said the expansion was about half done when it was abandoned, and that he liked the GTA 4 model of extra stories arriving afterwards.

Those are the views of someone who no longer works at Rockstar, about a decision made years ago. They show that story DLC was tried and shelved once before. They are not a plan for GTA 6.

**Tip:** treat any "GTA 6 will get the DLC that GTA 5 lost" headline as opinion.

## An online mode is the more likely form of new content

Rockstar describes the launch game as a single-player experience. A new online mode has not been named, dated or detailed, as our [GTA 6 online guide](/online/gta-6-online/) explains. Zelnick avoided questions about it on the November 9 call.

If the pattern of the last two games holds, new vehicles, missions and activities would arrive there, not in the story. That is an inference from history, not an announcement, and until Rockstar says something it should be read as one.

**Why it matters:** if you are waiting for more GTA 6 to play, an online launch is the thing to watch for first. A [PC version](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/) is the other unannounced release people ask about.

## Updates so far have added nothing new

GTA 6 has been patched, but the patches have been technical. Two updates arrived before launch, and Rockstar has published no patch notes that add content. Our [GTA 6 update page](/blog/gta-6-update/) tracks every version and its size.

**Tip:** a download prompt on your console is not DLC. Check the version number against the update page before assuming something new has been added.

## What is not confirmed

- **Any story DLC, expansion or season pass.** None has been announced.
- **A date or name for an online mode.** Rockstar has given neither.
- **A PC release.** Not announced.
- **Whether single-player will receive new content through free updates.** Nothing has been said either way.
- **Claims from leaks, datamining or unnamed insiders.** We do not repeat them, and none has been confirmed by Rockstar.

The launch game holds far more than its missions. If the credits have rolled and you want more, there is a long list before any DLC would be needed.

1. **Replay the finale.** Guides report that after the credits you are placed back before the last mission; see [GTA 6 ending explained](/blog/gta-6-ending/).
2. **Use the cheats.** The cheat list unlocks only after the story, and each code has its own unlock condition: [GTA 6 cheats](/cheats/gta-6-cheats/).
3. **Work through the challenges.** There are 80, and they unlock vehicles and cheats: [GTA 6 challenges](/missions/gta-6-challenges/).
4. **Hunt the collectibles.** Start from the [collectibles hub](/map/gta-6-collectibles/).
5. **Finish the optional missions.** [Strangers and Freaks](/missions/gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/) lists them.
6. **Aim for 100%.** Every requirement can be finished in free roam after the story: [100% completion checklist](/missions/gta-6-100-completion/).

## What this means for you

Do not buy anything, or hold off buying anything, on the expectation of GTA 6 DLC. Nothing exists to plan around. The game sold 32.5 million units before it launched, as our [pre-order sales page](/blog/gta-6-pre-order-sales/) explains, so Rockstar has every commercial reason to keep players engaged; how it chooses to do that is the open question. For now, the game on your console is the whole of GTA 6.
