**GTA 6 easter eggs range from a UFO that abducts you outside a motel to an actual Easter egg in a bird's nest above Vice City, and players have found at least 17 of them since launch on November 19.** Rockstar has confirmed none of them, which is how easter eggs work. This page lists the ones that appear in at least two independent lists, with where they are, how to trigger them and what they refer to, then the rumours we have left out and a route for seeing the best ones in one evening.

How we checked: every egg below is described by at least two of three lists published since launch (BuzzFeed, GTAside and GameRiv). Where a location or trigger comes from only one of them, we name it. Mild spoilers for side content; none for the story.

## 1. The UFO abduction

All three lists describe it, and two agree on the place: Kelly County, on a rural road east of [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/), near the Starlet Motel. It happens at night, after Chapter 1. A car sits abandoned at the roadside with its lights on. A trail leads from it into the woods and ends at a glow among the trees and a close encounter.

GTAside adds detail we have not seen elsewhere: around 2 AM in-game, a woman held in a beam of light asks you to fetch her car, and after the abduction you wake up three in-game days later. Both it and GameRiv name the reward, the trophy **The Truth Is Out There**.

**Tip:** if the car is not there, GTAside says to come back on another night. This is the one easter egg that counts toward the platinum; see the [GTA 6 trophy guide](/missions/gta-6-trophies/).

## 2. The alien rescue

BuzzFeed and GTAside both say the abduction has a sequel later in the game: you free a live alien from an Area 51-style site. Only GTAside explains how. It describes a dream sequence aboard the ship with a puzzle that points to a restricted site in the south of Leonida, and a permanent boost to health, stamina and Focus as the reward.

**Why it matters:** if the reward is real, this is the most useful secret in the game. It rests on one detailed source, so we mark the steps as unconfirmed.

## 3. The Skunk Ape

GTA 6's Bigfoot is the Skunk Ape of Florida folklore. All three lists place it in the southern wetlands around Gullet Marsh and Gambit Bay, and all describe the same odd follow-up: you lose the creature, find a figure made of sticks and rope, examine it and black out.

GameRiv and GTAside both mention a side mission about it involving a character called Kirk. GTAside says it starts from flyers and only at night.

**Tip:** we cover the sighting spots and the Kirk questline in [GTA 6 Bigfoot](/blog/gta-6-bigfoot/). The claim that you can hunt the creature and be transformed afterwards comes from social media posts and is listed below as unconfirmed.

## 4. The giant crab in Gullet Marsh

Two lists put a giant mutant crab in Gullet Marsh; all three link it to the "giant enemy crab" joke from a 2006 games show. It attacks you inside a building.

GTAside gives the trigger: at an isolated outpost in the marsh, switch on the power at the breaker box outside, read the journal on the computer, and step away from the screen.

**Tip:** bring a strong weapon and body armor before you touch the breaker box. For the wildlife that is meant to be there, see [GTA 6 animals](/map/gta-6-animals/).

## 5. The conspiracy house in Belville

In 2021, the remastered GTA San Andreas showed a photo of a house that fans took as a first look at GTA 6. All three lists say that house is in the game, in Belville, and that its garage is the hideout of a conspiracy theorist who believes the world is a simulation.

GTAside quotes more of the notes: he cannot buy a plane ticket to Los Santos or drive past the state line, and "J and L" do not follow the same rules as everyone else.

**Why it matters:** it is Rockstar answering five years of fan theories with a joke about the edge of the map. Find Belville on the [GTA 6 map](/map/gta-6-map/).

## 6. The serial killer's apartment

On Washington Island in [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) stands an apartment building modelled on the home of the forensic analyst from the TV series Dexter. Two lists describe a kit on the roof (a knife, syringes and rope in a toolbox), and two describe a boat at the nearby dock that looks like the one from the show.

GTAside says the apartment itself cannot be entered and that the spot is reachable as soon as the map opens up.

**Tip:** this is one of the easiest to see early, because it needs no mission and no time of day.

## 7. The movie car

BuzzFeed and GTAside both describe a green tuner car hidden in a garage, a close copy of the hero's car from the first The Fast and the Furious film. You can break in and drive it away. GTAside places the garage at a villa on Vespucci Island.

**Why it matters:** it is a free, distinctive car with no mission attached. For cars with stats behind them, see [GTA 6 cars](/vehicles/gta-6-cars/).

## 8. The literal Easter egg

Two lists agree on this one almost word for word. At the top of a very tall tower in Vice City, after about seven minutes of ladders, there is a bird's nest with coloured eggs in it. Take one and throw it off the edge, and a short hidden scene plays.

GTAside connects it to GTA Vice City (2002), which hid a chocolate egg in a secret room.

**Tip:** save before you climb. A fall near the top costs you the whole climb; see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

## 9. The talking parrot

A parrot says the word "generator", and checking a generator nearby turns up a hidden stash of cash. BuzzFeed and GTAside both report it and both link it to Tony, the parrot from GTA San Andreas. GTAside locates it at a liquor store in the Leonida Keys, after a brawl you have to win.

**Why it matters:** it is one of the few eggs that pays. For reliable income, use [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).

## 10. Cool Dog

Swim or drift too far from shore and a dog in goggles arrives on a jet ski to bring you back. Both BuzzFeed and GTAside describe it. GTAside adds that if you stay on, he takes you to a sandy island full of dogs and digs up some cash; it attributes that part to players.

**Tip:** GTAside suggests Gambit Bay, off [Jason's](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) side of the Keys, as a place to try it.

## 11. The pentagram in Capri

Two lists describe an abandoned building in Capri with a pentagram on the floor. Knock out a passer-by, lay them on it, and a supernatural sequence starts. BuzzFeed labels it "reportedly"; GTAside describes it in detail, placing the building north of Capri near Tortoise Lake.

**Tip:** GTAside advises going at night and saving first. Knocking out a bystander is still an assault, so it may count against your criminal profile.

## 12. The red mutant farmer

GameRiv and BuzzFeed both describe a side mission in which a farmer's chemical experiments turn him into a huge red-skinned brute, a parody of a comic-book hulk. GameRiv says he is trying to grow giant strawberries. GTAside files the same character under unconfirmed leads, so we rate this one a notch lower than the rest.

**Why it matters:** it shows how strange the side content gets. More of it is in [GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks](/missions/gta-6-strangers-and-freaks/).

## 13–17. Five nods to GTA 5 and Red Dead

These are small, and each appears in both BuzzFeed's and GTAside's lists:

| # | Nod | Where | Refers to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | A yacht whose navigation screen shows the Los Santos map | Moored near Jason's house | Michael's stolen yacht in GTA 5 |
| 14 | The "Wind Chimes" ringtone | Phone settings | Michael's ringtone in GTA 5 |
| 15 | Two customers discussing a bunker with jetpacks under a mountain | Outside a bar early in the story | The Mount Chiliad mystery |
| 16 | A Western novel about a cowboy from New Austin | Jacob's Tavern | Red Dead Redemption |
| 17 | A hat very like Arthur Morgan's | BuzzFeed: $5 at a yard sale | Red Dead Redemption 2 |

**Tip:** the ringtone is the quickest of all to check: open the phone settings.

One more carried over from the first trailer: a "Petty Forever" sticker on the door of a liquor store, a tribute to a Florida-born rock musician. Beebom spotted it in the trailer and BuzzFeed reports it in the game.

## What is not confirmed

We left these out of the list because they rest on one source, on social media, or on footage nobody has explained:

- **A giant kraken on the seabed.** GameRiv itself calls it unconfirmed.
- **An unkillable man** who reacts when you aim a sniper rifle at him. Two lists mention it; both say it may be a bug.
- **Turning into a frog** after hunting the Skunk Ape. Sourced to posts on X.
- **A fixed keypad code** for the alien rescue site. GTAside lists it as a lead, and its own description says the code comes from the puzzle.
- **A Love Fist sticker** at the Starlet Motel. Reported on one list, a lead on another.
- **The Monument of Leonidan Supremacy** in Manifest Canyon and its possible Morse code. One list, one player's report, unsolved.
- **A snake in the trunk** of the movie car.
- **A link between the crab and a research facility** in Pelican Valley.
- **Magazine, comic and licence-plate references** that appear on a single list without a location.

## An easter egg route: what to do first

A sensible order if you want to see the best of them without crossing the map five times:

1. **Any time, anywhere:** check the Wind Chimes ringtone in the phone settings.
2. **In the Keys, early:** look at the yacht near Jason's house.
3. **Once you reach Vice City in Chapter 3:** visit the apartment on Washington Island, then climb the tower for the literal Easter egg. Save first.
4. **At night, after Chapter 1:** drive to the Starlet Motel east of Port Gellhorn for the UFO. [Fast travel](/map/gta-6-fast-travel/) gets you close.
5. **With armor and a good weapon:** go to Gullet Marsh for the crab, and look for the Skunk Ape in the same swamps.
6. **Last:** the pentagram in Capri, since it means knocking out a bystander.

## What this means for you

GTA 6's secrets fall into three kinds. A few are full encounters with their own scenes: the UFO, the crab, the Skunk Ape and the pentagram. A few are places to visit: the Belville house, the apartment and the tower. The rest are small references you notice in passing. Only one affects completion, the UFO trophy, so there is no pressure to chase the others. If you do, treat locations from a single source as leads, not directions.
