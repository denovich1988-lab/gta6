    GTA 6 Cover Art: What Is on It and Who the Cover Girl Is

    The GTA 6 cover explained: when Rockstar revealed it, who and what is in each panel, what is known about the cover girl, and where to get the artwork.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar revealed the official GTA 6 cover art on June 18, 2026, together with the June 25 pre-order date.
    • It is a panel collage in the series' usual style, with Jason and Lucia in the centre and an alligator, a helicopter, a speedboat and a motorbike around them.
    • Rockstar has not named the woman people call the GTA 6 cover girl.

    The GTA 6 cover is a panel collage in the classic Grand Theft Auto style, with Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in the centre. Rockstar Games revealed it on June 18, 2026, in the same post that announced pre-orders for June 25. This page explains what is in the panels, what is and is not known about the "cover girl", and where to find the artwork. We describe the cover rather than show it: this is a fan site and we do not host Rockstar's images.

    GTA 6 cover at a glance

    Detail Info
    Revealed June 18, 2026
    Where Rockstar's social media, with the pre-order announcement
    Style Multi-panel collage, as on earlier GTA covers
    Centre Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos
    Other panels Alligator, helicopter, speedboat, motorbike, sports car, supporting characters
    Official download On Rockstar's GTA VI site

    When and how Rockstar revealed it

    Rockstar did not hold an event for it. On June 18, 2026 it posted that pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI would begin on June 25 at digital storefronts and other select retailers, and added the official cover art, also offered as downloadable artwork. Outlets noted that no new trailer came with it. The post was widely read as a sign that the November 19 date was safe; see our release date history.

    What is on the cover, panel by panel

    Rockstar has not published a description of the panels. The list below is what several outlets independently reported seeing:

    Panel subject Reported by
    Jason and Lucia, in the centre PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom, Shacknews
    An alligator PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom, Kotaku, Shacknews
    A helicopter PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom
    A speedboat PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom
    A rider on a motorbike PC Gamer, GTABase, GTABoom
    A sports car PC Gamer, GTABase
    Boobie Ike GTABase, GTABoom, Shacknews
    Raul Bautista GTABase, GTABoom
    A woman in a bikini PC Gamer, GTABoom

    Boobie Ike and Raul Bautista are both characters Rockstar introduced before launch; they are covered in GTA 6 characters. Their names on the cover come from the outlets' reading of the artwork, not from a Rockstar caption.

    The alligator is no surprise. Leonida's wildlife has been part of the marketing since the first trailer; see GTA 6 animals.

    Who is the GTA 6 cover girl?

    GTA covers have long included a panel that fans call the cover girl, and GTA 6 keeps the habit: one panel shows a woman in a bikini who is not one of the named cast.

    • Rockstar has not named her. No official source says who she is or whether she appears in the game.
    • GTABoom's analysis lists her as an unnamed character.
    • PC Gamer reads a tattoo near her bikini strap as "Vice Baby". That is the outlet's reading of the image.

    What is different this time is that the cover's most prominent woman is a playable lead. Lucia Caminos stands in the centre with Jason.

    How it compares with earlier GTA covers

    The grid of panels is the series' established look, and GTA 6 does not break from it. Shacknews describes it as the traditional picture-collage format. PC Gamer and Kotaku both compare it directly with the cover of GTA: Vice City, which fits: GTA 6 goes back to Vice City.

    The GTA 6 logo

    The cover carries the game's VI logo. Rockstar has not published design notes or credits for the logo or the cover illustration, and none of the reports we checked name an artist. We will add a credit here if Rockstar gives one.

    Is the cover on a real box?

    Yes, but the box does not hold a disc. The boxed edition contains a download code; details are in GTA 6 physical copy. For what each edition includes, see price and editions.

    Where to get the cover art

    1. Go to Rockstar's official GTA VI site, where the cover is offered as downloadable artwork.
    2. Avoid "4K cover" downloads from unofficial sites; they are often altered or fan-made.

    What is not confirmed

    • The identity of the woman in the bikini panel.
    • Who drew the cover.
    • Whether every panel shows a scene that is in the game.

    We will update this page if Rockstar says more about the artwork. For the videos released before launch, see all GTA 6 trailers.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is on the GTA 6 cover?

    A collage of panels with Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in the middle, surrounded by an alligator, a helicopter, a speedboat, a motorbike, a sports car and supporting characters that outlets identify as Boobie Ike and Raul Bautista.

    Who is the GTA 6 cover girl?

    Rockstar has not said. One panel shows a woman in a bikini who is not one of the named characters, and no official source identifies her. Lucia, one of the two leads, is in the centre of the cover.

    When was the GTA 6 cover art revealed?

    On June 18, 2026. Rockstar posted it on social media with the news that pre-orders would open on June 25.

    Is the GTA 6 logo on the cover?

    Yes, the game's VI logo is on the cover. Rockstar has not published any notes about the logo's design, and we do not host the image.
