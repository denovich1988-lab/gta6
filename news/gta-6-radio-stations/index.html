    GTA 6 Radio Stations: Every Station, Host and Genre

    The GTA VI radio stations confirmed so far: Flash FM, Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6 and Dirty South Classics, with hosts.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar detailed six GTA VI radio stations on October 8, 2026, each with celebrity hosts.
    • More station names, including Vice City FM, V-Rock and Emotion 98.3, are reported by launch coverage.
    • You can listen in vehicles and on foot through the phone and earbuds, and there are on-demand podcasts.

    1. The six stations Rockstar has detailed

    Rockstar has officially detailed six GTA VI radio stations, each with well-known hosts. More stations are in the game; the names reported so far are listed further down.

    The six stations Rockstar has detailed

    Station Music Hosts
    Flash FM Pop Robyn and Alex Consani
    Cocoteo FM Modern Latin Bad Bunny (as El Martillo) and RaiNao (as La Gata)
    Back Country Radio Country Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey (as Delta Dawne)
    AfroBank FM Amapiano and African classics Burna Boy and Palms Trax
    The Chamber 106.6 Heavy metal Kerry King and Tom Araya of Slayer
    Dirty South Classics Southern rap Trick Daddy and Trina

    Flash FM is a returning name: it was the pop station in 2002's GTA: Vice City.

    Other stations reported in the game

    These names appear in more than one launch-week report. Rockstar has not published details for them yet, so treat the genres as reported:

    • Vice City FM (80s pop and new wave)
    • V-Rock (rock)
    • Emotion 98.3 (soft rock)
    • Radio On-U (reggae)
    • Symphony FM (classical)
    • Kaleidoscope FM (global pop)
    • La Ola (classic Latin)
    • Stockyard (indie and dance)
    • The Wipe (talk)

    One launch-day list counts 23 stations in total. We will publish the complete list, with every track, once we have checked it in the game.

    New ways to listen

    • On foot. Jason and Lucia can listen through their phones with earbuds.
    • Podcasts. Rockstar has confirmed on-demand podcasts alongside the stations.
    • In vehicles, as in every GTA.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has officially detailed six. Launch-day coverage reports 23 stations in the finished game; we will confirm the full list from the game.

    Who hosts the GTA VI radio stations?

    Hosts include Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Burna Boy, Robyn, Trick Daddy, Trina and Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya.

    Is Flash FM back in GTA 6?

    Yes. Flash FM, the pop station from GTA: Vice City, returns and is hosted by Robyn and Alex Consani.

    Can you listen to the radio on foot in GTA 6?

    Yes. Rockstar says Jason and Lucia can listen through their phones with earbuds, not only in vehicles.
