    GTA 6 Gameplay: What the Extended Look Showed

    GTA 6 gameplay from the official Extended Look: six wanted stars, a heat system, new car theft, activities, a real race track and 30 FPS on consoles.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • The Extended Look is about 26 minutes of GTA 6 gameplay captured on a base PS5.
    • It shows a six-star wanted level with heat icons, hands-on car theft and many side activities.
    • Rockstar says the game targets 30 FPS on every console.

    The best look at GTA 6 gameplay before launch is Rockstar's Extended Look, a roughly 26-minute video captured on a base PlayStation 5. Here is what it confirmed.

    Police and the wanted level

    • The wanted meter goes up to six stars.
    • Red heat icons under the stars show what the police know about you: your face, your outfit and your vehicle.
    • Witnesses and cameras feed that information to the police.

    Full details: GTA 6 wanted level explained.

    Stealing cars

    Car theft takes more than one button press. The footage shows Jason working on a car window in a short timed sequence before driving away, and the pair later burn a getaway car to cover their tracks.

    Two protagonists in the same scene

    Jason and Lucia appear together in missions, for example with one driving while the other shoots. How switching works in free roam is something we will document from the game.

    Activities shown

    Activity Seen in the Extended Look
    Gym workouts Yes
    Jet skis, kayaks and swimming Yes
    Car racing on a track Yes
    Base jumping Yes
    Store and gas station robberies Yes

    The racing footage shows wheel-to-wheel racing on a purpose-built circuit, a first for the series.

    Performance

    Rockstar has said GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. See does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.

    How long it is

    One Rockstar studio head put his own playthrough at about 80 hours. See how long GTA 6 is.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there official GTA 6 gameplay footage?

    Yes. Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look, released on August 27, 2026, is about 26 minutes of gameplay captured on PS5.

    How many wanted stars does GTA 6 have?

    Six. The sixth star returns for the first time in a main GTA game since GTA 4.

    What activities are in GTA 6?

    Official footage shows the gym, jet skis, kayaking, swimming, car racing and base jumping, among others.
