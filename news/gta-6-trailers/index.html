    GTA 6 Trailers: Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and the Extended Look

    Every official GTA 6 trailer in order: Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and the 2026 Extended Look that took the place of Trailer 3, with dates and what each showed.

    Quick answer

    • Trailer 1 arrived in December 2023 and Trailer 2 on May 6, 2025.
    • Instead of a short Trailer 3, Rockstar released GTA VI: An Extended Look on August 27, 2026.
    • The Extended Look runs about 26 minutes and was captured on a PS5.

    There are three official GTA 6 videos: Trailer 1, Trailer 2 and a long gameplay presentation called An Extended Look, which filled the slot fans expected Trailer 3 to take.

    Every official GTA 6 trailer

    Video Date What it showed
    Trailer 1 December 2023 Leonida, Vice City and Lucia; a 2025 release window
    Trailer 2 May 6, 2025 Jason and Lucia's story, the supporting cast and the regions of Leonida
    GTA VI: An Extended Look August 27, 2026 About 26 minutes of gameplay captured on PS5

    Trailer 1

    The first trailer introduced the state of Leonida and its biggest city, Vice City, and put Lucia at the centre. It used Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road".

    Trailer 2

    Trailer 2 focused on both leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. On the same day Rockstar's site added character and location pages, which we summarise in our characters and map guides.

    An Extended Look (the "Trailer 3")

    On August 27, 2026 Rockstar released a gameplay presentation instead of a third short trailer. It premiered on Netflix and went up on YouTube and Rockstar's site later the same day. It is the first footage of the game being played: missions, police chases, car theft, activities and life between jobs.

    We break down what it revealed in GTA 6 gameplay: what the Extended Look showed.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a GTA 6 Trailer 3?

    Not under that name. Rockstar released a long gameplay presentation called Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look on August 27, 2026.

    When did GTA 6 Trailer 2 come out?

    On May 6, 2025, four days after the first delay was announced.

    Where can I watch the GTA 6 Extended Look?

    It premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026 and was then published on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the official GTA VI site the same day.
