    GTA 6 Cars: Full Vehicle List and How to Keep a Car

    Partly confirmedUpdated November 23, 20265 min readChecked against 11 sources

    Short answer: Every GTA 6 car named by launch guides, sorted by class, plus the bikes, boats and aircraft, the edition vehicles and how to steal, register and keep a car.

    Key facts

    • GTA 6 has hundreds of vehicles under fictional brands; launch guides have named well over 100 cars so far, from the Ganado pickup to the Zorrusso supercar.
    • You cannot just keep a stolen car: from Chapter 2 you register it at Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn, and later at Pay 'n' Spray.
    • Unregistered cars disappear when you leave them, so register the cars you want and keep a garage slot free.

    GTA 6 cars number in the hundreds, all under fictional brands, and launch guides have already named well over 100 of them. This page lists every car that at least two launch guides name, sorted by class, then covers bikes, boats and aircraft, the vehicles tied to the editions, and the part most players get wrong: a stolen car is not yours until you register it. Rockstar has not published an official vehicle list, so names come from guides that agree with each other, and the page stays marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    How to steal, register and keep a car

    Stealing a car does not make it yours. Guides agree that an unregistered car disappears once you leave it, die or quit, so registration is the real way to build a garage.

    1. Finish the Chapter 2 mission Moses. Registration unlocks in the Moses walkthrough, which also gives Lucia the Slim Jim for quiet break-ins, the Scooter Bros app that delivers your car, and a free registered vehicle.
    2. Scan before you steal. Use the WAiNK phone app to check a car's tracker. Moses only takes cars with no tracker, and he refuses cars stolen right next to his garage.
    3. Respray and register. Drive into Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn, pick a new colour, then choose Register instead of Sell.
    4. Keep a slot free. You start with one garage slot, so register a car you will actually drive. More space comes with new properties as the story goes on.
    5. Use Pay 'n' Spray later. After Welcome to Vice City, Moses texts that Pay 'n' Spray shops can buy and register cars with standard trackers. Cars with advanced trackers need the Key Cloner from Chapter 3.

    Tip: a registered car used in a crime and left inside the police search area can be impounded. The Scooter Bros app returns it for a fee; the Impounded Vehicle walkthrough covers it.

    Selling stolen cars

    The cars you do not keep are income. Moses buys untracked cars from Chapter 2, Pay 'n' Spray buys cars with standard trackers later, and Randy buys high-end cars from Chapter 3. Cars in good condition sell for more. Payouts are in how to make money in GTA 6.

    Frequently asked questions

    What cars are in GTA 6?

    Launch guides list sports cars such as the Banshee, Coquette D10 and Itali GTO, supercars such as the Zorrusso, Tempesta, Emerus and Thrax, muscle cars such as the Dominator and Gauntlet, and SUVs, pickups and classics. The full list by class is on this page.

    How do you keep a car in GTA 6?

    Register it. From the Chapter 2 mission Moses, take a car without a tracker to Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn, respray it and choose Register. Unregistered cars vanish once you leave them.

    Can you buy cars in GTA 6?

    Launch guides describe stealing and registering cars rather than a dealership. Your first registration with Moses is free, and some vehicles come from missions, editions or cheats.

    Are there real car brands in GTA 6?

    No. Vehicles use fictional brands such as Pegassi, Grotti, Vapid and Declasse, with designs that resemble real cars.
