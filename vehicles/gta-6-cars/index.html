    GTA 6 Cars and Vehicles: What Is Confirmed

    What is known about GTA 6 cars, bikes, boats and aircraft before launch, and how our verified vehicle list will work.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 18, 2026
    Read
    1 min

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar has not published an official GTA 6 vehicle list.
    • Trailers and screenshots show cars, motorcycles, boats, airboats, planes and helicopters.
    • Vehicles in GTA use fictional brands; we list a vehicle only once its in-game name is verified.
    On this page 4
    1. Vehicle types seen in official footage
    2. Car names and brands
    3. What our vehicle list will include

    Rockstar has not published a GTA 6 car list. What we know before launch comes from the official trailers and screenshots.

    Vehicle types seen in official footage

    • Cars: sports cars, muscle cars, sedans, SUVs, pickups and lowriders
    • Motorcycles: sport bikes, cruisers and dirt bikes
    • Boats: speedboats, fishing boats and swamp airboats
    • Aircraft: small planes and helicopters
    • Others: trucks, police and emergency vehicles

    Car names and brands

    GTA does not use real manufacturers. Vehicles carry fictional brands that long-time players will recognise from earlier games. Until the game is out, most names attached to trailer vehicles are fan identifications, so we do not list them as fact.

    What our vehicle list will include

    For each verified vehicle:

    1. In-game name and class
    2. Where to find or buy it
    3. Top speed and handling notes from our own testing
    4. Customisation options

    Frequently asked questions

    What cars are in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not released an official list. Trailers show a wide range of cars, bikes, boats and aircraft, and we will list each one with its in-game name once verified.

    Are there real car brands in GTA 6?

    No. As in earlier GTA games, vehicles use fictional brands that resemble real-world cars.

    Can you customise cars in GTA 6?

    Vehicle customisation has been part of GTA for years, and trailers show modified cars, but we will detail the options once we have checked them in the game.
