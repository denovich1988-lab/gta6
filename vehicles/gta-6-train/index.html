    GTA 6 Train and Metro: Can You Ride Them?

    Yes, you can ride trains in GTA 6. How the Vice City metro, trams and buses work, how to skip the journey, what it costs and the mission that uses the metro.

    Partly confirmed

    Quick answer

    • You can ride trains in GTA 6: the Vice City metro, trams and buses all take you as a passenger.
    • Pick a stop after you board and hold Triangle (Y on Xbox) to skip the trip; guides say it costs less than a taxi or RydeMe.

    Yes, you can ride the train in GTA 6: the Vice City metro, trams and buses all carry you as a passenger, and you can skip the journey once you are on board. That is a big change from GTA 5, where the train was mostly scenery. This page explains how public transport works, how to skip a ride, what it costs compared with taxis, and the story mission built around the metro.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    GTA 6 public transport at a glance

    Option Where Skip the trip? Cost
    Metro (elevated train) Vice City Yes Fare by trip
    Trams Tram stops Yes Fare by trip
    Buses Bus stops across Leonida Yes Fare by trip
    Paradise Taxi Call from your phone Yes, $100 extra Fare + $100 to skip
    RydeMe Phone app, after Buck Me Yes, $100 extra Fare + $100 to skip

    A working rail and bus network

    Before launch, Rockstar's head of development Phil Hooker told Edge magazine that GTA 6 has working buses and rail, and that he had memorised the routes, as Beebom and Insider Gaming both reported. The elevated metro also appears in Rockstar's trailers and screenshots, running above the streets of Vice City.

    Why it matters: trains do not get stuck in traffic and you do not need a car, so the metro is a calm way to cross the city after a chase or when your car has been impounded.

    How to ride the train or metro

    1. Walk to a station and climb the stairs to the platform.
    2. Board the train when it stops.
    3. Pick the stop you want.
    4. Ride along and watch the city, or skip to your stop. Dot Esports says you hold Triangle on PS5 (Y on Xbox) to skip.

    Buses and trams work the same way from their stops. Guides do not yet publish a route map or timetable.

    Tip: use the train for trips inside Vice City and a taxi or RydeMe for long trips across Leonida; see GTA 6 fast travel for those.

    What it costs

    There is a fare that depends on the journey. Dot Esports says public transport is cheaper than a taxi or RydeMe, both of which charge the normal fare plus $100 to skip the ride.

    The metro in Crosstown Traffic

    In the Chapter 3 mission Crosstown Traffic, Lucia has to catch the metro to confront a gang member on board while Jason chases boats on the river. She misses the first train, so you sprint under the elevated tracks shown on the minimap to the next station, climb the stairs and board the rear car.

    • Driving a train. No guide describes a way to take the controls.
    • Freight trains outside Vice City and whether they can be boarded.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you ride the train in GTA 6?

    Yes. Trains, trams and buses work as public transport. Board at a station or stop, choose where to get off and ride along or skip the journey.

    Is there a subway or metro in GTA 6?

    Yes. Vice City has an elevated metro, seen in Rockstar's trailers and screenshots, and Lucia rides it in the Chapter 3 mission Crosstown Traffic.

    Can you drive the train in GTA 6?

    Guides do not describe a way to drive a train. Treat it as public transport you ride as a passenger.

    How much does the train cost in GTA 6?

    There is a fare that depends on the trip. Guides say public transport is cheaper than a taxi or RydeMe, which add a $100 fee to skip the ride.
