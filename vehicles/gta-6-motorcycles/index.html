    GTA 6 Motorcycles: Every Bike and How to Get One

    GTA 6 motorcycles named so far: sport bikes, dirt bikes and cruisers, which ones you can win, rent, steal or spawn, and how to keep a bike for good.

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 motorcycles include sport bikes (Carbon RS, Double-T, Alvino V1), dirt bikes (Sanchez, Manchez, Enduro) and cruisers (Avarus, Zombie Bobber, Nightblade).
    • Your first free bike is the Highwayman Sanchez Billy gives you in the Chapter 2 mission Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, and you can register it with Moses to keep it.
    • After the story, cheats spawn the Maibatsu Sanchez, Nagasaki Carbon RS and Western Zombie Bobber.

    GTA 6 motorcycles come in three families: sport bikes for speed, dirt bikes for the swamps and back roads, and cruisers for style. This page lists every motorcycle that at least two launch guides name, then explains how to get one in story mode, which bikes the cheats spawn and how to keep a bike. Rockstar has not released an official list or top speeds, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    Sport bikes

    The Nagasaki Carbon RS, Dinka Double-T, and Principe Alvino V1 are the fast road bikes. The Alvino V1 is the one new name; the others return from earlier games.

    Tip: sport bikes are the quickest way through Vice City traffic, but one crash throws you off. Use them for errands and races, not for missions with a lot of shooting.

    Dirt bikes

    The Maibatsu Sanchez and Maibatsu Manchez are the dirt bikes you meet first. In the optional Chapter 2 mission Thrillbillies, you rent one at Roostere's Rentals for the Mud Club race. Both PowerPyx and GTABase say the Sanchez costs more but is faster, and a better finish pays back the difference.

    The Dinka Enduro is one of the seven vehicles Rockstar names in its edition descriptions; see GTA 6 cars for the full set.

    Why it matters: Leonida has huge stretches of mud, trails and swamp around Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga, and a dirt bike is the fastest land vehicle there.

    Scooters and cruisers

    The Pegassi Faggio is the city scooter. Cruisers include the LCC Avarus and the Western Nightblade; Moses asks for a Nightblade in his optional Steal Vehicles for Moses jobs, so you can get paid to find one. The Western Zombie Bobber is a cheat spawn.

    Your first free motorcycle

    In Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, Billy's second Chapter 2 job, he turns up with a Highwayman Sanchez for the armored truck robbery. Guides suggest parking it in a garage, such as the one in Port Gellhorn, and registering it at Moses' shop so it does not disappear. Read the Moses walkthrough first if registration is not unlocked yet.

    Motorcycle cheats

    Three bikes can be spawned after you finish the story and unlock their cheats. The codes and the full list are on GTA 6 cheats.

    Bike Unlock task
    Maibatsu Sanchez Complete 5 criminal challenges
    Nagasaki Carbon RS Complete all exploration challenges
    Western Zombie Bobber Complete all criminal challenges

    The challenge list is in GTA 6 challenges.

    How to keep a motorcycle

    Bikes follow the same rules as cars:

    1. Steal or win the bike.
    2. Take it to Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn (from Chapter 2) or a Pay 'n' Spray later on.
    3. Respray it and choose Register.
    4. Make sure you have a free garage slot.

    An unregistered bike vanishes once you leave it, just like a car.

    Frequently asked questions

    What motorcycles are in GTA 6?

    Guides name sport bikes such as the Nagasaki Carbon RS, Dinka Double-T and Principe Alvino V1, dirt bikes such as the Maibatsu Sanchez and Manchez and the Dinka Enduro, the Pegassi Faggio scooter and cruisers such as the LCC Avarus and Western Nightblade.

    How do you get a motorcycle in GTA 6?

    Steal one, rent a dirt bike at Roostere's Rentals during Thrillbillies, keep the Highwayman Sanchez from Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, or spawn a bike with a cheat after the story. Register a bike with Moses to keep it.

    What is the fastest motorcycle in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not published top speeds, and pre-launch rankings were estimates. We will name the fastest bike only after testing.
