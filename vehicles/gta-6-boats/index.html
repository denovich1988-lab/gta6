    GTA 6 Boats: Every Boat, Jet Ski and Airboat

    GTA 6 boats named so far, from the Seashark jet ski to the Squalo speedboat and swamp airboats, the missions that use them and the boats you can spawn.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read

    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 boats include the Speedophile Seashark jet ski, Pegassi Speeder, Shitzu Squalo and Tropic speedboats, the Dinka Marquis sailboat and airboats for the swamps.
    • Boats are part of the story from the first missions: Gone Fishing starts on the water, and Boobie Time ends in a Seashark chase.
    • After the story, cheats spawn the Speeder 700 GT and the Airboat 09; the Shitzu Squalo is an Ultimate Edition vehicle.

    GTA 6 boats matter more than in any earlier game: Leonida is a state of keys, canals, rivers and swamps, and several story missions are fought on the water. This page lists the boats that launch guides agree on, the missions where you use them, where to find one in free roam and which boats the cheats spawn. Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    Jet skis and speedboats

    The Seashark jet ski is the boat you will use most in the story. In Boobie Time, thieves grab a cash bag and Jason chases them down the river on one; you ride over the bag to pick it up. Day Release includes a jet ski race, and Dubious Provenance has another Seashark chase.

    The Pegassi Speeder and the Shitzu Tropic are the open-water speedboats. The Shitzu Squalo is one of the seven vehicles named in Rockstar's edition descriptions, and GamesRadar+ notes that the Ultimate Edition version comes with an explosives crate on board.

    Tip: take a jet ski rather than a speedboat for chases in canals and rivers; every river chase in the story so far is ridden on a Seashark.

    Airboats for the swamps

    Airboats skim over shallow water and mud where propeller boats get stuck, which makes them the right choice in Grassrivers and the Everglades-style swamps of Leonida. Guides list a standard Airboat and the Airboat 09, which is also a cheat spawn.

    Missions that use boats

    Mission What you do on the water
    Gone Fishing Collect packages by boat in the tutorial
    Day Release Speedboat to a yacht, then a jet ski race
    Home Sweet Home Optional: take a boat or jet ski from the Key Lento docks
    Boobie Time Seashark chase down the river
    Dubious Provenance Follow a fleeing boat on a Seashark
    Crosstown Traffic Follow the boats, hijack the shipment and defend the boat

    Where to find a boat

    Boats sit at docks and marinas along the coast and in the keys. The easiest early spot is the docks by the houses in Key Lento, near Jason's home, where Home Sweet Home lets you take a boat or a jet ski.

    Why it matters: the Keys are linked by one long highway, so the water is often the more direct route between islands.

    • Top speeds for any boat.
    • Fishing boats and yachts. Several appear in official screenshots without agreed in-game names.

    Frequently asked questions

    What boats are in GTA 6?

    Guides name the Speedophile Seashark jet ski, Pegassi Speeder, Shitzu Squalo, Shitzu Tropic, Dinka Marquis, Nagasaki Dinghy, Clarion, Crest Kayak and swamp airboats, including the Airboat 09.

    Where do you find boats in GTA 6?

    At docks and marinas along the coast, such as the docks by the houses in Key Lento, where you can take a boat or jet ski. Story missions also hand you boats.

    Can you spawn a boat with a cheat in GTA 6?

    Yes, after the story: the Speeder 700 GT unlocks with 5 combat challenges and the Airboat 09 with 5 survival challenges.
