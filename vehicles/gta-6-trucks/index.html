    GTA 6 Trucks: Pickups, Semis and Heavy Vehicles

    GTA 6 trucks named so far: pickups like the Bison and Caracara, semi trucks like the Packer and Phantom, tow and armored trucks, and the heavy vehicles cheats spawn.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated

    Read

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 trucks range from pickups (Vapid Ganado, Bravado Bison, Vapid Caracara 4x4) to semi trucks (MTL Packer, JoBuilt Phantom Custom, Hauler).
    • Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup is the only vehicle you have in Chapter 1.
    • After the story, cheats spawn the MTL Pumper fire truck, the HVY Bulldozer and the Rhino tank.

    GTA 6 trucks cover everything from Jason's beaten-up Ganado pickup to 18-wheel semis, tow trucks, armored trucks and, after the story, a tank. Pickups are the everyday vehicle of rural Leonida, and the game leans into them more than any earlier GTA. This page lists the trucks at least two launch guides agree on, where trucks matter in the story and which heavy vehicles the cheats spawn. Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    GTA 6 trucks at a glance

    Type Models Notes
    Pickups Ganado, Bison, Caracara 4x4, Sandking XL, Yosemite 1500 Best all-rounders outside the city
    Semi trucks Packer, Phantom Custom, Hauler Slow, heavy, hard to stop
    Service trucks Tow truck, armored truck Story and job vehicles
    Monster trucks Several, names not agreed yet Seen in launch lists
    Heavy (cheats) MTL Pumper fire truck, HVY Bulldozer, Rhino Tank After the story

    Pickup trucks

    Pickup Brand Notes
    Ganado Vapid Jason's car; the only vehicle you have in Chapter 1
    Bison Bravado Several generations appear
    Caracara 4x4 Vapid Off-road performance pickup
    Sandking XL Vapid Heavy-duty pickup
    Yosemite 1500 Declasse Moses asks for one in his optional theft jobs

    Why it matters: much of Leonida outside Vice City is swamp, farmland and dirt road. A pickup keeps its speed on rough ground where sports cars bog down, and it is a sensible first car to register (see GTA 6 cars).

    Tip: the Ganado gets a Retro Build version with the Ultimate Edition; see GTA 6 Ultimate Edition.

    Semi trucks

    Launch guides list three semis: the MTL Packer, a modern cab with a large sleeper; the JoBuilt Phantom Custom, a classic long-nose 1980s rig; and the Hauler, a flat-nosed cab.

    Tow trucks and armored trucks

    • Tow trucks appear in the world and in missions. In Unwanted Attention, the car you are after sits hooked to a tow truck in the Hamlet impound, and you release it with R3.
    • Armored trucks are robbery targets. In Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, you chase one on dirt bikes; do not shoot it, follow it until it crashes, then blow the rear doors.

    Heavy vehicle cheats

    After you finish the story, three heavy vehicles can be spawned once their cheat is unlocked. Codes are on GTA 6 cheats.

    Vehicle Unlock task
    MTL Pumper (fire truck) Discover it in the Progress menu
    HVY Bulldozer Discover it in the Progress menu
    Hauners Rhino Tank Complete all survival challenges

    What is not confirmed yet

    • Monster truck names and locations. Guides list monster trucks but name them differently.
    • Trailers and hauling jobs for the semi trucks.
    • Top speeds for any truck.

    Frequently asked questions

    What trucks are in GTA 6?

    Pickups such as the Vapid Ganado, Bravado Bison, Vapid Caracara 4x4, Vapid Sandking XL and Declasse Yosemite 1500; semi trucks such as the MTL Packer, JoBuilt Phantom Custom and Hauler; plus tow trucks, armored trucks and monster trucks.

    Can you get a monster truck in GTA 6?

    Monster trucks appear in launch guides' vehicle lists, but they do not agree on names or where to find one yet.

    Is there a tank in GTA 6?

    Yes. The Hauners Rhino Tank is a cheat spawn that unlocks after completing all survival challenges, once the story is finished.
