    GTA 6 Planes and Helicopters: Every Aircraft and How to Fly

    GTA 6 planes and helicopters named so far, when you first fly, where to find aircraft, how to land a seaplane and which aircraft the cheats spawn.

    Quick answer

    • You first fly in Chapter 1: Gone Fishing ends with a seaplane flight, and Down to Earth is the first full seaplane mission.
    • Planes include the Mammoth Dodo seaplane, the Lapwing Mallard stunt plane and the Duster biplane; helicopters include the Maverick, SuperVolito and Nagasaki Sea Sparrow.
    • Land slowly: hitting the water or runway too hard destroys the plane.

    GTA 6 planes and helicopters are part of the story from the first chapter: Jason flies a seaplane home at the end of Gone Fishing, and Brian's drug runs keep him in the air after that. This page lists the aircraft that launch guides agree on, explains when flying unlocks, how to take off and land without wrecking the plane, and which aircraft the cheats spawn. Rockstar has not published an official aircraft list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    When you first fly

    1. Gone Fishing (Chapter 1). The tutorial mission ends with Jason flying the seaplane back to the marina. See the Gone Fishing walkthrough.
    2. Down to Earth (Chapter 1). Brian's first flying job: Jason and Lucia fly to Bobcat Bay, make drops and land on water. See the Down to Earth walkthrough.
    3. Free roam. After that, you can take planes from airstrips and helicopters from helipads whenever you like.

    Why it matters: flying is the fastest way to uncover the map. MetaForge and GamesFuze both recommend a crop duster for clearing the fog region by region, because it does not draw unwanted attention and carries plenty of fuel.

    How to fly and land

    1. Build speed on the runway or water, then pull back on the left stick to take off.
    2. Steer with the left stick; use the rudder buttons the game shows to turn the nose without banking.
    3. Raise the landing gear on planes that have it.
    4. To land, line up early, cut the throttle and hold the slow-down button (L2 on PS5) while you descend.
    5. Touch down level. On water, steer with the rudder to the dock.

    Tip: VGC warns that hitting the water too hard in Down to Earth destroys the plane and fails the mission. Slow, shallow approaches always work.

    Where to find planes and helicopters

    PowerPyx, MetaForge and GamesFuze agree on these early spots:

    • Key Lento, Mariana County: a crop duster on the small airstrip near Jason's house, the earliest plane you can reach
    • West of the swamps, Mariana County: a backup plane
    • East of Hamlet: a helicopter on a helipad
    • Vice City skyscrapers: helicopters on rooftop helipads marked (H) on the map; enter the lobby and take the lift up

    Tip: fuel runs out, and an empty plane crashes. Land at the nearest airstrip and swap planes when the gauge turns yellow, and turn on the fuel gauge in the HUD settings before long flights.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you fly planes in GTA 6?

    Yes. You fly a seaplane at the end of Gone Fishing in Chapter 1, and Down to Earth is a full seaplane mission. Planes and helicopters then appear at airstrips and helipads across Leonida.

    What planes are in GTA 6?

    Guides name the Mammoth Dodo, Lapwing Mallard, Duster biplane, Mammatus, Nimbus jet, Shamal jet and a blimp. The P-996 LAZER fighter jet is a cheat spawn.

    What helicopters are in GTA 6?

    The Maverick and Police Maverick, the SuperVolito, the Nagasaki Sea Sparrow, which can land on water, and the police Buzzard.

    How do you land a plane in GTA 6?

    Slow down well before you touch down; on PS5 guides recommend holding L2. Come in level and steer with the rudder once you are on the water or runway.
