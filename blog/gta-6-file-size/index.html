    GTA 6 File Size: How Many GB on PS5 and Xbox

    GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space. Here is the file size on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, why boxed copies still download, and how to make room.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    5 min

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console.
    • The PS5 download is about 210 GB; the Microsoft Store lists just under 210 GB on Xbox.
    • Boxed copies contain a download code, so they need the same space.

    The GTA 6 file size is about 210 GB, and Rockstar says to have up to 212 GB of free storage. Sony gives PS5 owners the same 212 GB figure as a minimum. That makes GTA 6 one of the largest console installs so far, and it is the same whether you bought it digitally or in a box. Below: the size on each console, the updates released so far, how long the download takes, and the quickest ways to make room.

    How we checked: the 212 GB figure is Rockstar's own, and the per-console sizes and update sizes are each reported by at least two outlets.

    GTA 6 file size at a glance

    Console Download Free space to plan for
    PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro About 210 GB 212 GB
    Xbox Series X and Series S About 209.9 GB (Microsoft Store listing) 212 GB
    PC No PC version Not applicable

    Rockstar's wording is "up to 212 GB depending on your device", and it has not given a separate number for each Xbox model. Planning for 212 GB on any console is the safe choice.

    The updates so far

    Two updates were released between pre-load and launch. Neither came with patch notes.

    Update Date PS5 Xbox Series X|S
    01.002.000 November 13, 2026 About 1 GB Not reported
    01.002.001 November 16, 2026 About 602 MB About 1.6 GB

    Why it matters: updates need working room while they install, so a drive with exactly 212 GB free can still refuse a patch. Keep a few gigabytes spare beyond the game itself.

    Why the boxed copy does not save space

    The physical edition of GTA 6 has a download code in the box instead of a disc. You redeem the code and download the full game, so a boxed copy takes exactly as much space as a digital one, and it cannot be installed without an internet connection. Boxed copies reached shops from November 12, a week before launch. More in GTA 6 physical copy.

    Tip: a boxed copy as a gift still means a 210 GB download on the day. Redeem and download it ahead of time if you can.

    How much of your console that is

    Console Usable space when new Share GTA 6 takes
    PS5 (launch model, 825 GB drive) Roughly 667 GB Close to a third
    Xbox Series S (512 GB model) Roughly 364 GB More than half
    Xbox Series X (1 TB) Roughly 800 GB About a quarter

    Usable space is always lower than the number on the box, because the system software takes its share.

    Why it matters: on a 512 GB Series S, GTA 6 and one other large game are about all that fits. If that is your console, an expansion card is worth considering before anything else. Our best console for GTA 6 comparison weighs storage alongside price and picture quality.

    How long the download takes

    These are simple calculations for a 210 GB download at a steady speed. Real downloads are slower at busy times.

    Connection speed Approximate time
    50 Mbps About 9 hours 20 minutes
    100 Mbps About 4 hours 40 minutes
    300 Mbps About 1 hour 35 minutes
    500 Mbps About 55 minutes
    1 Gbps About 30 minutes

    Tip: use a network cable if you can, and start the download before you go to bed. Both consoles keep downloading in rest mode when that option is switched on.

    How to make room

    1. Open storage settings and sort games by size.
    2. Delete large games you are not playing. You can download them again later.
    3. Move PS4 or older Xbox games to an external USB drive. Current-generation games must run from internal or official expansion storage.
    4. If you are still short, add an M.2 SSD on PS5 or a Storage Expansion Card on Xbox.
    5. Clear old video captures; they add up quickly.
    6. Leave a few gigabytes above 212 GB free for updates.

    If the download gets stuck

    Launch week put heavy load on the stores, and PS5 owners had PlayStation Network sign-in errors on November 18. If your download stalls:

    1. Pause and resume first. Cancelling throws away what has already been downloaded.
    2. Check the service status page for PlayStation or Xbox before changing anything on your console.
    3. Update the console. On PS5, a system software update is required before GTA 6 will start.
    4. Check the account. You must be signed in to the account that owns the game. On PS5, if the game shows as not owned, use Settings, Users and Accounts, Other, Restore Licenses.
    5. Only then delete and download again.

    Is there a way to install less?

    Not at the moment. Rockstar has not offered separate packs, such as leaving out some audio languages, and the game is a single download. GTA 6 is also single-player only at launch, so there is no separate online component to skip; see GTA 6 online.

    What is not confirmed

    • A PC file size. There is no PC version and no PC requirements; see GTA 6 PC requirements.
    • Exact sizes per Xbox model. Rockstar gives one "up to" figure for all devices.
    • Future growth. Updates will add to the size over time, but Rockstar has announced none after launch.

    What this means for you

    If you have a PS5 or a Series X with a few other games installed, expect to delete two or three of them. If you have a 512 GB Series S, plan around GTA 6 being most of your drive. Either way, sort out storage before you sit down to play: the download is the slow part, not the install.

    When you could download it

    Pre-loading opened on November 10, 2026 for digital pre-orders, and the game unlocked at local midnight on November 19. For the timing in each region, see what time GTA 6 came out. Prices and editions are in our editions guide, and the supported consoles are listed in GTA 6 platforms.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many GB is GTA 6?

    Up to 212 GB of free space, according to Rockstar. The download itself is about 210 GB on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

    Is the GTA 6 file size different on Xbox?

    Barely. The Microsoft Store lists about 209.9 GB, close to the roughly 210 GB download on PS5.

    Does the physical copy of GTA 6 save storage space?

    No. The boxed edition contains a download code, not a disc, so it uses the same space as a digital copy.
