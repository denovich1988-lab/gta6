    How Much Money Has GTA 6 Made? Copies Sold and Revenue So Far

    How much money GTA 6 has made so far: no official figure yet, but Sensor Tower estimates 24.6 million copies and $2.36 billion through launch day.

    How much money has GTA 6 made? Officially, nobody outside Take-Two knows yet: the publisher has not released a sales figure as of November 20, 2026. The best available number is an estimate from the analytics firm Sensor Tower: about 24.6 million copies and $2.36 billion through launch day, with pre-orders counted in. Below is what that number covers, how it grew over 48 hours, how it compares with GTA 5's record, and when a real figure is likely.

    How we checked: every figure here appears in at least two independent reports, or is marked with the single outlet that reported it. Estimates are labelled as estimates throughout.

    There is no official number yet

    Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, has not said how many copies GTA 6 has sold. Before launch its statements were deliberately vague. On its earnings call on November 9, chief executive Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders were far in excess of the company's expectations and, in the line most outlets quoted, that "we still aren't ready to declare victory."

    Why it matters: every number in a headline this week is someone else's estimate. In 2013 it was the other way round: Take-Two announced GTA 5's first-day sales itself, one day after launch.

    The Sensor Tower estimate: 24.6 million copies, $2.36 billion

    Sensor Tower tracks digital store activity and publishes running estimates. By the evening of launch day, November 19, its tracker showed about 24.58 to 24.59 million copies and roughly $2.36 billion in revenue. Neither Take-Two nor Rockstar has confirmed it.

    Three things to know about the figure:

    • It is cumulative. It includes months of pre-orders, not only copies bought after the midnight unlock.
    • It covers PlayStation and Xbox. Those are the only platforms the game is on; see GTA 6 platforms.
    • It does not subtract refunds, as TweakTown points out.

    Tip: when you see "GTA 6 sold 24 million in a day", read it as "24 million through its first day, counting pre-orders".

    How the estimate grew in 48 hours

    The same tracker was quoted at three points, which is why you will find three different numbers online.

    When Estimated copies Estimated revenue
    Wednesday, November 18, midday About 17.1 million About $1.65 billion
    Thursday, November 19, morning About 19.1 million About $1.84 billion
    Thursday, November 19, evening About 24.6 million About $2.36 billion

    The game unlocked at local midnight in each region, starting in New Zealand on November 18; see what time GTA 6 came out. The Wednesday figure is therefore almost entirely pre-orders.

    Why it matters: on these estimates, roughly 7.5 million copies and about $700 million were added between Wednesday midday and Thursday night. Most of the launch-day total was already in the bank before anyone played.

    PlayStation vs Xbox

    Sensor Tower's split is about three to one in PlayStation's favour.

    Platform Estimated copies Estimated revenue
    PlayStation 5 About 18.4 million About $1.77 billion
    Xbox Series X and Series S About 6.2 million About $588 million

    The per-platform figures were reported by GameSpot and repeated by Make Tech Easier. Before launch, Sony said GTA 6 was the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation Store, and Microsoft said the same for its own store. Neither gave a count.

    Why it matters: if you are choosing where to play with friends, far more of them are likely to be on PlayStation. Our best console for GTA 6 comparison covers the other differences.

    How it compares with GTA 5's record

    GTA 5 set the bar every big launch has been measured against since 2013. Its numbers were official:

    GTA 5 (2013) GTA 6 (2026, estimate)
    First day, copies 11.21 million About 24.6 million
    First day, revenue $815.7 million About $2.36 billion
    Source Take-Two, recorded by Guinness World Records Sensor Tower
    Time to $1 billion Three days Passed before launch on pre-orders, by the estimate

    On paper GTA 6 is at more than twice the copies and almost three times the revenue. Two cautions apply. The GTA 5 figure came from the publisher and the GTA 6 figure from an outside firm. And a dollar in 2013 is not a dollar in 2026: the base game costs more now, which is the next point.

    Why revenue rose faster than copies

    GTA 5 launched at $59.99. GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. Dividing Sensor Tower's revenue by its copies gives an average of about $96 per copy. That is our arithmetic on their estimate, not a reported figure, but it suggests either that a large share of early buyers chose the more expensive edition or that the estimate runs high.

    Tip: if you have not bought yet, the difference between the two is covered in GTA 6 price and editions and what the Ultimate Edition adds.

    What Take-Two and the platform holders have said

    • Take-Two has described demand with phrases such as "unprecedented" and "far exceeding expectations", without numbers.
    • Zelnick has said official sales data will be shared soon, and that the company prefers not to claim success before it happens.
    • Sony and Microsoft each called GTA 6 their store's most pre-ordered game ever, on November 10.

    One comparison Zelnick did give is for the previous game: GTA 5 has now sold nearly 235 million copies. That is the long-run number GTA 6 will eventually be measured against.

    Has GTA 6 made its budget back?

    Rockstar has never published a budget. Reports citing Reuters put the development cost at an estimated $1 billion or more. If the Sensor Tower estimate is close, launch-day revenue alone is more than twice that, before the stores and retailers take their share and before marketing is counted. Our GTA 6 budget page goes through what is and is not known about the cost, and how long GTA 6 was in development explains where the money went.

    What analysts expect next

    These are forecasts from individual firms, each reported by one outlet we checked:

    Firm Forecast Reported by
    Wedbush About 30 million copies and $3 billion by the end of 2026 Men's Journal
    Oppenheimer About $1.85 billion gross over the first three days AccountShark
    Wells Fargo About 65 million copies in Take-Two's fiscal year to March 2027 AccountShark

    Take-Two's shares rose about 6% on Wednesday, November 18, and slipped about 1% on launch day itself.

    What is not confirmed

    • Any official unit or revenue figure. Everything above is an estimate or a forecast.
    • Sales of boxed copies as a separate number. The box contains a download code, so retail copies are redeemed on the same stores; see GTA 6 physical copy.
    • Regional sales. No report we checked breaks the estimate down by country.
    • Profit. Revenue is not profit, and Take-Two has not discussed either for launch.

    How to read GTA 6 sales headlines

    1. Check the source. "Take-Two announced" is official. "Sensor Tower estimates" or "analysts expect" is not.
    2. Check the window. "First day", "through launch day", "first 24 hours" and "first three days" are different measures.
    3. Check for pre-orders. Almost all early figures include them.
    4. Check the unit. Copies and dollars are often swapped in headlines; 24.6 million is copies, $2.36 billion is revenue.
    5. Ignore numbers with no source at all. Round figures passed around on social media usually trace back to nothing.

    What this means for you

    For players, the size of the launch matters in two practical ways. A game that sells like this gets long support, so patches and extra content are a safe bet even though Rockstar has dated nothing; see GTA 6 online for what has been said about multiplayer. And critics agree with the buyers: the game sits at 99 on Metacritic, as covered in GTA 6 reviews.

    What we will add to this page

    • Take-Two's first official sales announcement, the moment it is published. One report said it could come as early as Friday, November 20; the company has only said "soon".
    • First-weekend and first-week figures.
    • The numbers from Take-Two's next earnings report. The launch falls in its fiscal third quarter, which ends on December 31, 2026.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much money has GTA 6 made?

    There is no official number yet. The analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates about $2.36 billion through launch day, November 19, 2026, including months of pre-orders. Take-Two has not confirmed it.

    How many copies of GTA 6 have sold?

    About 24.6 million through launch day according to Sensor Tower's estimate: roughly 18.4 million on PS5 and 6.2 million on Xbox Series X|S. It is an estimate, not a publisher figure.

    Did GTA 6 beat GTA 5's first-day sales record?

    On the estimates, easily. GTA 5 sold 11.21 million copies worth $815.7 million in its first 24 hours. The comparison is not like for like, because the GTA 6 figure is a third-party estimate that includes pre-orders.
