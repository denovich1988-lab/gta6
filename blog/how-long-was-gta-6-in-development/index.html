    How Long Has GTA 6 Been in Development?

    GTA 6 was in development for about 11 years, counting from early work in 2015. Most of the team joined after 2018. The verified timeline and team size.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar's head of development says GTA 6 has been in development since 2015, about 11 years before release.
    • Most of the team moved onto the game after Red Dead Redemption 2 shipped in October 2018.
    • Bloomberg reported that Rockstar grew from 2,441 staff in 2018 to 4,711 in August 2026.

    GTA 6 has been in development for about 11 years, counting from the first work in 2015 to its release on November 19, 2026. That is the longest answer. Most of the team only joined after 2018, so the main production took about eight years. Both numbers are right; they count different things. This page sets out each way of counting, the dated timeline, how big the studio became, how that compares with Rockstar's earlier games and what is still not on record.

    Dates below come from Rockstar's own announcements. Everything about the early years comes from named Rockstar staff or from reporting that at least two outlets carried, and is labelled as such.

    GTA 6 development time at a glance

    Counted from Length Why people use it
    GTA 5's release, September 2013 13 years The gap between the two games. It is not development time.
    Early work, 2015 About 11 years Rockstar's Rob Nelson says the game has been in development since 2015.
    After Red Dead Redemption 2, late 2018 About 8 years When most of the team moved onto GTA 6.
    Public confirmation, February 2022 4 years 9 months How long players knew it was being made.

    When did GTA 6 start development?

    In 2015, in a small way. Rob Nelson, Rockstar's head of development, told the French outlet TroisCouleurs that the game has been in development since 2015, as GamesRadar+ reported in September 2026.

    Bloomberg's Jason Schreier described the same starting point in his October 2026 feature on the making of the game. According to reports on that feature, Rockstar staff began research trips to Miami in 2015, and the project carried the code name Project Americas. The early plan was wider than the finished game and was later cut back to Vice City and the state of Leonida. These details come from Bloomberg's reporting, not from a Rockstar announcement.

    Why it matters: "11 years" does not mean thousands of people worked on GTA 6 for 11 years. The first years were research and planning by a small group while the rest of the company built another game.

    When did full production begin?

    Rockstar has never given a date. The evidence points to late 2018.

    • Red Dead Redemption 2 came out in October 2018, and most of Rockstar worked on it until then.
    • A former Rockstar North environment artist, David O'Reilly, said in a video that he worked on GTA 6 from 2018 to 2023, after Red Dead Redemption 2 wrapped. GamesRadar+ and Push Square reported this in May 2025.
    • Reports on the Bloomberg feature put the main development at more than eight years, which matches a 2018 start.

    So "since 2018" is a well-supported estimate, not an official statement.

    Tip: when you see "8 years" and "11 years" in different headlines, they are not in conflict. Check which starting point the article uses.

    The 13-year gap is not development time

    GTA 5 came out on September 17, 2013, and GTA 6 on November 19, 2026: thirteen years and two months apart. Rockstar spent the first five of those years on other work, above all Red Dead Redemption 2 and the ongoing GTA Online.

    Why it matters: the gap explains the hype, and the development time explains the cost. They are separate questions, and the second is covered in how much GTA 6 cost to make.

    How the delays added to the total

    The game moved twice: from a 2025 window to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19, 2026. Together that is about a year. The second step alone was 177 days.

    Rockstar's public reason both times was more time for polish. Without the two moves, the count from 2015 would have been about ten years.

    Why it matters: the delays are a small part of the total. Even on the original 2025 plan, this would have been Rockstar's longest project. The details are in how many times GTA 6 was delayed.

    How big is the GTA 6 dev team?

    There is no official count of the people who worked on GTA 6. What we have is the size of Rockstar as a whole, from Bloomberg's October 2026 feature:

    When Rockstar employees
    2018, at Red Dead Redemption 2's release 2,441
    August 2026 4,711, across 12 offices

    The company nearly doubled while GTA 6 was being made. Bloomberg also reported that Rockstar set up a Los Angeles studio dedicated to creating the game's non-player characters, and that Rob Nelson said the studio added staff when it saw how many characters had to be written and animated.

    Why it matters: not every Rockstar employee works on GTA 6 directly, so 4,711 is an upper limit, not a team size.

    How it compares with Rockstar's earlier games

    Rockstar does not publish development times, so the fairest comparison uses release dates, which are on record.

    Game Released Gap since the previous game in its series
    Grand Theft Auto V September 17, 2013 About 5 years after GTA IV
    Red Dead Redemption 2 October 26, 2018 About 8 years after Red Dead Redemption
    Grand Theft Auto VI November 19, 2026 About 13 years after GTA V

    Each gap is longer than the last, and each game was delayed at least once on the way.

    Why it matters: the pattern is useful if you are waiting for anything Rockstar has not dated, such as a PC version. See GTA 6 PC release date.

    Why it took so long

    Rockstar's public reason for both delays was polish. The Bloomberg feature, as reported, and Rockstar's own descriptions of the game add the rest:

    • A late start. Most of the studio was finishing Red Dead Redemption 2 until October 2018.
    • The size of the world. Rockstar's developers describe Leonida as about twice the size of GTA 5's map; see GTA 6 map size.
    • The number of characters. Enough that Rockstar opened a studio for them.
    • Density. More than 170 animal species and far more interiors than earlier Rockstar games; see GTA 6 new features.
    • Length. A story that one Rockstar studio head put at about 80 hours in his own playthrough; see how long GTA 6 is.

    What is not confirmed

    • An official start date. 2015 comes from one Rockstar executive in an interview and from Bloomberg; Rockstar has not put it in an announcement.
    • The date full production began. Late 2018 is an estimate.
    • The size of the GTA 6 team. Only company-wide headcount is reported.
    • The code name and the wider early plan. Reported by Bloomberg, not confirmed by Rockstar.
    • What the extra year of delays was spent on. Rockstar has only said polish.

    How to read a "years in development" claim

    1. Find the starting point: 2013, 2015 or 2018.
    2. Check whether it counts research and planning or only full production.
    3. Look for the source: a named Rockstar employee, Bloomberg's reporting, or neither.
    4. Remember the two delays add about one year to any count made before 2025.
    5. Treat team sizes as company headcount unless the source says otherwise.

    What this means for you

    The short answer to "how long has GTA 6 been in development" is eleven years from first work and eight years of full production. For players, the practical point is the pace: Rockstar now takes most of a decade per game, so what you are playing has to last. That is one reason the launch game is as large as it is, and one reason reviews were so strong; see GTA 6 reviews.
