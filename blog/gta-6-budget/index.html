    How Much Did GTA 6 Cost to Make?

    The GTA 6 budget has never been published. Analysts estimate $1 billion to $1.5 billion. What Take-Two has said and how it compares with GTA 5.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    6 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar and Take-Two have never published a budget for GTA 6.
    • Analyst estimates reported in 2026 put development at about $1 billion to $1.5 billion.
    • GTA 5 reportedly cost about $265 million including marketing.

    The GTA 6 budget has never been published: Take-Two has only said the game was expensive, and the figure most often quoted, $1 billion to $1.5 billion, is an analyst estimate. Higher numbers exist, and so do lower ones for older games, but none of them comes from the company's accounts. This page sorts the figures by where they come from, explains why they differ, and sets them beside what the game has to earn.

    Every number below is attributed to the outlet or person who gave it. Nothing here is an official budget, because there is none.

    GTA 6 budget figures at a glance

    Figure Source What it covers Status
    No figure Take-Two Interactive n/a Official position: not disclosed
    $1 billion to $1.5 billion Industry analysts quoted by Business Insider, May 2026 Development; reports say it probably excludes marketing Estimate
    About $2 billion Josh Chapman of Konvoy, cited by PC Gamer Total investment assumed for a break-even model Estimate

    $3.4 billion Dan Dawkins, cited by PC Gamer Includes pre-production and Rockstar payroll over six years Estimate

    What Take-Two has actually said

    Chief executive Strauss Zelnick has been asked about the cost several times in 2026 and has never given a number.

    • To Business Insider in May, he said the game was expensive, as GamingBolt and Gfinity both report.
    • In a Bloomberg interview the same month, covered by PC Gamer, he said development costs keep rising and that AI has not yet brought them down, and described spending at this level as a bet for "big boys only" that he is comfortable with.
    • Destructoid reports that on CNBC he called production costs high, said the figure has not been disclosed, and said the company "seeks perfection".

    Take-Two does not break out budgets for individual games in its financial reports. Gfinity notes that the company has never confirmed a budget for Red Dead Redemption 2 either.

    Why it matters: any headline that says "GTA 6 cost $X" is quoting someone outside the company. The honest answer to the question is a range with a label on each end.

    The analyst estimate: $1 billion to $1.5 billion

    This is the range you will see most. It comes from unnamed industry analysts who spoke to Business Insider in May 2026. GamingBolt's report says it probably leaves out marketing, and PC Gamer describes it as the low end of the estimates in circulation.

    Why it matters: if marketing is excluded, the total spend to get the game onto shelves would be higher than this range even if the analysts are right about development.

    The higher estimates: $2 billion and above

    Three larger figures are in circulation, and they measure different things.

    • About $2 billion. PC Gamer cites Josh Chapman of the investment firm Konvoy, who used it as the budget in a model of how quickly the game pays for itself.
    • More than $2 billion in salaries. GamingBolt reports this from UK public records and notes that neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed it and that it contradicts the analyst range.
    • $3.4 billion. PC Gamer cites an estimate from Dan Dawkins that counts pre-production and Rockstar's payroll across six years.

    Tip: when you compare figures, check three things: does it include marketing, does it include pre-production, and does it count all of Rockstar's staff costs or only the GTA 6 team? The spread from $1 billion to $3.4 billion is mostly a disagreement about what to count.

    How it compares with other games

    Game Reported cost Note
    GTA 5 (2013) About $265 million Widely quoted estimate for development and marketing
    Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) $350 million to $550 million Estimates, per Destructoid; never confirmed
    Cyberpunk 2077 About $526 million As listed by Gfinity
    GTA 6 (2026) $1 billion to $1.5 billion or more Analyst estimates

    Gfinity's summary is that GTA 6 cost more than four times as much as GTA 5 at the low end of the estimate. Even that low end would put it among the most expensive entertainment products ever made.

    Why it cost so much

    • Time. Thirteen years separate GTA 5 and GTA 6. First work dates back to 2015 and most of the team joined after 2018; see how long GTA 6 was in development.
    • Scale. A whole state to explore, about twice the size of GTA 5's map according to Rockstar; see GTA 6 map size.
    • Density. More than 170 animal species, far more interiors and a story of about 80 hours by one Rockstar studio head's own playthrough; see how long is GTA 6.
    • Talent and music. Full performance capture for the cast and a licensed soundtrack with well-known radio hosts; see GTA 6 radio stations.
    • People. Rockstar's studios around the world worked on it, and salaries over many years are the largest cost in any of the estimates.
    • Delays. The game moved twice before landing on November 19, 2026, and every extra month is another month of payroll; see GTA 6 delays.

    Why it matters: none of these costs is unusual on its own. What is unusual is all of them at once, for this long.

    What it has to earn back

    The Standard Edition costs $79.99; prices for every edition are in GTA 6 price and editions.

    Two forecasts frame the other side of the ledger:

    • Destructoid reports that Take-Two told investors it expects about $8 billion in revenue in fiscal 2027, the year that includes the game's release. That is a company-wide figure.
    • PC Gamer cites Chapman's projection of roughly $7.6 billion in revenue within 60 days of launch, with break-even inside a month on a $2 billion budget. That is one analyst's model.

    Why it matters: on any of the estimates above, the game is expected to cover its cost quickly. The early sales picture, with estimates labelled as estimates, is in how much money has GTA 6 made.

    What is not confirmed

    • Any official budget. Take-Two has not published one and has not said it will.
    • The marketing spend. No source gives a reliable figure.
    • The UK salary figure. It is reported from public records but not confirmed by the companies.
    • Team size. None of the reports we checked gives a verified headcount for GTA 6.
    • Whether it is the most expensive game ever. Probably, if the estimates are right, but it cannot be proved without the real number.

    How to read a new budget headline

    1. Find the source. Is it Take-Two, a named analyst, or "reports"?
    2. Check the scope: development only, development plus marketing, or all Rockstar payroll.
    3. Check the date. Estimates from before the final delay will be low.
    4. Compare it with the ranges in the table at the top of this page.
    5. Treat any single precise number with suspicion; the people who know have not said.

    What this means for you

    For players, the budget changes nothing about the game on your console. It matters as context: it helps explain the $79.99 price and the long wait, and why so much attention is on launch sales.

    What we will add to this page

    • Any figure Take-Two discloses in an earnings report or filing.
    • New named-analyst estimates, with their scope.
    • Official sales and revenue numbers as they are reported, to set against the cost.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much did GTA 6 cost to make?

    There is no official figure. Analyst estimates reported in May 2026 range from about $1 billion to $1.5 billion.

    Is GTA 6 the most expensive game ever made?

    If the estimates are right, yes. But the estimates are not confirmed by Take-Two.

    Did GTA 6 cost $2 billion?

    That figure is unverified. Higher numbers usually add marketing to development and are not from Take-Two.
