    How Many Chapters Are in GTA 6? Chapter List and What Locks

    How many chapters are in GTA 6? Five, after a Prologue. Where each chapter is set, how many missions it has, what unlocks and what you can miss.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read

    By

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has five numbered chapters, opened by a playable Prologue.
    • Guides count between 91 and 122 missions depending on whether short tasks and optional errands are included; VGC's recap-based list has 95.
    • Chapter ends are the points of no return: Chapter 1 closes Jason's house projects and two missable errands, and Chapter 2 asks for $15,000 before the move to Vice City.

    There are five chapters in GTA 6, and a playable Prologue comes before Chapter 1. Every major walkthrough divides the story the same way. What they do not agree on is how many missions those chapters hold, because the game mixes full missions with one-line tasks and optional errands. This page lays out the chapters without story spoilers: where each is set, how big it is, what it unlocks and what closes when it ends.

    We compared four independent mission lists (VGC, PowerPyx, GTABase and Dexerto). A fact is stated here only when at least two agree; where they differ, the table shows each count.

    GTA 6 chapters at a glance

    Chapter Setting and premise (spoiler-light) Missions (VGC) Missions (Dexerto)
    Prologue Lucia; the opening counted in Chapter 1 counted in Chapter 1
    Chapter 1 Jason in the Leonida Keys, preparing for Lucia's release 22 22
    Chapter 2 Kelly County: the Starlet Motel and Port Gellhorn 13 11
    Chapter 3 Vice City 28 26
    Chapter 4 The two leads go undercover 25 25
    Chapter 5 The finale 7 7
    Total 95 91

    The one-line premises are VGC's, from its spoiler-free chapter guide. Chapters are numbered in VGC's list rather than named.

    Why the mission count ranges from 91 to 122

    You will see several different totals quoted:

    Source Total What it counts
    Dexerto 91 Main missions
    VGC 95 Main missions in the order of the in-game story recap
    PowerPyx Not totalled; the longest list Main missions plus optional and missable tasks, each with its own entry
    GTABase 122 numbered entries Every story entry, including short tasks such as paying a fee or waiting for a call

    The difference is bookkeeping. Chapter 2 shows it well: VGC lists 13 missions there, while PowerPyx lists 27 entries because it gives separate pages to one-step tasks such as Overnight Charges, New Look and Behind on Payments, plus nine optional jobs.

    Why it matters: when a guide says "mission 31" and another says "mission 25", they usually mean the same mission. Our GTA 6 mission list follows VGC's numbering and notes the alternatives.

    Prologue

    The game opens with a Prologue played as Lucia. PowerPyx and GTABase list it before Chapter 1; VGC and Dexerto count it as the first mission of Chapter 1. Either way it is a single mission, covered in our Prologue walkthrough.

    Tip: nothing in the Prologue is missable, so play it at your own pace and use it to learn the controls.

    Chapter 1: the Keys

    Chapter 1 belongs almost entirely to Jason. It runs from Gone Fishing to Discharge Time, on both PowerPyx's and VGC's lists, and teaches the basics: driving, shooting, the phone, banking and the wanted level. Lucia is playable only in parts of a few missions.

    What closes at the end:

    • Jason's house projects. All four repairs have to be done before Lucia is released; see Jason's house projects.
    • Two missable errands. PowerPyx and GTABase both flag Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter as missable.
    • Free roam, for a while. The last three missions play back to back.

    Tip: read the checklist in the Recalibration walkthrough before starting Tourist Trap, the first of the three.

    Chapter 2: Kelly County

    Chapter 2 moves the story north to the Starlet Motel and Port Gellhorn, with Lucia in the lead while Jason recovers. It is the shortest of the middle chapters by mission count, but it switches on several systems:

    Unlock Where
    Slim Jim, car registration, selling stolen cars Moses
    Scooter Bros vehicle delivery app Moses
    First garage and the Vice City Real Estate app After Daily Driver
    Switching freely between Jason and Lucia During the chapter; see how to switch characters

    The chapter also has a money gate. GTABase lists Prepare for Vice City, near the end, as needing $15,000 to unlock.

    Why it matters: this is the first point where being broke stops the story. Start selling cars to Moses early; other methods are in how to make money in GTA 6.

    Chapter 3: Vice City

    Chapter 3 opens with the move to Vice City and is the largest chapter on every list: 28 missions for VGC, 26 for Dexerto, 38 entries for PowerPyx. Both PowerPyx and GTABase show it building towards a multi-part heist, with separate missions for scouting and preparation before the job itself. Some missions are marked for Jason only or Lucia only, so expect to switch often.

    New tools arrive too: PowerPyx and GTABase both place the Key Cloner, which deals with advanced car trackers, in Chapter 3.

    One optional street-racing mission in this chapter is flagged as time-sensitive and missable by both PowerPyx and GTABase.

    Tip: when a side contact's mission is labelled time-sensitive, do it before the next main mission. Missions cannot be replayed later; see can you replay missions in GTA 6.

    Chapter 4: undercover

    VGC sums up Chapter 4 as Jason and Lucia going undercover. It has 25 missions on VGC's and Dexerto's lists and 39 entries on PowerPyx's, and it follows the pattern of Chapter 3: a long run of preparation missions leading to a second major heist. It contains the other time-sensitive optional mission that both PowerPyx and GTABase mark as missable, this one for Lucia.

    Why it matters: Chapters 3 and 4 together hold more than half of the main missions on every list. If you are pacing yourself, the end of Chapter 2 is roughly the one-third mark by mission count, not the halfway point.

    Chapter 5: the finale

    Chapter 5 is short: 7 missions for VGC and Dexerto, 10 entries for PowerPyx and GTABase. VGC redacts three of its titles to avoid spoilers, and so do we.

    Tip: make a separate manual save before starting Chapter 5. With no mission replay, it is the only way to see the ending again without a new game. How to save in GTA 6 explains manual slots.

    What unlocks after the story

    Finishing Chapter 5 is the condition for the Cheats menu: all 61 cheats stay hidden until the story is done, and each then has its own unlock task. The list and the codes published so far are on our GTA 6 cheats page. Free roam continues, which is where cheats, challenges and collectibles are finished.

    Why it matters: the story is only one line of the 35 on the in-game checklist, so the credits are a midpoint for completionists. See the 100% completion checklist.

    How this compares with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2

    Game Story structure
    GTA 5 No chapters; missions unlock by contact and character
    Red Dead Redemption 2 Six chapters and a two-part epilogue
    GTA 6 A Prologue and five chapters

    GTA 6 borrows the Red Dead approach: each chapter has its own home base and its own cast of contacts, and moving on means leaving a place behind. In GTA 5 almost nothing closed as the story advanced. Here, the end of a chapter can shut a location for a while; VGC notes, for example, that the motel from Chapter 2 is out of reach for a time once that chapter is over.

    Why it matters: if you played GTA 5 by ignoring side content until the end, change the habit. Finish what interests you in a region before its chapter finale. More differences are in GTA 6's new features.

    What is not confirmed

    • Chapter titles. VGC numbers the chapters only. One list shows titles for each chapter; we have not been able to verify them against a second source, so we are not repeating them.
    • Hours per chapter. Per-chapter times circulated before launch came from leaks. The only figure from Rockstar is about 80 hours for one studio head's full playthrough; see how long is GTA 6.
    • An epilogue. Some pre-launch articles expected one. None of the four launch lists has a separate epilogue.
    • An official mission count. Rockstar has not published one.

    What to do before each chapter ends

    1. Chapter 1: finish the four house projects and the optional errands, bank your cash, then start Tourist Trap.
    2. Chapter 2: build up $15,000 for Prepare for Vice City, register a car with Moses, and clear the optional jobs around Port Gellhorn you care about.
    3. Chapter 3: do time-sensitive side missions as soon as they appear.
    4. Chapter 4: the same, and keep a manual save before the heist.
    5. Chapter 5: make a fresh manual save before the first mission.
    6. Every chapter: keep one manual save from its start. It is your only replay option.

    What this means for you

    Five chapters sounds brief, but they are uneven: two medium chapters to learn the game, two very large ones in Vice City, and a short ending. The ends of Chapters 1 and 2 are where players lose things, so slow down there. Past that, the main risk is skipping a time-sensitive side mission in Chapters 3 and 4.

    GTABase notes that the missable optional missions are not needed for trophies or 100%, so a missed one is a lost scene rather than a lost completion.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many chapters are in GTA 6?

    Five. PowerPyx, VGC, GTABase and Dexerto all divide the story into Chapters 1 to 5, with a Prologue at the start.

    How many missions are in each GTA 6 chapter?

    By VGC's count, which follows the in-game story recap: 22 in Chapter 1 including the Prologue, 13 in Chapter 2, 28 in Chapter 3, 25 in Chapter 4 and 7 in Chapter 5, for 95 in total.

    Which is the longest chapter in GTA 6?

    Chapter 3 has the most missions on every list we compared, with Chapter 4 close behind. No guide has published reliable hours per chapter yet.

    Can you go back after finishing a chapter in GTA 6?

    The map stays open, but some things close. Jason's house projects and two optional errands end with Chapter 1, and missions cannot be replayed, so keep a manual save before each chapter finale.
