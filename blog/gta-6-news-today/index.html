    GTA 6 news today, November 20, 2026: the game has been out for one day, early sales estimates are far above GTA 5's record, and Rockstar has not yet published patch notes, an online date or a PC version. This round-up covers everything that changed around launch, in order of how much it affects you if you are playing right now. We update it through the week.

    How we checked: each item is confirmed by Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft, or reported by at least two independent outlets. Anything that rests on one report or on player posts is labelled that way.

    The week at a glance

    Date What happened

    November 13 Update 01.002.000 released, about 1 GB on PS5
    November 16 Update 01.002.001 released, about 602 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox
    November 17 PlayStation Support said a PS5 system software update is required to play

    GTA 6 is out

    Rockstar's launch post is short: Grand Theft Auto VI is available worldwide for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It unlocked at midnight local time on November 19, region by region, starting with New Zealand. The date set a year earlier held.

    Why it matters: nothing about the launch platforms changed at the last minute. There is no PC version and no previous-generation version. See GTA 6 platforms.

    Sales: about 24.6 million copies through launch day, by estimate

    Take-Two has not announced sales. The analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates about 24.6 million copies and roughly $2.36 billion through the evening of November 19, pre-orders included, with about three in four copies on PlayStation. GTA 5's official first-day figure in 2013 was 11.21 million copies and $815.7 million.

    Why it matters: the estimate is not an official number, and it counts months of pre-orders. Our full breakdown is in how much money GTA 6 has made.

    Reviews: 99 on Metacritic

    Reviews went live on November 18. GTA 6 holds a Metascore of 99 from about 150 critics, level with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest on the site. Metacritic's user reviews were scheduled to open at midday Pacific time on November 20.

    Tip: user scores for huge launches swing wildly in the first days. The critic average is the steadier guide. Details: GTA 6 reviews.

    Updates: two before launch, no patch notes

    Update Date PS5 size Xbox size Notes published
    01.002.000 November 13 About 1 GB Not reported No
    01.002.001 November 16 About 602 MB About 1.6 GB No

    Both updates rolled out in waves on PS5. Rockstar has not said what either changed, has not called either a "day-one patch", and as of November 19 its support site listed no title update notes for GTA 6.

    Tip: install both before you start. On PS5, highlight the game, press Options and choose Check for Update. On Xbox, look in My games & apps under Manage and Queue. Storage needs are in GTA 6 file size.

    PS5 owners need the latest system software

    On November 17, PlayStation Support said a PS5 system software update must be installed before GTA 6 will start, even if the game is already pre-loaded. Reports give different version numbers for it, so we are not quoting one.

    Tip: go to Settings, then System, then System Software and install whatever is offered. If the game refuses to launch, this is the first thing to check.

    PSN sign-in errors on November 18

    As the first regions unlocked, PS5 owners reported being unable to sign in to PlayStation Network for a couple of hours, with a message that services were busy. Outage trackers logged more than a thousand reports. Sony did not give a cause, and its status page showed services as normal for much of that time. No similar Xbox outage was reported.

    Why it matters: GTA 6 is single-player, but a boxed copy is a download code and a digital copy needs a licence check, so you must be able to sign in at least once. If sign-in fails, check the PlayStation status page and wait before retrying; reinstalling does not help when the problem is on Sony's side.

    Boxed copies: it is a code, so check it early

    The retail box holds a download code, not a disc, and you have to sign in to redeem it. In the days before launch there were scattered reports of boxes with a missing or unreadable code. Neither Rockstar nor Sony has confirmed a widespread problem. PlayStation's guidance is that a buyer with a missing or unreadable code should go back to the retailer, while account, download and redemption problems go to the platform's support.

    Tip: if you bought a box as a gift, open it and check the code now rather than on the day. More in GTA 6 physical copy.

    Bugs: player reports, nothing confirmed

    Rockstar has not acknowledged any bug or published a known-issues list. Launch trackers checked on November 18 and 19 found no confirmed gameplay bugs. One launch-day round-up collected player reports of crashes in the opening mission and of characters falling through the map; those are unverified posts, and the same round-up notes that many players report no problems.

    Tip: GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots. Keep two or three in rotation rather than relying on the autosave. Our beginner's guide shows where the save menu is.

    Cheats: 18 codes confirmed, a 19th reported

    The picture has not changed since launch morning: 61 cheats exist, all locked until the story is finished, and 18 button codes are published by two or more guides. A code for the Drunk cheat now appears on two sites, but one of them says it comes from a single guide, so we are holding it back until it is independently confirmed. Full table: GTA 6 cheats.

    Walkthroughs: the first eleven missions

    Mission guides are filling in quickly. PowerPyx lists five chapters for the story. We added eight walkthroughs today, from Lab Fees through Coming Home, which takes our coverage to the point where Lucia rejoins Jason. The running order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Online and PC: still nothing dated

    • Online. Both console stores describe GTA 6 as a single-player experience. Rockstar has not announced an online mode or a date. See GTA 6 online.
    • PC. No PC version has been announced, so there are no system requirements either. Take-Two's chief executive said on November 9 that news about other versions will come from Rockstar. See GTA 6 PC requirements.
    • Cloud and Remote Play. Xbox Cloud Gaming is listed for Xbox consoles, including Xbox One, not for PC or browsers. Rockstar says the game supports Remote Play from your own PS5 or Xbox, which is the only official way to see it on another screen.

    What is not confirmed

    • A dated first patch. Posts claiming Rockstar has apologised and scheduled a fix for a specific day are unverified; nothing on Rockstar's own channels supports them.
    • Official sales. Take-Two has said only that figures will be shared soon.
    • Patch contents. Guesses that the pre-launch updates were about stability or security are guesses.
    • GTA Online transfers, a PC date, a Switch 2 version. None announced.

    What to do today if you are playing

    1. Install the PS5 system software update, then both game updates.
    2. Make a manual save before each mission and keep a spare slot.
    3. Bank your cash as soon as the BuckMe account opens; carried money is lost when you are wasted or busted.
    4. Do the optional errands before Coming Home; they affect the relationship between the leads.
    5. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go. Progress made during the story counts toward 100% completion.
    6. Be careful with links promising unlock tools, free codes or PC versions. None of those exist.

    What this means for you

    One day in, the launch has been steady for a release of this size: the game works for most people, the only widespread problem was a short PlayStation Network sign-in issue the day before, and the reviews are as good as reviews get. The open questions are all about what comes next rather than what is broken. If you were waiting to see whether the launch was a mess, it was not.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the GTA 6 news today?

    As of November 20, 2026: the game is out, Sensor Tower estimates about 24.6 million copies through launch day, the Metacritic score is 99, and Rockstar has not yet published patch notes, an online mode date or a PC version.

    Is there a GTA 6 day-one patch?

    Two updates were released before launch: 01.002.000 on November 13 (about 1 GB on PS5) and 01.002.001 on November 16 (about 602 MB on PS5, about 1.6 GB on Xbox). Rockstar has not published notes for either.

    Are the GTA 6 servers down?

    GTA 6 is a single-player game at launch, so there are no game servers to go down. PS5 owners had PlayStation Network sign-in errors on November 18; Sony gave no cause.
