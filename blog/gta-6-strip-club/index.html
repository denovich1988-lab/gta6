    GTA 6 Strip Club Locations: All 3 Clubs and What You Can Do

    Every GTA 6 strip club: NINE1NINE and Jack of Hearts in Vice City and Delights in Port Gellhorn, when each opens, what a private dance costs and what is unconfirmed.

    GTA 6 has three strip club locations: NINE1NINE and the Jack of Hearts in Vice City, and Delights in Port Gellhorn. Two of them matter to the story, one is open from the first hours, and the prices differ a lot between them. This page covers where each club is, when you can get in, what you can do there, which missions use them and which details still rest on a single guide.

    A fact appears here as confirmed only when Rockstar or at least two independent guides state it. Everything else is in the section on what is not confirmed.

    NINE1NINE: the club you can visit first

    NINE1NINE is the large venue in central Vice City. Beebom and Skycoach place it in the north of Downtown; Xbox Play and Overdrive describe the same spot as south of Crosstown. Beebom adds that it stands next to a nightclub called The Vault and has its name on the front.

    Xbox Play and Overdrive both say it is free to enter and available from the start. Inside, guides describe a dance floor, a bar and a VIP area. Overdrive and Skycoach both call it a parody of a well-known Miami club.

    Why it matters: if you just want to see what the clubs are, this is the only one that needs no story progress. The city itself is covered in our Vice City guide.

    Jack of Hearts: Boobie Ike's club

    The Jack of Hearts is in the north of Crosstown; two guides put it on the same street, though they spell the street name differently. It belongs to Boobie Ike. Rockstar's own character description gives him interests in property, a strip club and a recording studio, and he co-runs the label Only Raw Records with Dre'Quan Priest. See GTA 6 characters.

    The club opens through the story. In Welcome to Vice City, the first mission of Chapter 3, Jason and Lucia meet Dre'Quan here, are turned away by the bouncer at the front and go in through the entrance on the other side. The recording studio is upstairs.

    Why it matters: this is a mission hub as much as a club. You will come back to it several times in Chapter 3, so learn the side entrance and the stairs.

    Delights: the Port Gellhorn club

    Delights, which several guides call Delights Cabaret, is in Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. It is where Vicki works. Beebom describes it as the cheapest club on the map.

    Guides disagree on when it opens. Beebom says it is available early; Overdrive says it opens with story progress. What both mission guides agree on is that the Chapter 2 mission Afternoon Delight takes place here.

    Tip: during that mission it is ladies' night and the bouncer admits only Lucia. As Jason, wait for the two bouncers to walk off and climb the fence behind the building.

    What you can do inside

    Across the guides, the activities are the same at each club:

    • Watch the stage shows.
    • Buy drinks at the bar.
    • Throw money at the stage. The story teaches this in Welcome to Vice City: hold L2 (LT on Xbox) to focus on a dancer and press R1 (RB) to throw.
    • Pay for a private dance in a separate VIP area.

    To ask for a private dance, hold L2 (LT) to focus on a dancer and choose the option that appears. Beebom gives the button as a short hold of Triangle (Y). Beebom adds that you can also book one at the reception desk near the entrance of NINE1NINE.

    Why it matters: the game is rated for adults, and the clubs are part of the reason. If you are deciding whether it is suitable for someone, read how old you have to be to play GTA 6.

    Prices: what a visit costs

    Cost NINE1NINE Jack of Hearts Delights
    Private dance $300 $1,000 $40
    Tip during a dance $50 $50 $10
    Sources Beebom, Xbox Play, Overdrive Beebom only Beebom only

    Only the NINE1NINE figures are confirmed by our two-source rule. Beebom's own page is not fully consistent: its general instructions quote $300, while its club sections give the three different prices above.

    Tip: you pay in cash. Carried cash is lost if you are wasted or busted, so take what you plan to spend and bank the rest. The reasons are in how to make money in GTA 6.

    Why it matters: none of these require you to use the club's activities. In Welcome to Vice City, Dre'Quan offers cash to throw and both walkthroughs say you can decline.

    Jason, Lucia and the relationship

    GTA 6 is built around one couple, and Rockstar removed the paid encounters of earlier games to keep the focus there, as Dexerto and Beebom both report. Private dances are as far as the clubs go.

    Whether a visit changes the relationship level between Jason and Lucia is less clear. Overdrive says it does not, and that the two can go together or alone, but gives no source. How the relationship meter works, and what does raise it, is in GTA 6 romance.

    What Rockstar has said

    Rockstar has not published a guide to the clubs. What it has confirmed is narrower:

    • Boobie Ike's official description ties him to a strip club, and outlets identify it as the Jack of Hearts.
    • Club interiors appeared in the Extended Look gameplay video in August 2026, which Rockstar said was captured on PS5.
    • Rob Nelson told IGN that characters across the game have more variety in height and body shape than before. IBTimes notes this was about the whole game, not the clubs specifically.

    What is not confirmed

    • The number of clubs. Beebom and Overdrive count three. Xbox Play lists two and says more may exist. Skycoach treats NINE1NINE as a nightclub.
    • Prices at the Jack of Hearts and Delights. One guide only.
    • When Delights opens. Two guides disagree.
    • Effect on the relationship. One guide, unsourced.
    • Rules. No guide we read describes being thrown out, weapon checks or a wanted level for misbehaving. Beebom's advice to leave weapons in the car at Delights is not repeated elsewhere.
    • Other nightlife. Skycoach lists bars and lounges such as The Vault, Effluvia and Hoodwinks. We have confirmed only the Rusty Anchor, the Key Lento bar from the Coming Home mission.
    • Trophies and cheats. No trophy, challenge or cheat is reported to depend on the clubs.

    How to visit a club: step by step

    1. Deposit most of your cash at an ATM and keep what you intend to spend.
    2. For the earliest visit, go to NINE1NINE in central Vice City. For the Jack of Hearts, reach Chapter 3 first.
    3. Walk in on foot. If a bouncer blocks the front door during a mission, look for a second entrance.
    4. Hold L2 (LT) to focus on a dancer for the throw and private dance options.
    5. Leave when your cash is gone; nothing inside is needed for 100%.

    What this means for you

    The clubs are scenery with a few optional activities, plus two locations the story sends you to anyway. If you are playing for completion, you can ignore them outside the four missions above. If you are exploring, NINE1NINE is the one to see first, and the Jack of Hearts is the one you will know best by the end of Chapter 3. The full story order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the strip club in GTA 6?

    There are three. NINE1NINE sits between Downtown and Crosstown in Vice City, the Jack of Hearts is in the north of Crosstown, and Delights is in Port Gellhorn.

    How many strip clubs are in GTA 6?

    Three according to Beebom and Overdrive. One guide lists only the two in Vice City, and another files NINE1NINE as a nightclub rather than a strip club.

    How much is a private dance in GTA 6?

    $300 at NINE1NINE, with tips of $50, according to three guides. Beebom alone gives $1,000 at the Jack of Hearts and $40 at Delights.

    Does visiting a strip club affect Jason and Lucia's relationship?

    One guide says it does not, without giving a source. No second guide has tested it, so treat that as unconfirmed.
