    What Time Does GTA 6 Come Out? Launch and Pre-Load Times

    GTA 6 came out at midnight local time on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Launch times by region, pre-load times and what to do first.

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 unlocked at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, and is out now.
    • It was a rolling launch: New Zealand played first, and each region followed at its own midnight.
    • Pre-loading opened on November 10; two updates and a PS5 system update must be installed before you play.

    What time does GTA 6 come out? It came out at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, and it is available now in every region. The launch covered PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; there is no PC version. This page keeps the full timetable for reference: how the rolling midnight launch worked, when pre-loading opened, what happened on the night, and what to do before your first session if you are starting late.

    How we checked: launch and pre-load times come from Rockstar and the platform holders, and the launch-night details are each reported by at least two outlets.

    GTA 6 launch times at a glance

    Event When
    Pre-load opened November 10, 2026
    Boxed copies in shops From November 12
    Reviews published November 18
    First region to unlock New Zealand, midnight NZDT on November 19 (11:00 UTC on November 18)
    Unlock everywhere else Midnight local time, November 19
    Platforms PS5, Xbox Series X and Series S

    How the midnight launch worked

    The game unlocked when the clock passed 12:00 a.m. on November 19 where you were. It was not one worldwide moment.

    Region When it unlocked
    New Zealand 12:00 a.m. NZDT, November 19 (first in the world)
    Australia (east coast) 12:00 a.m. AEDT, November 19
    Japan and South Korea 12:00 a.m. local time, November 19
    Central Europe 12:00 a.m. CET, November 19
    United Kingdom 12:00 a.m. GMT, November 19
    United States and Canada Midnight on November 19; see the note below

    Why it matters: a rolling launch meant that guides, streams and spoilers from New Zealand and Australia were online for most of a day before players in the Americas could start. If you are still avoiding spoilers, that head start is why they are everywhere.

    The United States: one unlock or four?

    Before launch, some reports said US store listings would unlock for the whole country at Eastern midnight, which is 11:00 p.m. Central and 9:00 p.m. Pacific on Wednesday, November 18. At least one launch-day guide describes the US unlock that way. Rockstar's own wording was simply midnight local time.

    Tip: the question no longer matters for buying, since the game is live everywhere. It is worth remembering for future Rockstar releases: your own local midnight is the time you can rely on, and anything earlier is a bonus.

    Pre-load times

    Platform Pre-load opened
    PlayStation 5 November 10 at midnight local time
    Xbox Series X|S November 10 at 12:00 a.m. GMT, worldwide
    Boxed copies From November 12, after redeeming the code in the box

    The download needs up to 212 GB of free space. See GTA 6 file size for the numbers, download times and how to make room.

    What happened on launch night

    • The date held. After two delays, November 19 did not move; see how many times GTA 6 was delayed.
    • PlayStation Network sign-in errors. On November 18, as the first regions unlocked, PS5 owners reported a "services are busy" message when signing in, for a couple of hours. Sony did not give a cause. No similar Xbox outage was reported.
    • Two updates were already waiting. Version 01.002.000 arrived on November 13 and 01.002.001 on November 16. Rockstar published no notes for either.
    • A PS5 system update was required. PlayStation Support said on November 17 that the console's system software had to be updated before GTA 6 would start.
    • Reviews were already in. The embargo lifted on November 18, and the game opened at 99 on Metacritic; see GTA 6 reviews.

    Why it matters: none of the launch-night problems were in the game itself. The one widespread issue was signing in on PlayStation, and it cleared within hours. The daily picture is in our GTA 6 news round-up.

    Does it unlock at the same time on Xbox and PlayStation?

    Yes for consoles: both followed local midnight. On Xbox, the unlock follows the store region of your Microsoft account. Xbox Cloud Gaming, which lets Xbox One owners stream the game, is listed for Xbox consoles only, not for PC or browsers. The supported hardware is set out in GTA 6 platforms.

    Buying now: how long until you can play?

    Now that the game is out, the wait is the download, not the clock.

    1. Buy digitally or redeem the code from a boxed copy. The box has no disc; see GTA 6 physical copy.
    2. Start the download: about 210 GB. On a 100 Mbps connection that is close to five hours at full speed.
    3. Install the game updates when the download finishes.
    4. On PS5, install the latest system software.
    5. Sign in to the account that owns the game before you launch it.

    Prices and what each edition includes are in GTA 6 price and editions.

    Before your first session: a short checklist

    1. Check that no update is waiting. On PS5, highlight the game, press Options and choose Check for Update.
    2. Keep a few gigabytes free beyond the 212 GB.
    3. Turn on automatic updates and leave the console in rest mode with internet access.
    4. Pick your first manual save slot early. There are 15, and cheats or crashes are easier to live with when you have a recent save.

    What is not confirmed

    • A PC release time. No PC version has been announced; see GTA 6 on PC.
    • An online mode launch. GTA 6 is single-player at launch and Rockstar has dated nothing; see GTA 6 online.
    • The first post-launch patch. No date has been given.

    Frequently asked questions

    What time does GTA 6 come out?

    It came out at 12:00 a.m. local time on November 19, 2026, on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is available now in every region.

    Does GTA 6 unlock at the same moment worldwide?

    No. It unlocked at midnight in each region, so New Zealand and Australia got it hours before Europe and the Americas.

    Can I pre-load GTA 6?

    Pre-loading ran from November 10 until launch. Now that the game is out, buying it or redeeming a boxed code starts the full download straight away.
