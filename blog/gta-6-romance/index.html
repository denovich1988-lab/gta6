    GTA 6 Romance: How Jason and Lucia's Relationship Works

    GTA 6 romance explained: the four relationship levels, what raises them, why you cannot romance Mandy or Vicki, and which cheats need time with your partner.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    9 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has one romance: Jason and Lucia. Rockstar says it is optional and grows only if you look after it.
    • PC Gamer's launch guide describes a four-step relationship meter, from Running on Empty to Hot Together, raised by small everyday actions.
    • You cannot romance Mandy, Vicki or anyone else, and six cheats unlock through time and activities with your partner.

    GTA 6 romance comes down to one couple: Jason and Lucia are the only relationship in the game, it is optional, and it moves up or down depending on how you treat your partner. There are no girlfriends to collect as in San Andreas and no dating site as in GTA 4. Instead a relationship level follows the two leads through the whole story. This guide explains how that level works, what raises it, who you cannot romance, and what the relationship unlocks.

    Statements from Rockstar come from interviews with Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North. Gameplay details come from launch guides; where only one guide reports something, we name it.

    GTA 6 romance at a glance

    Question Answer
    Who can you romance? Only your partner: Jason with Lucia, Lucia with Jason
    Is it required? No. Rockstar calls it optional
    How is it tracked? A relationship level with four steps (PC Gamer)
    How do you raise it? Time together, small gestures, texts, shared activities and jobs
    Can you romance Mandy? No
    Can you romance Vicki? No
    Can you cheat on your partner? No. Reviewers found no way to kiss anyone else
    Does it unlock anything? Six cheats need time or activities with your partner
    When can both be together in free roam? From Chapter 2

    The romance is optional, and Rockstar says so

    In an interview with IGN ahead of the Netflix extended look, reported by TechRadar in August 2026, Rob Nelson said players who like the idea will find the romance worth it and players who do not can leave it alone, adding that it is not something the studio wants to "foist upon players". The story keeps the two together either way: at the very least they get through it as partners in crime.

    Speaking to Edge magazine, as reported by GamesRadar+ on November 1, Nelson put it more sharply: the pair begin the game wanting to be together, and after that the relationship is yours to keep or lose. He traced the idea back to Arthur Morgan's inner conflict in Red Dead Redemption 2.

    Why it matters: you are not graded on being a good partner, and no mission asks for a minimum level in any guide we have read. Treat it as a side system with its own rewards.

    The four relationship levels

    PC Gamer's romance guide, published the day before launch, describes a four-point meter:

    Level Name
    1/4 Running on Empty
    2/4 Partners in Crime
    3/4 Crazy in Love
    4/4 Hot Together

    According to the same guide, the couple start Chapter 1 in fairly good shape, reaching 4/4 takes a while, and each gain is marked by a chime and a small heart at the right of the screen.

    PC Gamer is the only outlet we have seen list the level names, so treat the wording as reported rather than confirmed. The system itself is not in doubt: Rockstar has described it, and mission guides list "relationship increase" as a reward.

    Tip: your first chance to move the meter comes before Lucia is even out of prison. The four repairs in Jason's house projects affect where the relationship starts, and they close for good when she is released.

    What raises the relationship

    PC Gamer's list is long, and most of it is ordinary behaviour rather than set-piece dates:

    Type Actions
    Everyday gestures Holding hands, greeting each other, starting a conversation, a kiss or hug when the heart prompt appears, opening the car door
    At home Sitting on the couch together, going to bed at the same time, choosing Go Out when both are home
    Going out Minigolf, scuba diving, dancing at a club, getting hair done together, parachuting from a plane together
    Shopping Buying clothes or accessories with both of you in the store, complimenting a new outfit or haircut, buying food or drinks for your partner
    On the phone Answering texts, checking in, sending selfies, saying goodnight when apart
    Work Successful crimes together, paying money into the shared BuckMe savings account

    TechRadar's summary of the IGN interview mentions the same kind of thing: trips, holding hands, walks on the beach and crimes as a pair.

    Tip: the cheapest gains are the ones you would otherwise skip. Answer texts when the phone buzzes, hold hands when you walk somewhere together, and go to bed at the same time when you save. The savings account is introduced in Buck Me.

    Story missions that move the meter

    Several early missions list a relationship increase as their reward. In Chapter 2, Lucia stocks the motel room in Just Moved In; PowerPyx and VGC both report a relationship boost, and VGC says you need to collect all the groceries to get it. The background mission Take Care of Jason rewards regular visits to the motel room in the same way.

    Chapter 1 also has a run of optional errands before Lucia's release, including buying flowers and a necklace. They are covered in the Coming Home walkthrough.

    Why it matters: if you ignore the dating side completely, the story still nudges the level up now and then. You have to neglect your partner actively to sink it.

    What lowers it

    Two sources agree on neglect. GamesRadar+, citing the Edge interview, says ignoring too many late-night calls from your partner causes problems, and Insider Gaming says Rockstar has indicated that neglecting the relationship for too long can end it.

    Tip: when you play long stretches as one character, send a text now and then. Switching between the two leads is explained in how to switch characters in GTA 6.

    Can you romance Mandy?

    No. Mandy appears in the Chapter 1 mission Pipework, where Jason visits her house over unpaid rent and she makes her interest obvious. VGC tested it: however long Jason stays, nothing happens. He can ask for the plunger, turn her down, and keep talking until her lines repeat; eventually she tells him to leave. After the mission she sends one text, and there is no way to follow it up.

    Why it matters: there is no hidden branch here, so you lose nothing by finishing the mission quickly.

    Can you romance Vicki?

    No. Vicki is a side character in Chapter 2 who works at the Delights club in Kelly County and lives at the same motel as Lucia. You spend most of Girl's Trip with her. VGC's guide says Lucia cannot romance her whatever the relationship level, and that after the chapter she is mostly heard from by text or phone.

    Can you cheat on Jason or Lucia?

    Not in any way reviewers have found. Dexerto's launch report collects the evidence: Bloomberg's Jason Schreier said a visit to a strip club was the closest thing to cheating he found and that you cannot kiss anyone except your partner; Shacknews reported that picking up sex workers, possible since GTA 3, is gone; and The Guardian's review confirmed that private dances remain, alone or as a couple. Strangers around Leonida still flirt with Jason, but it goes no further.

    Rockstar's explanation, quoted in the same report, is that it kept the features that fit this story, which the studio describes as a couple in a bind.

    Why it matters: this is the first mainline GTA built around a committed pair. If you were looking for the girlfriend missions of San Andreas, they are not here. Other differences are in GTA 6's new features.

    What the relationship unlocks

    The clearest rewards are cheats. Six of the game's 61 cheats have unlock tasks that need your partner:

    Cheat Unlock task
    Golden Hour (screen filter) Spend 4 hours holding hands
    Dollhouse (screen filter) Spend 8 hours with your partner
    Videotape (screen filter) Complete 50 activities with your partner
    Faded Glory (screen filter) Rob 50 stores with your partner
    Pinhole (screen filter) Clear 25 gang operations with your partner
    Drunk Spend $2,500 on alcohol; it affects both of you

    All of them stay locked until the story is finished, like every cheat. The full table with codes is on our GTA 6 cheats page.

    PC Gamer also describes practical help on jobs: a partner who assists with aiming, controls bystanders, shoots at police tyres and carries loot. And the guide says intimate scenes appear only at set moments, marked by a double-heart icon over your home on the map or a timed prompt; you cannot start them at will.

    Tip: the holding-hands and time-together counters are slow. Start them early and they will be done by the credits without any grinding.

    What is not confirmed

    • Whether a break-up changes the story. Nelson says the romance can be lost and that the pair then carry on as partners in crime. No launch guide we have read documents what a low level changes in missions or in the ending.
    • Whether a break-up can be forced. A second GamesRadar+ headline says the game will not let you split the two however badly you treat them, which sits awkwardly beside the interview. Until two guides test it, we are not stating either version as fact.
    • Marriage and multiple endings. Insider Gaming says Rockstar has neither confirmed nor denied marriage. Treat anything more specific as speculation.
    • Exact numbers. No guide lists how many points each action is worth or how fast the level falls.
    • Phone calls. GamesRadar+ mentions calls; PC Gamer lists only texts.

    How to build the relationship: a quick routine

    1. Finish all four house projects before the mission Coming Home in Chapter 1.
    2. Pay into the BuckMe Family Savings account when you can spare the money. It cannot be withdrawn again, so keep enough in checking for story costs.
    3. Answer every text from your partner, and say goodnight by text when you are apart.
    4. From Chapter 2, when both are at home, choose Go Out for an activity.
    5. Hold hands whenever you walk somewhere together; it also counts towards a cheat.
    6. Shop for clothes together and compliment the result.
    7. Take your partner on store robberies and gang operations to combine relationship gains with cheat progress and cash. See how to make money in GTA 6.
    8. Do not go silent for days when playing solo missions.

    What this means for you

    If you enjoy the idea, the romance is a low-effort layer on top of things you already do: travelling, shopping, robbing stores. If you do not, Rockstar has said plainly that you can ignore it, and nobody has found a mission that punishes you for doing so. The one group who should pay attention regardless are completionists, because five screen-filter cheats depend on hours and activities spent as a couple.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does GTA 6 romance work?

    Jason and Lucia start the game wanting to be together, and a relationship level tracks how it is going. Holding hands, going out, answering texts, shopping together and pulling off jobs as a pair raise it; neglect lowers it.

    Can you romance Mandy in GTA 6?

    No. VGC's guide says Jason cannot romance Mandy in the mission Pipework however long he stays at her house; she eventually tells him to leave.

    Can you romance Vicki in GTA 6?

    No. VGC says Lucia cannot romance Vicki at any relationship level. She is a Chapter 2 side character who helps look for painkillers.

    Can Jason and Lucia break up in GTA 6?

    Rockstar's Rob Nelson has said the romance is yours to keep or lose and that the two can end up as partners in crime only. What a break-up changes in the released game is not yet documented by two guides.
