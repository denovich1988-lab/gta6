    How Many Times Was GTA 6 Delayed? Full Delay Timeline

    GTA 6 was delayed twice: from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026. The full timeline and why Rockstar moved the date.

    GTA 6 was delayed twice: first from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19, 2026, the date it actually launched. There was no third delay, despite a year of rumours. This page gives the full delay timeline, what Rockstar and Take-Two said each time, how long each delay really was, how it compares with Rockstar's earlier games, and what players are still waiting for now that the game is out.

    Every date here comes from Rockstar's own announcements or from at least two news reports that agree.

    GTA 6 delay timeline at a glance

    Announced Release target What changed
    December 2023 2025 Trailer 1 gives a 2025 window
    May 2, 2025 May 26, 2026 First delay
    November 6, 2025 November 19, 2026 Second delay
    November 19, 2026 Released The date held

    The original plan: 2025

    Trailer 1, in December 2023, ended with a single year: 2025. Rockstar never attached a day to it. Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, later narrowed it to fall 2025 in its financial reporting, and that is the window most reports use when they describe the first delay.

    Why it matters: the game never had a public 2025 date to miss, only a window. That is why some counts say "delayed once" and others say "twice". Counting from the first window Rockstar itself showed, it is twice.

    The first delay: to May 26, 2026

    On May 2, 2025, Rockstar announced that GTA 6 would release on May 26, 2026. It was the first time the game had an exact day. The studio said it needed more time to deliver the quality players expect.

    Four days later, on May 6, 2025, Rockstar released Trailer 2 and added character and location pages to its site, which softened the news. See all GTA 6 trailers.

    The second delay: to November 19, 2026

    On November 6, 2025, Rockstar moved the game again, from May 26 to Thursday, November 19, 2026. The announcement was posted on X on the same day as Take-Two's quarterly results.

    The message opened with an apology for adding more time to an already long wait, and gave the reason as extra months to finish the game with the level of polish players "have come to expect and deserve".

    Tip: one news report from that day gives November 22 in its body text. That was a typo in the article; Rockstar's date was always November 19.

    How the second delay was announced

    The news did not arrive as a surprise press conference. Three details from that day are worth knowing:

    • It came with earnings. GamesRadar, Windows Central and TechRadar all note that the announcement landed alongside Take-Two's quarterly results, which is when the publisher has to update investors on its release schedule anyway.
    • It was a short statement. Most outlets cite a post on X; GadgetGuy describes a brief post on Rockstar's own site. Either way it was a few sentences, not a video or a trailer.
    • Fans noticed first. Tom's Hardware reported that players had spotted the May 26 date disappearing from Rockstar's website shortly before the statement went out.

    Many fans had been hoping that week would bring a third trailer instead. That trailer never came; the next video was the Extended Look, nine months later.

    Did the delays affect pre-orders?

    No. Pre-orders had not opened when either delay was announced; TechRadar confirmed that at the time of the second one. They opened on June 25, 2026, with November 19 already fixed, so nobody had a pre-order moved or needed a refund because of a date change.

    Tip: if you bought a console or a bundle early in 2026 expecting the May date, nothing about that purchase changed. The game runs the same on a console bought in March as on one bought in November: see GTA 6 platforms.

    What Take-Two said about the second delay

    Take-Two's chief executive, Strauss Zelnick, spoke about the move around the earnings call:

    Outlet What Zelnick said, in substance
    IGN, via GamesRadar He was highly confident in the new date and did not expect another delay
    Windows Central The extra time came from wanting to release a "perfect" product
    TechRadar The company was excited and confident about delivering a blockbuster

    GamesRadar pointed out that he had sounded just as confident about May 26. This time the date held.

    How long each delay was

    Step From To Length
    First delay Fall 2025 May 26, 2026 Roughly six to eight months, depending on where in the fall you count from
    Second delay May 26, 2026 November 19, 2026 177 days, just under six months
    Total slip Fall 2025 November 19, 2026 About a year

    From Trailer 1 to release, the wait was just under three years.

    Was there a third delay?

    No. After November 2025 the date did not move. Through 2026, rumours of another delay, including a slip to 2027, appeared regularly, and each time the public milestones kept arriving on schedule:

    Date Milestone
    June 25, 2026 Pre-orders open, with prices and editions confirmed
    August 6, 2026 Rockstar announces An Extended Look
    August 27, 2026 The Extended Look premieres on Netflix and YouTube
    November 10, 2026 Pre-loading begins
    November 13 and 16, 2026 Two pre-launch updates
    November 19, 2026 Release

    Take-Two also repeated the November 19 date in its financial updates through the year.

    Why it matters: pre-orders are the clearest signal. A publisher does not take money against a date it expects to move, and from June 25 onwards a third delay was very unlikely. More on those steps: price and editions, GTA 6 on Netflix and GTA 6 updates.

    Why Rockstar delayed it

    Rockstar gave the same reason both times: more time to finish the game to the standard players expect. It did not name a specific problem, a platform or a feature. Anything more detailed than that, such as claims about a particular system being unfinished, did not come from the studio.

    The result is at least consistent with the reason. The game launched to very high review scores, with frame rate the main complaint: see GTA 6 reviews and how long GTA 6 was in development.

    How this compares with other Rockstar games

    Two delays is normal for Rockstar's biggest releases:

    Game First target Released Delays
    Grand Theft Auto V Spring 2013 September 17, 2013 One
    Red Dead Redemption 2 Fall 2017 October 26, 2018 Two
    Grand Theft Auto VI 2025 November 19, 2026 Two

    Why it matters: if you are waiting for something Rockstar has not dated yet, such as a PC version, history suggests treating the first date you hear as a target rather than a promise.

    How the delays affected other games

    When the second delay was announced, Windows Central wrote that many developers would be relieved, because May 2026 was suddenly free of the biggest release in years, and wondered whether smaller games planned for late 2026 would move out of the way instead. That was commentary at the time, not a list of confirmed moves.

    What is not confirmed

    • A detailed reason. Rockstar has only ever said "polish".
    • Internal dates. Reports about earlier internal targets are not official.
    • What was cut or changed. Nothing on record says the delays removed or added specific features.

    What players are still waiting for

    The delays are over, but three things have no date:

    1. A PC version. Not announced. See GTA 6 PC release date.
    2. An online mode. Rockstar describes the launch game as single-player. See GTA 6 online.
    3. A 60 FPS option. Every console targets 30 FPS. See does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.

    If you are deciding whether to buy now or wait for one of these, decide on what exists today.

    How to judge the next Rockstar date

    1. Look for an exact day. A year or a season is a window, and GTA 6's window moved. Its exact days moved once.
    2. Watch the earnings calendar. Both the second delay and most date confirmations came with Take-Two's quarterly results.
    3. Wait for pre-orders. Once money is being taken, the date is as firm as it gets.
    4. Ignore unsourced "internal" dates. None of the 2027 rumours came from Rockstar or Take-Two, and none came true.

    The short version

    GTA 6 slipped twice, by about a year in total, and then arrived on the day Rockstar last promised. For the complete history of announcements, see GTA 6 release date and delay history. For launch-day timing by region, see what time GTA 6 came out.

    What we will add to this page

    If Rockstar dates a PC version or an online mode, we will add those to the timeline, along with any later change to them.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many times has GTA 6 been delayed?

    Twice. From fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19, 2026.

    Did GTA 6 get delayed again?

    No. After the November 2025 announcement the date did not move, and the game launched on November 19, 2026.

    Was GTA 6 delayed to 2027?

    No. Claims of a 2027 delay were rumours. The game came out in 2026.
