    GTA 6 vs GTA 5: 14 New Features That Change How You Play

    The biggest new features in GTA 6 compared with GTA 5: two leads, six wanted stars and heat, a map twice as big, banking, 80 challenges, cheats and more.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated

    Read

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 changes the core loop more than any GTA since San Andreas: two leads instead of three, a six-star wanted level with heat, and cash you can lose.
    • The map is about twice the size of GTA 5's, with a much denser Vice City, more interiors and more than 170 animal species.
    • Progress systems are new too: 80 challenges, 61 cheats that unlock after the story, and a 35-line 100% checklist.

    GTA 6's new features change the basics you know from GTA 5: who you play, how the police hunt you, what happens to your money, and how you unlock extras. Thirteen years separate the two games, and you notice it within the first hour. Below are the 14 changes that matter most in practice, what each one means for how you play, and what GTA 6 still does not have.

    Everything here is confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides. Where the guides disagree, we say so.

    GTA 6 vs GTA 5 at a glance

    Feature GTA 5 (2013) GTA 6 (2026)
    Playable characters Three: Michael, Franklin, Trevor Two: Jason and Lucia
    Switching characters Available almost from the start Unlocks in Chapter 2
    Wanted level Five stars Six stars, plus heat icons
    Map San Andreas: Los Santos and Blaine County Leonida: Vice City and five more regions, about twice the size
    Your cash Safe on the character Carried cash is lost when wasted or busted; bank it
    Fast travel Taxis RydeMe app, with a paid skip
    Cheats Button codes from the start 61 cheats that unlock after the story
    Side goals Scattered tasks and collectibles 80 challenges in 8 categories
    Radio In vehicles In vehicles and on foot with earbuds
    Online at launch GTA Online followed two weeks later Single-player only; no online mode dated
    Frame rate on new consoles 60 FPS mode in the re-release 30 FPS target on every console

    1. Two leads instead of three

    GTA 5 split its story between three men with separate lives. GTA 6 follows a couple, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and many missions put them in the same scene: one drives while the other shoots, or one keeps watch while the other works.

    Why it matters: the story feels more personal, and missions are built around two people cooperating rather than three running in parallel. Expect fewer "pick your character" moments and more shared jobs.

    2. Switching unlocks later, and works differently

    In GTA 5 you could jump between characters almost from the start. In GTA 6, switching opens up in Chapter 2. You hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 (RB on Xbox).

    Tip: until Chapter 2 you are locked to the character the story gives you, so do not worry if the option is missing early on. Our beginner's guide covers the controls for the first hours.

    3. Six wanted stars, and the heat system

    GTA 5's wanted meter stopped at five stars. GTA 6 goes to six, and adds a second layer underneath: red heat icons that show what the police know about you, such as your face, your clothes and your vehicle.

    Why it matters: escaping is no longer only about breaking line of sight. If police still know your car and outfit, they can pick you up again. Swap vehicles, change clothes and stay out of the area they were searching. Full breakdown: GTA 6 wanted level explained.

    4. A map about twice the size of GTA 5's

    Rockstar's developers say Leonida is about twice the size of the GTA 5 map and about three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's. Vice City on its own is said to be about twice the size of Los Santos.

    The bigger change may be density. Rockstar North has said the roads are narrower than in GTA 5, which leaves room for more buildings, and that there are far more interiors than in any earlier Rockstar game. See how big the GTA 6 map is and the region-by-region map guide.

    5. Your cash can be lost

    In GTA 6, money in your pocket is at risk. Carried cash is lost when you are wasted or busted, so the safe move is to put it in the bank once your BuckMe account opens.

    Tip: after any big job, such as a jewelry store robbery, find an ATM before you start something risky. The bank app on your phone shows the nearest one. More in how to make money in GTA 6.

    6. The phone runs your life

    The phone is now the hub for everyday systems:

    • BuckMe is your bank, with an ATM finder.
    • RydeMe is a ride service that works as fast travel. You can watch the drive or pay $100 to skip it.
    • Reminders collects messages and contacts waiting for a reply.

    GTA 5's phone was mainly for calls, missions and the internet. In GTA 6 it is closer to the phone you actually use.

    7. Stealing a car takes more than one button

    Official gameplay shows car theft as a short hands-on sequence, working on a window before you drive off, rather than an instant break-in. After a job, the leads burn a getaway car to cover their tracks, which ties into the heat system above.

    8. Racing on real circuits

    GTA 5 raced mostly on public roads. GTA 6 adds purpose-built race tracks, with wheel-to-wheel racing shown in Rockstar's Extended Look. Three venues appear by name in cheat unlock conditions: Gellhorn Raceway, Thrillbilly Mud Club and Boone Speedway. See Port Gellhorn.

    9. 80 challenges with real rewards

    GTA 6 groups its side goals into 80 challenges, ten in each of eight categories: combat, criminal, exploration, fitness, shopping, sports, survival and vehicle. Finishing five in a category, and then all ten, unlocks a vehicle spawn cheat. All 80 unlock the hardest one, the Hauners P-996 LAZER jet.

    Why it matters: side activities now feed directly into rewards you can use, instead of only ticking a box. Full list: GTA 6 challenges.

    10. Cheats are a reward, not a shortcut

    GTA 5 let you type cheat codes from the first minute. GTA 6 has 61 cheats, but the Cheats menu only opens after you finish the story, and each cheat has its own unlock task, such as shooting-range medals or collectibles. The money cheat, which GTA 5 never had, gives $250,000.

    Codes have been published for 18 of the 61 so far. See the full table in GTA 6 cheats.

    11. A much bigger collectible hunt

    The numbers are large: 18 collectible card sets with 480 cards, 125 cocaine flamingos, 34 luxury watches, plus WAiNK scans, underwater discoveries and meditation spots. Many of them unlock cheats or count toward 100%. Start with GTA 6 collectibles.

    12. A 100% checklist you can actually read

    Instead of a hidden percentage, GTA 6 shows a 35-line checklist after the story, split into Story & Tasks, Exploration & Activities, and Discovery. Most lines ask for less than the full total, for example 300 of 397 stunts, so you can skip the activities you like least. Details: GTA 6 100% completion.

    13. A living Leonida: animals and radio

    • Wildlife. Rockstar says GTA 6 has more than 170 animal species, including alligators, flamingos, pelicans and manatees, all new to Rockstar's games. See GTA 6 animals.
    • Radio on foot. You can listen through the phone with earbuds, not only in a car. Rockstar has detailed six stations with hosts such as Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Morgan Wallen. See GTA 6 radio stations.

    14. More saves and a longer story

    There are 15 manual save slots, reached from the pause menu under Game. The story is far longer than GTA 5's: launch guides report 91 main missions, and one Rockstar studio head put his own playthrough at about 80 hours. See how long GTA 6 is.

    What GTA 6 does not have (yet)

    Not everything is an upgrade on day one:

    • No online mode. Rockstar describes GTA 6 at launch as a single-player experience. GTA Online arrived two weeks after GTA 5; nothing similar has been dated for GTA 6. See GTA 6 online.
    • No PC version. It is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. See GTA 6 on PC.
    • No 60 FPS mode. Every console targets 30 FPS, including the PS5 Pro. See does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.
    • No three-way switching. Two leads, not three.

    Which changes to learn first

    If you are starting today, these four make the biggest difference in the first hours:

    1. Bank your cash as soon as BuckMe opens, so a bad chase does not wipe out a job.
    2. Learn the heat icons before your first robbery, so you know when to swap cars or clothes.
    3. Open the challenge list early. Workouts, races and shooting ranges you do during the story already count.
    4. Save often. With 15 slots, keep one before every big mission.

    Is GTA 6 better than GTA 5?

    Critics overwhelmingly say yes. GTA 6 holds a Metacritic score of 99, with top scores from outlets including IGN, GameSpot and Game Informer. The most common complaints are the 30 FPS target and occasional performance dips. Our summary of the reviews is here: GTA 6 reviews.

    We will expand this comparison as we test more systems in the released game, including property, businesses and how far the economy goes after the story. If a patch changes any of the features above, we will update the table and the date at the top.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the biggest new features in GTA 6?

    Two playable leads, a six-star wanted level with a heat system, a map about twice the size of GTA 5's, carried cash you can lose, a bank and ride app on your phone, 80 challenges, and 61 cheats that unlock after the story.

    Is GTA 6 better than GTA 5?

    Critics think so: GTA 6 holds a Metacritic score of 99. The main complaint is the 30 FPS target on consoles.

    What does GTA 5 have that GTA 6 does not?

    At launch, GTA 6 has no online mode, no PC version and no 60 FPS option, and its cheats are locked until the story is finished. GTA 5 also lets you switch between three characters rather than two.
