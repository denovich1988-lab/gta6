    GTA 6 Heists: Both Story Heists, Setup Missions and Robberies

    GTA 6 heists explained: the Cartel Heist in Chapter 3 and the VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4, every setup mission in order, smaller robberies, safe codes and tools.

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has two story heists: the Cartel Heist at Sinfrontera National Bank in Chapter 3 and the VCIA Takedown at the airport in Chapter 4.
    • Each is a chain of setup missions; walkthrough lists tag eight missions for the Cartel Heist and fifteen for the VCIA Takedown.
    • Heists cannot be replayed, and their payouts, crew options and approaches are not yet confirmed by two guides.

    GTA 6 heists come in two sizes: two big story heists, the Cartel Heist and the VCIA Takedown, and a long list of smaller robberies you can run between missions. The story heists are multi-mission operations that close out Chapter 3 and dominate Chapter 4. This page lists every setup mission in order, the smaller jobs that lead up to them, the tools and safe codes confirmed so far, and what guides have not yet agreed on.

    Mission names and order here match in two independent walkthrough lists, PowerPyx and GTABase. Where only one source reports something, we say so.

    How heists work in GTA 6

    A heist is not one mission. It is a chain: scouting the target, recruiting people, stealing equipment, a planning step and then the job itself. Lists mark the planning steps (Heist Planning, Heist Plans in Place) as their own entries, though PowerPyx has no walkthrough page for them, which suggests they are short.

    Why it matters: setup missions are split between the two leads. Several are marked for Jason only or Lucia only, so if a heist seems stalled, switch character and check the map. The controls are in how to switch characters in GTA 6.

    Who runs the heists: Raul Bautista

    Rockstar introduced Raul Bautista before launch as an experienced bank robber who is always looking for talent. In the game he tests the pair before he trusts them. Lucia's first job for him is Car Service, a car theft from a gang chop shop, and the missions that follow lead to his crew.

    Tip: Raul's missions arrive by message after a wait, not from a map marker you can visit at will. If nothing new appears, let a day of game time pass.

    The Cartel Heist (Chapter 3)

    The first story heist is a bank job on Sinfrontera National Bank. PowerPyx tags these missions as part of it, in this order:

    # Mission Notes from the lists
    1 Sinfrontera National Bank Scope out the bank
    2 Security Expert
    3 Heist Planning Planning step
    4 Company Property Meet Brian
    5 Safe Word Steal a practice safe
    6 The Missing Link Meet Andrea; Lucia only
    7 Hidden Charges Meet at the planning room; prepare the explosives
    8 The Cartel Heist Start the heist

    GTABase lists the same eight names in Chapter 3, with a slightly different order in the middle, and its heist guide describes Safe Word as the mission where Lucia learns a new skill on the practice safe. Both lists end Chapter 3 with The Cartel Heist.

    Why it matters: because the heist closes the chapter, finish Chapter 3's optional missions first. Two of them are time-sensitive, as covered below.

    The VCIA Takedown (Chapter 4)

    The second heist targets the airport and takes up much of Chapter 4. PowerPyx tags fifteen entries:

    Phase Missions Played as
    Lucia's side Making Gainz, Blood Sweat and Gear, Amateur Hour, Call Nate, Cash Injections, Dirty Work, Nate Will Be in Touch, Power Play, Team Building Mostly Lucia
    Jason's side Too Much Baggage, Close Surveillance, Delivery Method Jason
    Together Fully Catered, Heist Plans in Place, VCIA Takedown Both

    GTABase's heist guide adds two details: in Too Much Baggage Jason scouts the airport, and Fully Catered is about stealing a catering truck. After the heist both lists place a mission called Tie Up Loose Ends.

    Tip: this is the longest preparation in the game, and it runs alongside unrelated Chapter 4 missions. Keep a manual save from before Making Gainz if you think you may want to see the build-up again.

    Smaller scripted robberies along the way

    The story teaches robbery in steps before Raul appears. All of these have walkthroughs on this site, linked from the mission list:

    Mission Chapter What it is Confirmed payout

    Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab 2 Armored truck $2,000
    Hit Avery Pawn 2 Pawn shop
    Hit Kingpin Bowling 2 Gang-held bowling alley
    Afternoon Delight 2 Club safe, code 2008 $10,000
    Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs 3 Three jewelry stores $20,000

    Why it matters: each one introduces something the heists reuse, such as safes with codes, security footage, alarms and escaping with a bag.

    The free-roam robbery system

    Outside missions, the map index has a Robberies section that lists targets. Guides describe these types:

    • Stores, restaurants and gas stations, held up at the register.
    • Smash 'n' Grabs: jewelry stores and pawn shops.
    • Banks and Payday Loan stores.
    • Bank vaults, which open late in Chapter 4. Guides report $100,000 or more per vault and an instant four-star wanted level.
    • Gang operations: guarded compounds with cash and valuables.
    • Safes and ATMs, including ATMs you can pull out with a winch.

    The 100% checklist counts them: 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 Payday Loan jobs and 8 vaults, all of which are required. See GTA 6 100% completion. A silver trophy asks for five bank vaults.

    Getting the money home

    Stealing is half the job. Loot other than cash goes into a bag and has to reach a fence. Three open in early Chapter 3: Yasmani for jewelry and marked bills, Tyrone for goods and Randy for vehicles. Cash in your pocket is lost if you are wasted or busted, so deposit it at an ATM. The full breakdown is in how to make money in GTA 6, and police behaviour is in GTA 6 wanted level.

    Missable missions before each heist

    Two optional missions are marked time-sensitive and missable by both lists:

    • Race Against Yago in Chapter 3.
    • This Is a Free Call From… in Chapter 4, for Lucia.

    PowerPyx says such missions show an hourglass on the map and that missing them does not affect trophies or 100%.

    How GTA 6 heists differ from GTA 5

    GTA 5 built its story around a handful of heists where you chose an approach and hired a crew from a board. For GTA 6, no guide we have read documents a choice of approach or crew, and GTABase's heist guide does not mention one. What is clearly different is scale: fifteen tagged missions for one heist is far more preparation than any GTA 5 job needed. Other changes are in GTA 6 new features.

    What is not confirmed

    • Payouts for the Cartel Heist and the VCIA Takedown.
    • Approaches and crew. Whether you choose either.
    • Fail states and choices inside the two finales. We have not yet matched two walkthroughs.
    • Raul's Fleeca decoy. PowerPyx describes a mission, First Impressions, in which Raul uses a bank robbery as a distraction. We are waiting for a second guide.
    • That's Where the Money Is… An optional Chapter 4 mission on both lists; its content is not yet described by two guides.

    What to do before each heist

    1. Make a manual save in a spare slot. See how to save and why missions cannot be replayed.
    2. Finish optional missions, especially any with an hourglass.
    3. Sell loot and bank your cash.
    4. Stock ammunition and healing items for both characters.
    5. Check both characters' maps for a setup mission you have not started.

    What this means for you

    If you came for heists, expect to wait: the first one ends Chapter 3, many hours in. The game fills that time with smaller jobs that pay well and teach the same skills, and the free-roam robberies go on after the story. Treat the two story heists as the peaks and Yasmani's stores, vaults and gang operations as the regular income. For where the heists sit in the full story, see how many chapters are in GTA 6 and the mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many heists are in GTA 6?

    Two story heists: the Cartel Heist in Chapter 3 and the VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4. Around them are smaller scripted robberies and a free-roam robbery system with banks, vaults and gang operations.

    What is the first heist in GTA 6?

    The Cartel Heist, a job on Sinfrontera National Bank organised by Raul Bautista. It is the last mission of Chapter 3.

    Can you replay heists in GTA 6?

    No. Missions cannot be replayed, so make a manual save before the final mission of each heist if you want to play it again.

    How much do GTA 6 heists pay?

    No two guides give matching figures for the story heists yet. Confirmed smaller payouts include $20,000 for Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs and $10,000 for Afternoon Delight.
