    GTA 6 is Grand Theft Auto VI, an open-world crime game from Rockstar Games in which you play a couple, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, across a fictional version of Florida called Leonida. It came out on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, thirteen years after GTA 5. If you have heard the name everywhere and never played a GTA game, this page explains what it is, what you actually do in it, what it costs, who it is suitable for and how to start, with a link to a deeper guide for each point.

    The facts below are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    What kind of game it is

    Grand Theft Auto is a series of open-world games: you are dropped into a large, detailed place and can go almost anywhere, on foot or in any vehicle you can get into. A long story is told through missions, and between missions the world is yours. GTA 6 is the newest main entry, and it is played from behind your character by default, with an optional first-person mode.

    Why it matters: you do not need to have played the earlier games. Each GTA has its own cast and story, and GTA 6 explains its systems as it goes.

    Where it is set

    The game takes place in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. Rockstar names six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Vice City is the series' version of Miami, first seen in 2002's GTA: Vice City.

    Rockstar's developers say the map is about twice the size of GTA 5's, with far more buildings you can enter.

    Tip: the story starts in the Keys and does not reach Vice City until Chapter 3, so do not expect the neon skyline in the first hours. See the GTA 6 map, how big it is and our Vice City guide.

    Who you play

    There are two playable leads.

    • Jason Duval grew up around grifters, joined the Army to get away, and now works for drug runners in the Keys.
    • Lucia Caminos grew up in Liberty City, learned to fight from her father and has just been released from Leonida Penitentiary. She is the first female lead in a mainline 3D GTA.

    They are a couple, and many missions put both in the same scene. From Chapter 2 you can switch between them in free play.

    Why it matters: the relationship is a system, not only a storyline. Time spent together is tracked and feeds into rewards. The wider cast is on our GTA 6 characters page.

    What the story is about

    Without spoilers: a job goes wrong, and Jason and Lucia are pushed out of the Keys, through the countryside and into Vice City, where the scores get bigger and the people they owe get more dangerous.

    The story is a playable prologue plus five chapters. Launch guides count 91 main missions, and more than 100 if you include short tasks and optional ones. The only playtime figure from Rockstar is about 80 hours, from one studio head's own playthrough with optional goals included.

    Tip: missions cannot be replayed after you finish them, and each chapter ends with a point of no return. Our mission list marks those points, and how long GTA 6 is breaks down the numbers.

    What you actually do

    A typical hour mixes several of these:

    • Story missions: driving, shooting, stealth, chases by car, boat, bike and helicopter, and multi-part heists.
    • Making money: selling stolen cars, robbing stores and jewelry shops, later bank vaults.
    • Side characters and activities: optional missions, races on real circuits, shooting ranges, workouts, fishing and more.
    • Collecting: cards, hidden flamingos, watches and photo scans.
    • Just living there: radio in the car or on foot, clothes, haircuts, dates.

    Why it matters: almost everything optional counts toward something. There are 80 challenges, a 35-line checklist for 100% and 55 trophies. Start with how to play GTA 6.

    How the police work

    Crime attracts a wanted level of up to six stars. Under the stars, red heat icons show what the police know about you: your face, your clothes, your vehicle. Getting away means changing what they can recognise, not only driving out of sight.

    Tip: if a chase keeps restarting, swap cars and change clothes. The full system is in GTA 6 wanted level explained.

    How it differs from GTA 5

    If GTA 5 is the one you know, these are the headline changes:

    GTA 5 GTA 6
    Leads Three Two, a couple
    Wanted level Five stars Six stars, plus heat icons
    Your cash Safe on the character Carried cash can be lost; bank it
    Cheats From the first minute Unlock after the story
    Online at launch Followed two weeks later Not announced

    Why it matters: habits from GTA 5 can cost you here, especially walking around with a full wallet. All 14 major changes are compared in GTA 6 new features.

    What it costs and which edition to buy

    Edition US price What you get
    Standard $79.99 The game; sold digitally and in a box
    Ultimate $99.99 The game plus an upgrade with 16 in-game items; digital only

    Boxed copies contain a download code, not a disc. For most new players the Standard Edition is enough, because the Ultimate extras are items, not story. Details: GTA 6 price and editions.

    What you need to play it

    • A PS5 (any model, including Pro) or an Xbox Series X or Series S.
    • Up to 212 GB of free storage and an internet connection for the download, even with a boxed copy.
    • Every console targets 30 frames per second; there is no 60 FPS mode.

    It does not run on PS4, Xbox One or Switch 2. See GTA 6 platforms and the file size guide.

    Who it is suitable for

    GTA 6 is an adult game. It is rated M (Mature 17+) in North America, PEGI 18 in Europe and the UK, and R18 in Australia and New Zealand, with ESRB descriptors that include blood and gore, nudity, strong language, and use of drugs and alcohol. It is a crime story about adults, and it is not suitable for children.

    Tip for parents: console parental controls can block the game by age rating. Our age rating guide lists every content descriptor.

    What GTA 6 does not have (yet)

    • No online multiplayer. Rockstar describes the launch game as a single-player experience and has not dated an online mode. See GTA 6 online.
    • No PC version. None has been announced. See GTA 6 on PC.
    • No cheats from the start. The 61 cheats unlock only after the story. See GTA 6 cheats.
    • No way to earn real money. See can you make real money in GTA 6.

    How to start: your first evening

    1. Buy the Standard Edition unless you specifically want the extra items.
    2. Start the download early. At around 210 GB it can take hours.
    3. Play the prologue and the first missions in order. They teach driving, shooting and the phone.
    4. Save by hand from the pause menu under Game. There are 15 slots; keep one per chapter.
    5. Bank your cash once the BuckMe app opens, because carried cash is lost when you are wasted or busted.
    6. Do not rush to Vice City. The Keys have their own side content, some of which closes at the end of Chapter 1.

    Is it worth playing?

    Critics think so: GTA 6 launched with a Metacritic score of 99 and top marks from the largest outlets, with the 30 FPS target as the main complaint. If you like open-world games, crime stories or simply exploring a place at your own pace, it is the most complete example of the form so far. If you want competitive multiplayer today, or you play only on PC, it is worth waiting. The reviews are summarised in GTA 6 reviews.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is GTA 6?

    GTA 6, officially Grand Theft Auto VI, is an open-world action game by Rockstar Games. You play a couple, Jason and Lucia, through a crime story in Leonida, a fictional state based on Florida, and can explore its cities, swamps and islands freely between missions.

    What is GTA 6 about?

    It follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, who start out working for drug runners in the Leonida Keys and are pushed toward Vice City and bigger robberies. The story runs through a prologue and five chapters.

    What can you play GTA 6 on?

    PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is not on PS4, Xbox One or Switch 2, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version.
