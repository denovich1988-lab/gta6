    GTA 6 Update: Every Patch So Far, Sizes and Patch Notes

    The latest GTA 6 update is version 1.002.001, released on November 16, 2026, and there have been two updates in total, both before launch. Neither came with patch notes, and as of November 20, the day after release, Rockstar had not put out a post-launch patch. This page lists every update with its date and size on each console, explains what is and is not known about the contents, shows how to check that you are on the newest version, and separates the rumours from what Rockstar has said.

    Dates and sizes below match across at least two independent trackers. Where they disagree, both figures are shown. Nothing here comes from datamined files.

    GTA 6 updates at a glance

    Version Released PS5 size Xbox Series X|S size Patch notes
    1.002.000 November 13, 2026 About 1.07 GB About 1.07 GB (one outlet: 2.18 GB) None published
    1.002.001 November 16, 2026 602.5 MB About 1.59 GB None published
    Post-launch patch Not released as of November 20 - - -

    Xbox shows the second update as 1.0.2.1, according to RockstarINTEL. It is the same patch.

    Update 1.002.000 (November 13)

    The first update arrived six days before launch, while the game was still in its pre-load period. Beebom and Of Zen and Computing put it at about 1.07 GB on both consoles; KeenGamer reported about 2.18 GB on Xbox. On PS5 it appeared in the download list without a proper name.

    It rolled out in stages on PlayStation rather than to everyone at once. KeenGamer, citing the PlayStation Game Size account, reported the first half of eligible consoles getting it and the rest about 11 hours later.

    Why it matters: if your console was offline between November 13 and launch, this is the first thing it will download, so allow a few minutes before you can play.

    Update 1.002.001 (November 16)

    The second update followed three days later. Four trackers agree on the sizes: 602.5 MB on PS5 and about 1.59 GB on Xbox Series X|S. RockstarINTEL noted that the installed size on Xbox afterwards was 211.9 GB.

    Xbox consoles received it straight away. On PS5 it again came in waves, reported as roughly a third of consoles at a time, with the last group getting it on the morning of November 17.

    Tip: the PS5 figure can vary slightly by region. If yours shows a few megabytes more or less, it is the same patch. Total install sizes are covered in GTA 6 file size.

    How to read the version number

    The same patch is written three ways depending on where you look. Trackers that copy the PS5 listing write 01.002.001, most news sites shorten it to 1.002.001, and RockstarINTEL reports that Xbox shows 1.0.2.1. They are all the second pre-launch update.

    Between the two patches so far, only the last block changed, from 000 to 001. So when you compare your console with a list like the one above, match the final digits: a console showing 1.002.000 is one patch behind.

    Tip: on PS5 the first update was listed without a proper title, which led some players to think it was a mistake or a different game's file. It was the 1.002.000 patch.

    Has there been a GTA 6 update since launch?

    No. GTAside, GTA6DB and Output Lag each checked on or after November 19 and none found a new title update or a Rockstar announcement of one. The version you should see on a fully updated console is still 1.002.001.

    Why it matters: if a site or video claims to list "day-one patch notes", it is describing one of the two pre-launch updates above, or guessing.

    GTA 6 patch notes: what Rockstar has published

    Nothing so far. Every tracker we checked says the same: Rockstar released both updates without patch notes, and Rockstar Support had no title update article for GTA VI on launch day. GTA6DB points out that Rockstar normally posts title update notes as separate support articles, so that is where the first real notes are likely to appear.

    Some outlets called the second update a "bug fixing" patch, and some described both as mandatory. Neither description comes with a Rockstar source. What is certain is practical: both are small, and an online console with automatic updates installs them without you doing anything.

    The PS5 system update from November 17

    Sony released PS5 system software 26.07-14.23.00 on November 17. Beebom, Of Zen and Computing and SixWanted all report that it is needed to play GTA 6 at launch. Of Zen and Computing adds that neither Sony nor Rockstar announced that requirement in those words.

    Tip: install it anyway. Go to Settings > System > System Software. An out-of-date console is one of the few launch problems you can rule out in two minutes.

    How to check for a GTA 6 update on PS5

    1. Highlight GTA 6 on the home screen.
    2. Press the Options button.
    3. Choose Check for Update.
    4. Let it install, then start the game.

    To have patches install while the console sleeps, turn on automatic updates under Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates, and allow the console to stay connected to the internet in rest mode under the power saving settings.

    How to update GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S

    Xbox handles game patches automatically when Keep my games & apps up to date is ticked under Profile & system > Settings > System > Updates. Pending downloads appear under My games & apps > Manage > Queue.

    Trackers differ on a manual check. Of Zen and Computing describes opening Manage game and add-ons from the game's menu and looking at the Updates tab; GTA6DB says Xbox Support does not document a per-game check. If the game starts and does not ask for an update, you are current.

    Storage: leave room for patches

    Rockstar's own requirements say to keep up to 212 GB free. The two updates so far are small, but future ones will not always be. If you are choosing hardware or short on space, read which console is best for GTA 6 and GTA 6 platforms.

    Launch problems that were not the game

    Two trackers, GTAside and SixWanted, report that PlayStation Network sign-ins failed for a period on November 18, just as the game was unlocking in the first time zones. That was a PSN issue. Reinstalling the game or rebuilding the console database does not fix a network outage.

    Tip: before you troubleshoot, check Rockstar's service status page and your console's network status page. If either shows a problem, wait.

    What is not confirmed

    • What the two updates changed. Posts about altered story files, shaders or scripts come from community file comparisons. Of Zen and Computing and KeenGamer both label them unconfirmed.
    • A patch on November 25. Output Lag mentions the date as a rumour. Rockstar has announced no patch and no date.
    • Known bugs. Rockstar has no known-issues article for GTA 6 yet. One troubleshooting guide collected crash, audio and overheating reports from launch night; Rockstar has acknowledged none of them, and GTAside lists no bug as confirmed.
    • A 60 FPS mode. No update adds one. The game targets 30 FPS on every console: see does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.
    • GTA Online. Nothing has been dated. See GTA 6 online.

    What future GTA 6 updates could bring

    Only two things are on record. Rockstar describes the launch game as single-player, so an online mode would have to arrive through a later update; GTA Online followed GTA 5 by two weeks, but no schedule exists this time. And there is no PC version, which is a separate release rather than a patch: see GTA 6 on PC.

    One practical note for players using cheats: codes are entered on the D-pad and could in principle change with a patch. We recheck the published codes after every update.

    What to do right now

    1. Check for a game update using the steps above. You want 1.002.001.
    2. Install the latest system software on your console.
    3. Turn on automatic updates and rest-mode downloads.
    4. Keep spare storage rather than filling the drive to the last gigabyte.
    5. If something breaks, look at the status pages first, then restart the console before anything drastic.

    What this means for you

    For now the answer to "is there a new GTA 6 update?" is short: two small patches before launch, none since, no notes for either. The first update with real patch notes will be the one worth reading closely. For how the launch version was received, including the performance complaints, see GTA 6 reviews.
