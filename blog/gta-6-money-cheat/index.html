    GTA 6 Money Cheat: The $250,000 Code and How to Unlock It

    The GTA 6 money cheat gives $250,000: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. How to unlock it, how to enter it and what it switches off.

    Quick answer

    • The reported GTA 6 money cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.
    • It gives $250,000 and unlocks only after you finish the main story.
    • Using it disables saving and trophies for that session.

    The GTA 6 money cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right, and it adds $250,000 to your cash. It is entered on the D-pad through the in-game phone and is the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The catch is timing: like every cheat in the game, it only works after you have finished the story. This page covers the code, the exact steps, what it switches off, and what to do for money before the credits.

    The code matches on every cheat list we have compared since launch, including GTABase, Red Bull and Beebom. Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page stays marked partly confirmed.

    GTA 6 money cheat at a glance

    Detail Info
    Code Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right
    Effect Adds $250,000
    Platforms PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; D-pad only, so identical on both
    Unlock condition Finish the main story
    Extra unlock task None reported
    Saving Disabled while a cheat is active
    Trophies and achievements Disabled for the session
    Works online No; the game launched without an online mode

    How to enter the money cheat

    1. Finish the main story. The Cheats section appears under the Progress tab after the credits.
    2. Save your game, because an active cheat turns saving off. See how to save in GTA 6.
    3. In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to open the phone.
    4. Hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S. Some guides word it as holding L1 (LB) and then pressing R1 (RB).
    5. Enter: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.

    Tip: the code is a simple pattern. The first four inputs go clockwise around the D-pad from Up; the last four go anticlockwise from Up.

    Why it is locked until the end

    Launch guides agree that the Cheats menu does not exist until the credits have rolled, and that entering a code before then does nothing. The money cheat is one of the few with no further task: finishing the story is its only condition.

    Why it matters: the cheat cannot solve the problems money causes during the story, such as the motel debt in Behind on Payments or the $15,000 that GTABase lists for the Chapter 2 mission Prepare for Vice City. It is a free-roam reward. How far away that is depends on the count you use; see how many chapters are in GTA 6.

    What it costs you

    Effect While a cheat is active
    Saving Disabled
    Trophies and achievements Disabled for the session
    Story progress Not affected, since the story is already finished

    GTABase adds the detail that matters most for this particular cheat: because saving is off until you quit, anything you do after entering a code is lost when you leave the session, and loading your earlier save restores trophies.

    Why it matters: if that description is right, the $250,000 is spending money for the current session rather than a permanent top-up to your save. We have not seen a second guide spell this out, so test it with a spare manual slot before you rely on it either way.

    Can you use it more than once?

    No guide we have checked states a limit or a cooldown, and none confirms that repeated entries stack. We will add the answer when two sources report the same result. Beebom alone says a cheat can be switched off by entering its code again, which would not apply to a one-off cash payment in any case.

    Tip: whatever the answer, enter the code only after a manual save, so you can return to a clean file with trophies enabled.

    How it compares with older GTA games

    Game Money cheat
    GTA 3 and GTA: San Andreas Yes, available from the start; San Andreas also gave $250,000
    GTA 4 No direct money cheat
    GTA 5 No money cheat in story mode
    GTA 6 Yes: $250,000, after the story

    GTA 6 brings the money code back but treats it as a reward for finishing rather than a shortcut. The same goes for the other 60 cheats, most of which also ask for a separate task such as shooting-range medals or stunt jumps. The full list is on our GTA 6 cheats page.

    Money before the credits: what works instead

    Until the cheat unlocks, the income has to be earned. Our money guide covers the methods that at least two launch guides agree on:

    Method Available from Reported payout
    Store hold-ups Start of the game Small
    Selling stolen cars to Moses Early Chapter 2 About $1,000 or a little more per ordinary car
    Daily Driver for Moses Chapter 2 $1,250, plus $500 for good condition
    Jewelry store robberies Chapter 3 About $10,000 to $20,000
    Bank vault robberies Late Chapter 4 $100,000 or more per job

    Tip: cash you carry is lost when you are wasted or busted. Open the bank account in Buck Me and deposit at an ATM before anything risky. That habit is worth more than any single job.

    What is not confirmed

    • An official code list. Rockstar has not published one; every code comes from launch guides.
    • Repeat use. Whether the cheat can be entered again and again in one session.
    • Whether the cash can be kept. The reports that saving stays off until you quit suggest it cannot, but only one guide says so directly.
    • A PC version. The game is not out on PC, so there are no console commands or typed codes.

    Checklist before you use it

    1. Story finished and the Cheats section visible under Progress.
    2. Manual save made in a slot you will not overwrite.
    3. In free roam, not in a mission.
    4. Phone open with Up on the D-pad, shoulder buttons held.
    5. Code entered: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.
    6. When you are done, quit and reload the manual save to get saving and trophies back.

    What this means for you

    If you have just started, forget the money cheat for now; it is dozens of hours away and the story is balanced around earning. If you have finished, it is a quick way to try the expensive cars, weapons and properties in a throwaway session. Either way, the wanted system still applies to whatever you do with the cash: see how the wanted level works.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the money cheat in GTA 6?

    Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right on the D-pad gives $250,000, according to several launch guides.

    Can you use the GTA 6 money cheat at the start of the game?

    No. The Cheats menu is reported to unlock only after the main story is finished.

    Does the money cheat work in GTA 6 online?

    No. Cheats are for story mode, and the game launched without an online mode.
