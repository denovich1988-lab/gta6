    GTA 6 on Netflix is not the game: it is Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look, a gameplay video that Rockstar premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026, six hours before it went up everywhere else. The game itself cannot be played or streamed through Netflix. This page explains what the Netflix title is, when and why it appeared there, what it shows, where to watch it now without a subscription, and why so many people still search for a GTA 6 Netflix series that does not exist.

    Dates, times and the running length below are confirmed by Netflix's own article and at least one independent outlet. Viewing figures come from a single report and are labelled as such.

    GTA 6 on Netflix at a glance
    Title on Netflix Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look
    What it is An official gameplay presentation, not a series, film or playable game
    Announced August 6, 2026, by Rockstar Games
    Netflix premiere Thursday, August 27, 2026, 3 p.m. ET
    Free release 9 p.m. ET the same day, on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the GTA VI site
    Netflix head start Six hours
    Length About 26 minutes (Netflix lists it as 27 minutes)
    Captured on PlayStation 5, in-game footage
    Rating on Netflix TV-MA
    Can you play GTA 6 on Netflix? No

    What the Extended Look is

    It is the third official GTA 6 video and the first to show the game being played. Trailer 1 in December 2023 and Trailer 2 in May 2025 were cinematic. The Extended Look runs for close to half an hour and walks through missions, police chases, car theft and the things you do between jobs. Netflix describes it as captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5.

    Why it matters: fans had spent more than a year waiting for a "Trailer 3". Rockstar skipped the short trailer and went straight to a long gameplay showcase, so if you are looking for Trailer 3, this is it. All three videos are covered in all GTA 6 trailers.

    When it premiered, hour by hour

    Rockstar announced the video on August 6, 2026, three weeks ahead. The schedule on the day:

    Time (ET), August 27, 2026 What happened
    3 p.m. Premiere on Netflix, worldwide
    9 p.m. Release on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the official GTA VI site

    That gave Netflix a six-hour window. Netflix's own article used the same figure, saying members could watch six hours before anyone else.

    Tip: if you read that the Extended Look was "a Netflix exclusive", that was true for those six hours only.

    Who could watch it on Netflix

    According to Netflix, the video was available globally to all subscribers, with subtitles in 13 languages. No particular plan was required. It has its own title page on the service, so searching for "Grand Theft Auto" in the Netflix app finds it.

    Netflix files it oddly. TechSpot noted that the service counted it as an English-language film in its charts, which is part of why people assume there is a GTA movie.

    Where to watch it now

    1. YouTube: on the Rockstar Games channel. Free, no account needed.
    2. Rockstar's site: on the official GTA VI page.
    3. Netflix: where it premiered. Availability there can change, as with any Netflix title.

    You do not need a Netflix subscription. The YouTube version is the same video.

    What it shows

    Our full breakdown is in GTA 6 gameplay: what the Extended Look showed. The short version:

    Shown in the video What we learned
    Police and wanted level Six stars, plus red heat icons for what the police know about you
    Car theft A short hands-on sequence instead of one button press
    Two leads Jason and Lucia working the same mission, one driving while the other shoots
    Activities Gym workouts, jet skis, kayaks, base jumping, store robberies
    Racing Wheel-to-wheel racing on a purpose-built circuit

    Why it matters: the systems in the video are the ones launch guides now document in the released game. The wanted system is explained in GTA 6 wanted level, and the race venues are covered in Port Gellhorn.

    Extended Look vs the two trailers

    Trailer 1 Trailer 2 An Extended Look
    Released December 2023 May 6, 2025 August 27, 2026
    Type Cinematic trailer Cinematic trailer Gameplay presentation
    First shown on YouTube YouTube Netflix, then YouTube six hours later
    Focus Leonida, Vice City, Lucia Jason and Lucia's story and the cast How the game plays

    PCWorld, writing when the Netflix premiere was announced, put the two earlier trailers at nearly 300 million and about 200 million views, and pointed out that no real gameplay had been shown officially before August 27.

    Why it matters: if you only have time for one video before buying, the Extended Look tells you far more about what you will actually be doing than either trailer.

    Is there a GTA 6 Netflix series or film?

    No. The Netflix title is one video of under half an hour. There is no announced GTA series, documentary or film on Netflix or anywhere else. The confusion comes from three things: the premiere was promoted like a show, Netflix lists it among films, and it carries an age rating like any other title on the service.

    Can you play GTA 6 through Netflix?

    No. Netflix's games catalogue does not include GTA 6, and the game is not streamed through the service. It is sold for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. See GTA 6 platforms for what it runs on and GTA 6 price and editions for what it costs.

    Tip: any site offering to "unlock GTA 6 on Netflix" or asking for a login to play it there is not genuine. There is no such version.

    Why Rockstar premiered it on Netflix

    Neither company has given a detailed reason. The one statement on record is from Netflix: Brandon Riegg, its vice president of nonfiction series, said the company was honored that Rockstar had partnered with it to show the next part of the story to Netflix members first.

    What can be said is that it was a first. Rockstar's earlier GTA 6 videos went straight to YouTube.

    How many people watched it

    Netflix has not published a figure in its own article. TechSpot reported these numbers, without naming where the Netflix figure came from:

    Reported figure Source
    About 31 million views on Netflix in the first four days TechSpot
    Number one in its category in 87 of 93 tracked countries TechSpot
    More than 20 million views on Rockstar's YouTube channel TechSpot
    Netflix mobile app use up 50% on the day Sensor Tower, via TechSpot

    Treat them as estimates from one report. For sales of the game itself, see how much money GTA 6 has made.

    What is not confirmed

    • Official Netflix viewing figures. Only the TechSpot report exists.
    • How long it will stay on Netflix. No end date has been given.
    • Any further Netflix project. Nothing has been announced by Rockstar, Take-Two or Netflix.

    How to watch it in the best quality

    1. Open the Rockstar Games channel on YouTube on a TV or console rather than a phone.
    2. Pick the highest resolution your screen supports in the player settings.
    3. Watch it once straight through, then read our gameplay breakdown for the details that are easy to miss.
    4. If you have already bought the game, compare it with what you see on your own console: the footage was captured on a standard PS5 at the game's 30 FPS target. More on that in does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS.

    What this means for you

    If you searched for GTA 6 on Netflix hoping to play it, you need a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S. If you wanted the video everyone talked about in August, it is free on YouTube and still worth half an hour, especially before you start the story. And if you were looking for a GTA series, there is none.

    No. The Netflix title is a single gameplay presentation of about 26 minutes.

    Do I need Netflix to watch the GTA 6 Extended Look?
