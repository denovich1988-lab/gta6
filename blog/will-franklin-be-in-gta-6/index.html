    Will Franklin Be in GTA 6? Every Returning Character and Cameo

    Will Franklin be in GTA 6? What the released game shows for Franklin, Michael, Trevor, Lester and Tommy Vercetti, plus the cameos that are really in it.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    9 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Franklin, Michael and Trevor are not reported in person in GTA 6; the launch coverage we checked finds them only as references, mainly in a true-crime podcast.
    • Three familiar faces from earlier games do turn up in the story, two of them as short cameos in Chapters 4 and 5.
    • Tommy Vercetti is a name and a set of Vice City nods, and no launch guide we read reports Lester at all.

    Will Franklin be in GTA 6? Not in person: one week after launch, no reputable guide reports Franklin Clinton on screen, and he exists in the game only as a reference. The same is true of Michael and Trevor. A few characters from older games really do appear, though, and one of them is a much bigger surprise than any GTA 5 lead. Below is a character-by-character verdict, where each reference is, and what is still open.

    A character is marked "yes" here only when at least two independent outlets describe the same appearance. Everything else is listed as a reference or as unconfirmed. Mild spoilers follow for Chapters 4 and 5: we name who appears and in which mission, but not how the story ends.

    Is Franklin in GTA 6?

    No launch guide we read places Franklin in a mission, a cutscene or the open world. Game Rant's list of returning characters, published on November 20, has three names, and Franklin is not one of them. What the game does have is a true-crime podcast that revisits the Los Santos crimes of GTA 5, and GamesRadar+ reports that its hosts talk about three dangerous criminals connected to a string of bank robberies. That is Franklin, Michael and Trevor, discussed as a cold case.

    The question has a long history. On November 14, 2023, GamesRadar+ reported that Franklin's actor, Shawn Fonteno, posted and quickly deleted a cryptic GTA 6 tease. The same article notes that he had earlier said Franklin would not be in the game, then called it a mystery. Nothing in the released game backs up the tease.

    Why it matters: if you were holding off for a Franklin mission, there is none to wait for. The story belongs to Jason and Lucia; see the full cast in GTA 6 characters.

    Is Michael in GTA 6?

    Michael De Santa is the most referenced of the three, but he is not seen either. Besides the podcast, two nods point straight at him:

    • The yacht. A Marquis is moored outside Jason's house in the Keys. KeenGamer ties it to the boat Jimmy sells behind his father's back in GTA 5, and the lists we used for our own GTA 6 easter eggs page add that its navigation screen shows Los Santos.
    • The ringtone. "Wind Chimes", Michael's ringtone in GTA 5, is in the phone settings.

    Tip: the yacht is the easiest GTA 5 reference to see for yourself, because it sits next to your first safehouse. Walk down to the water before you leave the Keys for good.

    Will Trevor be in GTA 6?

    Trevor Philips is never on screen, and the podcast comes closest to him. Its subject is Wade Hebert, Trevor's hanger-on from GTA 5, and the deaths of Wade's cousin Floyd and Floyd's fiancée Debra. Rolling Stone Brasil reports that the show has Wade arrested at a strip club and sentenced in 2014 to 50 years for the two killings, with the host doubting he did it. GamesRadar+ adds that the hosts question how much Wade really knew about his friend. GTA 5 players know who was in that apartment.

    Steven Ogg, who played Trevor, was asked about GTA 6 on Michael Rosenbaum's podcast, and GamesRadar+ covered it on February 5, 2025. He denied hating the character and said he would not be playing the new game. That is a comment about playing it, not about Trevor's role in it.

    Why it matters: the podcast picks up a thread GTA 5 left hanging, what became of Wade, so it is worth hearing even if you only care about the old cast. Podcasts sit alongside the GTA 6 radio stations.

    Will Lester be in GTA 6?

    No guide we checked reports Lester Crest in GTA 6, as a character or as a reference. Two pre-launch theories explain why people keep asking:

    • The Cal Hampton theory. Fans thought Jason's conspiracy-minded friend Cal sounded like Lester's actor, Jay Klaitz. TechRadar reported on April 14, 2026 that Klaitz denied voicing Cal in an interview with the marketplace Eldorado.
    • The retirement theory. In GTA Online's Agents of Sabotage update, Lester sells the player his garment factory and says he has retired. Dexerto reported on December 11, 2024 that fans read this as Rockstar clearing him for GTA 6.

    Neither theory has been borne out so far. We list Lester as "not reported" and not as a firm "no" because a week is a short time for a game this size.

    Tip: if a video claims a Lester call or email, check whether it shows the mission name and chapter. None of the published walkthroughs has one.

    Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

    Tommy Vercetti is in GTA 6 as a name, not as a person. The clearest reference is in Rockstar's own edition text: the Ultimate Edition includes revolvers with Vice City styling that are said to come from the Vercetti Estate. That puts his mansion, at least by name, in this version of Vice City. See what else is in the Ultimate Edition.

    KeenGamer lists three more nods: the palm-tree weapon pattern in the Vintage Vice City Pack, which echoes Tommy's shirt; the Ocean View Hotel in Ocean Beach, his first safehouse in 2002; and a trophy called "In The Beginning…", earned in Ocean Beach.

    Why it matters: a walking, talking Tommy was always unlikely. Ray Liotta, who voiced him, died in 2022, and the 2002 game sits in an older continuity than GTA 4, GTA 5 and GTA 6. A name on a gun case lets Rockstar honor him without recasting him.

    Jimmy De Santa: the one GTA 5 character you meet

    Michael's son Jimmy is the only GTA 5 character reported in person. Game Rant places him in the Chapter 4 main mission Headliners, where Jason and Lucia work security at a club and you decide whether Jimmy gets in. He does not come back afterwards. Danny Tamberelli, who played him in GTA 5, returns for the role.

    Tip: Headliners is part of the long setup for the Chapter 4 airport job, so you cannot miss it, but the choice at the door is quick. Make a manual save first if you want to see both outcomes; here is how to save in GTA 6.

    Niko Bellic: the GTA 4 lead returns

    The biggest returning name is Niko Bellic, the protagonist of GTA 4. Game Rant, KeenGamer and esports.gg all place him in the Chapter 5 main mission Taking Liberties, and KeenGamer and SoapCentral count two cutscenes. He is older, he is not playable, and he has business with a shipping heir, Giorgio Gallo, that crosses Jason and Lucia's path.

    Rockstar kept it quiet in the credits. Michael Hollick, Niko's original actor, is listed as "The Hitman", and SoapCentral noted on November 20 that Rockstar had not commented on the role.

    Why it matters: GTA 5 mentioned Niko without ever showing him, so this is his first appearance on screen since 2008, and it ties Liberty City directly to Leonida. If you want to reach it quickly, see how many chapters GTA 6 has.

    Valentina: from GTA Online to the main story

    Valentina first appeared in GTA Online's Money Fronts update in 2025 as a client of Mr. Faber. Game Rant counts her as a returning character and names Josephine Phoenix as her actor. In GTA 6 she is a Chapter 4 contact who houses Jason and Lucia at the Y Hotel and hands out work.

    Tip: her first scene is in our Ocean Beach walkthrough, and the rest of her jobs are in the GTA 6 mission list.

    What is not confirmed

    • The podcast's name. GamesRadar+ calls it Vice City True Stories; Rolling Stone Brasil calls it Final Act and says five podcasts ship with the game. We have not been able to settle which is right.
    • How the trio are named on air. GamesRadar+ describes allusions; fan sites say Michael and Franklin are named and Trevor is not. We could not confirm the exact wording from two reputable outlets.
    • The cast list. Fan sites report that Shawn Fonteno, Ned Luke and Steven Ogg are absent from Rockstar's credits. We could not open the credits page ourselves, so treat that as likely, not proven.
    • A post-launch statement from Steven Ogg. Several fan sites say he has confirmed Trevor is not in the game, without a date or a link. The only dated comment we found is the 2025 one above.
    • Phil Cassidy. A gun-store owner resembles him and the Phil's Ammu-Nation name is back, but Beebom treats it as a lookalike, and no credit ties him to the Vice City character.
    • Whether Valentina is officially the same woman. Outlets treat her as such; Rockstar has not said so.
    • Lester and Lamar. No reports so far, which is not the same as proof they are absent.

    How to find the references yourself

    1. Visit the yacht first. At Jason's house in the Keys, walk to the water and look for the moored Marquis.
    2. Open the phone settings and scroll the ringtones for "Wind Chimes".
    3. Drive in free roam with a podcast on. GamesRadar+ and KeenGamer both heard the Wade episode on a vehicle radio outside missions.
    4. Walk Ocean Beach on foot once the story moves to Vice City, and look for the Ocean View Hotel's pink neon sign. Our Vice City guide covers the district.
    5. Keep a save before Headliners in Chapter 4 and before Taking Liberties in Chapter 5, so you can rewatch the two cameos.
    6. Check the Liberty City and Los Santos cards in the card sets. KeenGamer found a Los Santos Panic card in the Keys; see GTA 6 collectibles.

    Verdict: who is really back

    GTA 6 does not bring back Franklin, Michael or Trevor as characters. It treats them as local legend: something a podcast host argues about, a boat that ended up in the wrong harbor, a ringtone. The people who actually walk into a scene are a step removed, a son, a client from GTA Online, and one man from Liberty City nobody expected. If you are choosing whether to play for the old cast, play for Jason and Lucia instead, and enjoy the nods as you find them.

    What we will add to this page

    We will update the table when the credits are confirmed by a second reputable outlet, when the podcast's name and episode order are settled, and if players find Lester, Lamar or any other familiar face. If Rockstar or one of the GTA 5 actors makes a dated, on-the-record statement about the game, it will go in the section for that character.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will Franklin be in GTA 6?

    Not in person, as far as launch coverage shows. Franklin is referenced in an in-game true-crime podcast about GTA 5's Los Santos crimes, but no reputable guide reports him on screen, and Rockstar has not announced a role for him.

    Is Michael in GTA 6?

    Michael De Santa is referenced, not seen. The podcast alludes to him, a Marquis yacht near Jason's house recalls his stolen boat, and his son Jimmy De Santa has a short cameo in the Chapter 4 mission Headliners.

    Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

    Only as a reference. The Ultimate Edition revolvers are described as coming from the Vercetti Estate and the Ocean View Hotel stands in Ocean Beach, but Tommy himself does not appear in any launch guide we checked.
