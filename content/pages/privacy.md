You can use {{SITE}} without an account. We don't run advertising or analytics trackers.

## Data we process

- **Server and security logs.** Our hosting and security providers record technical data when pages are requested (IP address, time, page, browser type). We use it only to deliver and protect the site, and logs are kept for a short period.
- **Email.** If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.
- **Search.** The site search runs in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.

## Service providers

Cloudflare (delivery and security), Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting in the EU) and GitHub (source files; receives no visitor data).

## Cookies

We set no analytics or advertising cookies. Our security provider may set strictly necessary cookies to tell people from bots.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to processing. Email [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}).

## Changes

If we add analytics or advertising, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
