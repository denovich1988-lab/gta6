**This GTA 6 VCIA Takedown walkthrough covers the airport heist that the whole of Chapter 4 builds toward, from Lucia's walk through the terminal to the final landing.** It swaps between the two leads again and again, and it cannot be replayed, so make a manual save before you walk into the planning room. The steps below describe what happens in the mission, so expect story spoilers from the "Step-by-step" section onward.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Into the airport

As Lucia, follow Nate through the terminal. As Jason, drive the stolen catering truck with the crew to the cargo terminal.

### 2. Janice (Lucia)

Nate spills a drink on Janice, the wine expert Nate has been escorting. Follow her into the restroom and knock her out from behind; Lucia hides her in a stall automatically.

### 3. To the vault (Lucia)

Follow Nate as he pushes the crate behind the vault manager. In the elevator the manager asks about the wine, and you can answer.

### 4. The rooftop run (Jason)

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Timer | 5 minutes |
| Goal | The network terminal, in a caged area on the upper level, toward the middle of the roof |
| Guards | On the roof and on higher platforms |
| How | Silenced pistol or melee |

**Tip:** look up as well as ahead. Both guides point out guards on the raised platforms, and a missed one costs time you do not have.

### 5. Open the vault (Lucia)

Follow the manager into the vault and inspect the wine crate. On Kordell's signal, knock the manager out. Run back down the hallway and press both buttons to open the gates. After the guards are tied up, return to the vault; Francisco takes the bags and the group heads back to the elevator.

### 6. Hide Omar (Jason)

Knock Omar out, pick him up and put him in the marked container before anyone sees.

### 7. Load the plane (Lucia)

Drive the baggage cart with Kordell's crew to the plane at the south terminal. Carry the bags from the cart to the loader ramp.

### 8. Police arrive

Jason fights his way toward the plane, then Lucia holds off the police until the two meet.

### 9. Chase the plane, then the truck (Jason)

Take the truck and drive up on the **right side of the plane, near the landing gear**, so Lucia can climb aboard. After that, chase the other truck, with police behind you, until it reaches the highway.

### 10. Fight Raina (Lucia)

Inside the plane, Lucia fights Raina hand to hand. Both guides say she goes down more easily than the big man Jason fought in Headliners.

### 11. Stop the truck (Jason)

A gunman in the back of the truck throws things at you. Use Focus to shoot him. When the truck crashes, go and look: Omar crawls out.

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| Shoot Omar in the leg, or knock him out | Professional bonus |
| Kill him | No bonus mentioned |

### 12. Find Raul (Lucia)

Move toward the front of the plane. The enemies here are hired gang members, and some hide between the seats. When Kordell grabs Lucia, follow the button prompts to fight back, then shoot him as he is dragged to the door.

### 13. Defend Andrea (Jason)

Hold off the NOOSE agents while Andrea loads the bags.

**Tip:** pick up Francisco's LMG-6 from the ground here. PowerPyx says you only keep it if you grab it during the mission.

When Andrea calls, get in the van.

### 14. Land the plane (Lucia)

Keep the plane steady until it comes down. PowerPyx's input is to hold the right trigger and pull the left stick down. The plane ends up in Vice City Bay and the mission is complete.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. These are the stages they single out:

- **The rooftop timer.** It is the one section both guides put a clock on.
- **The plane pickup.** Approach on the right, by the landing gear; anywhere else and Lucia cannot board.
- **The cabin.** Enemies crouch in the seat rows, so clear each row before moving up.
- **Omar.** A careless shot costs the Professional bonus.
