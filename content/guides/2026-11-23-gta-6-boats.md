**GTA 6 boats matter more than in any earlier game: Leonida is a state of keys, canals, rivers and swamps, and several story missions are fought on the water.** This page lists the boats that launch guides agree on, the missions where you use them, where to find one in free roam and which boats the cheats spawn. Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

## Jet skis and speedboats

The **Seashark** jet ski is the boat you will use most in the story. In [Boobie Time](/missions/gta-6-boobie-time-walkthrough/), thieves grab a cash bag and Jason chases them down the river on one; you ride over the bag to pick it up. Day Release includes a jet ski race, and Dubious Provenance has another Seashark chase.

The **Pegassi Speeder** and the **Shitzu Tropic** are the open-water speedboats. The **Shitzu Squalo** is one of the seven vehicles named in Rockstar's edition descriptions, and GamesRadar+ notes that the Ultimate Edition version comes with an explosives crate on board.

**Tip:** take a jet ski rather than a speedboat for chases in canals and rivers; every river chase in the story so far is ridden on a Seashark.

## Airboats for the swamps

Airboats skim over shallow water and mud where propeller boats get stuck, which makes them the right choice in Grassrivers and the Everglades-style swamps of [Leonida](/map/gta-6-map/). Guides list a standard **Airboat** and the **Airboat 09**, which is also a cheat spawn.

## Where to find a boat

Boats sit at docks and marinas along the coast and in the keys. The easiest early spot is the docks by the houses in Key Lento, near Jason's home, where Home Sweet Home lets you take a boat or a jet ski.

**Why it matters:** the Keys are linked by one long highway, so the water is often the more direct route between islands.
