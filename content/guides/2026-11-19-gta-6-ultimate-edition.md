**The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition costs $99.99 and adds 16 extra in-game items** to the complete game. Nothing in it is needed to finish the story.

## Standard vs Ultimate

| | Standard | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| US price | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| Full game | Yes | Yes |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (16 items) | No | Yes |
| Boxed version | Yes (download code in the box) | No, digital only |
| One month of GTA+ | Yes | Yes |
| Vintage Vice City Pack if bought before November 20, 2026 | Yes | Yes |

## What the upgrade contains

Rockstar describes 16 exclusive additions in five groups:

- Vehicles
- Weapons
- Clothing and outfits
- Special locations such as garages and shops
- In-game activities

Check your console store listing for the exact list in your region.

## Is it worth the extra $20?

- **Buy Standard** if you only want the story and the open world.
- **Buy Ultimate** if you want more vehicles and gear from the first hours and do not mind a digital-only copy.

There is no collector's edition and no deluxe edition.

The pre-order bonus is separate and comes with both editions: see the [Vintage Vice City Pack](/buying/gta-6-vintage-vice-city-pack/). For all prices, see [GTA 6 price and editions](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/).
