**This GTA 6 Gear Up walkthrough covers the third mission of the game, where Jason buys his first long gun.** PowerPyx also lists it under the task name "Pick Up Another Gun". It opens after [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/) and takes only a few minutes.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Gear Up (mission 3, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Pick Up Another Gun |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | The Ammu-Nation near Jason's house in Key Lento |
| Requirement | Finish Gone Fishing |
| Reward | The long gun you choose |
| Next mission | Lab Fees |

VGC's guide says to collect the cash from the mailbox outside Jason's house before you leave, and that mission pay arrives in that mailbox. The other guides do not mention this, but it costs nothing to check the mailbox on the way out.

## Objectives

1. Go to Ammu-Nation.
2. Approach Andrew and buy a long gun.
3. Deal with the Kingfishers outside.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go to Ammu-Nation

Take the car parked outside Jason's house and drive to the Ammu-Nation marked on the map. It is close by.

### 2. Buy a long gun from Andrew

Go into the store and talk to Andrew, the clerk. He offers a choice of long guns. PowerPyx and GTABase list two: a shotgun and a tactical rifle. Both say you will most likely only have enough money for the shotgun at this point. Guides do not give the prices.

VGC says the choice does not matter for the mission, so take whichever you can afford. For the full list of guns, see [GTA 6 weapons](/weapons/gta-6-weapons/).

### 3. Leave the store

When you walk out, three Kingfishers pull up in a truck and come for Jason. The guides suggest different ways to handle it:

| Source | Advice |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx, GTABase | The Kingfishers open fire. Shoot back. Once they are down, a phone call ends the mission. |
| VGC | Fight them with your fists. Shooting brings the police, and they react much more slowly to a fist fight. |

Both ways work. If you use the gun, expect a [wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/) and be ready to leave the area.

### 4. The phone call

After the fight there is a phone call. VGC describes it as Jason calling Donnie to warn him that the Kingfishers are looking for him. The call ends the mission.

## Where players fail

- **Not having enough cash.** If you can only afford one gun, that is expected. Take the shotgun.
- **Walking out unprepared.** The ambush starts as soon as you leave the store. Have a weapon ready or be ready to fight.
- **Worrying about dying.** PowerPyx and GTABase report that if Jason dies here, he wakes up at his house and the mission ends anyway, with no lasting effect.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Reward | The long gun you picked at Ammu-Nation |
| Optional goals | None listed in any guide |
| Medals | None. PowerPyx reports that GTA 6 has no mission medals and no mission replay. |

After Gear Up the map opens up. VGC notes that you can explore, collect Brian's rent or visit Coastal Auto. In story order the next mission is Lab Fees. See the [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/) for everything that follows.
