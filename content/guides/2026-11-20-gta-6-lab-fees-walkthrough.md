**This GTA 6 Lab Fees walkthrough covers the fourth mission of the game, where Jason collects rent for Brian and walks out with acid instead of cash.** PowerPyx also lists it under the task name "Collect Brian's Rent". The two door codes you need are **0420** and **7700**.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Lab Fees (mission 4, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Collect Brian's Rent |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | A small shop in Key Lento, marked on the map |
| Requirement | Finish [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $600, delivered to Jason's mailbox (PowerPyx) |
| Next mission | [Just a Scratch](/missions/gta-6-just-a-scratch-walkthrough/) |

VGC notes that once you control Jason, several early missions can be done in any order. We follow the order of the in-game story recap, the same order both guides use.

## Objectives

1. Talk to the clerk.
2. Enter the lab and collect the acid.
3. Call Brian.
4. Stash the acid in Brian's lockup.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go to the shop and call Brian

Drive to the mission marker in Key Lento. The shop sits in a small cluster of stores and bars; PowerPyx calls it a gift shop, VGC a trinket shop behind a surf shop. Open the phone with **Up on the D-pad** and call Brian. He tells you to go in and get his money.

On the way, VGC points out a WAiNK scan nearby: the large whale statue. Point the phone at it to log it. See [GTA 6 collectibles](/map/gta-6-collectibles/) for what scans count toward.

### 2. Deal with the clerk

Talk to the clerk (**L2 + X** on PS5, **LT + A** on Xbox). He says he has no rent. The guides show three ways to change his mind:

| Approach | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Punch him once | He offers the acid in the back room | PowerPyx |
| Aim your gun at him | He gives up the code | VGC |
| Wait for him to pull a baseball bat, then take it | He backs down and gives up the code | VGC |

All three end the same way. VGC adds that killing him still lets you use the code but brings the police, so there is no reason to do it.

### 3. Open the lab with 0420

Use the keypad on the door in the corner and enter **0420**. Pick up the acid. Both guides say there is cash to take as well: PowerPyx mentions the register in the shop, VGC a cash box in the back room. Then leave.

### 4. Call Brian and drive to the lockup

Call Brian again. He sends you to his lockup at the southern end of the Keys, shown as a green icon on the map.

### 5. Stash the acid with 7700

Enter **7700** on the lockup door, go inside and put the acid in the stash. That ends the mission.

## Where players fail

- **Killing the clerk.** It is not needed, and VGC reports it alerts the police. See [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
- **Looking for the pay straight away.** The reward is not handed over at the lockup. PowerPyx reports it arrives in Jason's mailbox after a while, because he has no bank account at this point.
- **Forgetting the second call.** The lockup marker appears only after you phone Brian from outside the shop.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Reward | $600 by mail (PowerPyx; VGC does not list a reward) |
| Optional loot | Cash in the shop |
| Nearby scan | The whale statue, for WAiNK |
| Unlocks | Brian's lockup as a place to sell drugs |

Brian's lockup stays useful after the mission: both guides say you can sell drugs there. PowerPyx adds that fences also buy jewelry and marked bills, that more of them open up as the story goes on, and that stolen goods travel in a bag you can leave in your car trunk.
