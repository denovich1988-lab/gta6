**This GTA 6 Down to Earth walkthrough covers Brian's first flying job: a boat to clean, a seaplane run to Bobcat Bay with Lucia, a deal that turns into a gunfight, and a race home before her parole officer finds the drugs.** PowerPyx lists it as "Down to Earth (Meet Brian)". Both PowerPyx and VGC count it as the fourteenth mission.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |

## Objectives

1. Follow Brian.
2. Clean the boat with the pressure washer.
3. Go to the plane, fly to Bobcat Bay and land near the boat.
4. Incapacitate or take out the enemies.
5. Fly and land near the drops.
6. Land near the dock.
7. Hide the narcotics in the shed.
8. Support Lucia.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Clean the boat

Follow Brian outside; PowerPyx says he takes you to Cal at one of the boats. Pick up the pressure washer and spray until all the green grime is gone.

| Guide | Pressure washer input |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx | L2 + R2 on PS5, LT + RT on Xbox |
| VGC | "R2 or R1", like firing a weapon |

Lucia turns up partway through and wants to see the job.

### 2. Fly to Bobcat Bay

Board the seaplane with Lucia and fly to the marker. PowerPyx places it in the far north-west of Mariana County. Land on the water beside the waiting boat.

### 3. The pickup and the fight

PowerPyx describes the smugglers throwing a package into the water, with Lucia steering while you fish it out. They expect payment and you have none, so it turns into a gunfight.

**Tip:** PowerPyx notes that shooting arms and legs leaves enemies alive and builds a "professional" reputation, while killing them builds a "violent" one. It says either approach finishes the mission.

### 4. Make the drops

Fly to each marked spot. PowerPyx counts three: you land for the first two and Lucia drops the package for you. After the second, the coastguard becomes suspicious, so the third is dropped from the air. VGC describes this last one as flying alongside a boat.

### 5. Get home

Lucia finds a pile of missed calls from her parole officer, who is on the way to the house. Fly back and land near the dock.

### 6. Hide the drugs

Put the package in the washing machine in the shed below the house, then join Lucia at the front while she talks the officer round.

### 7. Meditation

After the cutscene, PowerPyx says Lucia shows Jason how to meditate on the terrace, which unlocks the first of 18 meditation spots.

## Where players fail

- **Hard water landings.** VGC: hit the water too fast and the plane explodes, which fails the mission. Come in low and slow.
- **Landing too far from a drop.** Once you are down, the plane steers like a boat for a short distance, so taxi closer.
