**This GTA 6 Attend Eddie Acosta's Party walkthrough covers an optional Chapter 4 mission that is really a scam: Eddie invites you to a rooftop party, locks you outside and asks for $50,000.** The short answer is do not pay. The longer answer is how to get off the roof without him.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Attend Eddie Acosta's Party (Chapter 4, optional) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), alone |
| Given by | Eddie Acosta |
| Starts at | Heathcliff Heights, central [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | [Pool Day](/missions/gta-6-pool-day-walkthrough/), then Eddie's text a few days later |
| Reward | None (paying costs $50,000) |

You meet Eddie at the Y Hotel pool bar in Pool Day, when he starts talking while you fetch Lucia a drink. The invitation arrives by text later. Go alone: both guides mark the mission as solo only.

**Tip:** bank your cash before you go. It does not change the mission, but it is a good habit before any meeting with a stranger who talks about investments. See [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).

## Objectives

1. Attend Eddie Acosta's party.
2. Meet Eddie Acosta at the elevator.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go to Heathcliff Heights

The building is marked on the map in central Vice City. When you walk into the lobby, your character phones Eddie automatically.

### 2. Ride up with Eddie

Eddie steps out of the lobby elevator and spends the ride up selling his crypto "investment".

### 3. The rooftop

You walk out onto the roof, and Eddie locks the door behind you. He wants $50,000 before he will open it. Both guides call this what it is: a scam.

## Pay or refuse?

| Choice | What happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Pay $50,000 | Eddie opens the door and joins you on the balcony; you receive an NFT picture with no value | $50,000 |
| Refuse in dialogue | You stay locked out and have to find your own way off the roof | Nothing |
| Shoot at him | The door is bulletproof and he is not hurt; you still have to leave by the roof | Nothing |

There is no reward on either path, so refusing is simply $50,000 better.

## How to escape the roof

1. Take the bike that is on the roof.
2. Build up to full speed and ride over the row of solar panels. They work as a ramp.
3. Land on the neighbouring roof.
4. Take that building's elevator down.

**Tip:** give yourself the longest run-up the roof allows. Both guides stress full speed.

## After the rooftop

- **If you did not shoot at him,** Eddie comes out onto the roof after your jump and talks to you. Later he waits by the lobby elevator for his next target. You can intimidate him there; he runs out of the front door, and you can chase him.
- **If you did shoot at him,** he runs off straight away and does not wait in the lobby.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. The only real mistake is paying.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cash | None; minus $50,000 if you pay |
| Trophy progress | Does not count toward the Strangers and Freaks trophy (both guides) |
| 100% | GTABase's page marks it as required; PowerPyx does not say |

Because the two guides do not agree on whether this counts for 100%, it is safest to play it; it takes a few minutes and costs nothing if you refuse.
