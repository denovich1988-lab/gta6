**This GTA 6 Hidden Charges walkthrough shows where to plant all three explosive charges on the downtown cell towers.** Karim's plan is to blow the towers at the start of the bank job: it distracts the police and cuts the bank's emergency communications. Every charge you place buys more time in the heist, so it is worth doing all three.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Hidden Charges (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Karim |
| Starts at | Planning room, Stockyard |
| Requirement | [Company Property](/missions/gta-6-company-property-walkthrough/) and [Cooties](/missions/gta-6-cooties-walkthrough/), then Karim's call |
| Reward | More time in The Cartel Heist for each charge planted |

The mission appears on the map as Prepping the Explosives after Company Property. It becomes playable when Karim calls.

## Objectives

1. Get in the van.
2. Drive to the garage.
3. Park in the garage.
4. Plant a charge at one of the cell towers.
5. Plant a charge at one of the cell towers.
6. Plant the charge at the cell tower.

## How many charges you get

| Cargo lost in Company Property | Charges |
|---|---|
| None | 3 |
| One box | 2 |
| Two boxes | 1 |

Both guides give the rule as a maximum of three, one fewer per box lost.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Karim and take the van

Karim explains the plan at the planning room, and Jason and Lucia talk to Raul. Outside, get in the van that Karim is loading with explosives and drive to the marker downtown. Park inside the marked garage in the alley.

**Tip:** use both characters. Both guides say splitting the charges between Jason and Lucia is quicker and raises their relationship level; see [GTA 6 romance](/blog/gta-6-romance/).

### 2. East tower

The East tower is directly above the van.

1. Go to the front of the building on the west side; it shows a helipad on the map.
2. Take the elevator inside to the roof.
3. Climb the stairs to the helipad.
4. Cross the walkway to the next rooftop.
5. Climb up to the tower and plant the charge.

### 3. South tower

Switch to the other character, who is already near the South tower. See [how to switch characters](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/) if you have not used the swap much.

1. Walk past the pool to the elevator.
2. Ride it to the roof.
3. Climb the ladder and plant the charge.

### 4. West tower

If you have a third charge, the game asks who should place it. Switching characters puts you back at the van, which both guides say is slightly faster.

1. Grab the bag from the van.
2. Enter the building on the north side. It is a construction site, and the workers report you for trespassing straight away.
3. Take the elevator inside, on the left.
4. On the roof, go around to the far side of the grey tower.
5. Climb the ladder and plant the charge on the box.

### 5. Get away

Take a parachute from the box on the rooftop if you need one, jump and glide clear. The mission completes once you are outside the police search area and the search has ended.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

- **Arriving with fewer than three charges.** That is decided in Company Property and cannot be fixed here.
- **Lingering at the construction site.** You are reported the moment you walk in, so go straight to the elevator.
- **Jumping without a parachute.** Pick one up from the rooftop box first.
