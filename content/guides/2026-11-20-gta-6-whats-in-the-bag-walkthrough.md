**This GTA 6 What's in the Bag? walkthrough covers the opening mission of Chapter 2, a short scene in a motel room where Lucia finally looks at what she and Jason nearly died for.** It takes a couple of minutes, but it matters: it is where Chapter 1's trophy unlocks and where the open world comes back.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | What's in the Bag? (first mission of Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | A motel room; PowerPyx names the Starlet Motel in Kelly County |
| Requirement | Finish [Discharge Time](/missions/gta-6-discharge-time-walkthrough/); it follows directly |
| Reward | Paradise Lost trophy; free roam as Lucia |
| Next | [Ambulance Chaser](/missions/gta-6-ambulance-chaser-walkthrough/) appears when you leave the room |

The guides place the motel slightly differently: VGC says Kelly County, GTABase says Port Gellhorn, and PowerPyx uses both. It is the same room. See [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) for the area.

## Objectives

1. Wake up at the motel.
2. Look inside the bag and read the note.

GTABase lists the two steps above; PowerPyx shows only "Read the note".

## Step-by-step

### 1. Wake up

The morning after the hospital escape, the news is on. The police captain from the Visitor Center is on screen blaming the two of them for what happened.

### 2. The statue

Lucia goes through the bag. Pick up the small statue of a woman and turn it over. A note is fixed underneath:

> From my collection. She reminded me of someone.

**Tip:** VGC says to keep that line in mind for later.

### 3. The hard drive

The other item is a hard drive. There is no computer in the room, so nothing can be done with it yet. The mission ends.

## Where players fail

There is nothing to fail. It is close to a cutscene.

## What changes after this mission

| Change | Details |
|---|---|
| Free roam | Returns, with Lucia as the character you control (PowerPyx) |
| Jason | Recovering; VGC says early Chapter 2 is Lucia's and that you will soon be able to swap between the two |
| Trophy | Paradise Lost (Bronze, per PowerPyx) unlocks during the mission |
| 100% | GTABase marks the mission as required |

For the swap itself, see [how to switch characters in GTA 6](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/).

## Optional goals and rewards

Nothing optional is listed by any of the three guides.

PowerPyx lists a task called Take Care of Jason next. The first job on the map is getting rid of the getaway vehicle: [Ambulance Chaser](/missions/gta-6-ambulance-chaser-walkthrough/). The full order is in the [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
