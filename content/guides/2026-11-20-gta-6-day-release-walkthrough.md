**This GTA 6 Day Release walkthrough covers the long social mission with Mattia and Kat: a speedboat to a yacht, a jet ski race, a drive through Vice City at night and a club.** PowerPyx lists it as "Day Release (Meet Mattia & Kat)". It is the first time you play as Lucia since the Prologue, and almost every choice in it affects the relationship rather than success or failure.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Day Release (mission 15 in [our list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/), Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Meet Mattia & Kat |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) at the start and end, [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) for most of the party |
| Starts at | The Rusty Anchor, Key Lento |
| Requirement | A call after [Down to Earth](/missions/gta-6-down-to-earth-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | No cash; one dress for Lucia (VGC) |

## Objectives

1. Get in the speedboat.
2. Drive to the yacht.
3. Race the jet ski.
4. Shoot the champagne bottles.
5. Find Mattia.
6. Keep up with Mattia and drive to Huxley Motel.
7. Follow Mattia.
8. Dance with Jason.
9. Find Jason.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Speedboat to the yacht

At the Rusty Anchor, trigger the cutscene and board the boat behind the bar. Lucia's ankle monitor comes off before you leave; PowerPyx says Mattia's man Burim removes it. The yacht is marked off the coast and the trip is long.

### 2. Jet skis

After the party scene you choose how Lucia rides: alone, or with Jason.

**Tip:** ride with Jason. Both guides say it raises the relationship. Mattia then challenges you to a race back to the yacht with ramps along the way, and the result does not matter.

### 3. Champagne bottles

As Lucia, shoot the bottles in focus mode (**L3 + R3**). Hitting all three only changes a line of dialogue.

### 4. Choose a dress

Lucia goes to see Kat, who lets her pick a dress. VGC says your choice is unlocked permanently and calls it the earliest free formal outfit; the others can be bought later.

### 5. Find Mattia

He is in a room on the lowest deck, two decks below where you start (PowerPyx). VGC lists two optional photos on the yacht: some documents you can send to Cal, and the front of the boat, which raises the relationship.

### 6. Drive through Vice City

A cutscene puts you in a sports car at night. Follow Mattia, then follow the route to the Huxley Motel. VGC says he stops at a hotel to collect drugs and the next stretch is driven with a filter that makes the road harder to read.

**Tip:** if you crash, reset the car. PowerPyx says that repairs it fully.

### 7. The club

Follow Mattia into the club, which PowerPyx names as Nine1Nine. You can hold hands with Jason on the way in (PowerPyx shows R2, RT on Xbox). Talk to Mattia at his table, then move close to Jason with the left stick and dance. VGC says dance flourishes with L2, when prompted, raise the relationship.

### 8. The apartment

The party moves upstairs. Find Jason at the end of the hallway; PowerPyx says to take the right-hand door. Security turns up, and the two leave over the balcony. Hold hands again when the prompt appears.

You wake back in Key Lento as Jason.

## Where players fail

- **Wrecking the car.** It is the only fail state VGC lists.
- **Riding solo.** Not a failure, but you miss a relationship boost.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Choice | Effect |
|---|---|
| Jet ski with Jason | Relationship up |
| Photo of the boat's bow | Relationship up (VGC) |
| Dance flourishes, holding hands | Relationship up |
| Dress | Kept by Lucia (VGC) |
| Champagne bottles, race result, Mattia's handshake | No lasting effect |
