**GTA 6 is on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and nowhere else.**

## Where you can play GTA 6

| Platform | GTA 6? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | Yes | 30 FPS target |
| PS5 Pro | Yes | 30 FPS target, ray-traced shadows, PSSR upscaling |
| Xbox Series X | Yes | 30 FPS target |
| Xbox Series S | Yes | 30 FPS target, at a lower resolution |
| PlayStation 4 | No | Not supported |
| Xbox One | No | Not supported |
| Nintendo Switch 2 | No | Nothing announced |
| PC | No | Nothing announced; see [GTA 6 on PC](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/) |

## Which console should you use?

- **You own a PS5 or Series X:** you are set. They get the same game at the same frame rate target.
- **You own a Series S:** it is supported. Expect a lower resolution, and note that the download uses a large share of a 512 GB drive.
- **You own a PS5 Pro:** you get the best confirmed image quality, not a higher frame rate.
- **You own a PS4 or Xbox One:** you need a newer console.

Rockstar has not published a resolution table for each console.
