**GTA 6 has cheat codes, but you cannot use them from the start.** Guides published on November 18, 2026, the day before launch, report 61 cheats. Button codes are known for 18 of them. Rockstar has not released an official list, so we mark this page as partly confirmed: every code below appears in at least two independent guides with the same inputs, and we will retest each one in the released game.

## How to enter cheats in GTA 6

1. Finish the main story. The Cheats menu is reported to appear under the Progress tab only after the credits.
2. Complete the unlock task for the cheat you want (listed in the tables below).
3. Save your game. Cheats turn saving off.
4. In free roam, press **Up on the D-pad** to open the phone.
5. Hold **L1 + R1** on PS5 or **LB + RB** on Xbox Series X|S.
6. Enter the code on the D-pad.

All codes use only the D-pad, so they are the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## What cheats switch off

- **Saving** is disabled while a cheat is active.
- **Trophies and achievements** cannot be earned in that session.
- Cheats are for story mode free roam. Nothing here applies to online play.
