**GTA 6 has 61 cheats, but none of them work until you finish the story.** They come in five groups: player, combat, vehicle spawns, world and screen filters. Each one also has its own unlock task, such as shooting-range medals, races or collectibles. Button codes are confirmed for 21 cheats so far; the rest are listed below with their unlock tasks, and we add each code as soon as two independent sources agree on it. Rockstar has not released an official list, so this page is marked partly confirmed.

## How to enter cheats in GTA 6

1. Finish the main story. Launch guides report 91 story missions; the Cheats section appears under the Progress tab after the credits.
2. Complete the unlock task for the cheat you want.
3. Save your game. Cheats turn saving off.
4. In free roam, press **Up on the D-pad** to open the phone.
5. Hold **L1 + R1** on PS5 or **LB + RB** on Xbox Series X|S. Guides differ on the wording: some say to hold L1 (LB) first and then press R1 (RB).
6. Enter the code on the D-pad.

All codes use only the D-pad, so they are the same on PS5 and Xbox.

## Code patterns

- Vehicle spawn codes start with **Left, Up, Right, Down**.
- The three ammo cheats share the opening **Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up**.
- The screen filter codes start with **Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left**.

## What cheats switch off

- **Saving** is disabled while a cheat is active.
- **Trophies and achievements** cannot be earned in that session.
- Cheats are for story mode free roam only.

## Tips for unlocking cheats faster

1. Many unlock tasks count progress you make during the story, so play shooting ranges, races and workouts as you go.
2. Do challenge groups in pairs: five challenges unlock one vehicle, all of them unlock a second.
3. Unlock tasks that need your partner (hours together, store robberies, activities) are easiest to finish in free roam after the credits.
