**No GTA 6 cheat codes are confirmed yet.** Any "full list" published before the game is out is invented. This page is where we will list real cheats, each one tested before it goes up.

## How cheats worked in earlier GTA games

| Game | How cheats were entered |
|---|---|
| GTA 3, Vice City, San Andreas | Button combinations on the controller |
| GTA 4 | Phone numbers dialled on the in-game phone |
| GTA 5 | Button combinations, plus phone numbers in later versions |

Typical cheats included health and armour refills, weapon sets, wanted level changes, vehicle spawns and weather changes.

## What to expect in GTA 6

- Cheats, if present, will almost certainly be **single-player only**.
- Using them will likely **block trophies and achievements** for that session, as in GTA 5.
- A **money cheat is unlikely**; GTA 5 did not have one.
