**This GTA 6 Bad Driver! walkthrough covers a short optional mission with a real choice: Andrea wants Felipe dealt with for running out on the Cartel Heist, and you decide whether he lives.** It is also missable, so the first thing to know is the deadline.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Bad Driver! (Chapter 4, optional) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) (PowerPyx) |
| Given by | Andrea |
| Starts at | The marked bar in Millport, Lummox County |
| Requirement | [Party Hard](/missions/gta-6-party-hard-walkthrough/) |
| Missable | Yes: gone for good once you start Power Play |
| Reward | $20,000, or the follow-up mission Collect Felipe's Gift |

Felipe ran out on [The Cartel Heist](/missions/gta-6-the-cartel-heist-walkthrough/). Andrea, who gave you [The Missing Link](/missions/gta-6-the-missing-link-walkthrough/), has now heard he is in Millport.

**Tip:** make a manual save before you walk into the bar. Missions cannot be replayed, so a save is the only way to see the other outcome. See [can you replay missions in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-replay-missions/).

## Objectives

1. Look for Felipe.
2. Chase after Felipe.
3. Deal with Felipe.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Find Felipe

Drive to the bar in Millport, go inside and walk toward the back. Felipe comes out of the restroom, sees you and runs.

### 2. Chase him

He goes out of the back of the bar toward the woods.

1. Shoot him in the legs or the back to slow him down.
2. Run up and hit him. He gives up.

**Tip:** aim low. A leg shot stops him without ending the mission's choice for you.

### 3. Deal with Felipe

Hold **L2** (LT on Xbox, per PowerPyx) to focus on him and ask what happened. You can keep questioning him, spare him through the dialogue options, or shoot him at any point in the conversation.

## Kill or spare Felipe?

| Choice | How | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Kill Felipe | Shoot him at any time during the conversation | $20,000 from Andrea |
| Spare Felipe | Choose to let him go in the dialogue | Felipe contacts you later and unlocks the optional mission Collect Felipe's Gift, where you collect the vehicle he left for you |

Both guides describe the outcomes the same way. Neither names the vehicle or says what it is worth, so we cannot tell you which reward is bigger. What is certain: $20,000 is paid straight away, while the spare path adds one more optional mission to play.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition. The real risk is missing the mission altogether: it has to be done after Party Hard and before you start Power Play, a later mission in the VCIA Takedown chain.
