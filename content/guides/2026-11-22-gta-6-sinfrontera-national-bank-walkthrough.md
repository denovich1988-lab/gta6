**This GTA 6 Sinfrontera National Bank walkthrough covers the scouting visit that opens the Cartel Heist: Lucia stands in the lobby and picks out four pieces of security while Jason waits beside her.** Nobody draws a weapon. PowerPyx subtitles it Scope Out the Bank.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Sinfrontera National Bank (Chapter 3) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/); start it as either lead |
| Given by | Raul |
| Starts at | Sinfrontera National Bank, Tequesta, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | [Credit Check](/missions/gta-6-credit-check-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | No cash |

The bank is where the cartel La Polilla Negra launders its money, which is why Raul wants it.

## Objectives

1. Enter the bank.
2. Follow Jason and wait at the table.
3. Check for security features.
4. Join Raul.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Enter the bank

Climb the main steps and go in through the front door.

### 2. Wait with Jason

You control Lucia. Follow Jason to the nearby table and interact with it. Lucia leans on it, which lets the two of them stand around without drawing attention.

### 3. Inspect four security features

Turn on Focus (L3 + R3 on PS5, per PowerPyx). It highlights what matters. Hold the inspect button on each one:

| # | What to inspect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Security cameras | Above the counter |
| 2 | The man in the pink suit | Standing behind Jason |
| 3 | Two security guards | By the elevators |
| 4 | Security panel | Between the two elevators |

**Tip:** if one seems to be missing, it is usually the man in the pink suit. He is behind Jason, away from the counter and the elevators where the other three are.

### 4. Join Raul

Once all four are checked, Raul calls you upstairs. Go up to him and Andrea and use the chair to sit down. The mission ends with a new task: find Nicole Park, the bank's security expert.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists fail conditions, and there is nothing here that either of them describes as failable. The only way to stall is to inspect without Focus on, since the highlights are what show you the four targets.
