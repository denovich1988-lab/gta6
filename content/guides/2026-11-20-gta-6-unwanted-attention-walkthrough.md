**This GTA 6 Unwanted Attention walkthrough covers the sixth mission, where Jason and Cal lift a car from a police impound and the game teaches you how heat works.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Go See Cal". Keep the car in one piece: it is the one thing that can fail the mission.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Unwanted Attention (mission 6, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Go See Cal |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Cal, by the water in the north of Key Lento |
| Requirement | Finish [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Police radio scanner and $150 |
| Next mission | [Pipework](/missions/gta-6-pipework-walkthrough/) |

The guides place Cal slightly differently: PowerPyx says he is on the beach, VGC says he is fishing from a bridge. Follow the marker.

## Objectives

1. Drive to the Hamlet impound with Cal.
2. Search the impound for the Phoenix.
3. Lose the cops.
4. Take the Phoenix to the Pay 'n' Spray.
5. Deliver the Phoenix to Brian's garage.
6. Collect the cash.
7. Adjust your hat and glasses.
8. Buy an outfit at the gun store.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive to Hamlet

Get in Cal's truck and drive to Hamlet, a small town to the north-west. On the way Cal installs a **police radio scanner** on your phone. It runs in the background and lets you hear police chatter during crimes and chases.

### 2. Get into the impound lot

You have to climb in. The guides describe two routes:

| Source | Way in |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx | Over the fence, then up the containers in the back corner and over a second fence |
| VGC | Through a gap at the top of the wall on the left side of the lot |

Guards patrol inside. PowerPyx warns that knocking one out brings a police patrol, so either stay out of sight or move fast once you are seen.

### 3. Find and unhook the Phoenix

The car is a red **Imponte Phoenix** at the back of the lot, attached to a tow truck. Get into the tow truck and press **R3** to release the cable, then get into the Phoenix straight away.

**Tip:** PowerPyx suggests scanning the parked cars with WAiNK while you are here. Scans made during the story count toward [100% completion](/missions/gta-6-100-completion/).

### 4. Get out and lose the cops

Drive to the gate. PowerPyx says to hold the car in front of it until it slides open; VGC describes going straight through it. Either way the police are alerted. If a guard grabs the door, tap **Square** (X on Xbox) to shake him off.

Now leave the flashing search area on the minimap. VGC says the wanted level stays low and the car is quick, so this is not hard. The real risk is damage: **VGC reports the mission fails if the car stops being driveable.**

### 5. Respray, then deliver

Once the police give up, Brian wants the car resprayed. Drive into the Pay 'n' Spray in Hamlet, pick a colour, then park in the marked garage nearby.

### 6. Collect the cash and change your look

Pick up the **$150**. PowerPyx places it in the back room of the garage and VGC in the back of the car, so check both. Then:

- Hold **L1** (LB) to open the item wheel and press **R1** (RB) to reach headwear and glasses. Toggle each one. The icon changes from red to white when police no longer know that look.
- Go to the gun store next door and buy any outfit. The $150 covers it.

The mission ends when you have changed clothes and your heat is clear.

## Where players fail

- **Wrecking the Phoenix.** Take corners gently. Losing the police slowly is fine; a dead car is not.
- **Fighting the guards.** It brings a patrol before you are in the car.
- **Skipping the outfit.** The mission does not complete until you have bought and changed clothes.

## The heat lesson in short

After an escape, red icons at the top right show what police remember: your clothing, your headwear and glasses, and your vehicle. Respray or swap the car, change the outfit, and they turn white. PowerPyx adds two habits worth keeping: store a spare outfit in your car trunk, and hold **Circle** (B) while driving to duck behind the wheel. The whole system is explained in [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
