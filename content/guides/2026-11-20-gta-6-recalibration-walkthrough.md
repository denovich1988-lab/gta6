**This GTA 6 Recalibration walkthrough covers the calm before the end of Chapter 1: a beer run, a fishing trip with Cal, a barbecue, and the checklist to finish before you start the next mission.** PowerPyx lists it as "Recalibration (Talk to Lucia)". Nothing here can go badly wrong, but what comes straight after it can cost you money and free roam.

## Objectives

1. Go to the liquor store and buy a six-pack for Cal.
2. Go to Cal's.
3. Find Cal and speak to him.
4. Drive the boat to the fishing spot.
5. Catch fish.
6. Return to Cal's and pull up at his dock.
7. Open the grill and grill the fish.
8. Follow Lucia and return to your house with her.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Beer for Cal

Lucia suggests cheering Cal up. Drive to the marked liquor store and take any six-pack from the fridge. PowerPyx notes you pay as you pick it up; there is no register.

### 2. Find Cal

At his house, Cal is on a lounger out back by the water. Talk to him, then take the boat to the fishing spot. PowerPyx puts the trip at about two minutes.

### 3. Catch the fish

| Step | PS5 | Xbox |
|---|---|---|
| Cast | L2 + R2 | LT + RT |
| Attract a fish | Rotate the right stick | Rotate the right stick |
| Hook it on a bite | R2 | RT |
| Fight it | Left stick opposite to the fish | Left stick opposite to the fish |
| Reel | Rotate the right stick quickly | Rotate the right stick quickly |

If the fish swims right, push left, and the other way round. Jason lands it automatically once it is close (VGC). You need three (PowerPyx). The basics are the same as in [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/).

### 4. Grill

Back at Cal's dock, use the grill beside him. Cook all three fish and flip each until both sides have changed colour.

**Tip:** flip early. VGC's only warning for the whole mission is not to let them burn.

### 5. Follow Lucia

Lucia says she is not hungry and walks off. Follow her to the front of the house, talk, and drive her home. A cutscene ends the mission.

## Before you start Tourist Trap

VGC's warning page and PowerPyx agree on the main point: the last three missions of Chapter 1 run back to back, and you cannot free roam again until part-way into Chapter 2. PowerPyx attaches the warning to Recalibration; VGC says the lock begins when you start Tourist Trap. Use this checklist either way:

1. **Make a manual save** in its own slot and do not overwrite it.
2. **Bank your cash.** PowerPyx says pocket cash carried into the finale is lost, and recommends at least $5,000 in Family Savings.
3. **Finish what you want to do as Jason.** PowerPyx says you cannot play as him for a long time; VGC adds that his Ultimate Edition vehicles are out of reach during that stretch.
4. **Do not worry about side content.** PowerPyx says Kenton's treasure dives, fish for Cal and the Kingfishers hideouts pause and come back later.

**Why it matters:** PowerPyx reports that the Key Lento house is lost for good after these missions. Anything you wanted to do there, do now.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail state. Burning the fish only means cooking again.
