**GTA 6 100 completion means finishing the story and 34 other requirements on an in-game checklist.** The checklist has 35 lines in three groups. Most lines ask for less than the full total, so you can skip the stunts, races or collectibles you like least. The game only shows the checklist after the story ends.

## What counts for 100% in GTA 6

Only the 35 lines below count. The "Needed" column is the target. The "Total" column is how many exist in the game, where guides give it.

## What you must do in full

Eleven lines have no slack. You need every one of these:

- 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 7 Payday Loan jobs and 8 Vaults.
- 33 Underwater Discoveries.
- 42 Shooting Range gold medals and 42 Drone Time Trials.
- 2 Unique Playlists, 4 Video Games and 15 of Bret's Target Decks.
- The main story.

Everything else leaves room. You can skip 97 stunts, 10 challenges, 10 races, 77 base model vehicles and 155 animals.

## Can you miss anything?

No. Two guides state that nothing is missable for 100% and that every line can be finished in free roam after the story. A few things to know:

1. Some Optional Tasks can be missed during story missions. It does not matter, because there are enough others to reach 100.
2. Kenton is not available for a while after Chapter 1. He returns later.
3. The video games on the TV cannot be played during part of Chapter 2.
4. Bret's hunting tasks are for Jason only.
5. A Gang Operation that you did not clear fully comes back, but it takes a long time.

## GTA 6 100% checklist reward

The reward is **A Long Road**, a gold trophy on PS5 and a 100G achievement on Xbox. It is the largest achievement in the game and is needed for the platinum. Guides do not agree on any other reward, so we do not list one.

The checklist also covers most other trophies on the way. It asks for 300 stunts where the stunt trophy needs 250, and 70 challenges where the challenge trophy needs 50. One exception: the meditation trophy needs all 18 spots, while the checklist needs 15. The full list is in our [GTA 6 trophy guide](/missions/gta-6-trophies/).

## A sensible order

1. Play the story and do optional tasks, robberies and stranger missions as you meet them.
2. Scan vehicles and study animals from the start. These two lines take the longest.
3. After the credits, open the checklist and see what is left.
4. Finish the full-count lines first, then pick the easiest of the rest.
