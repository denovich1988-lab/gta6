**GTA 6 uses a six-star wanted level with a new heat layer on top.** Stars show how hard the police respond. Heat shows what they know about you. This page is based on Rockstar's Extended Look and hands-on previews, and we will add tested details from the released game.

## Stars: how hard the police come

The meter runs from one to six stars. GTA 5 capped it at five, so the top end is higher. In the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia reach four stars in a chase, with police cars and a helicopter already involved. Rockstar has not listed which crimes give which star level.

## Heat: what the police know

Under the stars sits a row of red icons. Each one is a piece of information the police have:

| Icon | What police know | How to clear it |
|---|---|---|
| Face | What you look like | Wear a mask during crimes, or change your look |
| Clothes | What you are wearing | Change into a different outfit unseen |
| Vehicle | What you are driving | Switch to another vehicle |
| Companion | Who you are with | Split up or stay out of sight |

Witnesses who call the police and security cameras are how this information gets collected.

## Hot areas

After you escape, the place where police were searching stays marked for a while. Coming back too soon, while they can still recognise you, can restart the chase. Previews report that at six stars the search covers the whole map.

## How to lose the cops

1. Break line of sight.
2. Get out of the search area.
3. Change what they can identify: car first, then clothes.
4. Stay away from the hot area until it cools.
