When Rockstar revealed the price of GTA 6 in June 2026, it also said the game would be a **single-player experience at launch**.

## What that means

- On November 19, 2026 you get the story mode and the open world of Leonida.
- There is no online multiplayer mode on day one.
- You do not need a console subscription to play the story.

## When is GTA 6 online coming?

Rockstar has not announced a date, a name or features for a new online mode. GTA 5 added GTA Online two weeks after launch in 2013, but that tells us nothing certain about GTA 6.

## What about GTA Online?

The existing GTA Online, built on GTA 5, continues to run. Whether characters, money or purchases will move to a future GTA 6 online mode is **not confirmed**.

## Crossplay

With no multiplayer at launch, there is no crossplay at launch either. Details are in our [GTA 6 crossplay guide](/online/gta-6-crossplay/).
