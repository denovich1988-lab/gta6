**This GTA 6 Home Sweet Home walkthrough covers the second of Chapter 1's three closing missions: Lucia alone at Jason's house, two intruders, a fire and a police search of Key Lento to slip through.** It is short and mostly about stealth. It starts automatically after [Tourist Trap](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/), so there is nothing to prepare.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Home Sweet Home (mission 21 in [our list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/), Chapter 1) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | Jason's house, Key Lento |
| Requirement | Starts automatically after Tourist Trap |
| Reward | No cash; a unique jacket on one escape route (VGC) |
| Next mission | Discharge Time, the last mission of Chapter 1 |

You cannot free roam between these missions. See the warning in our [Tourist Trap walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/).

## Objectives

1. Grab Jason's stash and escape the house.
2. Escape the area.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Enter the house

Lucia has come back to collect some of Jason's things before the two leave town. Inside are two masked intruders who have wrecked the place, are after the stash and have started a fire.

### 2. Fight the intruders

Beat both of them.

**Tip:** keep away from the flames. VGC warns that catching fire drains health very quickly, so pull the fight toward the parts of the room that are not burning.

### 3. Get out

Leave by the front door at once. PowerPyx says there is not enough time to reach the cash before the house explodes. VGC mentions grabbing the photos by the door on the way out, and that the blast throws Lucia off the balcony.

### 4. Escape the search area

Lucia wakes up with police on the way and a search area on the map. The guides describe three ways out:

| Route | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Andy's car | Call Andy, then sneak through the neighbourhood to the car | VGC: Andy takes Lucia to the hospital and gives her a unique jacket |
| The main road | Crouch through the dark to the road through the middle of Key Lento, steal a car or keep walking (PowerPyx) | VGC says open roads are very hard, with a helicopter overhead |
| The water | Take a boat or jet ski from the docks by the houses | VGC: stealing one alerts the police further |

**Tip:** stay crouched and stay off open ground. VGC notes that trailers and buildings can be cut through, though most have to be broken into first.

### 5. The edge of the area

As you near the edge of the search area a cutscene takes over and leads straight into Discharge Time. VGC says the other routes play a slightly different scene but end at the same place, the hospital.

## Where players fail

- **Standing in the fire.** It is the quickest way to die in the house.
- **Lingering inside.** Leave as soon as the second intruder is down.
- **Being seen by the police.** PowerPyx lists being spotted as what ends the escape. For how detection works, see [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
