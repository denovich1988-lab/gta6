This page is the home of our **GTA 6 mission list**. Rockstar Games has not published a list of missions, so we add missions here in story order as each one is verified in at least two independent walkthroughs. The first 34 are listed below, each with a walkthrough.

## How the mission list works

- **Story order.** Missions are listed in the order the story unlocks them, grouped by the character or contact who gives them.
- **One page per mission.** Each mission gets its own walkthrough, linked from this list.
- **Verified first.** A mission appears only when its name, objectives and steps have been checked against the game.

## What each walkthrough includes

1. **Before you start:** where the mission begins, which protagonist you play, and anything worth buying first.
2. **Objectives:** the on-screen goals in order.
3. **Step-by-step route:** what to do at each stage, with the safest approach.
4. **Where players fail:** the usual causes of a mission failure and how to avoid them.
5. **Optional goals and rewards:** bonus objectives and what the mission unlocks.

## What is confirmed about the story

- You play as two protagonists, [Jason Duval](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia Caminos](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/).
- The story is set across the state of Leonida; see the [GTA 6 map guide](/map/gta-6-map/).
- Rockstar describes the launch game as a single-player experience; see [GTA 6 online](/online/gta-6-online/).

Both PowerPyx and VGC end Chapter 1 with Discharge Time. The two guides swap a few neighbours (Lab Fees and Just a Scratch, Buck Me and Clean Up After Donnie, Day Release and Clear Skies), and several early missions can be played in any order, so the numbers are a guide to the order rather than a fixed count. PowerPyx also lists optional and missable tasks between these missions; we cover the ones before Lucia's release in the [Coming Home walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/). Between Clear Skies and Swamped, PowerPyx counts a short scene at the house, Building a Life, as its own mission; VGC folds it into Clear Skies.

**Point of no return:** Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time play back to back, and free roam does not return until Chapter 2. Read the checklist in the [Recalibration walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-recalibration-walkthrough/) before you start [Tourist Trap](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/).
