**The best look at GTA 6 gameplay before launch is Rockstar's Extended Look**, a roughly 26-minute video captured on a base PlayStation 5. Here is what it confirmed.

## Police and the wanted level

- The wanted meter goes up to **six stars**.
- Red **heat icons** under the stars show what the police know about you: your face, your outfit and your vehicle.
- Witnesses and cameras feed that information to the police.

Full details: [GTA 6 wanted level explained](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

## Stealing cars

Car theft takes more than one button press. The footage shows Jason working on a car window in a short timed sequence before driving away, and the pair later burn a getaway car to cover their tracks.

## Two protagonists in the same scene

Jason and Lucia appear together in missions, for example with one driving while the other shoots. How switching works in free roam is something we will document from the game.

## Activities shown

| Activity | Seen in the Extended Look |
|---|---|
| Gym workouts | Yes |
| Jet skis, kayaks and swimming | Yes |
| Car racing on a track | Yes |
| Base jumping | Yes |
| Store and gas station robberies | Yes |

The racing footage shows wheel-to-wheel racing on a purpose-built circuit, a first for the series.

## Performance

Rockstar has said GTA 6 targets **30 frames per second** on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. See [does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS](/blog/gta-6-60-fps/).

## How long it is

One Rockstar studio head put his own playthrough at about 80 hours. See [how long GTA 6 is](/missions/gta-6-story-length/).
