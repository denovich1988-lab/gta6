**This GTA 6 Prologue walkthrough covers the first mission of the game from the opening cell to the final cutscene.** Prologue is short and works as a tutorial. You play as [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/), and the mission leads straight into [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/).

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Prologue (GTA 6 first mission) |
| Playable character | Lucia |
| Starts at | Hamlet Correctional, in Lucia's cell |
| Requirement | None, it begins when you start a new game |
| Reward | None |
| Next mission | Gone Fishing |

You cannot prepare for this mission. There is no free roam before it, and no choice here changes the story.

## Objectives

The exact wording differs a little between guides, but the order is the same:

1. Talk to your cellmates.
2. Go with the prison guard.
3. Threaten the teller and grab the cash.
4. Lose the cops.
5. Return to the coffee shop.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Talk to your cellmates

Lucia starts at the window of her cell. Turn around and face one of the cellmates. Hold L2/LT to focus on a person, then use the prompt to speak. This is the game's first lesson in how talking to people works, and you will use it for the whole story.

### 2. Go with the prison guard

After a short wait, a guard, CO Wilson, opens the cell and walks Lucia through the block. He stops in the common area for a phone call, then carries on. Follow him down the corridor to the counselor's office. You can look at other inmates on the way, but the mission only moves on when you reach the office.

The counselor's questions start a flashback to the crime that put Lucia in prison.

### 3. Rob the payday loan store

You take control again inside a payday loan store.

1. Hold L2/LT to aim at the teller.
2. Press Square/X to threaten him. He starts handing over cash.
3. Pick up the money from the counter.
4. A customer grabs Lucia from behind. Press the button shown on screen to shake him off.

### 4. Lose the cops

Police arrive as Lucia leaves, and you have to run. Jump and climb over obstacles with X/A. The flashing blue and red area on the minimap is the zone the police are searching. For how that system works in the open world, see [GTA 6 wanted level](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

Guides do not agree on this section:

| Source | What it says about the chase |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx, GTABase | You cannot escape. The game cuts back to the counselor's office by itself. |
| VGC | You can be caught, which ends the mission in cutscenes, or you can get away on foot or in a vehicle, which leads to the coffee shop section. |

In practice this does not matter for progress. Both versions end the mission the same way, and none of the guides lists a fail state here.

### 5. Return to the coffee shop

When the chase is over, follow the marker on the minimap back to the coffee shop. Lucia is arrested as she gets close. This is scripted and cannot be avoided. A cutscene explains why she went back, and the Prologue ends.

## Where players fail

- **Trying too hard to win the chase.** The arrest is part of the story. If the police catch you or the scene cuts away, nothing has gone wrong.
- **Missing the talk prompt.** You have to hold L2/LT on a cellmate first. Walking up to them is not enough.
- **Not picking up the money.** The teller puts the cash on the counter. The robbery does not move on until you take it.

## Optional goals and rewards

Guides list no optional goals and no reward for the Prologue. PowerPyx also reports that GTA 6 has no mission medals and no mission replay, so there is nothing to come back for.

After the final cutscene the story moves to Chapter 1 and to Jason. Continue with the [Gone Fishing walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/), or see the full [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
