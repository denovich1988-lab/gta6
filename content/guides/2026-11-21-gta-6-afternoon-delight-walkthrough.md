**This GTA 6 Afternoon Delight walkthrough covers the last job before the end of Chapter 2: Vicki wants her boss's safe emptied, and she pays $10,000 for it.** The mission has one real decision, and both branches lead to the same safe, the same shootout and the same money. PowerPyx subtitles it Rob Vicki's Boss.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Afternoon Delight (Chapter 2) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/); either can take the call |
| Given by | Vicki, by phone |
| Starts at | Delights, [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) |
| Requirement | Finish [Coffee Break](/missions/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/), then wait for Vicki's call |
| Reward | $10,000 via BuckMe the next day |

## Objectives

1. Find a way backstage.
2. Watch the show.
3. Rob the Delights safe.
4. Leave Delights.
5. Escape the bikers.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Get inside

Go to the club. If you arrive alone, your partner is called in. It is ladies' night, so the bouncer lets only Lucia through the front.

As Jason: wait until the two bouncers walk off, climb the fence behind the building and go through the open door. It leads to the male dancers' dressing room, where the lead dancer, Mario, wants Jason to join the act.

### 2. Help or Don't Help

| | Help | Don't Help |
|---|---|---|
| What happens | Jason dances on stage with Mario and Lucky while Lucia slips to the back office | The dancers quit, Frankie takes the stage, and Jason fires into the ceiling to start the robbery |
| The safe | Security catches Lucia; Jason rescues her | The two open it together |
| The chase | Bikers have less time to call reinforcements | Bikers chase more aggressively |
| Afterwards | You appear at a clothing store to buy Jason clothes, then drive to Vicki | You appear at the docks and hand over the bag in a scene |
| Length | Longer, with extra scenes and dialogue | Quicker |

Both guides say the choice does not change the result. Whichever you pick, you briefly play as Lucia and walk from the bar to the stage.

### 3. Open the safe

The safe is in the back office. The code is **2008**. Opening it alerts every guard in the building.

### 4. Shoot your way out

Fight to the exit and drive off with the bikers behind you. Both guides call the escape easy.

### 5. Deliver the bag

Hand the cash to Vicki at the docks, after the clothes stop if Jason danced.

## Where players fail

- **Trying the front door as Jason.** Use the fence at the back.
- **Standing in the open after the safe.** Every guard is alerted at once.
- **Expecting the money immediately.** It arrives through BuckMe the next day.
