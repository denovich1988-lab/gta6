Rockstar has introduced the main cast of GTA 6 on its official site. Here is everyone confirmed so far.

## Protagonists

| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| [**Jason Duval**](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) | A former soldier now working for drug runners in the Leonida Keys |
| [**Lucia Caminos**](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) | Fresh out of prison and determined to build a better life with Jason |

## Supporting characters

| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Cal Hampton** | Jason's friend, a paranoid conspiracy fan who is happiest at home |
| **Boobie Ike** | A Vice City local legend turned businessman, with interests in property, a strip club and a recording studio |
| **Dre'Quan Priest** | A music hustler and Boobie's partner in the label Only Raw Records |
| **Real Dimez** | Rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records |
| **Raul Bautista** | A veteran bank robber who is always looking for talent for the next score |
| **Brian Heder** | An old-school drug runner in the Keys who lets Jason live rent-free in return for help |

## How the two protagonists work

Rockstar has confirmed that you play as both Jason and Lucia. Exactly how and when you switch between them is something we will document from the game after launch, on the [mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/) and in each walkthrough.

## Voice actors

Rockstar traditionally does not announce the cast before release, and it has not done so for GTA 6. We will add the cast from the game's credits.
