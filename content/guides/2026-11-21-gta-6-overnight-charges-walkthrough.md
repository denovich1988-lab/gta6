**This GTA 6 Overnight Charges walkthrough covers Lucia's first meeting with Linda, the manager of the Starlet Motel.** The room has to be paid for, Lucia has almost no money, and the conversation sets up a debt you clear later in the chapter. It takes about a minute, but two things in the reception are worth doing before you walk out.

## Objectives

1. See Linda, the manager.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Walk to reception

Leave the motel room. The reception is on your left.

### 2. Talk to Linda

Linda is not friendly and wants the room paid for now. GTABase puts the bill at **$900**, the rent plus 10%, and says Lucia starts Chapter 2 with only $146.

### 3. The necklace

If Jason bought the necklace during Chapter 1 (the optional task Buy Lucia a Necklace, covered in the [Coming Home walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/)), Lucia hands it to Linda as collateral. That satisfies her for now and the mission ends.

PowerPyx describes a timed "Give the necklace" prompt and says letting it run out changes nothing: Lucia gives the necklace anyway.

**Why it matters:** the necklace is not lost. Both guides say it comes back when you pay the $900 in [Behind on Payments](/missions/gta-6-behind-on-payments-walkthrough/).

### 4. Look around before you leave

| In the reception | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Map on the wall | Uncovers the [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) area on your map | PowerPyx, GTABase |
| Flyer | Marks the optional task Check Out McKittrick Farm | GTABase |
| Missing posters | Adds Gellhorn's Missing Tourists to the map | GTABase |

## Where players fail

- **Trying to dodge the necklace prompt.** It cannot be avoided if you own the necklace.
- **Leaving without the wall map.** It is the quickest way to clear the fog around town.
- **Expecting to pay now.** You are not meant to have $900 yet.
