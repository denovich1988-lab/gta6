**GTA 6 guns are sold at 24 Ammu-Nation stores across Leonida and cost from $550 for a cheap revolver to $12,000 for the launcher.** This page lists every gun by class with its price and stats, picks the best weapon in each class, explains the new carry limit and lists every gun store. Guides count the total differently, from 34 standard guns to more than 50 when variants are included, and Rockstar has not published an official list, so the page is marked partly confirmed.

Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides. Prices and stats come from Mobalytics; Tracker.gg shows the same prices for every gun we cross-checked. Stats use the in-game 0–10 scale.

## How many guns you can carry

GTA 6 drops the bottomless pockets of GTA 5. Guides agree you carry **two sidearms and two long guns**, with one long gun on your back and one in your hands, plus one melee weapon and explosives. Guns that do not fit go in your car's trunk, and some Ammu-Nation stores have a weapon locker for the rest. The full controls are in [GTA 6 weapon wheel](/weapons/gta-6-weapon-wheel/).

## All 24 Ammu-Nation gun stores

Every store sells weapons, ammo, body armour and parachutes, and each one has at least one unique weapon variant. Expanded stores carry the most, including the machine gun, sniper rifles and launchers. Store names match on PowerPyx and GTA Wiki; tiers are as PowerPyx lists them.

| County | Stores |
|---|---|
| Mariana County | Barrage Gun Supply, Chuck's Tackle & Shot (Limited), Guns Aplenty! (Hunting), Richardson Hunting Supplies, Woodhatch (Expanded) |
| Vice-Dale County | Big Shots (Expanded), Hamlet Traders, Phil's Ammu-Nation (Expanded), Tequesta Guns & Ammo, Vice City Firearms, Martez Empeños, Jackson Firearms, Vice City Gun & Pawn, Pawn & Gun – Crosstown, Quickscope, Black Eagle (Expanded), Zenon's Pawn Shop, Iron & Oak (Expanded), Prestige Firearms (Expanded) |
| Leonard County | Tactical Outlet Guns |
| Kelly County | Pawn & Gun – Port Gellhorn, Gellhorn Outfitters, Biff's Gun Supply |
| Lummox County | Rusty Willis Jr. Hunting Supplies (Hunting) |

Stores without a tier in brackets are Standard. Lucia's optional Chapter 2 task Pick Up a Gun sends you to Gellhorn Outfitters in [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/), where you can buy a gun or take one from the weapon locker.
