**The way to make money in GTA 6 changes with each chapter: sell stolen cars from Chapter 2, rob jewelry stores from Chapter 3 and hit bank vaults late in Chapter 4.** This page lists only legitimate in-game methods that at least two launch guides agree on. Where guides give different payouts, we say so. We will add our own tested numbers later.

## First, protect what you earn

Cash in your pocket is lost when you are wasted or busted. Three guides agree on this.

The fix is a bank account. It opens during the Chapter 1 story mission "Buck Me", which also puts the BuckMe app on your phone. After that, use the app's ATM Finder and deposit your cash at the nearest ATM before you try anything risky.

## Early game: hold-ups, races and activities

At the start you have few options, and none of them pay much.

- **Store hold-ups.** Aim your weapon at the clerk and take the cash from the register. Stores, restaurants and gas stations all work. Expect a wanted level; see [how the wanted level works](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
- **Races.** Race events are spread across the map and pay cash for a win.
- **Gang operations.** These combat-heavy locations reward cash and valuables.
- **Story missions.** They pay a little, and they unlock every better method below.

No two guides give matching payouts for these, so we do not list numbers.

## Chapter 2: sell stolen cars to Moses

Moses is the first real source of income in GTA 6. He appears early in Chapter 2, gives you a slim jim for breaking into cars and buys what you bring him.

1. Steal an ordinary, older car. Moses does not take cars fitted with trackers.
2. Keep it in good shape. Damage lowers the price.
3. Drive it to his auto shop and sell it.
4. Wait a short while. He will not buy another car straight away.

PowerPyx, PC Gamer and Beebom all put a regular car at about $1,000 or a little more. It is not a fortune, but each run takes only a few minutes.

Two more activities are worth knowing in this part of the game. The Demolition Derby at Ambrosia Raceway pays out for a win, and Bret pays for animals you hunt. Guides agree that both exist, but only one gives figures.

## Chapter 3: jewelry stores and better cars

Chapter 3 is where the money starts to grow.

### Jewelry store robberies

Jobs for Yasmani introduce jewelry store robberies, which the game calls Smash 'n' Grabs. Beebom reports $10,000 to $20,000 per store depending on what you take. PowerPyx reports $10,000 to $25,000. Either way, one store is worth more than ten cars sold to Moses.

Yasmani's work also leads to the snap gun, a tool that opens locked doors without setting off the alarm. Use it to reach back rooms and safes.

### Randy and Pay 'n' Spray

Randy is a car fence who arrives in Chapter 3 and wants expensive, high-end cars. He pays far more than Moses, but guides do not agree on the amount: PC Gamer puts car sales at up to $20,000 depending on model and fence, while PowerPyx reports $30,000 or more for a car in perfect condition.

Pay 'n' Spray locations also buy vehicles. GamesRadar says they become available in Chapter 1, while PowerPyx lists selling tracked cars there as a Chapter 3 option, so treat the timing as unconfirmed. For the cars themselves, see our [GTA 6 cars guide](/vehicles/gta-6-cars/).

## Chapter 4 and after: bank vaults

Bank vault robberies are the top earner. They unlock late in Chapter 4. The method is to follow a security van to a bank and get into the vault while it is open.

- **Payout:** Gamers Heroes reports about $100,000 per bank. PowerPyx reports $100,000 to about $200,000.
- **Risk:** an instant 4-star wanted level, according to PowerPyx and Beebom.

Plan your escape before you go in. A fast vehicle and a second one to switch to make a big difference.

## Smaller extras

- **ATMs.** With a winch fitted to your vehicle at a Mod Shop, you can pull out an ATM. It is loud and draws police.
- **The map index.** The Robberies section of the map index lists targets such as Smash 'n' Grab locations, Payday Loan stores and banks, so you can plan a route.
