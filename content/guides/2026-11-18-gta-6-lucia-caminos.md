**Lucia Caminos** is one of the two playable protagonists of GTA 6, and the first female lead in a mainline 3D Grand Theft Auto game.

## Lucia's backstory

According to Rockstar's official character description:

- Her father taught her to fight from a young age.
- She grew up in **Liberty City** and has had to fight for her family.
- That fight landed her in **Leonida Penitentiary**; the first trailer opens with her there.
- She got out through "sheer luck" and has decided to make smarter moves from now on.

## Lucia and Jason

Lucia is in a relationship with [Jason Duval](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/). The second trailer shows Jason picking her up on her release. Rockstar frames the story around the two of them: a simple score goes wrong, and they find themselves on the wrong side of a criminal conspiracy across Leonida, forced to rely on each other.

## What Lucia wants

Rockstar describes her as more committed than ever to the good life her mother has dreamed of since their days in Liberty City. Rather than daydreaming, Lucia is ready to take control.
