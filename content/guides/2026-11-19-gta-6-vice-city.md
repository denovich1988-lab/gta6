**Vice City is the heart of GTA 6.** It is the biggest city in the state of Leonida and Rockstar's modern take on Miami: beaches, high-rises, nightlife and money.

## What is confirmed

- **It is the main urban area** of the map, with the densest streets and crowds.
- **It is set today**, with phones, social media and modern cars. The 1980s version belongs to the 2002 game.
- **Ocean Beach exists by name.** The pre-order bonus garage, Shore Court, is described as being near Ocean Beach.
- **Local businesses matter to the story.** Boobie Ike, a Vice City figure, has interests in property, a strip club and a recording studio, and co-runs the label Only Raw Records. See [GTA 6 characters](/characters/gta-6-characters/).

## Vice City and the rest of Leonida

Vice City is one of six named regions. The others are the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/), Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The full overview is in our [GTA 6 map guide](/map/gta-6-map/).

## Things to do in the city

Official footage shows gyms, shops you can rob, beach activities and street life. Radio matters here too: Vice City FM is among the reported stations. See [GTA 6 radio stations](/news/gta-6-radio-stations/).
