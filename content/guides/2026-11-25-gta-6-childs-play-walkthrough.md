**This GTA 6 Child's Play walkthrough covers the rescue of Rafa, Andrés's kidnapped son, from a quarry south of the airport.** It is one of the longer action missions in Chapter 4: a stealth section with Lucia, a defence with Jason, an escort with a child in your arms, and a chase by car and then by bike.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Child's Play (Chapter 4) |
| Playable characters | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) and [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/), switching during the mission |
| Given by | Andrés de León |
| Starts at | Megamundo HQ, Downtown, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | [Fresh Evidence](/missions/gta-6-fresh-evidence-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $30,000 |

Three things to know first:

- **The timer is not real.** Both guides say the map makes the mission look timed, but it is not. The game forces it once every other Jason and Lucia mission before [Power Play](/missions/gta-6-power-play-walkthrough/) is finished, and you can start it sooner without changing the story.
- **How it appears.** PowerPyx describes a short waiting step after [Adventures in Babysitting](/missions/gta-6-adventures-in-babysitting-walkthrough/): once Jason's missions for Dre'Quan are done, a phone call about the kidnapping comes in and Child's Play is marked on the map. PowerPyx says the caller is the lawyer Abe Gonzalez; GTABase says Andrés calls for help.
- **Finish optional jobs.** Power Play comes next in the story, and the optional [Bad Driver!](/missions/gta-6-bad-driver-walkthrough/) is no longer available once Power Play starts.

**Tip:** bring body armour and a full rifle. Most of the mission is open combat, and part of it is fought from a moving vehicle.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Andrés and drive to the quarry

Go to Megamundo HQ as either character. After the opening scene, drive to the quarry south of VCIA and continue on to the meeting point. Lucia gets out of the car before the deal.

### 2. Find Rafa with the binoculars (Lucia)

As Lucia, look at the building ahead. Rafa is in the room on the upper level, below a row of red lights. Before you move, sweep the area with the binoculars and tag the guards you can see.

### 3. Sneak in and free Rafa

Lucia has a silenced pistol for this part. Both guides give the same route:

1. Head right, along the buildings.
2. Climb the ladder at the metal structures, then take the stairs up.
3. Follow the walkway to the door on the left.
4. Deal with the last guard inside the room, then go to Rafa.

GTABase adds a detail for the walkway: let two guards walk past, then take out the two at the corner.

### 4. Protect Andrés (Jason)

Control moves to Jason at the exchange. Shoot the kidnappers in front of you, stay near the cars and watch the right side, where more enemies come out of a building. Switch to Lucia when the game asks you to.

### 5. Escape with Rafa (Lucia)

Lucia now carries Rafa, and both guides warn that her aim is worse while she does.

1. Take him to the ladder and let him climb down on his own. Cover him from above.
2. Pick him up again on the lower platform and carry him down.
3. At ground level Lucia is grabbed. Mash the prompt to break free, then shoot the man holding Rafa.
4. Rafa gets on a forklift and drives it forward. Follow him and keep the path clear.
5. When the car arrives, pick Rafa up and carry him to Gustavo at the door.

### 6. Chase Gustavo by car

Gustavo drives off with Rafa instead of bringing him back. Get in a car and go after him. Jason drives, so switch to Lucia to shoot. The chase ends when your car flips. Climb out and protect Andrés again. [How to switch characters in GTA 6](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/) has the controls if you have not used a mid-mission switch for a while.

### 7. Continue by bike

Take a bike nearby and pick the chase back up. Lucia rides on the back and can shoot.

| Threat | What the guides say |
|---|---|
| Large truck | PowerPyx: shooting as Lucia helps against the enemies on it |
| Enemy bikes | PowerPyx: they close in from both sides |
| Helicopter | Both: when it appears, shoot the pilot or the gunmen |

### 8. Pull Rafa out of the SUV

Andrés, in another vehicle, forces Gustavo off the road. The SUV stops on train tracks. Run to it, cut Rafa's seatbelt and pull him out.

### 9. Deal with Gustavo

Gustavo runs. Follow him up to the highway, where he tries to steal a car and fails. Deal with him to finish the mission. PowerPyx's story summary says he dies here.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists fail conditions, so we do not claim any. Two objectives are literally "Protect Andrés", so keep him covered in both of those fights. The spots that ask the most of you:

- **The stealth approach.** Tag the guards first and stay on the route to the right.
- **Carrying Rafa.** Lucia's aim drops, so clear what you can from the ladder while he climbs down.
- **The grab at ground level.** Break free first, then shoot the man who has Rafa.
- **The bike chase.** Deal with the helicopter as soon as it shows up.
