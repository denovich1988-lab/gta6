**This GTA 6 Island Dreams walkthrough covers the ninth mission, the game's diving tutorial: a guided wreck dive that doubles as a treasure hunt.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Work for Kenton". It introduces scuba gear, the metal detector and the harpoon gun, and it opens one of the early ways to earn money.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Island Dreams (mission 9, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Work for Kenton |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Kenton's shop, Island Dreams, by the beach in Key Lento |
| Requirement | Finish [Hey Arnold!](/missions/gta-6-hey-arnold-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $400 (PowerPyx) |
| Next mission | [Feng Shui](/missions/gta-6-feng-shui-walkthrough/) |

Kenton buys treasure from the waters around Leonida. He will lend Jason his boat and scuba gear, but first Jason has to take a bachelor party out to a wreck.

## Objectives

1. Go to the diving spot.
2. Change into the scuba gear.
3. Escort the bachelor party through the trench.
4. Search for treasure near the wreck.
5. Rescue Dobbs.
6. Escort the bachelor party to the boat.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Talk to Kenton and take the boat out

Speak to Kenton in his shop. After the cutscene, board the boat with the bachelor party and drive it to the marker out at sea.

### 2. Put on the scuba gear

Interact with the scuba gear at the back of the boat, then jump in.

| Action | Control | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dive | Hold R1 (RB) | PowerPyx |
| Surface | X | PowerPyx |
| Swim faster | L3 | VGC |
| Strafe | L2 | VGC |

Oxygen is the green bar at the top left. PowerPyx says you have plenty for this dive.

### 3. Lead the group through the trench

Swim down through the trench, which looks like an underwater tunnel. The divers follow you to the shipwreck.

### 4. Find the treasure

Open the weapon wheel and select the **Metal Detector**. Aim it with **L2** (LT) and sweep the pink search area, which is outside the wreck. The light turns from red to green near treasure and, per VGC, flashes faster the closer you get. When you are on the spot, a prompt appears to collect it.

PowerPyx shows three treasure spots here and says the next step starts when all three are found. VGC describes picking up a couple of gold doubloons before the rescue prompt appears, and notes that you can come back later for anything you miss.

### 5. Free Dobbs

One of the divers, Dobbs, gets stuck under a grate on the wreck. Swim to him, lift the grate, and head for the boat.

### 6. Stop the shark

A shark shows up on the way back. Equip the harpoon gun, listed as the **Arras Bullhead**, from the weapon wheel and shoot it.

- PowerPyx: two hits and it leaves.
- VGC: aim for the head and get close, because the shot drops sharply over distance.

Then surface. A cutscene ends the mission.

## Where players fail

- **Searching inside the wreck.** The treasure in this mission is outside it.
- **Firing the harpoon from too far away.** The shot falls short. Close the distance first.
- **Standing around on land in scuba gear.** VGC notes the gear, the metal detector and the harpoon gun unequip themselves if you spend too long out of the water.

## Does it matter if Dobbs dies?

No. PowerPyx reports that if you do not shoot the shark it takes Dobbs, and that the only difference is the dialogue in the closing cutscene. The bachelor party does not come back later in the story either way.
