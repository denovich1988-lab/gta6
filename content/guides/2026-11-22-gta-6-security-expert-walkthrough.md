**This GTA 6 Security Expert walkthrough covers a stealth pick-up: Jason and Lucia take the bank's security specialist, Nicole Park, out of a house where the cartel's men are guarding her.** Done properly, no shot is fired.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Security Expert (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) and [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Given by | Raul |
| Starts at | Waning Sands, Leonard County |
| Requirement | [Sinfrontera National Bank](/missions/gta-6-sinfrontera-national-bank-walkthrough/); starts automatically after it |
| Reward | No cash |

## Objectives

1. Find Nicole: drive to the house and park.
2. Sneak into the house.
3. Pick up Nicole and escape with her.
4. Escape the area.
5. Take Nicole to the planning room.

PowerPyx shows seven on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into four.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive past, then park

In the neighbourhood, head south to the marked house. Jason tells you to drive past slowly. Do that, then park nearby.

### 2. Get in through the back

Continue on foot behind Jason, who leads you around to the back. Wait there until a guard comes out of the back door and walks away. Then go inside while Jason keeps watch.

### 3. Find the bedroom

Cross the hallway to the bedrooms. The one you want is in the north-east corner, with its door closed. Open it.

### 4. Carry Nicole out

After the scene Nicole is unconscious. Pick her up and follow Jason out of the back door, along the side of the house, to the front.

**Tip:** both guides give the same advice for this stretch: stay right on Jason's heels and the guards do not notice you.

### 5. Trunk, then leave

At your car, open the trunk and put Nicole inside. Leave the area quickly to get clear of the guards.

### 6. Drive to the planning room

Drive to Raul's planning room in Stockyard. Arriving completes the mission. In the scene that follows, Raul tells Nicole he knows she has been stealing from La Polilla Negra, and she agrees to join the crew.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. Where the stealth breaks down:

- **Going in before the guard has left the back door.** Wait for him to walk off.
- **Falling behind Jason on the way out.** His route is the safe one.
- **Lingering after closing the trunk.** Drive away at once.
