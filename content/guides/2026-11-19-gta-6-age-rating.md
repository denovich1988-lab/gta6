**GTA 6 is rated for adults everywhere it is sold**: M for Mature 17+ in North America and 18 in Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand.

## GTA 6 age ratings by region

| Region | Rating |
|---|---|
| United States and Canada (ESRB) | M, Mature 17+ |
| Europe and the UK (PEGI) | 18 |
| Australia | R18+ |
| New Zealand | R18 |

## ESRB content descriptors

The ESRB lists seven descriptors for Grand Theft Auto VI:

- Blood and Gore
- Intense Violence
- Mature Humor
- Nudity
- Strong Language
- Strong Sexual Content
- Use of Drugs and Alcohol

It also carries an In-Game Purchases notice.

## What parents should know

- The rating describes the kind of content in the game, not how often it appears.
- The story is about career criminals. Violence, drugs and sexual content are part of it and cannot be switched off.
- PlayStation and Xbox parental controls can block games above a chosen rating and require approval for purchases.
- The game launches as a single-player experience, so there is no online chat with strangers at launch. See [GTA 6 online](/online/gta-6-online/).

## Is it stricter than GTA 5?

The age categories are the same as GTA 5's. The labels in Australia and New Zealand name sex scenes specifically, which GTA 5's did not.
