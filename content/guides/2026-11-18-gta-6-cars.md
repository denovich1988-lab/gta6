Rockstar has not published an official vehicle list, so names come from guides that agree with each other, and the page stays marked partly confirmed.

## How to steal, register and keep a car

Stealing a car does not make it yours. Guides agree that an unregistered car disappears once you leave it, die or quit, so registration is the real way to build a garage.

1. **Finish the Chapter 2 mission Moses.** Registration unlocks in [the Moses walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/), which also gives Lucia the Slim Jim for quiet break-ins, the Scooter Bros app that delivers your car, and a free registered vehicle.
2. **Scan before you steal.** Use the WAiNK phone app to check a car's tracker. Moses only takes cars with no tracker, and he refuses cars stolen right next to his garage.
3. **Respray and register.** Drive into Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn, pick a new colour, then choose Register instead of Sell.
4. **Keep a slot free.** You start with one garage slot, so register a car you will actually drive. More space comes with new properties as the story goes on.
5. **Use Pay 'n' Spray later.** After [Welcome to Vice City](/missions/gta-6-welcome-to-vice-city-walkthrough/), Moses texts that Pay 'n' Spray shops can buy and register cars with standard trackers. Cars with advanced trackers need the Key Cloner from Chapter 3.

**Tip:** a registered car used in a crime and left inside the police search area can be impounded. The Scooter Bros app returns it for a fee; the [Impounded Vehicle walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-impounded-vehicle-walkthrough/) covers it.

## Selling stolen cars

The cars you do not keep are income. Moses buys untracked cars from Chapter 2, Pay 'n' Spray buys cars with standard trackers later, and Randy buys high-end cars from Chapter 3. Cars in good condition sell for more. Payouts are in [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).
