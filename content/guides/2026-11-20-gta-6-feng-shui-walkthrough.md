**This GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough covers the tenth mission, a quiet evening at Jason's house with Cal before Lucia is released.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Meet Cal". There is nothing to fight and nothing to fail: you move furniture, play two games-within-the-game and wake up to a phone call.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Feng Shui (mission 10, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Meet Cal |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Jason's house in Key Lento |
| Requirement | Finish [Island Dreams](/missions/gta-6-island-dreams-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None listed |
| Next mission | [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/), after a set of optional errands |

PowerPyx reports that a text message arrives after Island Dreams and a marker then appears on the house. Walk in to begin.

## Objectives

1. Sit on the couch.
2. Move the TV, the coffee table, the couch and the chair.
3. Play Tenebrae Lux.
4. Play Street Trash III.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Get a beer and sit down

Take a beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal. He decides the place is not good enough for [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) after her time inside and that the room needs rearranging.

### 2. Move the furniture

Help Cal carry four things, in this order: the **TV**, the **coffee table**, the **couch** and the **chair**.

After the coffee table you can say it is fine or offer to move it again. PowerPyx tried both: if you move it again, Cal cannot make up his mind and has you shuffle it back and forth until it is where it started. Nothing changes.

### 3. Play Tenebrae Lux

Sit back down and the console comes on. The first game, Tenebrae Lux, is a slow black-and-white first-person game.

| Source | What it reports |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx | Head for the locked door on the right and try to climb the hill. The game cannot be finished and ends by itself after a while |
| VGC | It has small chessboard-style puzzles where you clear the board. Finish the first two and Jason asks to play something else |

Either way, a few minutes of play is enough. Jason gets bored and the game switches.

### 4. Play Street Trash III

The second game is an arcade shooter; VGC describes it as a twin-stick shooter. PowerPyx notes an on-screen timer and says it cannot be won. Play until you are killed, or let yourself die straight away if you want to move on.

### 5. The morning after

A cutscene shows the two of them up through the night. In the morning Jason gets the call that Lucia is about to be released, which sets up [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/).

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail state. The only time sink is trying to beat the two games:

- **Tenebrae Lux** has no ending to reach in this mission.
- **Street Trash III** always ends in a loss.

## What to do before the next mission

Feng Shui is the last mission before Lucia returns, and the game gives you a set of optional errands first: groceries, flowers, a necklace, a haircut and collecting her clothes. Both guides say these affect the relationship between the two leads, so they are worth doing. Our [Coming Home walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/) lists them with the one price the guides give.
