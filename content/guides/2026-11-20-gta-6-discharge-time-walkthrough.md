**This GTA 6 Discharge Time walkthrough covers the final mission of Chapter 1: Lucia finds Jason in Vice City Hospital, the people hunting them arrive, and the two shoot their way out in a wheelchair and an ambulance.** It starts the moment [Home Sweet Home](/missions/gta-6-home-sweet-home-walkthrough/) ends, so you cannot prepare for it separately.

## Objectives

1. Go to the second floor, find Jason and go to his room.
2. Defend yourself.
3. Crawl to the gun.
4. Defend the ambulance.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Find Jason

From the entrance, go left and up the stairs. PowerPyx notes you can ask the receptionist, then follow the pink line on the floor through the door on the left to room 203. Wake Jason.

### 2. The wheelchair

Lucia puts Jason in a wheelchair and pushes him toward the exit. You control Jason's aim while she steers. Enemies come out of doors along the whole route.

**Tip:** VGC's advice is to use focus mode as often as it is available, because the number of enemies makes it very easy to die here.

### 3. Alone in the ambulance bay

Lucia leaves to find a vehicle. PowerPyx describes the next beat: a man with a shotgun comes at Jason, Jason takes it from him and uses it, and each shot rolls the wheelchair backwards. Then nearby gas canisters are hit and Jason is thrown to the floor.

### 4. Crawl to the gun

Crawl forward to the weapon on the ground. Lucia arrives in an ambulance and ends the fight.

### 5. Defend the ambulance

You are now in the back of the ambulance as Jason, with Lucia driving and vehicles in pursuit.

| Target | Effect |
|---|---|
| The driver | Stops that car |
| The passenger firing at you | Reduces incoming damage |
| Engine or wheels | Disables the car; VGC says a car with shot wheels can swerve into the ones behind it |

After a few minutes a cutscene takes over.

### 6. The motel

You wake the next day in a motel. PowerPyx says the pair fled west to Kelly County. Chapter 2 begins.

## Where players fail

- **Saving focus for later.** There is no later; use it in the corridors.
- **Shooting bodywork in the chase.** Wheels, engines and drivers stop cars. Doors do not.
