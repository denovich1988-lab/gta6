**This GTA 6 Gone Fishing walkthrough covers the second mission of the game, the first one you play as Jason.** It starts right after the [Prologue](/missions/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/) and is a long tutorial: a boat, binoculars, stealth, a fist fight, a shootout and a seaplane.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Gone Fishing (mission 2, Chapter 1) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Mission giver | Cal |
| Starts | On a boat with Cal, straight after the Prologue |
| Requirement | Finish the Prologue |
| Missable | Cash in a trailer during the escape |
| Next mission | Gear Up |

There is nothing to prepare. The mission starts automatically and gives you everything you need.

## Objectives

Guides word these slightly differently. The order is the same:

1. Follow the plane and collect the packages.
2. Watch the deal in the swamp.
3. Search the camp for Donnie.
4. Find a way into the shack and untie Donnie.
5. Head to the plane.
6. Fly back to Brian's Marina.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Follow the plane and collect the packages

Jason and Cal wait on a boat. Donnie, the pilot, flies over and drops packages into the sea. Drive the boat after the plane. The packages are marked on the map. Slow down next to each one, or Cal cannot hook it out of the water.

### 2. Watch the deal

After the last package, follow the route on the minimap into the swamps. When the game asks, take out the binoculars: open the weapon wheel with L1/LB and pick them from the bottom-right slot. Watch the meeting on the docks. It goes wrong, and the Kingfishers gang takes Donnie.

### 3. Search the camp for Donnie

Bring the boat to the docks near the camp. Jason goes in alone.

1. Crouch with Circle/B.
2. Come up behind a guard and grab him with R2/RT.
3. Press Square/X to knock him out.
4. Work your way to the far end of the camp. Donnie is held in a shack there.

VGC suggests using the tall grass on the right side of the camp for cover. Going in loud is possible, but you have only one weapon at this point, so stealth is the easier way.

### 4. Get into the shack and untie Donnie

Wait for the guard near the shack to move, then go in through the front door and untie Donnie. A large Kingfisher walks in and you have to fight him with your fists. Press Square/X to hit. The X/A button is your defence: PowerPyx calls it a block, VGC calls it a dodge.

He drops Zombix, the game's healing item. Pick it up, then equip a gun.

### 5. Head to the plane

The camp is now alert, and you fight your way to the dock. You do not have to beat every enemy. Reaching the plane is enough.

On the way there is a trailer with cash inside. Getting it is optional. VGC warns that an enemy jumps out when you go in, so be ready.

### 6. Fly back to Brian's Marina

Get in the seaplane and fly to the marker. Land on the water and bring the plane in to the marina. A cutscene introduces Brian, Jason's boss.

The guides that cover the ending (VGC and GTABase) agree that Jason then goes back to his house in the Keys and gets a phone call from Lucia. VGC adds that you drop Cal at his home first. That call ends the mission.

## Where players fail

- **Going too fast past the packages.** Cal needs the boat almost stopped beside each one.
- **Standing up in the camp.** Stay crouched and always approach guards from behind.
- **Skipping the Zombix.** Pick it up after the fist fight. You will want the healing during the run to the plane.
- **Fighting everyone on the dock.** The goal is the plane, not a clean sweep.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Trailer cash | Optional and missable. In a trailer near the route to the plane. Guides do not give the amount. |
| Unlock | PowerPyx lists Jason's house in Key Lento as the reward. GTABase shows one unlock without naming it. |
| Medals | None. PowerPyx reports that GTA 6 has no mission medals and no mission replay. |

PowerPyx and GTABase also describe a reputation system that starts here: quiet takedowns and non-lethal play push Jason one way, and killing pushes him another. We will cover it in a separate guide once the details are tested.
