Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 pricing in June 2026, and pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026.

## GTA 6 editions compared

| | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
|---|---|---|
| **US price** | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| **Full game** | Yes | Yes |
| **Extra in-game items** | No | Yes |
| **Vintage Vice City Pack** (buy before Nov 20, 2026) | Yes | Yes |
| **One month of GTA+** (digital pre-order) | Yes | Yes |

## Which edition should you buy?

- **Standard** if you just want to play the story. Nothing in the Ultimate Edition is required to finish the game.
- **Ultimate** if you want the extra in-game items from the start. Check the store listing for the exact contents in your region.

## Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

Both editions include the Vintage Vice City Pack if you buy before November 20, 2026. It is themed around the original GTA: Vice City.

## Platforms

GTA 6 is available on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**. There is no PS4 or Xbox One version, and no PC version has been announced; see [GTA 6 on PC](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/).

## Online purchases

GTA 6 launches as a single-player game. See [GTA 6 online](/online/gta-6-online/) for what that means.
