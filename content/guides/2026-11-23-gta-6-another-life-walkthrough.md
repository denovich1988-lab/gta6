**This GTA 6 Another Life walkthrough covers Lucia's flashback mission, the second mission of Chapter 4.** It takes a few minutes, has no enemies and cannot really go wrong, but it matters for two reasons: it unlocks a new bank account tier and a story trophy. We keep the story details light here.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Another Life (Chapter 4) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | Automatically after Ocean Beach |
| Requirement | [Ocean Beach](/missions/gta-6-ocean-beach-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | BuckMe Black account ($2,000,000 account limit); Vice City, Baby! trophy |

## Objectives

1. Pick up your phone.
2. Check on Tomás.
3. Pick up Tomás.
4. Comfort Tomás.
5. Turn up the radio.
6. Dance with Tomás.

These are PowerPyx's six lines. GTABase groups them as A Flashback, Check on Tomas, Comfort Tomas and Dance With Tomas.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Pick up the phone

The mission opens in Lucia's old house, on a morning before her arrest. Pick up the phone from the table.

### 2. Check on Tomás

Walk to the room at the end of the hallway. Interact with the crib to pick Tomás up.

### 3. Comfort him

Carry him back to the living room and interact with the objects there to calm him down. When the prompt appears, turn up the radio on the counter.

### 4. Dance

Follow the prompts to dance around the living room; PowerPyx gives the button as Triangle on PS5 and Y on Xbox. A few more scenes play and the mission ends.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a way to fail, a choice or a missable item. If the mission seems stuck, look for the next interaction prompt in the living room.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| BuckMe Black account | $2,000,000 account limit |
| Trophy | Vice City, Baby! unlocks when the mission ends |
| 100% | GTABase marks the mission as required |

**Why the account matters:** BuckMe is the banking app you have used since [Buck Me](/missions/gta-6-buck-me-walkthrough/) in Chapter 1. For ways to fill it, see [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).

When the flashback ends you are back in the suite as Jason, and [Pool Day](/missions/gta-6-pool-day-walkthrough/) begins. The trophy is listed with the others in our [trophy guide](/missions/gta-6-trophies/). Full order: [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
