**This GTA 6 Ocean Beach walkthrough covers the opening mission of Chapter 4, in which Jason and Lucia leave the Bayside apartment and move into a suite at the Y Hotel.** It is a short, quiet mission with no enemies. Its job is to introduce your new home, a new contact called Valentina and the hotel's concierge, Santino.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Ocean Beach (Chapter 4, first mission) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | Bayside apartment, Vice City |
| Requirement | [The Cartel Heist](/missions/gta-6-the-cartel-heist-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Room 701 at the Y Hotel |

**Tip:** the pair clear out the Bayside apartment for good at the start of this mission. If there is anything you still want to do in Chapter 3 free roam from that base, do it before you walk back in.

## Objectives

1. Drive to the Y Hotel.
2. Enter the hotel.
3. Meet with Valentina.
4. Follow Valentina.
5. Take the elevator to the 7th floor.
6. Go to Room 701.
7. Explore your new suite with Lucia.
8. Check out the wardrobe.
9. Join Lucia on the balcony.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Leave the apartment

After the heist, return to the Bayside apartment whenever you are ready. Jason and Lucia pack up and leave.

### 2. Drive to the Y Hotel

The hotel is on the Vice Beach side of the city. At the entrance your car is taken for you, so get out and walk inside.

### 3. Meet Valentina

She is sitting on the right side of the lobby. The suite is part of her money-laundering operation. She introduces Santino, the concierge, and promises a meeting with Abraham Gonzalez, a well-known Vice City lawyer who may be able to help the two of you start again. Follow her to the elevator.

### 4. Find Room 701

Ride the elevator to the 7th floor. Turn left and walk to the far end of the hallway.

### 5. Explore the suite

Go in and look around while Lucia opens each room. When she opens the bedroom, interact with the wardrobe. Both guides note that new outfits are available from this chapter on; for how wardrobes work, see [how to change clothes in GTA 6](/characters/gta-6-change-clothes-and-hair/).

### 6. Celebrate on the balcony

Keep following Lucia. When she steps onto the balcony she mentions champagne. Go back inside, take the two glasses from the counter and carry one out to her. That completes the mission.

## Where players fail

There is nothing to fail. Neither guide lists fail conditions, choices or missable items in this mission.
