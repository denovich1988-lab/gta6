**This GTA 6 Behind on Payments walkthrough is about money: Lucia owes the Starlet Motel $900 and has to find it.** The debt comes from [Overnight Charges](/missions/gta-6-overnight-charges-walkthrough/), where Linda took Lucia's necklace as security. There is no combat and no timer in either guide, so the only question is the fastest way to raise the cash.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Behind on Payments (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Linda |
| Location | Starlet Motel reception, Kelly County |
| Requirement | Finish Overnight Charges and have $900 |
| Reward | None listed; the necklace is returned |

## Objectives

1. Get $900.
2. Pay Linda for the motel room.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Raise $900

Both guides recommend doing other missions first rather than grinding.

| Method | What you get | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Daily Driver](/missions/gta-6-daily-driver-walkthrough/) | $1,250, plus $500 for good condition | Covers the debt in one job; needs [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/) first |
| Selling cars to Moses | Varies by car | Untracked cars only; the garage closes for a minute or two between sales |
| Mugging people | Small amounts | Named by both guides as a way to pay straight away |
| Store registers | Small amounts | Can bring a wanted level |

**Tip:** Lucia begins the chapter with $146 (GTABase) and spends $50 on pills in [Girl's Trip](/missions/gta-6-girls-trip-walkthrough/), which Vicki refunds. Hold off on the haircut and clothes errands if you want to clear the debt sooner; nothing in the guides says Linda's bill grows while you wait.

### 2. Pay at reception

With $900 in hand, go to the Starlet Motel reception and talk to Linda. The mission ends when the payment goes through.

### 3. Collect the necklace

If Linda was holding the necklace, she gives it back.

## Where players fail

- **Arriving with less than $900.** Both guides say to talk to Linda only once you have the full amount.
- **Losing cash on the way.** Carried money is at risk if you are wasted or busted; see [how the wanted level works](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).
- **Robbing stores next to the motel.** A wanted level outside reception slows everything down.
