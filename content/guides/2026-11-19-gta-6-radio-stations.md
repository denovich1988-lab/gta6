**Rockstar has officially detailed six GTA VI radio stations**, each with well-known hosts. More stations are in the game; the names reported so far are listed further down.

## The six stations Rockstar has detailed

| Station | Music | Hosts |
|---|---|---|
| Flash FM | Pop | Robyn and Alex Consani |
| Cocoteo FM | Modern Latin | Bad Bunny (as El Martillo) and RaiNao (as La Gata) |
| Back Country Radio | Country | Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey (as Delta Dawne) |
| AfroBank FM | Amapiano and African classics | Burna Boy and Palms Trax |
| The Chamber 106.6 | Heavy metal | Kerry King and Tom Araya of Slayer |
| Dirty South Classics | Southern rap | Trick Daddy and Trina |

Flash FM is a returning name: it was the pop station in 2002's GTA: Vice City.

## Other stations reported in the game

These names appear in more than one launch-week report. Rockstar has not published details for them yet, so treat the genres as reported:

- Vice City FM (80s pop and new wave)
- V-Rock (rock)
- Emotion 98.3 (soft rock)
- Radio On-U (reggae)
- Symphony FM (classical)
- Kaleidoscope FM (global pop)
- La Ola (classic Latin)
- Stockyard (indie and dance)
- The Wipe (talk)

One launch-day list counts 23 stations in total. We will publish the complete list, with every track, once we have checked it in the game.

## New ways to listen

- **On foot.** Jason and Lucia can listen through their phones with earbuds.
- **Podcasts.** Rockstar has confirmed on-demand podcasts alongside the stations.
- **In vehicles**, as in every GTA.
