**This GTA 6 New Look walkthrough is short because the mission is: Lucia needs a different hairstyle, and one purchase ends it.** After the escape that closes Chapter 1 she has to look less like herself. The mission pairs with [How Do I Look?](/missions/gta-6-how-do-i-look-walkthrough/), which does the same for her clothes.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | New Look (Chapter 2) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Lucia (self-started) |
| Location | Pearl House Hair Salon, [Port Gellhorn](/map/port-gellhorn-gta-6/) |
| Requirement | Finish [Girl's Trip](/missions/gta-6-girls-trip-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None listed |

## Objectives

1. Speak to the stylist and change your hair.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Go to the salon

Follow the map marker to the Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn.

### 2. Talk to the stylist

Start the conversation to open the list of styles.

### 3. Buy any hairstyle

Pick whichever you like and pay. The mission completes on the purchase.

**Tip:** you keep whatever you choose, so pick something you want to look at for the rest of the chapter. How salons, barbers and wardrobes work in general is in [how to change clothes and hair in GTA 6](/characters/gta-6-change-clothes-and-hair/).

## If you are short of cash

Lucia starts the chapter nearly broke. PowerPyx and GTABase both give the same fallback: mug someone on the street or empty the register of a different store. Either can bring a wanted level; see [how the wanted level works](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

A cleaner option once you have done [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/): sell him an untracked stolen car.

## Where players fail

There is no fail state in either guide. The only way to get stuck is not having enough money for the cheapest style.
