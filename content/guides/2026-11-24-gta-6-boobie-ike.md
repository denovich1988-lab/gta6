**GTA 6's Boobie Ike is the Vice City businessman who gives Jason and Lucia their first foothold in the city, and then makes them work for it.** Rockstar introduced him before launch; the released game shows what he actually does in the story. This page covers who he is, where to find him, each of his missions and the unlocks that make them worth doing early.

Character facts come from Rockstar's official description; mission facts come from walkthroughs by two guides that agree. There are light story details for Chapter 3 below.

## Who Boobie Ike is

Rockstar's character page describes Boobie as a local Vice City legend who has turned his reputation into legitimate-looking business: real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. He is partnered with [Dre'Quan Priest](/characters/gta-6-characters/), a music hustler, in the label Only Raw Records, whose signing is the rap duo Real Dimez, Bae-Luxe and Roxy.

In the game, outlets identify his club as the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown. It is the hub for his side of the story: Dre'Quan records upstairs, and Dealer's Choice and Boobie Time both start there. Our [strip club guide](/blog/gta-6-strip-club/) covers the venue itself.

## How you meet him

Chapter 3 opens with Welcome to Vice City. Jason calls Dre'Quan on the drive in and is told to come to the Jack of Hearts. After a walk through the club, Dre'Quan takes the pair upstairs to his boss. Boobie lets them stay in an apartment he has nearby, in Bayside.

**Why it matters:** the apartment is your Chapter 3 home, with your wardrobe and everything else a safehouse provides, but it is a favor with a price. See [GTA 6 safehouses](/map/gta-6-safehouses/).

## Boobie Ike's missions

| Mission | Who plays | Starts at | What happens | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dealer's Choice](/missions/gta-6-dealers-choice-walkthrough/) | Jason | Jack of Hearts | A pickup and sale with Dre'Quan as backup; Boobie calls it a favor for the apartment | Disputed between guides |
| [Boobie Time](/missions/gta-6-boobie-time-walkthrough/) | Jason | Jack of Hearts | Drive Boobie on two pickups, then chase bag thieves down the river on a Seashark | Yasmani unlocked |
| [Bricked Up](/missions/gta-6-bricked-up-walkthrough/) | Both | Bayside apartment | Collect cocaine bricks from a gang called San4San at Venture Apartments; a police raid follows | $3,500 if you carry the red bag out |

### Dealer's Choice

Started through Dre'Quan rather than Boobie himself, this is Jason's first job in the city. The Cut or Pure choice changes the scene at the sale, but both guides agree it has no effect on later missions.

### Boobie Time

Boobie waits in his car in front of the club and you drive. After two stops and a visit to a club by the river, thieves take the cash bag, and Jason follows on a Seashark. Both guides say the thieves end up circling in a dead end, so there is no need to hit them at speed. The mission needs Part 1 of [Dubious Provenance](/missions/gta-6-dubious-provenance-walkthrough/) first.

**Tip:** do this one as early as you can. It ends at a smoke shop where Boobie introduces Yasmani.

### Bricked Up

Late in Chapter 3, Boobie reminds the pair that living in his apartment means working for him. The pickup turns into a fight down through the building, and you begin it with pistols only, so bring body armor. The $3,500 is paid only if you take the red bag holding Boobie's bricks before you step into the hallway.

## What Boobie unlocks

- **The Bayside apartment,** your home for all of Chapter 3.
- **Yasmani.** Both guides say he pays more for jewelry and marked bills than the other drop-offs. He follows up with [Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs](/missions/gta-6-yasmanis-jewelry-jobs-walkthrough/): one item from each of three stores for $20,000.
- **Caught Slipping.** Finishing Boobie Time and First Impressions triggers [the burglary at the apartment](/missions/gta-6-caught-slipping-walkthrough/) the next time you go home.

**Why it matters:** Yasmani is the reason Boobie's missions pay off even when their own rewards are small. If you are holding jewelry from Chapter 2, sell it to him; see [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).

## Best order for his missions

1. Welcome to Vice City (automatic).
2. Dealer's Choice, which opens most of Chapter 3.
3. Part 1 of Dubious Provenance, then Boobie Time straight away for Yasmani.
4. Save before you return home after Boobie Time and First Impressions, because Caught Slipping starts at the door.
5. Bricked Up when Boobie gets in touch near the end of the chapter, with body armor.

## After Chapter 3

When [The Cartel Heist](/missions/gta-6-the-cartel-heist-walkthrough/) is done, Jason and Lucia move out of Boobie's apartment and into the Y Hotel in [Ocean Beach](/missions/gta-6-ocean-beach-walkthrough/). The label's story continues without him as mission giver: Dre'Quan gives [Photo/Shoot](/missions/gta-6-photo-shoot-walkthrough/) in Chapter 4.
