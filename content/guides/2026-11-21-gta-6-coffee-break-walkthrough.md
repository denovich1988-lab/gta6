**This GTA 6 Coffee Break walkthrough is short, but the mission is the turning point of Chapter 2: it gives you both leads at once.** After a $2 coffee, Jason and Lucia agree to raise money for a move to Vice City, and the game unlocks character switching and a set of jobs to fund it.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Coffee Break (Chapter 2) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | Outside the Starlet Motel, Kelly County |
| Requirement | Finish [Breakfast Run](/missions/gta-6-breakfast-run-walkthrough/); it follows without a break |
| Cost | $2 |
| Reward | Character switching and the partner menu |

## Objectives

1. Go to the motel kitchen with Lucia.
2. Buy Lucia a coffee.
3. Go to your car.
4. Hold Right on the D-pad to interact with your partner.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Walk to the kitchen

The kitchen is straight ahead, in the southern corner of the motel grounds. On the way, a man called Wyman recommends the Mud Club for dirt racing. That is the lead for [Thrillbillies](/missions/gta-6-thrillbillies-walkthrough/).

### 2. Buy the coffee

Lucia sits at the table. Use the coffee machine and pay **$2**. In the scene that follows, the two decide to work towards something bigger so they can move to Vice City.

### 3. Go back to the car

The follow-up jobs are now marked on the map.

### 4. Open the partner menu

| Action | PS5 | Xbox |
|---|---|---|
| Open the partner menu | Hold Right on the D-pad | Hold Right on the D-pad |
| Switch character | R1 | RB |

From the same menu you can give your partner commands when they are with you. Details and limits are in [how to switch characters in GTA 6](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/).

## The four jobs that open

| Job | What it is | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Thrillbillies | A dirt bike race at the Thrillbilly Mud Club | [Thrillbillies walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-thrillbillies-walkthrough/) |
| Hit Avery Pawn | Rob the pawn shop in Port Gellhorn | [Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-hit-avery-pawn-walkthrough/) |
| Hit Kingpin Bowling | Raid a gang-held bowling alley | [Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-hit-kingpin-bowling-walkthrough/) |
| Impounded Vehicle | Take Lucia's car back from the police | [Impounded Vehicle walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-impounded-vehicle-walkthrough/) |

Both guides say the order is up to you.

**Tip:** start with Impounded Vehicle if you want Lucia's car for the others, or with Thrillbillies for a low-risk payout of up to $2,500.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail state. The only thing people miss is the last objective: the mission does not move on until you hold Right on the D-pad by the car.
