**This GTA 6 Boobie Time walkthrough covers a driving job for Boobie Ike that turns into a river chase, and ends by introducing the best fence for jewelry in the game.** The unlock is the reason to do it early. PowerPyx subtitles it Talk to Boobie.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Boobie Time (Chapter 3) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) only |
| Given by | Boobie Ike |
| Starts at | Jack of Hearts, Crosstown, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | Part 1 of [Dubious Provenance](/missions/gta-6-dubious-provenance-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Yasmani unlocked as a goods drop-off |

## Objectives

1. Get in Boobie's car.
2. Go to the pick-up location.
3. Go to the club.
4. Retrieve the cash bag.
5. Return to Boobie.
6. Get in your car.
7. Go to the smoke shop.
8. Enter the smoke shop.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Drive Boobie

Follow Boobie out of the club and take the driver's seat of his car.

### 2. Two pickups

- **First stop:** a few blocks south. Wait while Boobie talks to the men in the parking lot.
- **Second stop:** a short drive south-west. Pull up, press **L3** to honk and wait while he talks to the man on the stairs.

### 3. The club by the river

Drive south to the city centre and stop beside the building near the river.

### 4. Chase the bag

After the scene inside, thieves take the cash bag from the car. Jason follows on a Seashark automatically. Chase them down the river; you can shoot as you ride.

**Tip:** you do not need to hit them at speed. Both guides say the thieves end up in a dead end and circle while shooting, which makes them much easier targets.

When they are down, ride over the bag and press **Triangle** (Y on Xbox) to pick it up.

### 5. Back to Boobie

Ride back up the river, get off the Seashark and return to Boobie's car.

### 6. Meet Yasmani

Drive a short way across the river to the smoke shop and go inside with Boobie. He introduces Yasmani, and the mission ends.

## Where players fail

- **Wasting ammo during the chase.** Wait for the dead end.
- **Missing the bag.** It floats where the thieves went down; you have to ride over it and press the button.
