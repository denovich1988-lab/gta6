**This GTA 6 Fully Catered walkthrough covers Raul's request for an airport catering truck, the last piece of equipment the crew needs for the airport job.** The truck yard is off-limits, and any worker who sees you will report you for trespassing. There is a route over the roof that avoids all of them.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Fully Catered (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Raul |
| Starts at | The marked spot at the airport (VCIA) |
| Requirement | [Power Play](/missions/gta-6-power-play-walkthrough/) |
| You need | The Slim Jim |
| Reward | None listed |

**When it appears:** both guides say the job shows up a little after [Close Surveillance](/missions/gta-6-close-surveillance-walkthrough/) and [Delivery Method](/missions/gta-6-delivery-method-walkthrough/), or straight after them if you do those first.

## Objectives

1. Steal a Plane & Simple catering truck.
2. Deliver the catering truck to the drop-off.
3. Pick up the cash and put it in the bag.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Reach the trucks over the roof

1. Go to the marked area at the airport, but do not walk in through the yard.
2. Circle around the **right side** of the building where the trucks are parked, staying out of sight.
3. Climb the lower section of the building to get onto the roof.
4. Walk across the roof to the row of catering trucks.
5. Drop down on the **left side of the middle truck**. Both guides say workers are not normally standing there.

### 2. Break in

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Tool | Slim Jim |
| Target | The middle truck |
| Pace | Do it quickly, before a worker walks round |

### 3. Deliver the truck

Drive out of the airport and take the truck to the marked drop-off in Rockridge.

### 4. Bag the fake cash

The mission ends back in the planning room. While Raul talks, pick up bundles of fake cash from the pile and interact with the bags to pack them. Repeat a few times and the mission completes.

**Why it matters:** GTABase notes that this fake cash is what the crew swaps for the real money during the [VCIA Takedown](/missions/gta-6-vcia-takedown-walkthrough/).

## Where players fail

Neither guide gives a hard fail condition. The risk is the trespassing report:

- **Walking through the yard.** Workers there will spot you.
- **Dropping on the wrong side.** The left side of the middle truck is the blind spot.
- **Fumbling the lock.** The longer you stand at the door, the more likely someone comes around.

## What comes next: Heist Plans in Place

PowerPyx lists a short entry after this mission that GTABase does not count.

| Stage | What you see |
|---|---|
| Before all setup is done | "Heist Planning" on the map, with no marker of its own |
| After the setup missions | It becomes Heist Plans in Place: wait for a call |
| The trigger | Finish Dre'Quan's missions through [Headliners](/missions/gta-6-headliners-walkthrough/), then play as Lucia until Nate phones the next morning |

Raul sends a message the morning after Fully Catered. If the call from Nate does not come, the usual reason is that Headliners is still open.
