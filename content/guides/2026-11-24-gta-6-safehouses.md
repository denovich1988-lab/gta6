**GTA 6 safehouses work differently from earlier games: instead of buying properties, Jason and Lucia are given one home per chapter, and each one is taken away when the chapter ends.** This page lists every home confirmed so far, in story order, with where it is, how you get it, what it is for and the exact mission that closes it.

Everything here comes from walkthroughs by at least two guides that agree. Chapter 5 is not included yet.

## All safehouses at a glance

| Chapter | Safehouse | Where | You get it in | You lose it in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jason's house | Key Lento, Leonida Keys | The prologue | [Home Sweet Home](/missions/gta-6-home-sweet-home-walkthrough/) |
| 2 | Starlet Motel room | Kelly County | [Discharge Time](/missions/gta-6-discharge-time-walkthrough/) | [Checking Out](/missions/gta-6-checking-out-walkthrough/) |
| 3 | Bayside apartment | Bayside, Vice City | [Welcome to Vice City](/missions/gta-6-welcome-to-vice-city-walkthrough/) | [Ocean Beach](/missions/gta-6-ocean-beach-walkthrough/) |
| 4 | Room 701, Y Hotel | Vice Beach side of Vice City | Ocean Beach | Not covered yet |

There is also one place that is storage rather than a home: the unit at Big House Storage, from [Caught Slipping](/missions/gta-6-caught-slipping-walkthrough/).

## What a safehouse does

- **Wardrobe.** Everything you buy or pick up is kept in the wardrobe at your current safehouse, and that is where you build outfits. See [how to change clothes in GTA 6](/characters/gta-6-change-clothes-and-hair/).
- **Bed.** At Jason's house, sleeping skips the night and restores health and stamina, according to KeenGamer.
- **Fast travel target.** The RydeMe app can take you to your current safehouse; see [GTA 6 fast travel](/map/gta-6-fast-travel/).
- **Mission starts.** Several missions begin at home, including chapter finales.

What it does **not** do is save your game. Saving is in the pause menu under Game and works anywhere in free roam; see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

## Chapter 1: Jason's house, Key Lento

Jason lives rent-free on a property owned by Brian Heder in the Keys, in return for helping with shakedowns. It is the only home with its own side content: four repair jobs, covered in [Jason's house projects](/missions/gta-6-jasons-house-projects/), which close when Lucia is released. Lucia moves in after [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/).

**How you lose it:** in Home Sweet Home, Lucia finds intruders in the house, a fire starts and the house explodes. PowerPyx says there is not enough time to reach the cash stash before it goes.

**Tip:** finish the house projects and anything else you want in the Keys before Tourist Trap, because the last three missions of Chapter 1 run without a break.

## Chapter 2: the Starlet Motel, Kelly County

Chapter 1 ends at a motel, and Chapter 2 opens there with [What's in the Bag?](/missions/gta-6-whats-in-the-bag-walkthrough/). Jason is stuck in bed at first, so Lucia stocks the room in [Just Moved In](/missions/gta-6-just-moved-in-walkthrough/). The motel's coffee room is also where the chapter's money goal, [Prepare for Vice City](/missions/gta-6-prepare-for-vice-city-walkthrough/), begins.

**How you lose it:** Checking Out, the chapter finale, starts at the bathroom door in your room once you have $15,000 and every other Chapter 2 main mission done. PowerPyx and GTABase both say starting it removes the motel as your home for good.

**Tip:** do not touch the bathroom door until you have finished the Chapter 2 side content you care about.

## Chapter 3: the Bayside apartment, Vice City

In Welcome to Vice City, Boobie Ike lets the couple stay in an apartment he owns near the Jack of Hearts. To get in, park in the covered spot, walk round to the front, climb to the second floor and take the first door in front of you. Both guides note that the door opposite the parking spots works as a shortcut afterwards.

The apartment comes with strings. Boobie treats the stay as a debt, which is how [Dealer's Choice](/missions/gta-6-dealers-choice-walkthrough/) and [Bricked Up](/missions/gta-6-bricked-up-walkthrough/) start; more on him in [Boobie Ike](/characters/gta-6-boobie-ike/). It is also burgled: Caught Slipping triggers at the apartment door after Boobie Time and First Impressions.

**How you lose it:** after [The Cartel Heist](/missions/gta-6-the-cartel-heist-walkthrough/), returning to the apartment starts Ocean Beach, and the pair pack up and leave for good.

## Big House Storage: not a home, but yours

At the end of Caught Slipping, Jason buys a unit at Big House Storage, south of the apartment, as a safer place to keep their things after the burglary. You return to a storage locker to collect that drive at the start of [Heavy Lifting](/missions/gta-6-heavy-lifting-walkthrough/) in Chapter 4.

## Chapter 4: Room 701 at the Y Hotel

Valentina puts the couple in a suite at the Y Hotel as part of her money-laundering operation. Ride the elevator to the 7th floor, turn left and walk to the far end of the hallway. Both guides say new outfits become available through the suite's wardrobe from this chapter on.

The hotel adds two conveniences:

- **Santino, the concierge,** gives the mission [Company Cars](/missions/gta-6-company-cars-walkthrough/), which hands each lead a free car.
- **The hotel's car service** works as a free taxi from the front of the building, per both guides.

**Why it matters:** from [Making Gainz](/missions/gta-6-making-gainz-walkthrough/) until the VCIA Takedown, this room is the only place Jason and Lucia can be together, so it becomes the base for anything that needs both of them.

## Checklist before every chapter finale

1. Make a manual save in a slot you will keep.
2. Finish missable and optional missions for that chapter; the [mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/) marks the points of no return.
3. Finish anything tied to the home itself, such as Jason's house projects in Chapter 1.
4. Bank your cash before the finale, since carried cash is lost when you are wasted or busted.

## What is not confirmed

- **Chapter 5.** We have not yet checked two walkthroughs for the final chapter, so we do not say whether the Y Hotel stays your home.
- **Buying property.** No two guides we have read confirm purchasable safehouses in story mode.
- **Garages and vehicle storage at each home.** Details differ between guides and will be added when they agree.
