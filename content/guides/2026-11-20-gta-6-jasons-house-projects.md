**Jason's house in GTA 6 has four repair projects, and all four have to be finished before Lucia is released from prison.** They take a few minutes, cost nothing that either guide mentions, and decide how the relationship between the two leads starts. Miss the window and they are gone for the rest of the game.

## The four projects at a glance

| Project | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Steps | Outside, by the stairs near the driveway | Replace the worn steps with the wood stacked beside them |
| Leaky roof | Up the ladder by the porch | Walk across the roof and repair the damaged section |
| Railing | Back deck, by the workout equipment | Face the house and fill the gap in the railing |
| Bedroom wall | Bedroom, wall opposite the bed | Finish the wall, then paint it |

## When they become available

VGC says the repairs open once you have full control of [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) after [Gone Fishing](/missions/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/), with the objective appearing when you return to the house.

## 1. The steps

A stack of wood sits beside the stairs outside. Stand next to it and use the prompt; Playnews gives it as holding **Triangle** on PS5 or **Y** on Xbox. VGC notes this one is required to get into the house, so you cannot miss it.

## 2. The leaky roof

Ladders stand by the front of the house on the porch. Climb up, cross the roof to the damaged part near the top, which VGC describes as covered by a tarp, and repair it.

## 3. The railing

Go out of the back door onto the deck and down to Jason's workout equipment. Turn to face the house. There is a gap in the railing next to a ladder, and the prompt appears when you look at it.

## 4. The bedroom wall

In the bedroom, the wall opposite the bed is unfinished, with boards leaning against it. Interacting starts a longer sequence: Jason closes the wall up and then paints it. You pick the colour.

**Tip:** choose whatever you like. VGC checked, and the colour does not change Lucia's reaction or the relationship gain.

## The deadline

Both guides are clear that the projects cannot be done after Lucia is collected in [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/). VGC adds that the game warns you before she returns, and that there are no further chances afterwards.

The same window covers five optional errands, such as flowers, groceries and a necklace. They are listed in the Coming Home walkthrough.

## What you get

| Result | Source |
|---|---|
| Lucia is happier with the house, which helps the relationship | Playnews |
| The starting relationship meter is much lower if you skip the repairs | VGC |

Neither guide mentions a trophy, a cheat or cash for the repairs. The relationship itself matters later: several [cheats](/cheats/gta-6-cheats/) unlock from time and activities with your partner.

## Suggested order

1. Steps, on your way in.
2. Bedroom wall, while you are inside.
3. Railing, out the back.
4. Roof, last.

Playnews suggests doing the errands in town first and the house afterwards, so that you are already home when Lucia's second call comes. The order above simply saves walking.

## If you already missed them

There is no way to redo them. Beebom uses these repairs as its example of why to keep a manual save before big story moments. If you have an older save from before Coming Home, load it: see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

The house itself does not stay with you for the whole story, so nothing here carries a long-term cost beyond the relationship. For what happens to it, see the warning in the [Tourist Trap walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/).
