**This GTA 6 Don Gone walkthrough lists every item you need in Donnie's trailer, what to do about the three men waiting outside, and the one thing you can miss for good: a unique pistol in the trunk.** PowerPyx lists it as "Don Gone (Head to Donnie's Trailer)". It follows directly from the events of [Swamped](/missions/gta-6-swamped-walkthrough/).

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Don Gone (mission 18 in [our list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/), Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Head to Donnie's Trailer |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Donnie's trailer, Key Lento |
| Requirement | Starts automatically after [Swamped](/missions/gta-6-swamped-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | Donnie's H&L .38 (missable) |

Brian wants the trailer to look as if Donnie packed up and left town.

## Objectives

1. Find and collect Donnie's bag.
2. Pack Donnie's belongings.
3. Get in the car and leave Key Lento.
4. Destroy Donnie's car.

## Step-by-step

### 1. The flag and the bag

VGC starts with taking down the flag outside the trailer. Inside, the bag is on the bed. Pick it up.

### 2. Pack the trailer

**Tip:** PowerPyx says focus mode (**L3 + R3**) highlights what you still need.

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Photo | On the wall next to the TV |
| Flight schedule | By the "Patriot" neon sign, on the nightstand |
| Clothes | Near the bed: a shelf (PowerPyx), drawers or wardrobe (VGC) |
| Donnie's ID | On a shelf: right of the door (PowerPyx), across from the fridge (VGC) |
| Map | On the fridge |

Two extras are in the room. PowerPyx lists a message to play on the phone. Both guides mention an optional prompt to "do one for Donnie"; PowerPyx says the drug lowers health, briefly boosts stamina and has no effect on the mission.

### 3. The three men outside

When you step out, the three military men from the bar fight in [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/) are waiting. VGC treats the fist fight as required and says they are a little more aggressive than before. PowerPyx says you can also run to Donnie's car and drive off.

If you fight: block first, then counter, as you did against D-Man.

### 4. Leave Key Lento

Load up and drive Donnie's car out of the region, following the route north.

### 5. Open the trunk first

Before you do anything to the car, open the trunk and take **Donnie's H&L .38**. PowerPyx marks it as a missable unique weapon variant.

### 6. Destroy the car

Pick a quiet spot. Any of these works:

- Take the gas can from the car, pour it over the car and light it.
- Shoot the engine until it explodes (VGC).
- Drive it into the ocean and let it sink (PowerPyx).

VGC ends the mission with a call from Brian as you leave the area.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail state. The real risk is destroying the car with the pistol still in the trunk.

## Optional goals and rewards

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Missable | Donnie's H&L .38, in the trunk (PowerPyx) |
| Optional | The phone message; "do one for Donnie" |

PowerPyx lists a follow-up task next, Donnie's Lost Drugs, before Lucia calls to start [Recalibration](/missions/gta-6-recalibration-walkthrough/). For other guns, see [GTA 6 weapons](/weapons/gta-6-weapons/).
