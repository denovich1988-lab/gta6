**This GTA 6 Checking Out walkthrough covers the Chapter 2 finale: a shootout at the Starlet Motel, a bike chase and the drive into Vice City.** It is short, but it closes the chapter behind you, so the most useful part of this page is the checklist before you start. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Move to Vice City.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Checking Out (Chapter 2 finale) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) first, then [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Starts at | Your room at the Starlet Motel, Kelly County |
| Requirement | $15,000 from [Prepare for Vice City](/missions/gta-6-prepare-for-vice-city-walkthrough/) and all other Chapter 2 main missions |
| Reward | None (PowerPyx) |
| Next | [Welcome to Vice City](/missions/gta-6-welcome-to-vice-city-walkthrough/), the start of Chapter 3 |

### Point of no return checklist

Both guides warn that starting the mission ends Chapter 2 and takes the motel away as a home. Before you touch the bathroom door:

1. Make a manual save in a spare slot. Missions cannot be replayed; see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).
2. Finish the optional jobs you care about: [Thrillbillies](/missions/gta-6-thrillbillies-walkthrough/), [Hit Avery Pawn](/missions/gta-6-hit-avery-pawn-walkthrough/), [Hit Kingpin Bowling](/missions/gta-6-hit-kingpin-bowling-walkthrough/) and [Impounded Vehicle](/missions/gta-6-impounded-vehicle-walkthrough/).
3. Stock up as Jason. GTABase notes he may be short on healing items if you mostly played Lucia in this chapter.

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists five; GTABase merges two of them and adds a "start the mission" step.

1. Regroup with Lucia.
2. Defend yourself.
3. Follow Jason.
4. Jump to the car.
5. Escape with Jason.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Start at the bathroom door

Once the requirements are met, your partner calls about an article Cal sent and the motel is marked on the map. Go to your room and interact with the bathroom door.

### 2. Regroup with Lucia

Pick up the shotgun from the bed. Corrupt cops have found the motel. Fight through them to the cafeteria at the side of the building, where Lucia is waiting.

**Tip:** GTABase suggests using any frag grenades you have saved if Jason is low on supplies.

### 3. Defend yourself

The game switches you to Lucia. Keep shooting while Jason heads for the road and takes a car.

### 4. Follow Jason on the bike

A biker rides at Lucia. Shoot him, take his bike and follow Jason, who shows as a pink dot on the minimap. Ride cleanly: a crash can throw you off.

### 5. Jump to the car

When the prompt appears, pull alongside and hold **Square** on PS5 or **X** on Xbox to jump across.

### 6. Escape

Jason drives. As Lucia, shoot at the police cars behind you; both guides say to aim for the tires. A scene follows, and you drive into Vice City.

## Where players fail

- **Dying on the bike.** This is the known bug: Jason can disappear as an objective and leave you in a police search area with no way out. Both guides give the same fix, which is to restart the mission.
- **Crashing during the chase.** Stay on the road and keep the pink dot in sight rather than cutting corners.
- **Starting too early.** Anything tied to the motel and Chapter 2 is gone once the mission begins.
