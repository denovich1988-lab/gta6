**This GTA 6 Prepare for Vice City walkthrough is a money plan: the mission has one objective, and it is to have $15,000.** It sits in your list from Coffee Break until the end of Chapter 2 and is the first place where the story stops for players who are broke. The good news is that the jobs already on your map pay almost all of it.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Prepare for Vice City (Chapter 2) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Lucia |
| Starts at | The coffee room of the Starlet Motel, Kelly County |
| Requirement | Finish [Coffee Break](/missions/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None; it unlocks the chapter finale |

## Objectives

1. Earn at least $15,000.

## Where the $15,000 comes from

Both guides recommend the same seven marked jobs. The payouts below are the ones the guides state.

| Job | Payout | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Afternoon Delight](/missions/gta-6-afternoon-delight-walkthrough/) | $10,000 | Paid by Vicki via BuckMe the next day; main mission |
| [Thrillbillies](/missions/gta-6-thrillbillies-walkthrough/) | Up to $2,500 | First place in the race |
| [Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab](/missions/gta-6-twist-wrist-smash-grab-walkthrough/) | $2,000 | Needs Thrillbillies first |
| [Hit Avery Pawn](/missions/gta-6-hit-avery-pawn-walkthrough/) | Varies | Fence the loot with Dunk |
| [Hit Kingpin Bowling](/missions/gta-6-hit-kingpin-bowling-walkthrough/) | Varies | Fence the loot; also a trophy |
| [Impounded Vehicle](/missions/gta-6-impounded-vehicle-walkthrough/) | None | Gets Lucia's car back |
| Veteran Hunted | Not stated | Jason only, per PowerPyx |

Earlier income counts too: $1,700 from Dunk after [Slam Dunk](/missions/gta-6-slam-dunk-walkthrough/) and up to $1,750 from [Daily Driver](/missions/gta-6-daily-driver-walkthrough/).

**Tip:** the three fixed payouts alone come to $14,500 with a race win. One fenced bag from either robbery, or a couple of cars sold to Moses, takes you over the line.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Work through the marked jobs

Do them in any order. Afternoon Delight needs a call from Vicki, which comes some time after Coffee Break.

### 2. Bank as you go

Both guides say to deposit cash in BuckMe as you earn it, because money you carry can be lost if you die. The account is explained in the [Buck Me walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-buck-me-walkthrough/).

### 3. Top up if needed

Sell untracked cars to [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/) or see [how to make money in GTA 6](/cheats/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6/).

### 4. Watch for the motel marker

When you have $15,000 and the other main missions are finished, the Starlet Motel room is marked. That is the start of Checking Out.

## Where players fail

- **Carrying everything in cash.** One death during a robbery can undo an hour.
- **Spending on cars and clothes first.** Registration and outfits come out of the same pot.
- **Walking into the finale unprepared.** Both guides warn that Checking Out ends Chapter 2 and that the Starlet Motel is gone as a home for good. Finish what you want in Port Gellhorn before you enter the room.
