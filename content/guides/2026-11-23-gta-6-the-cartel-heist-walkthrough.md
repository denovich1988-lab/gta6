**This GTA 6 The Cartel Heist walkthrough covers the Chapter 3 finale: the robbery of Sinfrontera National Bank with Raul's crew.** It is the longest mission so far, with thirty on-screen objectives in PowerPyx's count, and it switches between Jason and Lucia throughout. Everything you prepared in the setup missions is used here.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | The Cartel Heist (Chapter 3 finale) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Raul Bautista |
| Starts at | Planning room, Stockyard |
| Requirement | [Hidden Charges](/missions/gta-6-hidden-charges-walkthrough/) and every other Chapter 3 main mission |
| Reward | $120,000; Break the Bank trophy (PowerPyx) |

**Before you go:** missions cannot be replayed, so make a manual save first ([how to save](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/)). Stock up on armor, and carry a longer-range weapon as well as a shotgun. The mission ends Chapter 3, and the next one, Ocean Beach, moves you out of the Bayside apartment.

## Objectives

GTABase groups the heist into ten stages; we use them as headings below.

1. Blow the cell towers.
2. Take the lobby.
3. Down to the vault floor.
4. Clear the building and the roof.
5. Crack the vault.
6. Hold off the police.
7. Smoke out the street.
8. Get Nicole out.
9. Defend the bus.
10. Escape on the freeway.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Blow the cell towers

After the briefing, get in the black car outside with Lucia and Nicole. Drive downtown to the towers you rigged, park in the marked spot at the side of the road and set off the charges.

### 2. Take the lobby (Jason)

Drive south to the bank. The clock is now running.

| Towers destroyed | Time before police respond |
|---|---|
| All three | Just under 11 minutes |
| Fewer | Less; neither guide gives the figures |

Walk into the lobby and wait for Karim's signal. Then:

1. Knock out one of the marked guards.
2. Threaten each marked customer and employee so they move to the middle of the room. Check the side rooms too.
3. When Karim calls out someone heading for the door, deal with that person first.

**Tip:** if a guard pulls a gun, shoot it out of his hand rather than killing him. That keeps your Professional rating.

### 3. Down to the vault floor (Lucia)

Take the open elevator with Raul and Nicole. On the vault floor, threaten Lex, the security guard, until he walks into the security room. Keep the pressure on until he opens the door for the truck, then knock him out once he moves to the wall.

### 4. Clear the building and the roof (Jason)

La Polilla Negra security comes out of the elevators and down from the upper floor. Clear them, go to the second floor and ride the elevator to the roof. The guards up there are watching the helicopter, so you get the first shots. Clear the roof, climb the ladder to the upper level and secure the backup terminal.

### 5. Crack the vault (Lucia)

The steps are the ones you practised in [Safe Word](/missions/gta-6-safe-word-walkthrough/), slightly harder:

1. **Drill.** Hold both triggers to drill. This time both gauges move, so keep adjusting both.
2. **Cable camera.** Left stick moves the cable, right stick turns the view. The route makes a very sharp right turn; follow it to the gold hook at the end.
3. **Hold the door.** Hold both triggers and mash X (A on Xbox) while Nicole drills.

Inside, bag the big stacks of bills first. Both guides say they are worth more than the loose cash.

### 6. Hold off the police

As Jason, hold the second floor against police coming into the lobby and SWAT breaching upstairs. Swap the shotgun for a longer-range weapon. As Lucia, cover the left side. When prompted, regroup with Raul in the middle of the room.

A SWAT truck then reverses into the lobby and a flashbang goes off. Stay in cover until your sight returns, then finish the enemies in the lobby.

### 7. Smoke out the street

Run to the back of the SWAT truck. Lucia climbs in and hands Jason a smoke grenade launcher. Go outside and fire smoke at the police cars in front of the bank. As Lucia, run out of the front door to the bus.

### 8. Get Nicole out (Jason)

1. Shoot the enemies rushing the front, then help Nicole up.
2. Follow her through the door the enemies came from.
3. Turn right straight away and take the stairs down. Expect a flashbang on the landing.
4. Take the door on the left when Nicole points it out. It leads to the delivery bay.
5. Follow the driveway out and get in the Brigade SWAT vehicle at the top of the ramp. Drive off once Nicole is in.

### 9. Defend the bus (Lucia)

From the back of the bus, shoot the pursuing vehicles and listen to Raul: he calls out cars coming up on either side. After a truck tears the roof off, Raul passes you a grenade launcher with unlimited ammo.

**Tip:** the helicopter is easiest to hit when it settles directly behind the bus. If you keep missing, switch weapons and use Focus to shoot the pilot.

### 10. Escape on the freeway (Jason)

In the armored truck, catch up with the bus; you can ram police cars out of the way. Pull up on its left side to jump across. Then drive the bus along the freeway and keep left when Raul says so. A final scene ends the mission and the chapter.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

- **Too few charges.** The lobby and vault are on a timer that Hidden Charges sets.
- **Killing armed guards in the lobby.** It costs the Professional rating.
- **Loose cash first.** Take the large stacks before anything else.
- **Standing up after the flashbang.** Wait in cover until you can see.
