**This GTA 6 Amateur Hour walkthrough covers the short Lucia mission where she helps Francisco "Cisco" Delgado get money back from a director who owes him.** It is one of the quickest jobs in the build-up to the VCIA Takedown: a drive, a wait on a bench and one chase. The only part that can drag is the chase, and there is a simple way to end it early.

Everything below is described the same way by PowerPyx and GTABase unless we name one of them.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Amateur Hour (Chapter 4) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) only |
| Given by | Francisco "Cisco" Delgado |
| Starts at | The Trauma Floor, Ajax Gym, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | Lucia's first workout with Kordell's crew on the Trauma Floor (see [Making Gainz](/missions/gta-6-making-gainz-walkthrough/)) |
| Reward | $1,000 |

A note on the requirement: PowerPyx calls that first crew workout a separate mission, Blood Sweat and Gear, while GTABase treats it as the last stage of Making Gainz. It is the same workout either way. If Francisco is not on the stage yet, you still have that session to do.

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists ten on-screen steps.

1. Go to the Trauma Floor.
2. Change outfit at the locker.
3. Go to Francisco's car.
4. Go to the pick-up location.
5. Park the vehicle near the pick-up location.
6. Wait on the bench.
7. Stop the Coochie Camper.
8. Wait for Francisco to get the money.
9. Drop off Francisco at the back of Ajax Gym.
10. Park Francisco's car at his spot.

GTABase groups the same mission into five stages.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Talk to Francisco on the Trauma Floor

Go back to Ajax Gym as Lucia and head to the Trauma Floor, the crew's private workout area. Francisco is deadlifting on the stage. Talk to him and he asks for help with a debt.

### 2. Change at the locker

Use Lucia's locker on the Trauma Floor to change outfit. It is an on-screen objective here, not a cosmetic extra, so do it before you look for Cisco.

### 3. Follow Cisco to his car

Cisco leaves through the back door of the Trauma Floor. Follow him out to his car. Lucia drives.

### 4. Drive to the pick-up spot

The pick-up location is a short drive north, on Washington Island. Park in the marked space: parking near the pick-up location is its own objective, and you will be back in the car within a minute.

### 5. Wait on the bench

Sit on the bench nearby while Cisco hides. Then wait. The Coochie Camper turns up on its own, so there is nothing to do here except stay put.

### 6. Stop the Coochie Camper

Once the camper arrives, get back into Cisco's car and go after it. Cisco opens fire on it, and you can shoot as well.

**Tip:** go for the tyres. Both guides say that shooting them is how you stop the camper, and GTABase adds that it comes to a halt almost straight away once they are blown out. Neither guide suggests any other way to stop it. For what Lucia can carry, see [GTA 6 weapons](/weapons/gta-6-weapons/).

### 7. Let Cisco collect

When the camper stops, Judd gets out and Cisco takes his money back. You only have to wait for him to finish.

### 8. Drive back to Ajax Gym

Take Cisco to the back of Ajax Gym, where you picked him up, and park his car in his own space to finish the mission.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition for Amateur Hour, so we will not guess at one. The parts that slow players down are easy to name, though:

- **Skipping the locker.** Changing outfit is an objective, so do it before following Cisco.
- **Parking loosely.** The pick-up spot and Cisco's space at the gym are both marked places, and parking in them is part of the objective list.
- **Shooting the wrong thing.** The tyres are what stop the camper, so aim there from the start.

GTA 6 has no mission replay menu, so if you want a second go, make a manual save first. See [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/) and [replaying missions](/missions/gta-6-replay-missions/).

## What comes next

After Amateur Hour, Nate texts Lucia and asks her to get in touch. PowerPyx lists that call as its own entry, Call Nate, with no reward. GTABase folds it into the start of the next mission. Either way, the next job for Lucia is [Cash Injections](/missions/gta-6-cash-injections-walkthrough/), a much rougher outing on Ocean Beach.

Just before this mission, the story follows Jason in [Too Much Baggage](/missions/gta-6-too-much-baggage-walkthrough/) and [Watch It](/missions/gta-6-watch-it-walkthrough/). How all of these feed into the airport job is explained in [GTA 6 heists](/blog/gta-6-heists/), and the full order is in our [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).
