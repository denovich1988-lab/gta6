**This GTA 6 Coming Home walkthrough covers the eleventh mission, the night Lucia gets out: the errands worth doing first, the pickup at the prison, and a long evening at the Rusty Anchor that ends in a fight.** PowerPyx also lists it as "Lucia's Release". It is the longest mission so far and the point where the game's second lead joins Jason.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Coming Home (mission 11, Chapter 1) |
| Also listed as | Lucia's Release |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |
| Starts at | Hamlet Correctional, after Lucia calls |
| Requirement | Finish [Feng Shui](/missions/gta-6-feng-shui-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | No cash; Lucia moves into Jason's house |
| Next mission | Clean Up After Donnie |

## The five errands before the mission

The morning after Feng Shui, Lucia phones to say she is getting out soon. Before the second call, the game offers five optional tasks. Both guides say they affect the relationship with [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) and that you can skip them.

| Errand | What to do | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Groceries | Pick up food and drink at the store and bring it home | VGC: grab a few items before the game tells you to leave |
| Flowers | Buy flowers at the flower shop | |
| Necklace | Buy Lucia a necklace at the jewelry shop | $425 (PowerPyx), and it comes up again in Chapter 2 |
| Haircut | Visit the barber | VGC; PowerPyx's list has a task called "Get The Chop" |
| Lucia's clothes | Collect them from her friend, who works at a bar | Put them in your car trunk |

PowerPyx also recommends finishing the house repairs if you have not: the bedroom wall, the stairs, the roof and the balcony railing. With all five errands done, it reports the relationship meter starting at three out of four.

**Tip:** if the second call does not come, sleep at the house until morning. If it still has not come, sleep again or drive around for a while.

## Objectives

1. Head to the prison.
2. Drive to the bar and enter the Rusty Anchor.
3. Order a drink from a bartender.
4. Meet up with Lucia.
5. Hang out with your friends.
6. Dance with Lucia.
7. Find Mattia and play pool.
8. Deal with D-Man.
9. Follow Lucia.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Collect Lucia

Drive to the prison in Hamlet with her clothes in the trunk. Talk to the guard at reception. A short flashback plays, then Lucia comes out through the gate. Walk her to the car.

### 2. Drive to the Rusty Anchor

The bar is marked north of Key Lento. If you brought her clothes, Lucia changes when you arrive. Cal is inside, and Donnie, the seaplane pilot, turns up.

### 3. Buy the drinks

Use the bartender prompt (**L2 + Triangle** on PS5, **LT + Y** on Xbox) and order two beers; PowerPyx gives the price as $6. One goes into your hand, so pick up the second from the counter and carry both out to Lucia on the deck behind the bar.

### 4. Hang out

After a scene between the two leads, the evening opens up. The "hang out with your friends" objective moves on once you have done a few interactions. The ones the guides describe:

- **Cal** asks for help with a dating app. No consequence.
- **Donnie** offers cocaine. You can accept or decline; PowerPyx found no effect either way.
- **Rhonda** has lost her pet lizard (VGC). Use focus mode; it is in a bush across the street.

Kat, Lucia's former cellmate, and her boyfriend Mattia arrive during the evening. Lucia mentions that Mattia may have work for you.

### 5. Dance with Lucia

Approach Lucia when the dance prompt appears. VGC says you can decline, but dancing gave an early relationship boost, so do it.

### 6. Pool with Mattia

Find Mattia outside behind the bar and get him a drink. He suggests a game of pool: take a cue from the wall and play. The result does not matter. A group of military men, who have been bothering Donnie, interrupt and it goes outside.

### 7. Deal with D-Man

This is a fist fight.

| Action | PS5 | Xbox |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | Square | X |
| Block | Hold Cross | Hold A |

PowerPyx's tip: watch the shoulders. When they start to rotate, a punch is coming, so block and then hit back.

### 8. Run

The police arrive. Follow Lucia away from the bar to the beach. A cutscene takes the two of them home, and the mission ends.

## Where players fail

- **Leaving the clothes behind.** Collect them and put them in the trunk before you drive to the prison.
- **Mashing attack against D-Man.** Block first, then counter.
- **Staying to fight the police.** The objective is to follow Lucia, not to win.
