**This GTA 6 Team Building walkthrough covers Lucia's outing with Kordell, which turns into an underground cage fight and ends with a tense meeting in a waterside bar.** It is a short mission with one real test: three rounds of melee with nothing but your fists.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Team Building (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) only |
| Given by | Kordell |
| Starts at | Ajax Gym |
| Requirement | [Power Play](/missions/gta-6-power-play-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | None listed |

Ajax Gym is the same gym where Lucia trains with Nate in [Making Gainz](/missions/gta-6-making-gainz-walkthrough/).

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists six on-screen steps.

1. Follow Kordell.
2. Drive to the warehouse.
3. Win the fight.
4. Get in Kordell's car.
5. Go to Riverbank.
6. Follow Kordell.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Kordell

Talk to Kordell at the gym, follow him outside and get into his car in the alley.

### 2. Drive to the warehouse

Drive to the warehouse he names. The front is not the way in: walk around the **left side** of the building and go through the doorway there.

### 3. Win the cage fight

Lucia is pulled into the cage as soon as she is inside.

| Round | Opponent | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First fighter | Knock them out on your own |
| 2 | Second fighter | Steps in straight after; knock him out too |
| 3 | A third man and the rest | Kordell jumps in and you finish them together |

**Tip:** both guides give the same advice. Block and dodge first, using the on-screen prompt, then hit back with melee attacks. Swinging without dodging is how the fight is lost; timing the dodge is what makes each round short.

### 4. Drive to Riverbank

Once the man called Arlo has left, go back outside and get into Kordell's car again. Drive to the Riverbank Waterside Bar.

### 5. Follow Kordell into the bar

Follow him in. A scene plays out in the bar and the mission ends when it does.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists a fail condition beyond losing the fight.

- **The first two rounds are solo.** Kordell only steps in when the third fighter arrives, so do not wait for help.
- **The warehouse door.** The entrance is on the left side, not at the front.
