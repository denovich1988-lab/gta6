**This GTA 6 Take Care of Jason walkthrough explains the one Chapter 2 mission you cannot finish on demand.** Jason is laid up in the motel room after the events that end Chapter 1, and Lucia looks after him while she works through the rest of the chapter. The game also calls it Play Nurse. There is nothing to fight and nothing to fail; the only trick is knowing how it progresses.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Take Care of Jason (Chapter 2) |
| Also shown as | Play Nurse (pause menu during the cutscene, per PowerPyx) |
| Playable character | [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) |

## Objectives

1. Check in on Jason regularly.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Find it in the mission list

The mission has no icon on the map. Open **Map > Story** to see it listed with the other main missions.

### 2. Visit Jason when you are near the motel

Each in-game day, go back to the room at the Starlet Motel. From time to time a short scene plays in which Lucia takes care of him, and the relationship between the two improves.

### 3. Watch the motel icon

At certain points in Chapter 2 the story will not move until you return. The motel marker changes from **green to pink** when that happens.

**Tip:** you do not need to camp at the motel. Play the other missions and drop in when you come back to save or sleep; see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

### 4. Keep playing Chapter 2

Both guides say the mission closes by itself after **Breakfast Run**, late in the chapter. The missions that lead there include:

- [Just Moved In](/missions/gta-6-just-moved-in-walkthrough/)
- [Girl's Trip](/missions/gta-6-girls-trip-walkthrough/)
- [Moses](/missions/gta-6-moses-walkthrough/)
- [New Look](/missions/gta-6-new-look-walkthrough/) and [How Do I Look?](/missions/gta-6-how-do-i-look-walkthrough/)
- [Painkiller Supplier](/missions/gta-6-painkiller-supplier-walkthrough/)
- [Ambulance Chaser](/missions/gta-6-ambulance-chaser-walkthrough/)
- [Behind on Payments](/missions/gta-6-behind-on-payments-walkthrough/)

- Slam Dunk

## Where players fail

- **Looking for a marker.** There is none; use the Story tab.
- **Trying to finish it first.** It stays open for most of the chapter by design.
- **Ignoring a pink motel icon.** That is the game telling you the next story step is in the room.
