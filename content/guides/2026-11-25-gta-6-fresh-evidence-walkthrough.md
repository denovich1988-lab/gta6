**This GTA 6 Fresh Evidence walkthrough covers the night Jason and Dre'Quan break into a mansion on Starfish Island to copy blackmail material.** It is a stealth mission for the first two thirds, with a guard to hide, a tiger to work around and a small wall puzzle, and a shootout and boat chase for the last third.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Fresh Evidence (Chapter 4) |
| Playable character | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) only |
| Given by | Dre'Quan |
| Starts at | Dre'Quan's office, Jack of Hearts, Crosstown, [Vice City](/map/gta-6-vice-city/) |
| Requirement | Watch It and [Adventures in Babysitting](/missions/gta-6-adventures-in-babysitting-walkthrough/) |
| Reward | $10,000 |

Both guides call the place Dante's mansion. PowerPyx adds the story context: the owner is Dante Fresh, and the files are leverage on a promoter called Royce Jones, the man Dre'Quan was promised a meeting with at the end of [Photo/Shoot](/missions/gta-6-photo-shoot-walkthrough/).

**Tip:** the mission ends in a gunfight you cannot avoid, so arrive with body armour and ammo even though it starts quietly. A manual save first does no harm either: see [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

## Objectives

PowerPyx lists 19 on-screen steps. Shortened, they are:

1. Follow Dre'Quan and go to Starfish Island.
2. Enter the mansion.
3. Hide the body in the closet.
4. Find the bedroom and the key card.
5. Go to the elevator.
6. Find something to lure the tiger.
7. Find the surveillance room.
8. Play rock paper scissors with Dre'Quan.
9. Get the correct item from the mural and insert it.
10. Browse the files while the evidence copies.
11. Get back to the elevator.
12. Escape with Dante's speedboat and lose the cops.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Meet Dre'Quan at Jack of Hearts

Go to Dre'Quan's office as Jason, tell him you are ready and follow him to the car in the alley. Drive to Starfish Island. The break-in happens at night, so the game has you wait until dark.

### 2. Get inside

Dre'Quan stops at a wall. Climb it, then climb the boxes to the upper floor and go into the workout room. There is one guard on the far side of it.

1. Knock the guard out.
2. Carry the body to the closet Dre'Quan opens and leave it there.
3. Follow the walkway to the door at its end and open it with the Snap Gun.

### 3. Take the key card from the bedroom

Follow the hallways to the bedroom. A tiger lives in this house. On this stretch it wanders out onto the balcony, so keep away from it and keep moving. The key card is on the side table to the left of the bed.

### 4. Leave by the balcony and distract the guards

When you turn back, the tiger is in the hallway. Do not try to pass it. Follow Dre'Quan out onto the balcony and drop down onto the cabanas by the pool.

Two guards stand between you and the elevator. Follow the wall on the right side of the pool to the bar, pick up a bottle and throw it to the left of the guards. They go to look, and the way is clear.

### 5. Lure the tiger with raw meat

The tiger has moved again, this time into the living room you need to cross. Open the fridge on the right, take the raw meat and throw it to the far side of the room. Once the tiger goes after it, follow Dre'Quan to the elevator and ride down to the basement.

### 6. Open the hidden door

Downstairs, go to the round room with the large gold mural. Dre'Quan settles who has to open the door with a game of rock paper scissors. Both guides say Jason only has to solve the puzzle if he loses.

| If Jason loses | What to do |
|---|---|
| Find the item | Match the symbols on the mural to the empty slot in the wall |
| The right one | The item with a hand holding a microphone, on the blue part of the wall at the far right |
| Use it | Pull it off the wall, place it in the slot, then hold the prompt to open the door |

### 7. Copy the evidence

Squeeze through after Dre'Quan into the surveillance room. He starts copying the files. Interact with him to watch the videos while the copy runs.

### 8. Fight back to the elevator

The quiet part is over. There are guns on the wall of the surveillance room: take one or two before you leave. Guards come into the basement as you step out. Clear them and take the elevator back up.

### 9. Cross the upper floor and reach the dock

Fight across the upper floor to the far balcony. Enemies wait around the corners here, so check each one before you run through. Jump down at the same spot you used earlier and head for the dock. PowerPyx notes that you are still under fire on the way.

### 10. Escape by speedboat

Get into Dante's speedboat and drive away. The police join in at this point. Keep going until you lose them and the mission completes. ## Where players fail

Neither guide lists fail conditions, so we do not claim any. The places where the mission is most likely to go wrong follow from the steps both describe:

- **The hallway after the key card.** The tiger blocks it. The balcony is the intended way out.
- **The two elevator guards.** The bottle goes to their left, away from the route you want to take.
- **The living room.** Throw the meat to the far side so the tiger ends up away from your path.
- **The escape.** Guards arrive in the basement and on the upper floor. Take the wall guns and do not sprint around blind corners.
