**The GTA 6 pre-order bonus is the Vintage Vice City Pack**, a set of in-game items that look back to 2002's GTA: Vice City. Rockstar announced it on June 24, 2026 together with prices and pre-orders.

## What is in the pack

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| '55 Vapid Stanier | A 1950s-style sedan |
| Garage | The Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach, with the car inside |
| Outfits and hairstyles | One look each for Jason and Lucia in classic Vice City style |
| Weapon pattern | A tropical pattern for your guns |

## Who gets it

- Anyone who pre-ordered or buys the game **before November 20, 2026**.
- **Both editions**: Standard and Ultimate.
- **Digital and boxed** copies. With a boxed copy, the pack is on the same code as the game.

You do not redeem it separately. It is added to your game automatically.

## Other bonuses

A digital pre-order also includes one month of GTA+. The 16 extra items in the Ultimate Edition are a different set: see [GTA 6 Ultimate Edition](/buying/gta-6-ultimate-edition/).
