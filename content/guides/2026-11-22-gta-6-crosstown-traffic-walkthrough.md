**This GTA 6 Crosstown Traffic walkthrough covers Raul's river job: Lucia leans on a gang member aboard the metro while Jason chases a convoy of boats and takes the one carrying the shipment.** The game switches between the two several times, so each part is short.

## Before you start

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Mission | Crosstown Traffic (Chapter 3) |
| Playable characters | [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) |
| Given by | Raul |
| Starts at | Lucia: Courtyard Bar, Stockyard. Jason: the boatyard in La Perle (meet Karim) |
| Requirement | [Car Service](/missions/gta-6-car-service-walkthrough/), then Raul's call |
| Reward | $3,000 |

The target is a Guardia Brothers shipment moving on the Vice River.

## Objectives

1. Follow the boats.
2. Get to the next station and board the metro.
3. Confront the Guardia Brother.
4. Hijack the boat.
5. Push the emergency stop button.
6. Defend the boat.

PowerPyx lists reaching the station and boarding as two separate goals; GTABase combines them as Catch the Metro.

## Step-by-step

### 1. Follow the boats (Jason)

After the opening scene Jason is on a bike. Keep to the dirt paths along the river bank and stay with the boats.

### 2. Catch the metro (Lucia)

At the drop-off the game switches to Lucia, who misses her train. Sprint under the elevated tracks, which are shown on the minimap, to the stairs of the next station. Board the rear car.

### 3. Confront the Guardia Brother (Lucia)

Walk to the front of the car and intimidate the gang member. Once Lucia has him on the floor, pressure him, then take out his phone and make him place the call. That call is what hands Jason the boats.

### 4. Hijack the boat (Jason)

Back with Jason, the plan has been noticed and gang members open fire. Kill the two on the dock, get back on the bike and follow the dirt path. Karim tells you to turn right at the bridge. A jump then throws you onto one of the boats.

Now you are driving a boat and shooting. Clear the crew of the closer boat first, then the gunner on the boat with the shipment, and jump across when you are alongside.

### 5. Stop the train (Lucia)

More gang members attack Lucia in the carriage. Beat them in a fist fight, then go to the back of the train and press the emergency Stop button before the next station. Lucia jumps down to the boat.

### 6. Defend the boat (Lucia)

Jason drives. Open the rear seats to take an assault rifle and body armor; ammo is unlimited here.

- **Seasharks and boats** come from behind. Aim at the drivers: the vehicle crashes when the driver goes down.
- **The Coast Guard** follows. Deal with its boats the same way.
- **The helicopter.** Shoot the pilot through the front window and it crashes straight away.

**Tip:** take the armor before you start shooting. You do not have to destroy everything: after a while Karim drops containers from a bridge to block everyone behind you, and the mission ends.

## Where players fail

Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. Watch for these:

- **Losing the boats on the bike.** Stay on the dirt paths; do not cut inland.
- **Running along the street to the station.** The route is under the tracks.
- **Missing the Stop button.** It has to be pressed before the train reaches the next station.
