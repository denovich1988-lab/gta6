**How much money has GTA 6 made? Take-Two's official answer is $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales worldwide, with 32.5 million units sold before launch.** The publisher announced both figures on November 20, 2026 and called it the biggest entertainment launch in history. Before that announcement the best number was an estimate from the analytics firm Sensor Tower: about **24.6 million copies and $2.36 billion** through launch day. Below is what each number covers, how the estimate grew over 48 hours, how it all compares with GTA 5's record, and what is still unknown.

**Update, November 26:** this page was first written hours before Take-Two published its figures. The official numbers are now at the top of the table and in the next section; the sections on the Sensor Tower estimate are kept because they explain the platform split and the day-by-day growth, which Take-Two has not published. Our [GTA 6 pre-order sales page](/blog/gta-6-pre-order-sales/) covers the 32.5 million figure in detail.

How we checked: every figure here appears in at least two independent reports, or is marked with the single outlet that reported it. Estimates are labelled as estimates throughout.

## The official numbers: $3.79 billion and 32.5 million

Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, published its first figures in a press release dated November 20, 2026. It says day-one retail sales reached $3.79 billion, based on company estimates through the end of November 19 worldwide, and that the game sold 32.5 million units pre-release, a record across all platforms. It names no platform, edition or digital-versus-physical split, and The Next Web noted that the company did not say how the unit figure was calculated.

Before launch its statements were deliberately vague. On its earnings call on November 9, chief executive Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders were far in excess of the company's expectations and, in the line most outlets quoted, that "we still aren't ready to declare victory."

**Why it matters:** the two official numbers measure different things. The units are sales before release; the dollars run to the end of launch day. Take-Two has not yet said how many copies have been sold in total.

## The Sensor Tower estimate: 24.6 million copies, $2.36 billion

Sensor Tower tracks digital store activity and publishes running estimates. By the evening of launch day, November 19, its tracker showed about 24.58 to 24.59 million copies and roughly $2.36 billion in revenue. Neither Take-Two nor Rockstar has confirmed it.

Three things to know about the figure:

- **It is cumulative.** It includes months of pre-orders, not only copies bought after the midnight unlock.
- **It covers PlayStation and Xbox.** Those are the only platforms the game is on; see [GTA 6 platforms](/buying/gta-6-platforms/).
- **It does not subtract refunds**, as TweakTown points out.

**Tip:** when you see "GTA 6 sold 24 million in a day", read it as "24 million through its first day, counting pre-orders".

## How the estimate grew in 48 hours

The same tracker was quoted at three points, which is why you will find three different numbers online.

| When | Estimated copies | Estimated revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Wednesday, November 18, midday | About 17.1 million | About $1.65 billion |
| Thursday, November 19, morning | About 19.1 million | About $1.84 billion |
| Thursday, November 19, evening | About 24.6 million | About $2.36 billion |

The game unlocked at local midnight in each region, starting in New Zealand on November 18; see [what time GTA 6 came out](/blog/what-time-does-gta-6-come-out/). The Wednesday figure is therefore almost entirely pre-orders.

**Why it matters:** on these estimates, roughly 7.5 million copies and about $700 million were added between Wednesday midday and Thursday night. Most of the launch-day total was already in the bank before anyone played.

## PlayStation vs Xbox

Sensor Tower's split is about three to one in PlayStation's favour.

| Platform | Estimated copies | Estimated revenue |
|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | About 18.4 million | About $1.77 billion |
| Xbox Series X and Series S | About 6.2 million | About $588 million |

The per-platform figures were reported by GameSpot and repeated by Make Tech Easier. Before launch, Sony said GTA 6 was the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation Store, and Microsoft said the same for its own store. Neither gave a count.

**Why it matters:** if you are choosing where to play with friends, far more of them are likely to be on PlayStation. Our [best console for GTA 6](/blog/best-console-for-gta-6/) comparison covers the other differences.

## How it compares with GTA 5's record

GTA 5 set the bar every big launch has been measured against since 2013. Its numbers were official:

| | GTA 5 (2013) | GTA 6 (2026, estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| First day, copies | 11.21 million | About 24.6 million |
| First day, revenue | $815.7 million | About $2.36 billion |
| Source | Take-Two, recorded by Guinness World Records | Sensor Tower |
| Time to $1 billion | Three days | Passed before launch on pre-orders, by the estimate |

On paper GTA 6 is at more than twice the copies and almost three times the revenue. Two cautions apply. The GTA 5 figure came from the publisher and the GTA 6 figure from an outside firm. And a dollar in 2013 is not a dollar in 2026: the base game costs more now, which is the next point.

## Why revenue rose faster than copies

GTA 5 launched at $59.99. GTA 6 costs **$79.99** for the Standard Edition and **$99.99** for the Ultimate Edition. Dividing Sensor Tower's revenue by its copies gives an average of about $96 per copy. That is our arithmetic on their estimate, not a reported figure, but it suggests either that a large share of early buyers chose the more expensive edition or that the estimate runs high.

**Tip:** if you have not bought yet, the difference between the two is covered in [GTA 6 price and editions](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/) and [what the Ultimate Edition adds](/buying/gta-6-ultimate-edition/).

## What Take-Two and the platform holders have said

- **Take-Two** has described demand with phrases such as "unprecedented" and "far exceeding expectations", without numbers.
- **Zelnick** has said official sales data will be shared soon, and that the company prefers not to claim success before it happens.
- **Sony and Microsoft** each called GTA 6 their store's most pre-ordered game ever, on November 10.

One comparison Zelnick did give is for the previous game: GTA 5 has now sold nearly 235 million copies. That is the long-run number GTA 6 will eventually be measured against.

## Has GTA 6 made its budget back?

Rockstar has never published a budget. Reports citing Reuters put the development cost at an estimated $1 billion or more. If the Sensor Tower estimate is close, launch-day revenue alone is more than twice that, before the stores and retailers take their share and before marketing is counted. Our [GTA 6 budget](/blog/gta-6-budget/) page goes through what is and is not known about the cost, and [how long GTA 6 was in development](/blog/how-long-was-gta-6-in-development/) explains where the money went.

## What is not confirmed

- **Any official unit or revenue figure.** Everything above is an estimate or a forecast.
- **Sales of boxed copies as a separate number.** The box contains a download code, so retail copies are redeemed on the same stores; see [GTA 6 physical copy](/buying/gta-6-physical-copy/).
- **Regional sales.** No report we checked breaks the estimate down by country.
- **Profit.** Revenue is not profit, and Take-Two has not discussed either for launch.

## How to read GTA 6 sales headlines

1. **Check the source.** "Take-Two announced" is official. "Sensor Tower estimates" or "analysts expect" is not.
2. **Check the window.** "First day", "through launch day", "first 24 hours" and "first three days" are different measures.
3. **Check for pre-orders.** Almost all early figures include them.
4. **Check the unit.** Copies and dollars are often swapped in headlines; 24.6 million is copies, $2.36 billion is revenue.
5. **Ignore numbers with no source at all.** Round figures passed around on social media usually trace back to nothing.

## What this means for you

For players, the size of the launch matters in two practical ways. A game that sells like this gets long support, so patches and extra content are a safe bet even though Rockstar has dated nothing; see [GTA 6 online](/online/gta-6-online/) for what has been said about multiplayer. And critics agree with the buyers: the game sits at 99 on Metacritic, as covered in [GTA 6 reviews](/blog/gta-6-reviews/).
