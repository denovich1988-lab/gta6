**No. GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on every console**, including the PS5 Pro. Rockstar confirmed it on November 10, 2026, alongside the pre-load announcement.

## GTA 6 frame rate by console

| Console | Frame rate target | 60 FPS mode | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | 30 FPS | No | None listed |
| PS5 Pro | 30 FPS | No | Ray-traced shadows, PSSR upscaling |
| Xbox Series X | 30 FPS | No | None listed |
| Xbox Series S | 30 FPS | No | Lower resolution |

Rockstar has not published an official resolution table for each console.

## Why 30 FPS

Rockstar has said that the scale of the game made 30 FPS the target for this hardware generation from early on, and that it worked on balancing presentation, simulation and responsiveness. The official Extended Look was itself captured on a base PS5.

## What you can do to make it feel better

1. Turn on your TV's Game Mode to cut input delay.
2. Switch off motion smoothing; it adds lag and artefacts.
3. If your TV supports VRR, leave it on. It will not raise the frame rate but can help with consistency.
4. Check the in-game settings for motion blur and camera options.

## Could 60 FPS come later?

Rockstar has not announced it. GTA 5 received a 60 FPS mode only when it was re-released on newer consoles years later. A PC version, if it arrives, would be the first place to expect higher frame rates: see [GTA 6 on PC](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/).
