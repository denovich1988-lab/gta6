**GTA 6 itself is not on Netflix.** What is there is a gameplay video, Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look, which Rockstar premiered on Netflix on August 27, 2026 before releasing it everywhere else.

## What the Extended Look is

| | |
|---|---|
| What | An official gameplay presentation |
| Length | About 26 minutes |
| Captured on | PlayStation 5 |
| Netflix premiere | August 27, 2026 |
| Free release | Later the same day on YouTube and Rockstar's site |

## Where to watch it now

1. **YouTube:** on the Rockstar Games channel.
2. **Rockstar's site:** on the official GTA VI page.
3. **Netflix:** where it premiered; availability there can change.

You do not need a Netflix subscription. The version on YouTube is the same video.

## What it shows

Missions with Jason and Lucia, a six-star wanted level with a new heat system, hands-on car theft, robberies and a long list of activities. Our summary is here: [GTA 6 gameplay: what the Extended Look showed](/news/gta-6-gameplay/).

## Can you play GTA 6 through Netflix?

No. Netflix's games catalogue does not include GTA 6. The game is sold for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only: see [GTA 6 platforms](/buying/gta-6-platforms/).
