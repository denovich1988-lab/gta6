**The GTA 6 Bigfoot sighting is at Cottonmouth Lake, just below Hamlet, and you can drive there almost as soon as the game lets you roam.** Players found it on launch day. What you meet is brief and strange: a large hairy figure on the road, a chase you will lose, and a scene that ends before you get a proper look. This page explains where to go, what happens, what the game itself calls the creature, and which parts of the story are still reported by only one guide.

The location and the encounter are described the same way by two independent guides. The name Skunk Ape and the follow-up questline come from VGC alone and are marked that way below.

## Where to find it

Drive to Cottonmouth Lake. Both location guides describe it the same way: just below Hamlet in Vice Dale County. Hamlet Correctional, the prison where the Prologue begins, takes its name from the same town. If you need bearings, our [GTA 6 map guide](/map/gta-6-map/) covers the regions.

VGC describes the trigger from a different angle: most players see the creature while driving towards the Kingfishers' camp near the water south of Hamlet. The Kingfishers' swamp shack south-west of Hamlet is where the story sends Jason in [Clean Up After Donnie](/missions/gta-6-clean-up-after-donnie-walkthrough/), so many players pass through the area early. VGC does not name the lake, so we cannot say for certain that the two descriptions are the same spot, only that both are on the water south of Hamlet.

**Tip:** go on a clear run without a wanted level, and keep the radio down. The growl is the cue both guides give for being in the right place.

## What happens when you get there

Insider Gaming and Critical Hits give the same sequence:

1. The creature is on the road, standing over the body of an animal.
2. When you approach, it runs into the marshland beside the road. You can follow, but both guides say you will probably lose it.
3. Where it was standing, an odd effigy is tied to a tree.
4. If you stay near the flowers there, they appear to knock your character out.
5. A unique scene plays: you drift in and out of consciousness, the creature comes close, and all you see is a pair of large, hairy feet.

**Why it matters:** the scene by the flowers is the real payoff, and it is easy to miss if you sprint after the creature and never come back to the tree. Let it run, then wait.

## Is it actually Bigfoot?

Not according to VGC, which puts the name in quotation marks in its headline for that reason. Its guide says the creature in the widely shared clip is a character that locals call the Skunkape, and that posters of it can be seen around Key Lento near the start of the game.

That fits the setting. The skunk ape is Florida's own version of the Bigfoot legend, a swamp creature of local folklore, and Leonida is Rockstar's version of Florida. The other two guides use Bigfoot or Sasquatch and do not mention the posters.

**Why it matters:** if you search the in-game world for "Bigfoot" you will find nothing by that name. Look for Skunk Ape posters instead.

## The posters and the second trigger

VGC describes a second way to reach the sighting: find enough of the Skunk Ape posters around town, and the game places a marker on your map in the general area of the first sighting. The guide does not say how many posters count as enough.

**Tip:** the posters are around Key Lento, where Jason spends much of Chapter 1. If you are still early in the story, keep an eye out for them on your way between missions; the full early order is in the [GTA 6 mission list](/missions/gta-6-mission-list/).

## The questline: Kirk and the hunt

This section rests on a single source, VGC, so treat it as reported and not yet confirmed.

According to that guide, the sighting is the first step of something longer. After you have seen the creature, let a few in-game days pass. A man called Kirk, described as the leading expert on the creature, then contacts you and asks for help tracking it, which adds a marker to the map. VGC says the questline can only be started as [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/), and only at night. Its coverage of the questline itself is in a separate guide marked for spoilers.

Insider Gaming and Critical Hits were written a little earlier and both say it was unclear whether the sighting was a one-off or the start of something bigger. VGC's report answers that question, but until a second guide describes Kirk's missions we are not listing them.

**Why it matters:** if you are playing as Lucia in Chapter 2 and nothing happens after the sighting, that may be why. Switch to Jason once switching unlocks; see [how to switch characters in GTA 6](/blog/how-to-switch-characters-in-gta-6/).

## Rewards: what you get and what you do not

No guide reports a cash reward, a trophy, a collectible or a challenge for the sighting. Critical Hits says outright not to expect one. Our pages on [GTA 6 collectibles](/map/gta-6-collectibles/) and the [trophy guide](/missions/gta-6-trophies/) do not list anything tied to it either.

**Why it matters:** this is an easter egg in the old sense, worth doing for the scene. It will not move your completion percentage as far as anyone has shown.

## Bigfoot in earlier GTA games

| Game | Bigfoot |
|---|---|
| GTA: San Andreas | A long-running fan myth; never in the game |
| GTA 5 | Appears briefly in the story mission Predator; the re-release hid further Sasquatch secrets that took players years to solve |
| GTA 6 | A roadside sighting at launch, with a questline reported |

Rockstar has played with the legend for two decades, mostly by teasing. In GTA 6 the creature was found on launch day, on a public road, by players who were not even looking for it. That is a change of approach: the secret here is not whether it exists but what it wants.

## What is not confirmed

- **Kirk's questline.** Its missions, length and rewards. One source so far.
- **The night-only condition.** VGC's wording leaves it unclear whether night applies to Kirk's call or to starting the quest.
- **The number of posters** needed for the map marker.
- **Whether the sighting repeats.** No guide says if the creature can be seen there again.
- **Other locations.** Insider Gaming says more may be found elsewhere and that it will update if so.
- **Whether the two descriptions of the place match exactly.** Cottonmouth Lake in two guides; the approach to the Kingfishers' camp in the third.

## How to see it: step by step

1. Play until you can drive around freely.
2. Open the map and set a waypoint at Cottonmouth Lake, below Hamlet.
3. Drive slowly along the road by the water and listen for the growl.
4. Stop when you see the figure over the carcass. Approach on foot.
5. Let it run. Do not waste time on the chase.
6. Find the effigy on the tree where it stood.
7. Stand by the flowers and wait for the scene.
8. Afterwards, carry on with the story and, as Jason, watch your phone over the next few in-game days.

Make a manual save first if you want to be able to watch it again: [how to save in GTA 6](/missions/gta-6-how-to-save/).

## What this means for you

It takes a few minutes and costs nothing, and it is one of the more memorable things in the opening hours, so it is worth the detour the next time you are near Hamlet. Do not expect a prize. If VGC's report holds up, the sighting is also the door to a longer hunt, and seeing it early means Kirk's call can arrive sooner.
