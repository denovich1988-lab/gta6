**GTA 6 comes out at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026.** That applies to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version at launch.

## How the midnight launch works

The game unlocks when the clock passes 12:00 a.m. on November 19 where you are. It is not one worldwide moment.

| Region | When it unlocks |
|---|---|
| New Zealand | 12:00 a.m. NZDT, November 19 (first in the world) |
| Australia (east coast) | 12:00 a.m. AEDT, November 19 |
| Japan and South Korea | 12:00 a.m. local time, November 19 |
| Central Europe | 12:00 a.m. CET, November 19 |
| United Kingdom | 12:00 a.m. GMT, November 19 |
| United States and Canada | 12:00 a.m. in your own time zone, November 19 |

In practice, your launch session starts late in the evening of Wednesday, November 18. Some reports say US store listings may unlock for the whole country at Eastern midnight, which would be earlier in the evening further west. Treat your own local midnight as the time you can rely on.

## Pre-load times

| Platform | Pre-load opened |
|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 | November 10 at midnight local time |
| Xbox Series X|S | November 10 at 12:00 a.m. GMT, worldwide |
| Boxed copies | From November 12, after you redeem the code in the box |

The download needs up to 212 GB of free space. See [GTA 6 file size](/blog/gta-6-file-size/) for the numbers and how to make room.

## Before midnight: a short checklist

1. Check that the pre-load finished and that no update is waiting.
2. Keep a few gigabytes free for a day-one patch.
3. Turn on automatic updates and leave the console in rest mode with internet access.
4. Sign in to the account that owns the game before the unlock time.
