**GTA 6 pre-order sales reached 32.5 million units before the game launched, according to Take-Two Interactive, which called it a record across all platforms.** The company gave that figure on November 20, 2026, one day after release, together with $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales. Before that, every pre-order number in circulation was an outside estimate. This page sets out the official figure, the estimates that led up to it, why the two do not match, and what pre-order buyers should have received.

How we checked: the official numbers come from Take-Two's press release. Every estimate is attributed to the firm that made it and appears in at least two reports, or is marked with the single outlet that carried it.

## GTA 6 pre-order sales at a glance

| Measure | Figure | Who says so |
|---|---|---|
| Units sold pre-release | 32.5 million | Take-Two (official) |
| Day-one retail sales, worldwide | $3.79 billion | Take-Two (company estimate through November 19) |
| Estimated digital console pre-orders, final | About 24.6 million units | Sensor Tower |
| Estimated revenue, pre-orders and launch day | About $2.36 billion | Sensor Tower |
| PlayStation share of pre-orders | About 75% | Sensor Tower |
| Ultimate Edition share of pre-orders | 82% | Sensor Tower, via Wccftech |
| Previous pre-order record in the same tracker | $709 million (Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II, 2022) | Sensor Tower, via Wccftech |
| Pre-orders opened | June 25, 2026 | Rockstar |

## The official number: 32.5 million units pre-release

Take-Two's press release is dated November 20, 2026. It says Grand Theft Auto VI "sold 32.5 million units pre-release" and describes that as a new record across all platforms. It is the only pre-order figure that comes from the company itself.

The release does not break the number down. It names no split between PlayStation and Xbox, between the two editions, or between digital and boxed copies. The Next Web noted that Take-Two did not say how the figure was calculated.

**Why it matters:** "pre-release" is Take-Two's word, and it is not defined. The safest reading is the plain one: units sold before November 19. Anything more specific is someone's interpretation.

## The $3.79 billion is not a pre-order figure

The same release puts day-one retail sales at $3.79 billion. A footnote says this is based on company estimates through the end of November 19, worldwide. Take-Two calls it a record for entertainment in any medium.

That number covers more than pre-orders. A quick check shows why: $3.79 billion divided by 32.5 million units is roughly $117 a unit, which is more than the $99.99 [Ultimate Edition](/buying/gta-6-ultimate-edition/) costs in the US. The revenue figure therefore has to include copies bought on launch day itself, on top of the 32.5 million sold beforehand. That division is ours, not Take-Two's, and regional prices vary, but the direction is clear.

**Tip:** when a headline says GTA 6 "made $3.79 billion in pre-orders", it has joined two separate statements. The units are pre-release; the dollars run to the end of launch day.

## How the estimates grew before launch

Until November 20 the only public numbers were Sensor Tower's estimates of digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. They rose slowly for months and then very fast.

| When | Estimated units | Estimated revenue | Reported by |
|---|---|---|---|
| First week after pre-orders opened | Not given | About $300 million | Sensor Tower, via Wccftech |
| About six weeks in | Not given | About $400 million | Sensor Tower, via Wccftech |
| November 3 | 7.71 million | Not given | Reuters, via VICE |
| November 16 | 14.94 million | About $1.45 billion | VICE |
| November 18 | About 17 million | About $1.65 billion | Yahoo Finance and others |
| Just before launch | About 20 million | Not given | IGN and others |
| Final tally | About 24.6 million | About $2.36 billion | GamesIndustry.biz, Wccftech |

Sensor Tower's analyst said the total more than doubled in the last ten days before release. The estimate passed $1 billion about a week before launch, according to VICE.

**Why it matters:** most people did not pre-order in June. They waited for reviews, trailers and the final week. That pattern is worth remembering the next time an early pre-order estimate is used to predict a launch.

## Why the official figure is higher than the tracker

Take-Two says 32.5 million. Sensor Tower's final estimate was about 24.6 million. Neither party has explained the gap of roughly eight million, but the two numbers do not measure the same thing.

| | Take-Two | Sensor Tower |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Company figure | Third-party estimate |
| What it counts | "Units sold pre-release", not defined further | Digital pre-orders on PS5 and Xbox Series X\|S |
| Boxed copies | Not stated | Not included, according to VICE |
| Published | November 20, 2026 | Updated through the pre-order period |

VICE pointed out before launch that the tracker covered digital copies only, so pre-orders of the [physical edition](/buying/gta-6-physical-copy/) were unknown. That is the most likely source of the difference, but it has not been confirmed by either side.

## PlayStation vs Xbox

By Sensor Tower's estimate, PlayStation accounted for about 75% of pre-order revenue and Xbox for about 25%. VICE reported the same three-quarters share in the November 16 update. Wccftech adds that PlayStation pre-orders opened a day before Xbox's and that the split barely moved afterwards.

Both platform holders made their own claims on November 10: Sony said GTA 6 was the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation Store, and Microsoft said the same of its store. Neither gave a count. For the hardware side of that split, see [GTA 6 platforms](/buying/gta-6-platforms/).

**Why it matters:** there is no official platform breakdown. The 75% figure is an estimate of digital pre-orders, not a statement about the 32.5 million.

## Most pre-orders were for the Ultimate Edition

Wccftech, reporting Sensor Tower's data, says 82% of pre-orders were for the $99.99 Ultimate Edition and 18% for the $79.99 Standard Edition. This is a single-outlet figure, so treat it with some caution. It does fit the revenue numbers: the tracker's totals work out to roughly $96 or $97 a unit at both the November 16 and final readings, close to the Ultimate price.

**Tip:** if you bought the Standard Edition and are wondering what the other four in five buyers got, the difference is 16 in-game items, none of them needed for the story. Our [price and editions guide](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/) compares the two.

In the same tracker, GTA 6's pre-order revenue is more than three times the previous record. Against its own predecessor, GTA 6 sold almost three times as many units before launch as GTA 5 sold on its first day, which our [sales page](/blog/how-much-money-has-gta-6-made/) puts at 11.21 million.

**Why it matters:** the GTA 5 comparison flatters GTA 6 slightly, because the 2013 figures count sales after launch and the 2026 figure counts sales before it. It is still the larger number by a wide margin.

## What Take-Two said before launch

Take-Two avoided numbers until the game was out. On its earnings call on November 9, chief executive Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders were far in excess of the company's expectations, while adding that the company was not ready to declare victory. The first hard figure came eleven days later, in the launch release, alongside a statement from Rockstar president Sam Houser thanking players.

For what the game cost to make, and how quickly these sales cover it, see [how much GTA 6 cost to make](/blog/gta-6-budget/).

## What is not confirmed

- **How the 32.5 million was counted.** Take-Two has not said whether it includes boxed copies shipped to shops, digital pre-orders only, or both.
- **An official platform or edition split.** The 75% and 82% figures are Sensor Tower estimates.
- **Pre-order revenue on its own.** The $3.79 billion includes launch day; the $2.36 billion is an estimate.
- **Sales since launch day.** Take-Two has published nothing after November 19. Weekend and first-week totals quoted online are analyst estimates.
- **Pre-orders by country.** None of the reports we checked gives a regional breakdown.

## If you pre-ordered: check what you received

Pre-orders came with extras, and they are easy to overlook once you are playing.

1. **Vintage Vice City Pack.** Included with pre-orders and with any copy bought before November 20, 2026. Our [Vintage Vice City Pack guide](/buying/gta-6-vintage-vice-city-pack/) lists the contents.
2. **One month of GTA+.** Included with digital pre-orders of either edition. Check when the free month ends if you do not want it to renew.
3. **Ultimate Edition items.** If you bought the $99.99 edition, confirm the extra items are showing in game.
4. **Pre-load.** Pre-order buyers could download ahead of release; there was no early play. See [GTA 6 early access](/blog/gta-6-early-access/) for what each edition did and did not unlock.
5. **Missing something?** Restore licences on your console first, then contact the store you bought from, with your order confirmation to hand.

## What this means for you

For players, the pre-order record changes nothing about the game on your console. It does say something about what comes next. A launch this large gives Rockstar every reason to keep supporting the game, and it makes the unanswered questions, a [PC version](/pc/gta-6-pc-release-date/) and an [online mode](/online/gta-6-online/), a matter of when they are announced. The critical response was just as strong: see our [GTA 6 reviews round-up](/blog/gta-6-reviews/).
