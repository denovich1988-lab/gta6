**The GTA 6 money cheat is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right.** It adds $250,000. Several launch guides report the same code; Rockstar has not published an official list, and we will retest it in the released game.

## How to use the money cheat

1. Finish the main story. Cheats are reported to unlock after the credits.
2. Save your game, because an active cheat turns saving off.
3. In free roam, press **Up on the D-pad** to open the phone.
4. Hold **L1 + R1** on PS5 or **LB + RB** on Xbox Series X|S.
5. Enter: **Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right**.

The code is D-pad only, so it is identical on both consoles.

## What it costs you

| Effect | While a cheat is active |
|---|---|
| Saving | Disabled |
| Trophies and achievements | Disabled for the session |
| Story progress | Not affected, since the story is already finished |

## Why it is not available earlier

GTA 5 had no money cheat at all. GTA 6 has one, but locks it behind the ending, so it cannot shortcut the story's economy. It is a reward for free roam.
