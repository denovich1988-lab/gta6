**GTA 6 reviews are overwhelmingly positive: the game has a Metacritic score of 99 as of November 19, 2026.** Reviews went live on November 18, the day before launch. Scores move as more reviews arrive, so treat the numbers below as a launch-day snapshot.

## GTA 6 review score on Metacritic and OpenCritic

| Site | Score on November 19, 2026 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Metacritic | 99 | About 150 critic reviews; none mixed or negative |
| OpenCritic | 98 to 99 | As reported by review round-ups on November 18 and 19 |

A 99 ties the highest Metascore on record, held since 1998 by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. For comparison, GTA 4 scored 98, and GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 each scored 97.

Metacritic's review count differed slightly between parts of its page when we checked, and round-ups quote between 146 and 156 reviews. User scores were not yet open.

## What critics praise

- **The world.** The scale, density and detail of Leonida and Vice City is the most common point of praise.
- **Jason and Lucia.** Reviewers describe the two-lead story as more personal than earlier GTA games.
- **Systems that connect.** Driving, traffic, police response and robberies are singled out for how they work together.
- **Polish.** Several reviews note how finished the game feels for its size.

## What critics criticise

Complaints are few, and none of them pulled a major score below 9.

- **30 FPS.** The game targets 30 FPS on every console, with no performance mode. See [GTA 6 60 FPS](/blog/gta-6-60-fps/).
- **Minor performance dips.** VGC's review mentions them and says they do not spoil the game.
- **No online mode at launch.** GTA 6 launched as a story game only.

## Is GTA 6 good?

By the reviews, yes. No critic listed on Metacritic gave it a mixed or negative score on launch day. If a steady 60 FPS matters to you more than anything else, that is the one reason reviewers give to hesitate. The story is long, too; see [how long GTA 6 is](/missions/gta-6-story-length/).

## Launch issues and the day-one patch

- **Day-one patch.** Version 01.002.001 rolled out before launch, at about 602 MB on PS5, and is larger on Xbox. Rockstar has not published patch notes. Install size is covered in [GTA 6 file size](/blog/gta-6-file-size/).
- **PS5 system update.** PlayStation Support said on November 17 that a PS5 system software update is required before playing GTA 6.
- **PSN sign-in problems.** On November 18, hours before launch, outage trackers logged more than a thousand reports of PlayStation Network problems. Sony's status page later showed services restored. Neither Sony nor Rockstar linked it to GTA 6.
