**Nobody outside Take-Two knows exactly what GTA 6 cost.** The company has never published a budget for the game. The figures you see online are estimates.

## What is confirmed and what is estimated

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| An official GTA 6 budget | Never published |
| Take-Two's CEO calling the game expensive | Confirmed; he gave no number |
| $1 billion to $1.5 billion in development | Analyst estimate, reported in May 2026 |
| $2 billion or more | Unverified; usually includes marketing |

## What Take-Two has said

Asked about the cost, chief executive Strauss Zelnick has said only that the game was expensive. Take-Two does not break out budgets for individual games in its financial reports.

## How it compares

| Game | Reported cost |
|---|---|
| GTA 5 (2013) | About $265 million, development and marketing |
| GTA 6 (2026) | Estimated $1 billion to $1.5 billion in development |

Even at the low end of the estimate, GTA 6 would be among the most expensive entertainment products ever made.

## Why it cost so much

- **Time.** The gap between GTA 5 and GTA 6 was 13 years.
- **Scale.** An entire state, more than 170 animal species, full performance capture and a licensed soundtrack with celebrity radio hosts.
- **People.** Rockstar's studios around the world worked on it.

## Will it earn that back?

The game costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition, and analysts expect record launch sales. We will add Take-Two's official sales figures here when the company reports them. Prices are in [GTA 6 price and editions](/buying/gta-6-price-editions/).
