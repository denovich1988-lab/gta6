**GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free storage.** That is Rockstar's own figure for the pre-load file, and Sony gives PS5 owners the same number as a minimum. It makes GTA 6 one of the largest console installs so far.

## GTA 6 file size by console

| Console | Download | Free space to plan for |
|---|---|---|
| PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro | About 210 GB | 212 GB |
| Xbox Series X and Series S | About 209.9 GB (Microsoft Store listing) | 212 GB |

Rockstar's wording is "up to 212 GB depending on your device" and it has not given a separate number for each Xbox model. Planning for 212 GB on any console is the safe choice.

## Why the boxed copy does not help

The physical edition of GTA 6 has a download code in the box instead of a disc. You redeem the code and download the full game, so a boxed copy takes exactly as much space as a digital one. Boxed copies were due in shops from November 12, a week before launch.

## How much of your console that is

A launch PS5 has roughly 667 GB of usable space, so GTA 6 takes close to a third of it. An Xbox Series S with a 512 GB drive has far less room to spare, and GTA 6 will use more than half of what is usable.

## How to make room

1. Open storage settings and sort games by size.
2. Delete large games you are not playing. You can download them again later.
3. Move PS4 or older Xbox games to an external USB drive. Current-generation games must run from internal or official expansion storage.
4. If you are still short, add an M.2 SSD on PS5 or a Storage Expansion Card on Xbox.
5. Leave a few gigabytes above 212 GB free for the day-one update.
