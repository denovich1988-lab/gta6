**To switch characters in GTA 6, hold Right on the D-pad and tap R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox Series X|S, but the option only works once the story unlocks it in Chapter 2.** Until then the game decides who you play. That catches out a lot of people coming from GTA 5, where you could jump between three characters within the first couple of hours. This guide covers the controls, the exact points in Chapter 1 where you do play as Lucia, why the portrait is sometimes greyed out, and what to finish before the game takes Jason away for a while.

The controls and unlock points below are confirmed by at least two independent launch guides, and the design reasons come from a Rockstar interview. Where only one guide reports something, we name it.

## Switching characters at a glance

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Controls | Hold **Right on the D-pad**, tap **R1** (PS5) or **RB** (Xbox) |
| Playable leads | Two: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos |
| Who you start as | Lucia in the Prologue, then Jason |
| First free roam | As Jason, after Gone Fishing |
| First time back as Lucia | Day Release, inside the mission only |
| Free switching | Chapter 2 (SVG: about 35% story completion) |
| Greyed-out portrait | The other lead is unavailable right now |
| In missions | Some fix the character, some force a swap, some let you choose |

## The controls

Hold **Right on the D-pad**. Two portraits appear in the bottom-right corner of the screen. With Right still held, tap **R1** on PlayStation 5 or **RB** on Xbox Series X|S to move to the other lead. VGC, SVG and Insider Gaming all give Right on the D-pad as the input; Insider Gaming and GamesRadar add the shoulder button.

**Tip:** if you learned GTA 5's version, the direction has changed. GTA 5 used Down on the D-pad for its character wheel. In GTA 6, Up opens the phone and Right is the character prompt. The rest of the early controls are in our [beginner's guide](/missions/gta-6-beginners-guide/).

## Why you cannot switch in Chapter 1

[Lucia](/characters/gta-6-lucia-caminos/) begins the story in prison. After the [Prologue](/missions/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/), which you play as her, the game hands you [Jason](/characters/gta-6-jason-duval/) in Key Lento and keeps you with him. Even after her release in [Coming Home](/missions/gta-6-coming-home-walkthrough/), the two live in the same house but the switch prompt does nothing useful: VGC explains that she is on parole, and the story is not ready to let her roam.

**Why it matters:** nothing is broken if the prompt does not respond in your first ten hours. It is the design. Rushing will not unlock it much sooner either; SVG's advice is not to race through the early missions just to get there.

## Every time you play as Lucia before switching unlocks

Chapter 1 does give you Lucia, but only inside missions:

| Mission | Who you control | Free roam as Lucia? |
|---|---|---|
| [Prologue](/missions/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/) | Lucia | No |
| Gone Fishing to Down to Earth | Jason | No |
| [Day Release](/missions/gta-6-day-release-walkthrough/) | Jason, then Lucia for the yacht and club, then Jason | No |
| Clear Skies to Recalibration | Jason | No |
| [Tourist Trap](/missions/gta-6-tourist-trap-walkthrough/) | Jason, then Lucia after he is wounded | No |
| [Home Sweet Home](/missions/gta-6-home-sweet-home-walkthrough/) | Lucia | No |
| Chapter 2 | Lucia first, then both | Yes |

**Tip:** Day Release is worth playing slowly. The choices you make as Lucia there, such as sharing a jet ski and dancing, are the first chances to raise the relationship from her side.

## When free switching unlocks

Guides agree on the outline and differ on the exact moment:

- **VGC** says your first open-world time as Lucia comes after Chapter 1 ends, and that swapping becomes available as you play through Chapter 2.
- **SVG** is the most specific: a job goes wrong at about 30% of the story, you play as Lucia while Jason recovers, and free switching arrives at about 35% story completion during a run of robberies.
- **Insider Gaming** estimates three to four hours of rushing, or ten to twelve at a normal pace, to reach the point where Lucia is out of prison and back with Jason. That is when both leads are on screen together, not when you can swap in free roam.

So plan on finishing Chapter 1 and the opening stretch of Chapter 2 before the D-pad prompt does what you expect.

## The stretch where Jason is unavailable

The last three missions of Chapter 1, Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time, run back to back with no free roam. VGC's warning page says you cannot explore as both leads again for several hours of Chapter 2. PowerPyx goes further: you cannot play as Jason for a long time, pocket cash carried into Tourist Trap is lost, and the Key Lento house does not come back.

**Why it matters:** this is the one place where the two-character structure can cost you something. Anything you want to do as Jason in the Keys, do it before Tourist Trap. Our [Recalibration walkthrough](/missions/gta-6-recalibration-walkthrough/) has the checklist.

## Why the portrait is greyed out

Once switching is unlocked, a greyed-out portrait means the other lead cannot be selected right now. VGC and SVG both describe it. The usual reasons:

- The story has them busy or elsewhere.
- You are in a mission that fixes the character.
- VGC reports a quirk: after you swap, the lead you left may call a RydeMe and leave the area, which can briefly grey out the menu.

**Tip:** if it is greyed out in free roam, wait a minute or finish what you are doing. If it stays that way, check for a story mission marker; the game usually wants you to play one first.

## How switching works in missions

Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, described three kinds of mission in an interview with Famitsu that GamesRadar translated: missions where you are fixed to one character, missions where the game moves you to the other at a set moment, and missions where you can alternate freely. He said switching is sometimes limited by story progress because the studio wants certain sections seen from one character's point of view.

He gave examples. One early mission was changed during development so that Jason leads it. A getaway sequence moves you from Jason firing out of the car to Lucia driving. Other missions leave the choice to you.

**Why it matters:** if the prompt is missing mid-mission, there is nothing to fix. Tourist Trap is the clearest Chapter 1 case of a forced swap.

## What the other character does

Two guides describe the companion side of the system. SVG says a successful swap puts you at the other lead's current position on the map straight away, and that you can ask the lead you are not controlling to meet you or to help, for example by drawing police attention or shooting from the passenger seat. Beebom's guide likewise describes calling the other lead over and giving them a job, such as driving while you ride along.

Time together also feeds the relationship. PowerPyx notes a "Jason & Lucia" menu that tracks it, and several [cheats](/cheats/gta-6-cheats/) unlock from hours and activities with your partner.

## What is not confirmed

- **Separate cash, weapons and wardrobes.** None of the guides we checked says clearly how money, guns and clothes are split between the two leads. We will add it once two sources agree.
- **The exact unlock mission.** "About 35%" comes from one guide. No second source names the mission yet.
- **Instant travel on swap.** Only SVG states that you jump to the other lead's position immediately.
- **Switching at any wanted level.** Not documented yet; see [how the wanted level works](/missions/gta-6-wanted-level/).

## What to do before the game locks you to Lucia

1. Finish the Key Lento side content you care about as Jason.
2. Move pocket cash into the bank. [Buck Me](/missions/gta-6-buck-me-walkthrough/) explains how.
3. Make a manual save in a spare slot before Tourist Trap.
4. Play the three closing missions in one sitting if you can; there is no free roam between them.
5. In Chapter 2, keep following the story missions until the Right D-pad prompt shows two live portraits.

## What this means for you

GTA 6 treats character switching as something the story earns, not a menu option. Expect to spend Chapter 1 as Jason, a few hours of Chapter 2 as Lucia, and the rest of the game with both. If you came for Lucia, she is worth the wait; if you prefer Jason, do his business before the end of Chapter 1.
