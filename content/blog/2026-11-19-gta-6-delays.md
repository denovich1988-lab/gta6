**GTA 6 was delayed twice.** It then launched on its third announced date, November 19, 2026.

## GTA 6 delay timeline

| Announced | Release target | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| December 2023 | 2025 | Trailer 1 gives a 2025 window |
| May 2, 2025 | May 26, 2026 | First delay |
| November 6, 2025 | November 19, 2026 | Second delay |
| November 19, 2026 | Released | The date held |

## Why it was delayed

Rockstar gave the same reason both times: more time to finish the game to the standard players expect. In its November 2025 message the studio apologised for adding to a long wait and said the extra months were for polish.

## Was there a third delay?

No. Through 2026, rumours of another delay, including a move to 2027, appeared regularly. Take-Two repeated the November 19 date in its financial updates, pre-orders opened in June, the Extended Look followed in August, and pre-loading began on November 10.

## From first delay to launch

- The first delay added roughly half a year to the original 2025 window.
- The second added just under six months, from May 26 to November 19, 2026.
