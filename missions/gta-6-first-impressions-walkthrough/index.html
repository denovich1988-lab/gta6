    GTA 6 First Impressions Walkthrough: Code 2404 and the Decoy

    GTA 6 First Impressions walkthrough: open the Waxman Island garage with code 2404, pick up Raul, hold off the police and swap to the van for $3,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • First Impressions is a Chapter 3 mission from Raul for both Jason and Lucia; it unlocks after Car Service.
    • The garage on Waxman Island opens with the code 2404 on the wall panel.
    • Jumping the half-raised bridge to Rialto Islands loses the police; the mission pays $3,000.

    This GTA 6 First Impressions walkthrough covers the first job with Raul in person: a getaway from a bank robbery that turns out to be a decoy. It is mostly a chase, with Jason shooting and Lucia driving. PowerPyx subtitles it Meeting with Raul.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission First Impressions (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Given by Raul
    Starts at The garage on Waxman Island, Vice Beach; go there with both characters
    Requirement Car Service
    Reward $3,000

    Stock up on healing items first. Jason takes fire on foot in the middle of the mission.

    Objectives

    The two guides word the on-screen goals differently. This is the order both describe:

    1. Open the garage and get in the car.
    2. Go to the meetup and pick up Raul.
    3. Hold off the cops.
    4. Drive to Rialto Islands.
    5. Go to the parking lot and switch to the van.
    6. Drive Raul to the drop-off.

    Step-by-step

    1. Open the garage with 2404

    At the Waxman Island garage, use the panel on the wall and enter 2404. The game puts you in control of Lucia. Get in the car.

    2. Pick up Raul

    Drive to the marked spot on the south side of Ocean Beach. Raul gets in, and the police show up at once: he has just robbed a Fleeca bank.

    3. Hold off the cops

    You play Jason, shooting from the car while Lucia drives.

    Tip: for a Professional or Hardened rating, aim at tires and engines. Shooting drivers also crashes their cars, but doing it often counts as Violent.

    The car then crashes. Keep officers away from it; shoot the ones who come close in the legs. Watch Jason's health and heal from the inventory when you need to.

    4. Reverse out and drive to Rialto Islands

    Control passes to Lucia. Back the car fully onto the street, then head for Rialto Islands, the islands between the two halves of Vice City. Jason and Raul keep shooting; your job is to avoid the police cars in your path.

    The bridge ahead is partly raised. Jump it and the police are left behind.

    5. Switch vehicles

    Drive to the marked parking lot. Get out quickly and take the van parked nearby.

    6. Drop off Raul

    Drive to the marker in Bayside. Raul explains that the bank job was a decoy for a bigger robbery on the other side of the city, pays you and gets out. That ends the mission.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. These are the points where the mission goes wrong in practice:

    • Low health after the crash. Jason is exposed on foot. Heal before officers close in.
    • Driving forward out of the crash. Reverse fully onto the street first.
    • Ramming police cars on the way to Rialto Islands. Steer around them; the bridge jump ends the chase for you.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $3,000
    Rating Tires, engines and leg shots keep a Professional or Hardened rating
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Raul's next job is Crosstown Traffic, and First Impressions is also one of the two missions needed for Caught Slipping. For how the chase rules work, see GTA 6 wanted level. Raul's jobs lead to the Cartel Heist; see GTA 6 heists. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the garage code in the GTA 6 First Impressions walkthrough?

    2404. Enter it on the wall panel beside the garage on Waxman Island. PowerPyx and GTABase both give the same code.

    How do you start First Impressions in GTA 6?

    Finish Car Service. Raul then asks you to collect a car and meet him; go to the garage on Waxman Island in Vice Beach with both Jason and Lucia.

    How do you keep a Professional rating in First Impressions?

    Shoot tires and engines to stop police cars and shoot officers in the legs. Both guides say shooting drivers pushes the rating toward Violent.

    What is the reward for First Impressions?

    $3,000. GTABase also marks the mission as required for 100% completion.
