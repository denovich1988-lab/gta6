    GTA 6 Trophy Guide: All 55 Trophies and Achievements

    GTA 6 trophy guide with the full list of 55 trophies and achievements, how to unlock each one, the one that may be missable and a short platinum roadmap.

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. On Xbox the list is worth 1,000G.
    • All 55 belong to the single-player game. The hardest is A Long Road, for 100% completion.
    • Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude, because of a meditation spot in Chapter 1.

    This GTA 6 trophy guide lists all 55 trophies and achievements, what each one asks for, and the order to do them in. The list is the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Story trophies are kept vague here to avoid spoilers. We only give a requirement when at least two guides agree on it.

    Missable GTA 6 achievements

    None of the story trophies can be missed. Two guides flag one trophy as possibly missable:

    • Positive Mental Attitude. One of the 18 meditation spots is tied to Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether it stays available later. Meditate when the game first offers it in Chapter 1 to be safe.

    Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

    GTA 6 platinum roadmap

    1. Play the story. This gives 12 trophies. Meditate in Chapter 1. Do stranger missions as they appear.
    2. Finish the stranger strands. Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have a trophy, and 8 strands in total give Strangers & Freaks. Kenton is away for a while after Chapter 1 and comes back later.
    3. Work through the 100% checklist. It shows up after the story. It covers most other trophies on the way, because it asks for more: 300 stunts against 250, 70 challenges against 50, 200 vehicles against 100. See GTA 6 100% completion.
    4. Clean up the one-off trophies. The tank, the airport, the jewelry store, 4-star escape and the hidden ones.

    Two trophies need more than the 100% checklist does. Positive Mental Attitude needs all 18 meditation spots, while 100% needs 15. Top Fun needs a win at every drone race location.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a GTA 6 trophy guide for the platinum?

    Yes. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, needs the other 54 trophies. Finish the story, then work through the 100% checklist, and clean up the stranger missions and one-off trophies.

    How many trophies and achievements does GTA 6 have?

    55 on PS5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. The Xbox list has the same entries and adds up to 1,000G.

    Are any GTA 6 trophies missable?

    Guides flag one as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. One meditation spot is tied to Chapter 1, so meditate there before you move on.

    What is the hardest GTA 6 trophy?

    A Long Road, the gold trophy for 100% completion. It needs far more than any other trophy, for example 300 stunts and 70 challenges.
