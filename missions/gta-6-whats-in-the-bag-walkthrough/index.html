    GTA 6 What's in the Bag? Walkthrough: Chapter 2 Begins

    GTA 6 What's in the Bag? walkthrough: the first mission of Chapter 2. Flip the statue, read the note, check the hard drive and get the open world back as Lucia.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • What's in the Bag? is the first mission of Chapter 2 and one of the shortest in the game.
    • As Lucia, you look through the bag from the Visitor Center: a small statue with a note under it, and a hard drive.
    • The Paradise Lost trophy for finishing Chapter 1 unlocks here, and free roam returns with Lucia as the playable lead.

    This GTA 6 What's in the Bag? walkthrough covers the opening mission of Chapter 2, a short scene in a motel room where Lucia finally looks at what she and Jason nearly died for. It takes a couple of minutes, but it matters: it is where Chapter 1's trophy unlocks and where the open world comes back.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission What's in the Bag? (first mission of Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia
    Starts at A motel room; PowerPyx names the Starlet Motel in Kelly County
    Requirement Finish Discharge Time; it follows directly
    Reward Paradise Lost trophy; free roam as Lucia
    Next Ambulance Chaser appears when you leave the room

    The guides place the motel slightly differently: VGC says Kelly County, GTABase says Port Gellhorn, and PowerPyx uses both. It is the same room. See Port Gellhorn for the area.

    Objectives

    1. Wake up at the motel.
    2. Look inside the bag and read the note.

    GTABase lists the two steps above; PowerPyx shows only "Read the note".

    Step-by-step

    1. Wake up

    The morning after the hospital escape, the news is on. The police captain from the Visitor Center is on screen blaming the two of them for what happened.

    2. The statue

    Lucia goes through the bag. Pick up the small statue of a woman and turn it over. A note is fixed underneath:

    From my collection. She reminded me of someone.

    Tip: VGC says to keep that line in mind for later.

    3. The hard drive

    The other item is a hard drive. There is no computer in the room, so nothing can be done with it yet. The mission ends.

    Where players fail

    There is nothing to fail. It is close to a cutscene.

    What changes after this mission

    Change Details
    Free roam Returns, with Lucia as the character you control (PowerPyx)
    Jason Recovering; VGC says early Chapter 2 is Lucia's and that you will soon be able to swap between the two
    Trophy Paradise Lost (Bronze, per PowerPyx) unlocks during the mission
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    For the swap itself, see how to switch characters in GTA 6.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Nothing optional is listed by any of the three guides.

    PowerPyx lists a task called Take Care of Jason next. The first job on the map is getting rid of the getaway vehicle: Ambulance Chaser. The full order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    What do you do in the GTA 6 What's in the Bag walkthrough mission?

    Examine the bag as Lucia. Flip the small statue of a woman and read the note underneath, then look at the hard drive, which cannot be opened yet because the two have no computer.

    What does the note under the statue say?

    "From my collection. She reminded me of someone." All three guides we checked quote the same line, and VGC suggests remembering it.

    Can you free roam after What's in the Bag?

    Yes, as Lucia. PowerPyx lists open-world play as Lucia as the result of this mission, while Jason recovers.
