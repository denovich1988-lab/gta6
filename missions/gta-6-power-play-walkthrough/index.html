    GTA 6 Power Play Walkthrough: Heist Plans and Armored Truck

    GTA 6 Power Play walkthrough: how Jason gets into Kordell's base and finds the real heist plans, and how Lucia survives the armored truck job and escape.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Power Play is a Chapter 4 mission from Kordell that Lucia starts at the crew's base in Salton after Child's Play; it pays $40,000.
    • You play both leads: Jason sneaks into the base and photographs the back of the whiteboard, then Lucia covers the crew during an armored truck robbery.
    • The optional mission Bad Driver! is gone for good once Power Play begins, so finish it first.

    This GTA 6 Power Play walkthrough covers the mission where Lucia rides along on a job with Kordell's crew while Jason breaks into their base to find out what they are really planning. It has three very different parts: a quiet break-in, a street shootout around an armored truck, and a long escape on foot and by car. The third part is where most of the danger is.

    Read this first: Bad Driver! closes here

    The optional mission Bad Driver! is missable, and Power Play is the cut-off. PowerPyx's page for that mission says it becomes permanently unavailable after Power Play and should be finished before it. If you still want to deal with Felipe, do that now.

    PowerPyx's mission list also places a time-sensitive optional Lucia mission, This is a Free Call From…, just before Power Play. Time-sensitive missions show an hourglass icon on the map, so check for one before you head to Salton.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Power Play (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup)
    Playable characters Lucia and Jason
    Given by Kordell
    Starts at Kordell's crew base, Salton, Vice City; Lucia starts it
    Requirement Child's Play
    Reward $40,000

    Tip: make a manual save first. Missions cannot be replayed, and the second half is a long fight. See how to save in GTA 6.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists twelve on-screen steps.

    1. Ask the receptionist about Kordell.
    2. Go to Kordell.
    3. Find a way inside the building.
    4. Reach the planning room.
    5. Find the heist plans.
    6. Place the cones on the road.
    7. Follow Kordell.
    8. Pick up a weapon.
    9. Cover the gang.
    10. Follow Kordell.
    11. Steal a vehicle.
    12. Lose the cops.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Kordell (Lucia)

    Take Lucia to the crew's base in Salton and talk to the receptionist. You are sent down the hall to the room where Kordell and the crew are. After the scene, control passes to Jason.

    2. Get into the building (Jason)

    Jason sneaks in over the roof.

    1. Go to the right side of the building and climb the ladder.
    2. Cross the roof to the far side.
    3. Drop down to the lower level there.
    4. Go in through the open door.

    3. Reach the planning room

    Head down to the ground floor. The planning room is behind the door on the right. Both guides open it with the Snap Gun, the lock tool you get from Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs.

    4. Photograph the heist plans

    What to photograph Does it count?
    Front of the whiteboard Not enough on its own
    Back of the whiteboard Yes: this side has the real plans
    Shirts, bags and other items in the room Optional; GTABase says they do not matter for progress

    Interact with the whiteboard to flip it over, then take the picture of the reverse side. It is easy to get stuck here, because the room has several things you can photograph and only the back of the board moves the mission on.

    5. Block the road (Lucia)

    Back with Lucia, take the cones from the truck and set them in a line across the road so traffic stops. Then follow Kordell.

    6. Cover the crew

    Kordell knocks over a box at the side of the road. Take the assault rifle from it. The crew goes to work on the armored truck, and police arrive from every side of the junction. Your job is to keep them off the crew until the truck is emptied.

    Tip: do not fix on one street. Both guides warn that officers come from all directions, so keep turning and check behind you between targets.

    7. Escape through the construction site

    When the crew is done, follow Kordell under the highway and into the construction site.

    1. Work up through the floors. The sides of the building are open, so watch them for police.
    2. Clear the rooftop and the balcony.
    3. Go up one more floor and jump across to the highway.
    4. Move along the highway between the crashed cars. Expect enemies ahead and on the right.

    8. Steal a car and lose the cops

    Kordell and Nate tell you to grab a car.

    Car Take it?
    The car facing you No: that way is blocked
    The small car at the far end, pointing down the road Yes

    Run to the end of the area, take the small car and concentrate on driving. Take the first right on the highway to get around the roadblock, then keep going until the police lose you. Our wanted level guide has more on shaking a chase.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a formal fail condition. The trouble spots they point to are these:

    • The whiteboard. Photographing only the front, or only the other items, does not finish the objective.
    • The junction. Police come from all sides while the crew is at the truck.
    • The construction site. The open floors let officers shoot in from outside.
    • The getaway car. Taking the car that faces you sends you toward a blocked route, and missing the first right leads to the roadblock.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where are the heist plans in the GTA 6 Power Play walkthrough?

    In the planning room on the ground floor of Kordell's base, behind the door on the right. Photograph the whiteboard, then turn it over and photograph the back: the real plans are on the reverse side.

    How do you start Power Play in GTA 6?

    Finish Child's Play, then take Lucia to Kordell's crew base in Salton, Vice City, and ask the receptionist about Kordell.

    What is the reward for Power Play?

    $40,000. Both PowerPyx and GTABase list the same payout.

    What should you do before starting Power Play?

    Play the optional mission Bad Driver! if you want it. PowerPyx says it becomes permanently unavailable once Power Play starts. A manual save is also worth making, because missions cannot be replayed.
