    How Long Is GTA 6? Story Length and Missions

    How long is GTA 6 story mode? A Rockstar studio head took about 80 hours with some optional goals. What that number means and how it compares to GTA 5.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • The only figure from Rockstar is about 80 hours, from one studio head's own playthrough.
    • That run included optional goals, so it is not a story-only time.
    • Launch guides report 91 main story missions.
    On this page 4
    1. What the 80 hours does and does not mean
    2. GTA 6 compared with earlier games
    3. How many missions are there?
    4. Planning your playthrough

    GTA 6 takes about 80 hours to finish, going by the one number Rockstar has given. Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, said his own playthrough took around 80 hours. It covered the main story and some optional goals that affect how scenes play out.

    What the 80 hours does and does not mean

    • It is one developer's playthrough, reported in an interview in August 2026.
    • It includes some optional content, so the story alone may be shorter.
    • It is not a completion time. Collectibles, challenges and activities add many more hours.

    GTA 6 compared with earlier games

    Game Main story
    GTA 6 About 80 hours (developer playthrough with optional goals)
    GTA 5 About 32 hours

    How many missions are there?

    Several launch guides report 91 main story missions, the number you must finish before cheats unlock. Rockstar itself has not published a count. Our GTA 6 mission list grows as each mission is verified, and it will give the real total.

    Planning your playthrough

    1. Cheats unlock only after the credits, so the first run is cheat-free: see GTA 6 cheats.
    2. Several cheats are tied to side activities such as workouts, shooting ranges and races. Doing these as you go saves time later.
    3. If you have limited hours, follow the story missions and leave collectibles for free roam.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is GTA 6 story mode?

    About 80 hours, going by the playthrough of Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson, which included some optional goals.

    How many missions are in GTA 6?

    Launch guides report 91 main story missions. Rockstar has not published a count; we are building the list as missions are verified.

    Is GTA 6 longer than GTA 5?

    Yes, by a wide margin. GTA 5's main story takes about 32 hours.
