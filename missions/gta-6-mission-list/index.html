    GTA 6 Mission List and Walkthrough Hub

    Updated from launchUpdated November 26, 202615 min readChecked against 4 sources

    Short answer: The GTA 6 mission list in story order: the Prologue and Chapter 1 missions, with 93 walkthroughs so far and more added as each mission is verified.

    Key facts

    • GTA 6 opens with the Prologue; Chapter 1 runs from Gone Fishing to Discharge Time, and the story has five chapters.
    • We add each mission to this list in story order once its name, objectives and steps are verified.
    • Every walkthrough covers objectives, a step-by-step route, common fail points and optional goals.

    This page is the home of our GTA 6 mission list. Rockstar Games has not published a list of missions, so we add missions here in story order as each one is verified in at least two independent walkthroughs. The first 93 are listed below, each with a walkthrough.

    How the mission list works

    • Story order. Missions are listed in the order the story unlocks them, grouped by the character or contact who gives them.
    • One page per mission. Each mission gets its own walkthrough, linked from this list.
    • Verified first. A mission appears only when its name, objectives and steps have been checked against the game.

    What each walkthrough includes

    1. Before you start: where the mission begins, which protagonist you play, and anything worth buying first.
    2. Objectives: the on-screen goals in order.
    3. Step-by-step route: what to do at each stage, with the safest approach.
    4. Where players fail: the usual causes of a mission failure and how to avoid them.
    5. Optional goals and rewards: bonus objectives and what the mission unlocks.

    What is confirmed about the story

    • You play as two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
    • The story is set across the state of Leonida; see the GTA 6 map guide.
    • Rockstar describes the launch game as a single-player experience; see GTA 6 online.

    The rows from Lawyer Up onward are in PowerPyx's order. GTABase places Eddie Acosta's party before Company Cars and Party Hard before Heavy Lifting; both of those missions only need Lawyer Up, so either order works. PowerPyx also lists a short entry, Pay Abe's Fee, after Party Hard, which GTABase does not have.

    Missable: Bad Driver! disappears once you start Power Play. Plan ahead: after Making Gainz, Jason and Lucia cannot be seen together in public until the VCIA Takedown, so do It's a Date! and other couple activities first.

    The rows from Too Much Baggage to Power Play are in PowerPyx's order, and each mission's requirements match on GTABase. Too Much Baggage, Watch It and Adventures in Babysitting only need Making Gainz, so the game can offer them earlier. PowerPyx also counts several short entries that GTABase folds into the missions around them or does not list: Blood Sweat and Gear, Call Nate, Nate Will Be in Touch, Rafa's Kidnapping and the optional, missable This is a Free Call From…

    The rows from Close Surveillance to Starting From Zero are in PowerPyx's order. Close Surveillance, Delivery Method, Team Building and Fully Catered all open after Power Play and can be done in any order; GTABase lists Headliners before them, but both guides require it before the heist. PowerPyx also counts two waiting entries that GTABase does not list, Heist Plans in Place and Call Valentina Back; they are explained in the Fully Catered and Tie Up Loose Ends walkthroughs.

    Point of no return: collecting the note at the Y Hotel starts Sunset, and both guides say that moves the story into Chapter 5. PowerPyx adds that most of Chapter 5 must be finished before free roam returns. Do That's Where the Money Is… and any side activities first.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many missions are in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not announced a mission count. Launch guides report 91 main story missions; we will confirm the number once the full story has been verified.

    Are the walkthroughs spoiler-free?

    Mission titles and steps are unavoidable in a walkthrough, but we keep story twists out of titles, summaries and the list on this page.

    Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

    No. PowerPyx, Beebom and Insider Gaming all report that GTA 6 has no mission replay and no mission medals. Rockstar has not commented, so keep a manual save before any mission you may want to play again.
