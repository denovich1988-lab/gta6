    This page is the home of our GTA 6 mission list. Rockstar Games has not published a list of missions, so we add missions here in story order as each one is verified in at least two independent walkthroughs. The first 54 are listed below, each with a walkthrough.

    How the mission list works

    • Story order. Missions are listed in the order the story unlocks them, grouped by the character or contact who gives them.
    • One page per mission. Each mission gets its own walkthrough, linked from this list.
    • Verified first. A mission appears only when its name, objectives and steps have been checked against the game.

    What each walkthrough includes

    1. Before you start: where the mission begins, which protagonist you play, and anything worth buying first.
    2. Objectives: the on-screen goals in order.
    3. Step-by-step route: what to do at each stage, with the safest approach.
    4. Where players fail: the usual causes of a mission failure and how to avoid them.
    5. Optional goals and rewards: bonus objectives and what the mission unlocks.

    What is confirmed about the story

    • You play as two protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
    • The story is set across the state of Leonida; see the GTA 6 map guide.
    • Rockstar describes the launch game as a single-player experience; see GTA 6 online.

    Both PowerPyx and VGC end Chapter 1 with Discharge Time. The two guides swap a few neighbours (Lab Fees and Just a Scratch, Buck Me and Clean Up After Donnie, Day Release and Clear Skies), and several early missions can be played in any order, so the numbers are a guide to the order rather than a fixed count.     Point of no return: Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time play back to back, and free roam does not return until Chapter 2. Read the checklist in the Recalibration walkthrough before you start Tourist Trap.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many missions are in GTA 6?

    Rockstar has not announced a mission count. Launch guides report 91 main story missions; we will confirm the number once the full story has been verified.

    Are the walkthroughs spoiler-free?

    Mission titles and steps are unavoidable in a walkthrough, but we keep story twists out of titles, summaries and the list on this page.

    Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

    No. PowerPyx, Beebom and Insider Gaming all report that GTA 6 has no mission replay and no mission medals. Rockstar has not commented, so keep a manual save before any mission you may want to play again.
