    GTA 6 Tourist Trap Walkthrough: The Point of No Return

    GTA 6 Tourist Trap walkthrough: what to do before the Chapter 1 point of no return, the Visitor Center locker, the escape as Lucia and the boat to Cal's.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Tourist Trap starts the three-mission finale of Chapter 1; once it begins there is no free roam until Chapter 2.
    • You start as Jason and finish as Lucia, fighting out of the Visitor Center and getting Jason to a boat.
    • The mission fails if Jason dies. Shoot the red-glowing canisters at the dock to stop the cars chasing you.

    This GTA 6 Tourist Trap walkthrough starts with a warning: this mission is the point of no return for Chapter 1. PowerPyx lists it as "Tourist Trap (Take Up Mattia's Offer)". It, Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time play one after another, and you do not get the open world back until Chapter 2. The steps below include the story turn that causes that, so read the preparation section first.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Tourist Trap (mission 20 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Take Up Mattia's Offer
    Playable characters Jason, then Lucia
    Starts at South beach of Key Lento: talk to Lucia
    Requirement Finish Recalibration
    Reward None listed
    Next mission Home Sweet Home, immediately

    Do these first

    1. Manual save in a spare slot. PowerPyx recommends keeping it for good.
    2. Deposit your cash. PowerPyx says pocket cash brought into the mission is lost, and suggests at least $5,000 in Family Savings. See Buck Me for how banking works.
    3. Finish Jason's business. PowerPyx says you cannot play as him for a long time and that the Key Lento house is gone for the rest of the game.
    4. Carry adrenaline shots and ammo. VGC recommends using both in the escape.

    VGC's advice is simple: there is no rush. If you still want to explore, do not start this yet.

    Objectives

    1. Find the locker in the Visitor Center and get the bag.
    2. Return to the car.
    3. Follow the cop.
    4. Pick up the bag.
    5. Follow Jason, protect Jason and escape with Jason.
    6. Escape to open water.
    7. Go to Cal's house.

    Step-by-step

    1. The locker

    Lucia is collecting trash on the beach. Talk to her, and the two accept a pickup job from Mattia, who drops them at the Visitor Center in disguise. Go up the ramp and along the long path to the lockers at the far end, and take the bag.

    2. The arrest

    On the way back to the car, police stop you. The bag was bait. Jason and Lucia are taken to an interrogation room under the Visitor Center.

    3. The interrogation room

    A police captain arrives who wants the bag for himself, and his men turn on the other officers. Jason gets free. Shoot the men coming at you and go for the bag.

    4. Switch to Lucia

    Jason is shot and badly hurt, and control passes to Lucia. Deal with the attacker, then fight out of the building behind Jason.

    Tip: VGC says to pick up the weapons and ammo enemies drop, and to use Zombix and adrenaline shots when you can. PowerPyx notes that the officers you shoot here do not count against your criminal profile.

    5. Get Jason to the dock

    Stay close to him. At the docks Lucia drags Jason toward the boat with one arm and fires her pistol with the other.

    6. Stop the cars

    Vehicles race toward the dock. Enter focus mode with L3 + R3: anything glowing red is flammable. Shoot the canisters as the cars pass to blow them up.

    7. Open water, then Cal's

    Drive the boat out along the pink waypoint. Lucia calls Cal, and you steer to his house. Cal takes Jason on to a hospital in Vice City.

    Where players fail

    • Jason dies. It is the fail state VGC lists. Do not run ahead of him.
    • Ignoring the canisters. Shooting the cars' occupants one by one is slower than one explosion.
    • Starting unprepared. The cash and save warnings above cannot be fixed afterwards.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)
    Lost Pocket cash; the Key Lento house (PowerPyx)
    Paused Kenton's dives, fish for Cal, Kingfishers hideouts: PowerPyx says they return later

    Home Sweet Home begins as soon as Lucia is back at Jason's house. For when you get both leads back, see how to switch characters in GTA 6.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Tourist Trap the point of no return in GTA 6?

    Yes. VGC and PowerPyx both say that once Tourist Trap begins you are locked into the last missions of Chapter 1 and cannot return to the open world until Chapter 2. Make a manual save first.

    What should you do before the GTA 6 Tourist Trap walkthrough mission?

    Save in a separate slot, deposit your pocket cash (PowerPyx says it is lost, and suggests $5,000 in Family Savings) and finish anything you want to do as Jason in Key Lento.

    How do you protect Jason in Tourist Trap?

    Stay close to him on the way to the dock, pick up weapons and ammo from fallen enemies, and at the dock use focus mode (L3 + R3) to spot red flammable canisters and blow up the arriving cars.
