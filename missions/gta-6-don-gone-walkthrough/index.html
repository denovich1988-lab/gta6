    GTA 6 Don Gone Walkthrough: Donnie's Trailer and the .38

    GTA 6 Don Gone walkthrough: where every item is in Donnie's trailer, how to handle the three men outside, and the missable H&L .38 in the car trunk.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Don Gone is a short clean-up job for Brian: pack Donnie's things, drive his car out of Key Lento and destroy it.
    • Five items go in the bag: a photo, the flight schedule, clothes, Donnie's ID and the map on the fridge.
    • Open the trunk before you destroy the car: PowerPyx says a unique pistol, Donnie's H&L .38, is inside and can be missed.

    This GTA 6 Don Gone walkthrough lists every item you need in Donnie's trailer, what to do about the three men waiting outside, and the one thing you can miss for good: a unique pistol in the trunk. PowerPyx lists it as "Don Gone (Head to Donnie's Trailer)". It follows directly from the events of Swamped.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Don Gone (mission 18 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Head to Donnie's Trailer
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Donnie's trailer, Key Lento
    Requirement Starts automatically after Swamped
    Reward Donnie's H&L .38 (missable)

    Brian wants the trailer to look as if Donnie packed up and left town.

    Objectives

    1. Find and collect Donnie's bag.
    2. Pack Donnie's belongings.
    3. Get in the car and leave Key Lento.
    4. Destroy Donnie's car.

    Step-by-step

    1. The flag and the bag

    VGC starts with taking down the flag outside the trailer. Inside, the bag is on the bed. Pick it up.

    2. Pack the trailer

    Tip: PowerPyx says focus mode (L3 + R3) highlights what you still need.

    Item Where
    Photo On the wall next to the TV
    Flight schedule By the "Patriot" neon sign, on the nightstand
    Clothes Near the bed: a shelf (PowerPyx), drawers or wardrobe (VGC)
    Donnie's ID On a shelf: right of the door (PowerPyx), across from the fridge (VGC)
    Map On the fridge

    Two extras are in the room. PowerPyx lists a message to play on the phone. Both guides mention an optional prompt to "do one for Donnie"; PowerPyx says the drug lowers health, briefly boosts stamina and has no effect on the mission.

    3. The three men outside

    When you step out, the three military men from the bar fight in Coming Home are waiting. VGC treats the fist fight as required and says they are a little more aggressive than before. PowerPyx says you can also run to Donnie's car and drive off.

    If you fight: block first, then counter, as you did against D-Man.

    4. Leave Key Lento

    Load up and drive Donnie's car out of the region, following the route north.

    5. Open the trunk first

    Before you do anything to the car, open the trunk and take Donnie's H&L .38. PowerPyx marks it as a missable unique weapon variant.

    6. Destroy the car

    Pick a quiet spot. Any of these works:

    • Take the gas can from the car, pour it over the car and light it.
    • Shoot the engine until it explodes (VGC).
    • Drive it into the ocean and let it sink (PowerPyx).

    VGC ends the mission with a call from Brian as you leave the area.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail state. The real risk is destroying the car with the pistol still in the trunk.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Missable Donnie's H&L .38, in the trunk (PowerPyx)
    Optional The phone message; "do one for Donnie"

    PowerPyx lists a follow-up task next, Donnie's Lost Drugs, before Lucia calls to start Recalibration. For other guns, see GTA 6 weapons.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where are all the items in the GTA 6 Don Gone walkthrough?

    The bag is on the bed. The photo is on the wall next to the TV, the flight schedule is by the Patriot neon sign, the clothes are near the bed, Donnie's ID is on a shelf and the map is on the fridge.

    How do you get Donnie's H&L .38 in Don Gone?

    Open the trunk of Donnie's car before you destroy it. PowerPyx lists the pistol as a missable unique weapon variant.

    How do you destroy Donnie's car in Don Gone?

    Use the gas can in the car to burn it, shoot the engine until it blows (VGC), or drive it into the ocean and let it sink (PowerPyx).
