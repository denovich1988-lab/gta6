    GTA 6 Too Much Baggage Walkthrough: Scope Out the Airport

    GTA 6 Too Much Baggage walkthrough: scan the crates on Omar's tour, hitch the baggage tug, get the bag throw right and plant the bug in the security office.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Too Much Baggage is a Jason-only Chapter 4 mission given by Andrea that starts at Vice City International Airport after Making Gainz.
    • Jason works a shift as a baggage handler under Omar while scanning points of interest for Karim through camera glasses.
    • The goal is to plant a device behind the toilet in the security office restroom, then tell Omar you want the job.

    This GTA 6 Too Much Baggage walkthrough covers Jason's undercover shift as a baggage handler at Vice City International Airport. There is no shooting and no chase. The mission is a string of small tasks, and the two that slow players down are the bag-throwing power bar and finding the right door to the security office.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Too Much Baggage (Chapter 4); PowerPyx subtitles it Scope Out the Airport
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Andrea
    Starts at Vice City International Airport (VCIA), Vice-Dale County
    Requirement Making Gainz
    Reward None listed

    The mission is part of the setup for the airport job. At the end of Making Gainz, Raul decides that Jason will be the one to scope out the airport, while Lucia keeps working on Kordell's crew at the gym. Because Making Gainz starts the stretch where the couple cannot be seen together in public, this is a solo trip.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists thirteen on-screen steps.

    1. Enter the parking lot.
    2. Go to the office.
    3. Follow Omar.
    4. Get in the baggage tug.
    5. Attach the trailers to the baggage tug.
    6. Go to the plane.
    7. Pick up the bag.
    8. Go to the loading bay.
    9. Go to the security office.
    10. Enter the restroom.
    11. Plant the device.
    12. Return to Omar.
    13. Talk to Omar.

    Step-by-step

    1. Get into the airport

    Drive Jason to VCIA and stop at the gate to speak to the guard. Drive into the parking lot, park, and walk to the nearby office to meet Andrea. Output Lag adds that the entrance is the north gate and that Jason tells security he is there for orientation.

    2. Scan the crates on the ride with Omar

    Omar, the head baggage handler, drives Jason into the airport. Jason is wearing glasses that feed what he sees to Karim. During the ride the view is first person, and the glasses highlight white crates around you.

    Look at each highlighted crate and hold your view on it until the highlight turns green. Each one is a point of interest for Karim. If the camera feels awkward here, our guide to first-person mode explains the view controls.

    3. Hitch the baggage tug

    When Omar stops, follow him on foot. He leads you to the baggage carts. Get in the baggage tug next to them and reverse it into the two trailers to attach them.

    Tip: Output Lag's advice is to line the tug up straight with the first trailer and back up slowly. If you come in at an angle and it does not connect, pull forward, straighten and try again.

    4. Drive to the plane and load the bags

    Drive the tug to the plane that is being unloaded in the southern part of the airport. Here Jason throws bags to Omar, who stacks them on the trailer.

    Step What to do
    Pick up Take a bag when prompted
    Charge Hold the throw button to fill the power bar (PowerPyx gives R2 on PlayStation, RT on Xbox)
    Release Let go a little before the bar reaches the middle zone
    Repeat Keep going until every bag is loaded

    The reason for releasing early is that the bar keeps rising for a moment after you let go. Both PowerPyx and GTABase make the same point: aim short of the target zone and the overshoot carries it in.

    5. Slip away to the security office

    Once the bags are done, Jason makes an excuse to leave Omar. This is the part with the most room for getting lost, so take it one turn at a time:

    1. Leave the loading bay.
    2. Follow the wall south to the last double doors on the left and go in.
    3. Inside, keep to the left wall until you reach the hallway.
    4. Go through the blue office door. This is the security office.
    5. The restroom is immediately on your right. Go in.

    6. Plant the device

    Interact with the spot behind the toilet to place the device. Output Lag describes it as a Wi-Fi cloner that lets Karim pull data from the security system nearby; PowerPyx and GTABase simply call it a bug or device.

    7. Return to Omar and take the job

    Leave the restroom and you are taken back to Omar. When he talks to you, focus on him (PowerPyx gives L2 or LT) and tell him you want the job. That ends the mission.

    Where players fail

    None of the three guides lists a fail condition. The trouble spots are practical:

    • Bags falling short or flying long. Release before the middle of the bar, not on it.
    • Missing a crate on the ride. Keep looking at a highlighted crate until it turns green before moving to the next.
    • Wrong door. It is the last set of double doors on the left as you follow the wall south from the bay, then the blue door inside.
    • Trailers not attaching. Reverse straight and slowly.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed (PowerPyx)
    Unlocks Progress on the VCIA Takedown setup
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    No guide lists an optional objective for this mission. Missions cannot be replayed, so if you want a restore point before it, see how to save in GTA 6.

    What comes next

    Both PowerPyx and GTABase place Jason's next mission as Watch It, a job with Dre'Quan in Vice Beach that also needs only Making Gainz. PowerPyx notes that missions can show up in a slightly different order in the game, so do whichever marker appears first. The airport comes back much later in the chapter; GTA 6 heists outlines the whole chain, and the full order is in our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do you plant the device in the GTA 6 Too Much Baggage walkthrough?

    Behind the toilet in the restroom of the security office. Leave the loading bay, follow the wall south to the last double doors on the left, keep to the left wall into the hallway and go through the blue office door. The restroom is on your right.

    How do you throw the bags in Too Much Baggage?

    Hold the throw button to fill the power bar and let go just before it reaches the middle zone. The bar keeps moving for a moment after you release, so releasing early lands it in the right place.

    How do you start Too Much Baggage in GTA 6?

    Finish Making Gainz, then take Jason to Vice City International Airport. Talk to the guard, park in the lot and walk to the nearby office to meet Andrea.

    Is there a reward for Too Much Baggage?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. The mission is a setup step for the VCIA Takedown, and GTABase marks it as required for 100% completion.
