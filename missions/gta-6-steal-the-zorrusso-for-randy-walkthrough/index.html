    GTA 6 Steal the Zorrusso for Randy Walkthrough: Key Cloner

    GTA 6 Steal the Zorrusso for Randy walkthrough: install the Key Cloner, find the car at Hathaway Apartments and beat the tracker for up to $31,250.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Steal the Zorrusso for Randy is a Chapter 3 mission, also titled Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs; it unlocks after Crosstown Traffic.
    • The Zorrusso is on the ground level, east side, of the walled garage at Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach.
    • It pays $21,250 plus $10,000 if the car arrives in good condition, and unlocks the Key Cloner and Randy's Wish List.

    This GTA 6 Steal the Zorrusso for Randy walkthrough covers the mission that gives you the Key Cloner, the tool for stealing high-end cars. It is short, pays $21,250 or more, and opens Randy's Wish List. PowerPyx lists it under the title Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Steal the Zorrusso for Randy (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Jason or Lucia
    Given by Randy
    Starts at Hathaway Apartments, Ocean Beach, Vice Beach
    Requirement Crosstown Traffic
    Reward $21,250, plus $10,000 for good condition

    Randy is the dealer at Leonida Auto World who became a vehicle drop-off in Car Service.

    Objectives

    1. Get into the garage.
    2. Install the Key Cloner.
    3. Find and steal the Zorrusso.
    4. Drive the Zorrusso to Randy.
    5. Follow Randy to the Wish List.

    Step-by-step

    1. Climb into the garage

    The parking garage at Hathaway Apartments is walled. Climb the wall. Randy calls as you get in and explains two things: the Key Cloner, and the advanced tracker fitted to this car.

    2. Install the Key Cloner

    Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone and choose Install key_cloner_v1.6.9; PowerPyx says it is the bottom option. One install covers both Jason and Lucia.

    3. Steal the Zorrusso

    The car is on the ground level, on the east side. Interact with the door, then interact again to use the Key Cloner. It works like the Slim Jim: when the indicator turns pink, push the stick in the matching direction.

    4. Beat the tracker

    A red icon above the minimap is the tracker. A bar around it fills when you are not driving fast enough. If it fills, your position is sent to the police.

    Tip: the bonus needs a car in good condition, and the tracker punishes slow driving. Pick a route with wide roads before you pull out, so you can keep your speed without scraping traffic.

    Near Randy's showroom the icon turns grey: the tracker is off. Drive around the lot to the garage door and go inside.

    5. Follow Randy

    Randy comments on the state of the car. Good condition earns the extra $10,000; otherwise he mocks your driving. Follow him to the board, where he explains the Wish List.

    Randy's Wish List

    The Wish List is a set of rare cars Randy pays well for. Their locations start as unknown. You can find them on your own or ask Randy for tips, one at a time. PowerPyx compares it to Moses's car requests in Chapter 2; see the Moses walkthrough.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition; the tracker brings police, not a mission failure. What costs you:

    • Crawling through traffic. The tracker bar fills and the police get your position.
    • Scraping the car. It costs the $10,000 bonus.
    • Stopping at the front of the showroom. The delivery point is inside the garage, around the lot.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $21,250
    Bonus $10,000 for delivering the car in good condition
    Unlock Key Cloner for high-end cars; cars stolen with it can be sold to Randy (PowerPyx)
    Side content Randy's Wish List; all deliveries are needed for the Supply & Demand trophy
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the car in the GTA 6 Steal the Zorrusso for Randy walkthrough?

    In the walled parking garage of Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach, on the ground level on the east side. Climb the wall to get in.

    How does the Key Cloner work in GTA 6?

    Open the phone and install key_cloner_v1.6.9; it installs for both Jason and Lucia. At the car door, use the cloner and push the stick in the right direction when the indicator turns pink, as with the Slim Jim.

    How much does Steal the Zorrusso for Randy pay?

    $21,250, plus a $10,000 bonus if you deliver the car in good condition.

    Is Randy's Wish List required?

    Not for the story. Both guides say delivering every Wish List car is needed for the Supply & Demand trophy.
