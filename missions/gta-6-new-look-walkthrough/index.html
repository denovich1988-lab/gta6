    GTA 6 New Look Walkthrough: Change Lucia's Hair

    GTA 6 New Look walkthrough: visit the Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn, buy any hairstyle for Lucia and finish the mission in about a minute.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • New Look is a one-minute Chapter 2 mission: Lucia changes her hair at the Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn.
    • Buying any hairstyle completes it at once; there is no required style.
    • It unlocks after Girl's Trip and has no listed reward.

    This GTA 6 New Look walkthrough is short because the mission is: Lucia needs a different hairstyle, and one purchase ends it. After the escape that closes Chapter 1 she has to look less like herself. The mission pairs with How Do I Look?, which does the same for her clothes.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission New Look (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Lucia (self-started)
    Location Pearl House Hair Salon, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Girl's Trip
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    1. Speak to the stylist and change your hair.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go to the salon

    Follow the map marker to the Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn.

    2. Talk to the stylist

    Start the conversation to open the list of styles.

    3. Buy any hairstyle

    Pick whichever you like and pay. The mission completes on the purchase.

    Tip: you keep whatever you choose, so pick something you want to look at for the rest of the chapter. How salons, barbers and wardrobes work in general is in how to change clothes and hair in GTA 6.

    If you are short of cash

    Lucia starts the chapter nearly broke. PowerPyx and GTABase both give the same fallback: mug someone on the street or empty the register of a different store. Either can bring a wanted level; see how the wanted level works.

    A cleaner option once you have done Moses: sell him an untracked stolen car.

    Where players fail

    There is no fail state in either guide. The only way to get stuck is not having enough money for the cheapest style.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None (PowerPyx)
    Length About one minute
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Neither guide lists hairstyle prices. Next in the same errand run: How Do I Look?. Chapter order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the hair salon in the GTA 6 New Look walkthrough?

    It is the Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn. The mission is marked on the map once Girl's Trip is finished.

    Which hairstyle do you need for New Look?

    Any. PowerPyx and GTABase both say the mission ends as soon as you buy one.

    What if you cannot afford a haircut in New Look?

    Both guides suggest mugging someone or robbing another store's register first. After the Moses mission you can also sell an untracked stolen car.
