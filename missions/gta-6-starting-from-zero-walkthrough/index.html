    GTA 6 Starting From Zero Walkthrough: Chapter 4 Finale

    GTA 6 Starting From Zero walkthrough: find Andrea at Aqua Stadium without being spotted, swap cars, reach Big House Storage and what the penthouse choice does.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Starting From Zero is the last mission of Chapter 4 and begins the moment Sunset ends, at a junkyard in Southside.
    • Police are searching the city: if they spot you on the way, Andrea leaves the stadium and you collect what she left behind instead.
    • The choice in the penthouse changes nothing; the mission ends by starting Bird in the Hand, the first mission of Chapter 5.

    This GTA 6 Starting From Zero walkthrough covers the final mission of Chapter 4, in which Jason and Lucia cross a city full of police to collect two things and meet a new contact. There is very little shooting. It is a mission about not being seen, and it ends with a choice that looks important and is not.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Starting From Zero (Chapter 4 finale)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Starts Automatically after Sunset, at a junkyard in Southside, Vice City
    Reward No cash listed; PowerPyx lists the Top of the World trophy
    Next mission Bird in the Hand (Chapter 5)

    You cannot prepare for this one: it follows Sunset without a break, so any saving or shopping has to happen before that mission.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists eight on-screen steps.

    1. Go to the Aqua Stadium.
    2. Find Andrea.
    3. Go to Big House Storage Inc.
    4. Go to the elevator.
    5. Follow the hostess.
    6. Take the elevator to the terrace.
    7. Follow Giorgio.
    8. Follow Dardan.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive to Aqua Stadium

    Leave the junkyard and take the car parked nearby. Police are searching the city for the pair.

    If you are… What happens
    Not spotted You meet Andrea in the stands
    Spotted Andrea leaves and drops the hard drive for you to collect

    Tip: if you have cash, both guides mention an optional stop at a nearby clothing store. A change of outfit makes the pair harder to recognise. It is not required.

    2. Find Andrea

    Run up into the stands. Andrea is sitting near the southwest end.

    3. Swap cars

    Police pull up at the front of the stadium while you talk, so the car you arrived in is no good. Head south toward the boat storage area and take the car parked in front of the building there.

    4. Go to Big House Storage Inc.

    Drive carefully to the storage unit in Crosstown and walk in. The pair collect what they came for automatically. GTABase says it is the statue; a statue was also at the centre of Dubious Provenance in Chapter 3.

    5. The hotel

    A text from Mattia sends you to a hotel, and the game drives you there.

    1. As Lucia, go inside and take the elevator.
    2. Upstairs, a hostess stops you. Follow her to the table.
    3. When control passes to Jason, go in and take the elevator to the terrace.

    6. The penthouse test

    In Giorgio's penthouse you are handed a gun and a decision about Mattia.

    Option Result
    Pull the trigger The gun is not loaded; Mattia is unharmed
    Do nothing and let the time run out Same outcome

    Both guides agree it is a test by Giorgio and that neither answer changes anything.

    7. Follow Giorgio and Dardan

    Follow Giorgio around the penthouse, then follow Dardan to the bedroom. Jason and Lucia go to bed, the mission ends, and Bird in the Hand begins.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition. Being seen by police changes how you get the hard drive, but it does not end the mission.

    • The first drive. Stay away from patrols on the way to the stadium.
    • The stadium exit. Do not go back to the car at the front; police are there.
    • The drive to Crosstown. GTABase's word is "carefully": this is not the time to speed.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed
    Trophy Top of the World (PowerPyx)
    Choices Shoot or spare Mattia: no effect
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    That is the end of Chapter 4.     Frequently asked questions

    Where is Andrea in the GTA 6 Starting From Zero walkthrough?

    In the stands at Aqua Stadium, sitting toward the southwest end. Run up into the stands to find her.

    Should you shoot Mattia in Starting From Zero?

    It makes no difference. Both guides say the gun is not loaded and the moment is a test, so Mattia is unharmed whether you pull the trigger or let the time run out.

    What happens if the police see you in Starting From Zero?

    Andrea says she is leaving and drops the hard drive for you to pick up. The mission carries on.

    What comes after Starting From Zero?

    Bird in the Hand, the first mission of Chapter 5. It starts automatically.
