    GTA 6 Watch It Walkthrough: Crowex Dealer, Nola and Royce Jones

    GTA 6 Watch It walkthrough: meet Dre'Quan, survive the Crowex dealer ambush on the Cruda parking lot roof, pick an outfit at Nola and meet Royce Jones.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Watch It is a Jason-only Chapter 4 mission from Dre'Quan that starts at a store in Washington Beach after Making Gainz.
    • A fist fight with the Crowex dealers on the top floor of the Cruda parking lot turns into a shootout when a car of gunmen arrives.
    • The outfit choice at Nola is free and has no listed consequence; the mission ends at the Doight d'Or hotel with Royce Jones.

    This GTA 6 Watch It walkthrough covers Jason's errand with Dre'Quan that begins with a complaint about a watch and ends in a meeting with a nightclub owner. Most of it is driving and following. The one part that can go wrong is the parking lot, where a fist fight becomes a gunfight with very little warning.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Watch It (Chapter 4); PowerPyx subtitles it Meet Dre'Quan
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Dre'Quan Priest
    Starts at A marked store in Washington Beach, Vice Beach, Vice City
    Requirement Making Gainz
    Reward None listed

    Bring a loaded gun and some armor if you have it. You do not need them for the first few minutes, but you will on the parking lot roof. Our GTA 6 weapons guide lists what is available.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists nine on-screen steps.

    1. Meet up with Dre'Quan.
    2. Follow Dre'Quan.
    3. Go to the Cruda parking lot.
    4. Meet the Crowex dealer.
    5. Accompany Dre'Quan.
    6. Escape the area.
    7. Go to Nola.
    8. Go to Doight d'Or.
    9. Follow Dre'Quan.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Dre'Quan at the store

    Go into the marked store as Jason and head upstairs. Dre'Quan is talking to the clerk; wait for him to finish. Output Lag describes the place as a jewelry store where Dre'Quan has just been told that his watch is not what he paid for, which fits what happens next.

    Follow him back outside and get in your car.

    2. Drive to the Cruda parking lot

    Drive south to the marked parking lot. The men who sold Dre'Quan the watch are waiting there. Go in through the entrance and take the ramps all the way to the top floor, then park next to the marked car.

    3. The Crowex dealer: fist fight, then gunfight

    Dre'Quan walks over to talk to the men in the car. Stay close. The talk goes badly, they get out, and a fight starts.

    Phase What happens What to do
    1 Two men from the marked car attack Help Dre'Quan knock them out with your fists
    2 A car full of gunmen pulls up and opens fire at once Get behind something solid and shoot back
    3 More enemies arrive from the direction of the stairs Watch the stairwell and finish them

    Tip: the second phase is the dangerous one, because you go from melee to being shot at in the open. As soon as the two dealers are down, move toward cover near your car and get a gun out before the new car stops. The weapon wheel guide shows the quickest way to switch.

    4. Escape the area

    When the last enemy is down, get back in your car and leave. Both PowerPyx and GTABase say to go before the police arrive, so do not stop to look around. Output Lag's version has the police already on the scene at this point and recommends leaving by the stairs, not the ramp; we could not confirm that against the other two guides. If you do pick up attention, how the wanted level works covers losing it.

    5. Pick an outfit at Nola

    Drive to Nola, a store near the south end of Ocean Beach. Dre'Quan wants you both to look right for the meeting. Two outfits are on display on mannequins:

    • Choose either one. Dre'Quan pays.
    • He takes the one you leave.

    Neither PowerPyx nor GTABase lists any difference between the two beyond the look, so pick the one you prefer. For changing back later, see how to change clothes and hair.

    6. Meet Royce Jones at the Doight d'Or

    Drive north to the Doight d'Or hotel. Follow Dre'Quan through the building and out the back, where he meets Royce Jones. GTABase identifies Royce as the owner of the VYB nightclub. The mission ends when their conversation does.

    Output Lag adds that Dre'Quan is asking for a headline slot for the Dimez and that Royce wants a favor first. We have that from one guide only, so we will fill in the story details when a second source matches.

    Where players fail

    No guide lists a formal fail condition. In practice there are two ways to lose time:

    • Dying on the roof. The gunmen fire the moment they arrive and a second group comes from the stairs. Do not stand in the open after the fist fight, and keep an eye on the stairwell.
    • Staying too long afterward. Leave as soon as the area is clear so the police do not become a second fight.

    Missions cannot be replayed once finished. If you want a manual restore point first, see how to save in GTA 6.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed (PowerPyx)
    Outfit The one you pick at Nola, paid for by Dre'Quan
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    No guide lists an optional objective.

    Where it sits in the story

    PowerPyx and GTABase agree that Watch It follows Too Much Baggage, and both missions need only Making Gainz, so the game may offer them in either order. It continues the Dimez storyline from Photo/Shoot. After it, PowerPyx's list returns to Lucia with Amateur Hour. The full order is in our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which outfit should you pick in the GTA 6 Watch It walkthrough?

    Either one. Dre'Quan pays for the outfit you choose from the two mannequins at Nola and takes the other himself. Neither PowerPyx nor GTABase lists any effect on the story.

    How do you start Watch It in GTA 6?

    Finish Making Gainz, then take Jason to the marked store in Washington Beach, Vice Beach. Go upstairs and wait for Dre'Quan to finish talking to the clerk.

    Where is the Crowex dealer in Watch It?

    On the top floor of the Cruda parking lot, south of the store where the mission starts. Drive in, take the ramps to the top and park beside the marked car.

    Is there a reward for Watch It?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. Dre'Quan pays for the outfit you pick at Nola, and GTABase marks the mission as required for 100% completion.
