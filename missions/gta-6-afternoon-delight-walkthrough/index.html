    GTA 6 Afternoon Delight Walkthrough: Rob Vicki's Boss

    GTA 6 Afternoon Delight walkthrough: get backstage at Delights, choose Help or Don't Help, open the safe with code 2008, escape the bikers and earn $10,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Afternoon Delight is a Chapter 2 main mission from Vicki: rob the safe at Delights, the club where she works.
    • The safe code is 2008. Helping the dancers or refusing changes the scenes and objectives, not the outcome.
    • Vicki sends $10,000 through BuckMe the next day on either route, the biggest single payout of the chapter.

    This GTA 6 Afternoon Delight walkthrough covers the last job before the end of Chapter 2: Vicki wants her boss's safe emptied, and she pays $10,000 for it. The mission has one real decision, and both branches lead to the same safe, the same shootout and the same money. PowerPyx subtitles it Rob Vicki's Boss.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Afternoon Delight (Chapter 2)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia; either can take the call
    Given by Vicki, by phone
    Starts at Delights, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Coffee Break, then wait for Vicki's call
    Reward $10,000 via BuckMe the next day

    Objectives

    1. Find a way backstage.
    2. Watch the show.
    3. Rob the Delights safe.
    4. Leave Delights.
    5. Escape the bikers.

    Step-by-step

    1. Get inside

    Go to the club. If you arrive alone, your partner is called in. It is ladies' night, so the bouncer lets only Lucia through the front.

    As Jason: wait until the two bouncers walk off, climb the fence behind the building and go through the open door. It leads to the male dancers' dressing room, where the lead dancer, Mario, wants Jason to join the act.

    2. Help or Don't Help

    Help Don't Help
    What happens Jason dances on stage with Mario and Lucky while Lucia slips to the back office The dancers quit, Frankie takes the stage, and Jason fires into the ceiling to start the robbery
    The safe Security catches Lucia; Jason rescues her The two open it together
    The chase Bikers have less time to call reinforcements Bikers chase more aggressively
    Afterwards You appear at a clothing store to buy Jason clothes, then drive to Vicki You appear at the docks and hand over the bag in a scene
    Length Longer, with extra scenes and dialogue Quicker

    Both guides say the choice does not change the result. Whichever you pick, you briefly play as Lucia and walk from the bar to the stage.

    3. Open the safe

    The safe is in the back office. The code is 2008. Opening it alerts every guard in the building.

    4. Shoot your way out

    Fight to the exit and drive off with the bikers behind you. Both guides call the escape easy.

    5. Deliver the bag

    Hand the cash to Vicki at the docks, after the clothes stop if Jason danced.

    Where players fail

    • Trying the front door as Jason. Use the fence at the back.
    • Standing in the open after the safe. Every guard is alerted at once.
    • Expecting the money immediately. It arrives through BuckMe the next day.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $10,000 via BuckMe the next day, on either route
    Choice Affects this mission only (GTABase)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    The payout covers two-thirds of the $15,000 for Prepare for Vice City. Vicki's first appearance is in Girl's Trip. After this, PowerPyx lists only the chapter finale, Checking Out. Order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the safe code in the GTA 6 Afternoon Delight walkthrough?

    2008. PowerPyx and GTABase both give the same code for the safe in the back office at Delights.

    Should you choose Help or Don't Help in Afternoon Delight?

    Either. Both guides say the choice affects only this mission. Don't Help is quicker; Help adds scenes, dialogue and an errand to buy Jason clothes.

    How does Jason get into Delights?

    It is ladies' night, so the bouncer only admits Lucia. As Jason, wait for the two bouncers to walk away, climb the fence behind the club and use the open door.

    How much does Afternoon Delight pay?

    $10,000 from Vicki via BuckMe the next day, whichever route you take.
