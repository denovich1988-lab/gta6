    GTA 6 Tie Up Loose Ends Walkthrough: How to Speed Up the Wait

    GTA 6 Tie Up Loose Ends walkthrough: why Raul never calls, how calling Valentina back shortens the wait, and what to finish before the Y Hotel note appears.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Tie Up Loose Ends is a waiting mission that begins automatically after the VCIA Takedown; it has no map marker and no reward.
    • Calling Valentina back, from either lead's phone or from the map, makes the next mission arrive sooner.
    • It ends when you collect a note at the Y Hotel front desk, which starts Sunset, the point of no return for Chapter 4.

    This GTA 6 Tie Up Loose Ends walkthrough explains the quiet stretch after the airport heist, where the only objective is to wait. There is nothing to fight and nowhere to go. What matters is knowing how to shorten the wait, and what to finish before you end it, because the mission that follows closes off free roam.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Tie Up Loose Ends (Chapter 4)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Starts Automatically, after the VCIA Takedown
    Map marker None; it appears in the story list with a pink icon as a main mission
    Reward None

    The guides differ on the giver (PowerPyx lists Jason and Lucia themselves, GTABase lists Raul), but they agree on everything you actually do.

    Objectives

    Source Objectives
    PowerPyx Wait for a message
    GTABase Wait for Raul; Answer the other call; While you wait

    PowerPyx also counts a separate one-step entry, Call Valentina Back, which GTABase folds into this mission.

    Step-by-step

    1. Wait for Raul

    After the heist, Raul is supposed to get in touch about selling the hard drive. He does not. GTABase is direct about it: the call never comes, no matter how long you wait.

    2. Take Valentina's call

    Valentina phones with an update on the sale and says you can call her back if you want things to move faster.

    3. Call Valentina back

    Way to call How
    Phone Open Jason's or Lucia's phone and dial Valentina
    Map Select the objective on the map; the call is placed automatically

    Tip: the call is optional, but both guides say it makes the next mission appear much sooner. If you skip it, the wait is simply longer.

    4. Use the time

    This is the last comfortable window in Chapter 4. Starting the next mission, Sunset, moves the story into Chapter 5, and PowerPyx says most of Chapter 5 has to be finished before the open world is available again.

    Things worth doing first:

    1. Play Yasmani's optional bank job, That's Where the Money Is…. It opens during this wait and is worth about $80,000.
    2. Finish any open side missions; see Strangers and Freaks.
    3. Make a manual save you will not overwrite: how to save in GTA 6.
    4. Check your progress against the 100% completion checklist.

    5. Collect the note

    After more time passes, a note is left for you at the front desk of the Y Hotel. PowerPyx points out that this is the hotel's front desk, not Santino's desk. Picking it up starts Sunset and completes Tie Up Loose Ends.

    Where players fail

    You cannot fail this mission, and GTABase says it is not missable: it always ends when Sunset begins. The mistake to avoid is ending it too early.

    • Picking up the note before you are ready. Everything optional should be done first.
    • Waiting for Raul. Nothing happens until Valentina's call is dealt with and time passes.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details

    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you finish the GTA 6 Tie Up Loose Ends walkthrough faster?

    Call Valentina back when the objective appears. Use Jason's or Lucia's phone, or select the objective on the map to dial her automatically. Then let some in-game time pass until the note appears at the Y Hotel front desk.

    Why is Raul not calling in Tie Up Loose Ends?

    He never does. GTABase says the call does not come however long you wait; Valentina's call is what moves the story on.

    What should you do during Tie Up Loose Ends?

    Finish side activities and earn money. Yasmani's optional bank job, That's Where the Money Is…, opens during this wait, and the next mission, Sunset, locks the open world for a long stretch.
