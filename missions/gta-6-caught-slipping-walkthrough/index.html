    GTA 6 Caught Slipping Walkthrough: Catch Both Thieves

    GTA 6 Caught Slipping walkthrough: chase two burglars from the Bayside apartment, stop each with Focus and a leg shot, then drive to Big House Storage.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Caught Slipping starts on its own when you try to enter the Bayside apartment after Boobie Time and First Impressions.
    • Stop each thief with Focus and a shot to the legs, first as Jason and then as Lucia, and search the bags they drop.
    • The mission ends at Big House Storage, where Jason has bought a storage unit. It pays no cash.

    This GTA 6 Caught Slipping walkthrough covers a short mission you do not choose to start: Jason and Lucia come home to a burglary and each chases one of the thieves. It takes a few minutes and ends with the pair renting somewhere safer to keep their things.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Caught Slipping (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason, then Lucia
    Given by Nobody; it triggers at the apartment
    Starts at The Bayside apartment, Vice City
    Requirement Boobie Time and First Impressions
    Reward No cash

    Tip: because the mission starts when you approach the apartment door, do anything you planned at home, such as saving, before you finish both required missions. See how to save in GTA 6.

    Objectives

    1. Catch the first thief.
    2. Search the thief's bag.
    3. Catch the second thief.
    4. Search the thief's bag.
    5. Go to Big House Storage.
    6. Follow Jason.

    GTABase lists four of these; PowerPyx shows the second search and Follow Jason as separate lines.

    Step-by-step

    1. Chase the first thief (Jason)

    The first thief runs through the construction site next to the apartment. Follow on foot. Draw a gun, activate Focus (L3 + R3 on PS5, per PowerPyx) and aim at the legs.

    2. Search the bag

    Run up to the thief and check the bag.

    3. Chase the second thief (Lucia)

    The game switches to Lucia. Do the same again: Focus, one shot to the legs.

    4. Search the second bag

    Search the bag this thief drops. PowerPyx describes what the scene reveals: the burglars were after the previous tenant's belongings, and the hard drive Jason and Lucia are keeping is safe.

    5. Drive to Big House Storage

    Jason and Lucia meet outside the apartment. Drive south to the Big House Storage building.

    6. Follow Jason

    Follow Jason inside and take the elevator up to the storage unit he has bought. The mission ends there.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions. Two things make the chases harder than they need to be:

    • Sprinting without drawing a weapon. The thieves are stopped by a leg shot, so get the gun out early.
    • Aiming without Focus. Both guides use Focus to line up the legs on a moving target.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Caught Slipping walkthrough mission?

    Complete Boobie Time and First Impressions. The mission then starts automatically the next time you try to enter the Bayside apartment in Chapter 3.

    How do you catch the thieves in Caught Slipping?

    Draw a gun, use Focus and shoot the thief in the legs. Then run up and search the bag. You do it once as Jason and once as Lucia.

    Is there a reward for Caught Slipping?

    No cash. PowerPyx lists the reward as none; GTABase lists one unlock without naming it.
