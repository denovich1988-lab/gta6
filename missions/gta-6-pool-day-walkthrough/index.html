    GTA 6 Pool Day Walkthrough: Eddie Acosta's Invitation

    GTA 6 Pool Day walkthrough: join Lucia on the balcony, fetch a Tequila Sunrise, meet Eddie Acosta and people-watch before Valentina's call.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Pool Day is the third mission of Chapter 4; it is not marked on the map and starts when you join Lucia on the suite's balcony as Jason.
    • Eddie Acosta invites you to a party at the bar whether you accept or decline, which adds the optional mission Attend Eddie Acosta's Party.
    • There is no reward; Valentina's call at the end puts the next two missions on the map.

    This GTA 6 Pool Day walkthrough covers a short Chapter 4 mission in which Jason and Lucia spend an afternoon by the pool at the Y Hotel. Nothing is at stake, but two things happen that shape the next few hours: a stranger called Eddie Acosta hands you an invitation, and Valentina phones with work.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Pool Day (Chapter 4)
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Y Hotel suite, Washington Beach (Vice Beach)
    Requirement Another Life
    Reward None

    PowerPyx points out that Pool Day is not marked as a mission. It simply begins when control returns to Jason after Another Life.

    Objectives

    1. Get Lucia a cocktail from the bar.
    2. Give Lucia her drink.
    3. Walk with Lucia.

    GTABase splits the same mission into five stages: the balcony, the cocktail, Eddie Acosta, people-watching and the walk on the beach.

    Step-by-step

    1. Join Lucia on the balcony

    Walk out to the balcony of the suite and talk to Lucia. If you head for the door, she calls you back. When she suggests the pool, agree.

    2. Get Lucia a cocktail

    Once the two of you are settled at the cabana, stand up and walk past the pool on the right to the bar. Order a Tequila Sunrise.

    3. Meet Eddie Acosta

    Before you leave the bar, a man called Eddie Acosta stops you and invites you to a party.

    Answer Result
    Accept You are invited
    Decline You are invited anyway

    Either way, the optional mission Attend Eddie Acosta's Party is added to the map for later.

    4. People-watch

    Take the drink back to Lucia and interact with her to hand it over. Jason sits down and the two of them watch the crowd. Look at each of these in turn:

    1. The man floating on the inflatable flamingo. Lucia asks what you think he does for a living; pick an answer.
    2. The group of men sitting on the left. One of them is said to look like Cal.
    3. The woman in the large hat at the edge of the pool, on the right.

    Neither guide says the flamingo answer changes anything.

    5. Walk on the beach

    When Lucia gets up, follow her out to the beach. That ends the mission. Valentina calls shortly afterwards, and the next two missions appear on the map.

    Where players fail

    There is no fail state in either guide. The only thing that slows people down is trying to leave the suite at the start: the mission will not let you.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None
    Unlocks Optional mission Attend Eddie Acosta's Party
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    For how time with your partner is tracked outside missions, see GTA 6 romance.

    After Valentina's call, the concierge mission Company Cars is available in the hotel lobby. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start Pool Day in GTA 6?

    After Another Life you are back in the Y Hotel suite as Jason. Go out to the balcony and talk to Lucia, then agree to go to the pool. If you try to leave the suite, she calls you back.

    Do you have to accept Eddie Acosta's invitation in Pool Day?

    No, but it makes no difference to the invitation. Both guides say you are invited either way, and Attend Eddie Acosta's Party appears on the map as an optional mission.

    What drink do you get Lucia in the GTA 6 Pool Day walkthrough?

    A Tequila Sunrise, from the bar past the pool on the right.
