    GTA 6 Hit Avery Pawn Walkthrough: Rob the Pawn Shop

    GTA 6 Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough: threaten the staff, smash the display cases, open the back safe, lose a two-star wanted level and fence the loot with Dunk.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Hit Avery Pawn is an optional Chapter 2 robbery at the pawn shop in Port Gellhorn, marked after Coffee Break.
    • Threatening the staff can reveal the code for the back safe; breaking a display case or the door brings a two-star wanted level.
    • The mission is complete once you escape; selling the bag at Dunk's trailer is what turns the loot into cash.

    This GTA 6 Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough shows how to empty the pawn shop in Port Gellhorn and get away with a full bag. It is the first proper store robbery you plan yourself, and a tutorial for the loop that funds the rest of the chapter: threaten, grab, escape, fence.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Hit Avery Pawn (Chapter 2, optional)
    Playable characters Jason or Lucia
    Given by Lucia
    Starts at The pawn shop, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Coffee Break
    Reward Cash, depending on how much you steal and sell

    Tip: park a fast motorbike outside before you start. Both guides name it as the best getaway.

    Objectives

    1. Rob the pawn shop.
    2. Lose the cops.
    3. Fence the stolen goods (optional).

    Step-by-step

    1. Threaten the staff

    Walk in, draw your gun and aim at the staff. Keeping them under threat may get you the combination for the safe in the back.

    2. Smash the display cases

    Break the cases and take the jewelry.

    3. Open the back safe

    Break the door to the back room and enter the code on the safe. PowerPyx's code was 6499, with a warning that it may be randomized, so use the one the staff give you.

    Action Consequence
    Breaking a display case or the door The police are called: two-star wanted level
    Leaving quickly You can be gone before they arrive, with less loot
    Staying for everything More loot, and the police at the door

    4. Lose the cops

    Get out of the search area. A quick motorbike that can weave through traffic works well, and so does heading off-road. See how the wanted level works.

    5. Fence the loot

    Carry the bag to Dunk's trailer in Port Gellhorn. This step is not required to finish the mission, but an unsold bag is worth nothing. PowerPyx lists what fences take: jewelry, narcotics and marked bills.

    Where players fail

    • Leaving the getaway to chance. Sort the vehicle first.
    • Leading the police to Dunk. Lose the wanted level before you drive to the trailer.
    • Skipping the fence. The payout only arrives when the goods are sold.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash Varies with what you take; neither guide gives a figure
    Fence Dunk's trailer, available after Slam Dunk
    100% GTABase marks the mission as counting towards 100%

    The sister job is tougher: Hit Kingpin Bowling. Both feed the $15,000 for Prepare for Vice City; more ways to earn are in how to make money in GTA 6. Order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the safe code in the GTA 6 Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough?

    It may differ for each player. PowerPyx got 6499 but warns the code may be randomized; threaten the staff and they may give you yours.

    Where do you sell the loot from Avery Pawn?

    Take the bag to Dunk's trailer in Port Gellhorn. Dunk works as a fence once you have finished Slam Dunk.

    Do you have to fence the goods to finish Hit Avery Pawn?

    No. PowerPyx and GTABase both say fencing is optional for completing the mission, but it is where the money comes from.

    What wanted level do you get in Hit Avery Pawn?

    Two stars, triggered when you break a display case or the door.
