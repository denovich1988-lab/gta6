    GTA 6 Take Care of Jason Walkthrough (Play Nurse)

    GTA 6 Take Care of Jason walkthrough: how the Play Nurse mission works, why it has no map marker, when to check in at the Starlet Motel and when it ends.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Take Care of Jason, shown as Play Nurse when you pause, is a background mission that runs through most of Chapter 2.
    • It has no map marker: check on Jason at the Starlet Motel each day, and return whenever the motel icon turns from green to pink.
    • It cannot be failed or missed and completes by itself after the mission Breakfast Run.

    This GTA 6 Take Care of Jason walkthrough explains the one Chapter 2 mission you cannot finish on demand. Jason is laid up in the motel room after the events that end Chapter 1, and Lucia looks after him while she works through the rest of the chapter. The game also calls it Play Nurse. There is nothing to fight and nothing to fail; the only trick is knowing how it progresses.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Take Care of Jason (Chapter 2)
    Also shown as Play Nurse (pause menu during the cutscene, per PowerPyx)
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Jason
    Location Starlet Motel, Kelly County
    Requirement Finish What's in the Bag?
    Reward Relationship increase (PowerPyx)

    Objectives

    1. Check in on Jason regularly.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find it in the mission list

    The mission has no icon on the map. Open Map > Story to see it listed with the other main missions.

    2. Visit Jason when you are near the motel

    Each in-game day, go back to the room at the Starlet Motel. From time to time a short scene plays in which Lucia takes care of him, and the relationship between the two improves.

    3. Watch the motel icon

    At certain points in Chapter 2 the story will not move until you return. The motel marker changes from green to pink when that happens.

    Tip: you do not need to camp at the motel. Play the other missions and drop in when you come back to save or sleep; see how to save in GTA 6.

    4. Keep playing Chapter 2

    Both guides say the mission closes by itself after Breakfast Run, late in the chapter. The missions that lead there include:

    • Overnight Charges
    • Just Moved In
    • Girl's Trip
    • Moses
    • New Look and How Do I Look?
    • Painkiller Supplier
    • Ambulance Chaser
    • Behind on Payments
    • Daily Driver
    • Slam Dunk

    Where players fail

    • Looking for a marker. There is none; use the Story tab.
    • Trying to finish it first. It stays open for most of the chapter by design.
    • Ignoring a pink motel icon. That is the game telling you the next story step is in the room.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward Relationship increase (PowerPyx); GTABase lists one unlock without naming it
    Missable No: both guides call it mandatory
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Neither guide lists a fail state, a cost or a choice in this mission. The full order of the chapter is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you complete the GTA 6 Take Care of Jason walkthrough?

    You cannot finish it straight away. Keep playing the other Chapter 2 missions, visit Jason at the Starlet Motel when you pass, and the mission completes automatically after Breakfast Run.

    Why is there no marker for Take Care of Jason?

    The mission has no marker of its own. PowerPyx and GTABase both say it is listed under Map > Story, and the motel icon turns pink when the story needs you back there.

    Is Play Nurse the same mission as Take Care of Jason?

    Yes. PowerPyx notes that pausing during the cutscene shows the name Play Nurse; mission lists use Take Care of Jason.

    Can you miss Take Care of Jason in GTA 6?

    No. Both guides call it mandatory and say returning to the motel is forced at several points in Chapter 2.
