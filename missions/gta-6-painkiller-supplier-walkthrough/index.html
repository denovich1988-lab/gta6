    GTA 6 Painkiller Supplier Walkthrough: Wait for Dunk's Call

    GTA 6 Painkiller Supplier walkthrough: why the mission has no marker, how to trigger Dunk's two phone calls faster and which missions to play while you wait.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Painkiller Supplier is a waiting mission in Chapter 2: Dunk phones Lucia when he has a lead on pills for Jason.
    • It has no map marker and completes by itself; playing other missions is the fastest way to bring on the calls.
    • After the first call it is renamed Dunk Will Be in Touch; the second call starts the mission Slam Dunk.

    This GTA 6 Painkiller Supplier walkthrough explains a mission with one instruction: wait for a call. At the end of Girl's Trip, Dunk promises to ask his supplier for painkillers. The mission sits in your list until he phones, and players often think it is bugged because nothing on the map points to it. GTABase's mission list shows the same entry as Got Pills?.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Painkiller Supplier (Chapter 2)
    Renamed to Dunk Will Be in Touch, after the first call
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Dunk
    Map marker None; listed under Map > Story
    Requirement Finish Girl's Trip
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    1. Wait for a call.

    Step-by-step

    1. Play something else

    Dunk calls after a few in-game days. Both guides list the same missions to do in the meantime:

    Mission Why do it now
    Moses Gives Lucia a car and a way to earn money
    New Look One-minute haircut errand
    How Do I Look? One-minute clothes errand
    Ambulance Chaser Short chase near the motel
    Pick Up a Gun (optional) Marked in Port Gellhorn after Girl's Trip

    2. Take the first call

    When Dunk phones, the mission's name changes to Dunk Will Be in Touch. Nothing else is required yet.

    3. Wait for the second call

    Keep playing. The second call starts the next main mission, Slam Dunk.

    Tip: if nothing is left on the map and Dunk still has not called, head back to the motel and check on Jason for Take Care of Jason while in-game time passes. The guides say only that the calls arrive with in-game time and other mission progress; neither gives an exact number of days.

    Where players fail

    • Searching Port Gellhorn for a marker. There is none.
    • Assuming the rename is a new mission. Dunk Will Be in Touch is the same entry.
    • Standing still. Progressing other missions is the reliable trigger.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None (PowerPyx)
    Leads to Slam Dunk
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Neither guide lists a fail state, a cost or a choice. The order of Chapter 2 is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you complete the GTA 6 Painkiller Supplier walkthrough?

    Wait. Dunk calls after a few in-game days, then calls a second time. PowerPyx and GTABase both say doing other Chapter 2 missions is the best way to speed it up.

    Why is Painkiller Supplier not on the map?

    It has no marker. It appears under Map > Story after Girl's Trip and updates when Dunk phones.

    What is Dunk Will Be in Touch in GTA 6?

    It is the name Painkiller Supplier takes after Dunk's first call. You then wait for a second call.

    What comes after Painkiller Supplier?

    Slam Dunk, which starts when Dunk calls the second time.
