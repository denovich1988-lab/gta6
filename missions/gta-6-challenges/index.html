    GTA 6 Challenges: All 8 Categories and Their Rewards

    GTA 6 challenges explained: 80 challenges in 8 categories, where to track them, and the vehicle cheats you unlock for finishing 5 or all of a category.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has 80 challenges: 10 each in combat, criminal, exploration, fitness, shopping, sports, survival and vehicle.
    • 50 challenges earn the One Last Thing trophy and 70 of 80 count toward 100% completion.
    • Finishing 5 or all challenges in a category is the reported unlock task for a vehicle spawn cheat.

    GTA 6 challenges are 80 optional goals split into eight categories of 10. They are separate from story missions. They count toward a trophy and 100% completion, and each category is tied to vehicle spawn cheats. The cheat unlocks come from launch guides, not from Rockstar, so this page is marked partly confirmed.

    The eight challenge categories

    Category Challenges What it is about
    Combat 10 Kills and weapon skill
    Criminal 10 Robbery, theft and police
    Exploration 10 Finding places and collectibles across Leonida
    Fitness 10 Gym, running, cycling and swimming
    Shopping 10 Spending at stores and mod shops
    Sports 10 Basketball, golf and pool
    Survival 10 Hunting, fishing and shooting ranges
    Vehicle 10 Driving, racing and stunts

    That makes 80 in total. We do not list all 80 individual challenges here yet, because only one full list has been published and we wait for a second source to confirm the exact targets.

    Where to track GTA 6 challenges

    1. Open the menu (Touch Pad on PS5).
    2. Go to Progress.
    3. Open Challenges.

    The 100% checklist itself sits under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and appears only after you finish the story.

    How many you need

    Goal Challenges needed
    One Last Thing trophy / achievement Any 50
    100% completion checklist Any 70 of 80
    Every challenge cheat All 80

    For 100% you can leave out 10 challenges. If you want every cheat, you cannot skip any.

    GTA 6 challenge rewards

    Each category has two reported cheat unlocks: one for completing 5 challenges and one for completing all 10. Completing every challenge in the game is the unlock task for a jet.

    Category 5 challenges All 10 challenges
    Combat Speeder 700 GT (boat) Bravado Banshee (sports car)
    Criminal Maibatsu Sanchez (dirt bike) Western Zombie Bobber (motorcycle)
    Exploration Nagasaki Sea Sparrow (helicopter) Nagasaki Carbon RS (motorcycle)
    Fitness Tuff BMX (bicycle) '65 Vapid Dominator (muscle car)
    Shopping Maibatsu Boomazoom (car) Truffade Thrax (supercar)
    Sports Vapid Stanier G2 Taxi Brimstone Golfer (golf cart)
    Survival Airboat 09 Hauners Rhino Tank
    Vehicle Lapwing Mallard (stunt plane) Progen Emerus (supercar)
    All 80 challenges Hauners P-996 LAZER (jet fighter)

    These are spawn cheats, not vehicles delivered to a garage. Cheats only work after the main story, and an active cheat turns off saving and trophies. Codes and entry steps are on GTA 6 cheats.

    Tips

    1. Check Progress > Challenges early. Many challenges are things you do anyway during the story and free roam.
    2. Aim for 5 in every category first. That is 40 challenges and eight vehicle cheats.
    3. The tank needs all 10 survival challenges, and the jet needs all 80, so those two take the longest.
    4. Some exploration challenges overlap with GTA 6 collectibles, so do them together.
    5. Police-related criminal challenges are easier once you know how heat works: see GTA 6 wanted level.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many challenges are in GTA 6?

    80. There are eight categories with 10 challenges each: combat, criminal, exploration, fitness, shopping, sports, survival and vehicle.

    What are the GTA 6 challenge rewards?

    Each category is tied to two vehicle spawn cheats, one for 5 challenges and one for all 10. All 80 are the reported unlock task for the Hauners P-996 LAZER jet cheat. 50 challenges also earn the One Last Thing trophy.

    Do you need all GTA 6 challenges for 100% completion?

    No. The 100% checklist asks for 70 of the 80, so you can skip the 10 you find hardest.

    Where do you track challenges in GTA 6?

    In the menu under Progress > Challenges.
