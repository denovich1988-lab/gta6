    GTA 6 Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs Walkthrough: All 3 Safe Codes

    GTA 6 Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs walkthrough: safe codes 1680, 6815 and 0104, the best store order, how to get the Snap Gun and the $20,000 payout.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Yasmani wants three items from three Vice City stores: a buffalo skull chain, a diamond ring and an amethyst necklace.
    • The safe codes are 1680 at Casa de Empeno, 6815 at Richardson Jewelry and 0104 at Donovan Banks & Co.
    • Deliver the first item at once to get the Snap Gun, which opens back doors silently; the three items pay $20,000.

    This GTA 6 Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs walkthrough gives the three safe codes, the order that makes the robberies easiest and the tool you should collect after the first one. Yasmani, the fence from Boobie Time, wants one item from each of three stores and pays $20,000 for the set.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Given by Yasmani, who contacts you after Boobie Time
    Area Ocean Beach Banks, Vice City (PowerPyx)
    Requirement Finish Boobie Time
    Reward $20,000, the Snap Gun and a Yasmani pickup service

    All three safe codes

    Store Target item Safe location Code
    Casa de Empeno Buffalo skull chain Small back office 1680
    Richardson Jewelry Diamond ring North-east corner of the central room 6815
    Donovan Banks & Co. Amethyst necklace Middle of the long back hallway room 0104

    Objectives

    1. Plan your order.
    2. Hit Casa de Empeno.
    3. Hit Richardson Jewelry.
    4. Hit Donovan Banks & Co.
    5. Sell to Yasmani.

    The stores can be robbed in any order. Both guides recommend the order above.

    Step-by-step

    1. Casa de Empeno first

    This store has no back door, so there is nothing for the Snap Gun to open. It is also the smallest of the three, which makes a fast, loud robbery practical.

    1. Go to the small back office and open the safe with 1680.
    2. Use the security box to the left of the safe to destroy the video footage. If you are gone before the police arrive, they do not get your description.
    3. Smash the display cases on the way out for extra jewelry.

    2. Take the chain to Yasmani

    Deliver the first item straight away. Yasmani gives you the Snap Gun, which opens doors without setting off the alarm. The guides add that police take longer to arrive when you use it.

    3. Richardson Jewelry

    1. Open the back door with the Snap Gun. You enter the back room without alerting the shop floor.
    2. Disarm security at the panel to the right of the door into the central room.
    3. Destroy the footage at the box under the security screen in the central room.
    4. Open the safe in the north-east corner with 6815.
    5. Leave quietly, or go out through the shop and empty the display cases for more money.

    4. Donovan Banks & Co.

    1. Open the back door with the Snap Gun.
    2. In the small back office, destroy the footage and disarm security.
    3. Open the safe in the middle of the long back hallway room with 0104.
    4. Leave quietly, or clear the display cases at the front.

    5. Sell to Yasmani

    Return to Yasmani's shop and sell everything. That completes the mission.

    Where players fail

    • Doing all three before visiting Yasmani. You then rob two stores the hard way. Deliver the first item and collect the Snap Gun.
    • Skipping the footage. Destroying it is what keeps your description from the police. How that works is in GTA 6 wanted level.
    • Getting greedy at the front. The display cases are extra money but put you in view of the shop floor. Decide before you open the safe.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the safe codes in GTA 6 Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs?

    Casa de Empeno 1680, Richardson Jewelry 6815, Donovan Banks & Co. 0104. PowerPyx and GTABase give the same three codes.

    Which store should you rob first for Yasmani?

    Casa de Empeno. It has no back door, so the Snap Gun would not help there, and it is the smallest store. Deliver its item to Yasmani to get the Snap Gun for the other two.

    How much do Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs pay?

    $20,000 for the three requested items, plus whatever extra jewelry you take from the display cases.

    Are Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs optional?

    Guides differ. GTABase labels them an optional side mission but also required for 100%; PowerPyx counts them as a main mission.
