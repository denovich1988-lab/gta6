    GTA 6 Discharge Time Walkthrough: The End of Chapter 1

    GTA 6 Discharge Time walkthrough: find Jason in room 203, fight out of Vice City Hospital from a wheelchair and defend the ambulance to finish Chapter 1.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Discharge Time is the last mission of Chapter 1 and follows Home Sweet Home with no break.
    • You find Jason on the second floor of Vice City Hospital, then shoot from a wheelchair and from the back of an ambulance.
    • It ends at a motel, where Chapter 2 opens with What's in the Bag?

    This GTA 6 Discharge Time walkthrough covers the final mission of Chapter 1: Lucia finds Jason in Vice City Hospital, the people hunting them arrive, and the two shoot their way out in a wheelchair and an ambulance. It starts the moment Home Sweet Home ends, so you cannot prepare for it separately.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Discharge Time (mission 22 in our list, the last of Chapter 1)
    Playable characters Lucia first, then Jason for the shooting
    Starts at Vice City Hospital
    Requirement Starts automatically after Home Sweet Home
    Reward None listed
    Next mission What's in the Bag? (Chapter 2)

    Everything you needed to do before this point is in the checklist on our Tourist Trap walkthrough.

    Objectives

    1. Go to the second floor, find Jason and go to his room.
    2. Defend yourself.
    3. Crawl to the gun.
    4. Defend the ambulance.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find Jason

    From the entrance, go left and up the stairs. PowerPyx notes you can ask the receptionist, then follow the pink line on the floor through the door on the left to room 203. Wake Jason.

    2. The wheelchair

    Lucia puts Jason in a wheelchair and pushes him toward the exit. You control Jason's aim while she steers. Enemies come out of doors along the whole route.

    Tip: VGC's advice is to use focus mode as often as it is available, because the number of enemies makes it very easy to die here.

    3. Alone in the ambulance bay

    Lucia leaves to find a vehicle. PowerPyx describes the next beat: a man with a shotgun comes at Jason, Jason takes it from him and uses it, and each shot rolls the wheelchair backwards. Then nearby gas canisters are hit and Jason is thrown to the floor.

    4. Crawl to the gun

    Crawl forward to the weapon on the ground. Lucia arrives in an ambulance and ends the fight.

    5. Defend the ambulance

    You are now in the back of the ambulance as Jason, with Lucia driving and vehicles in pursuit.

    Target Effect
    The driver Stops that car
    The passenger firing at you Reduces incoming damage
    Engine or wheels Disables the car; VGC says a car with shot wheels can swerve into the ones behind it

    After a few minutes a cutscene takes over.

    6. The motel

    You wake the next day in a motel. PowerPyx says the pair fled west to Kelly County. Chapter 2 begins.

    Where players fail

    • Saving focus for later. There is no later; use it in the corridors.
    • Shooting bodywork in the chase. Wheels, engines and drivers stop cars. Doors do not.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)
    Trophy Paradise Lost, for finishing Chapter 1, unlocks in the next mission according to PowerPyx and GTABase

    Chapter 2 opens with a very short scene: What's in the Bag?. See the trophy guide for the rest of the list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Jason in the GTA 6 Discharge Time walkthrough?

    On the second floor of Vice City Hospital. PowerPyx gives room 203: go left from the entrance, up the stairs and follow the pink line on the floor. VGC describes the ward as the ICU.

    How do you survive the hospital fight in Discharge Time?

    Use focus mode as much as you can. VGC warns that it is very easy to die here because enemies come through every door while Lucia pushes Jason's wheelchair.

    What is the best way to stop cars in the ambulance chase?

    Shoot the driver, the shooter, or the engine and wheels. VGC says shooting the wheels can send one car into the others and clear the road quickly.

    Is Discharge Time the last mission of Chapter 1?

    Yes. Both VGC and PowerPyx end Chapter 1 here. You wake up the next day in a motel at the start of Chapter 2.
