    GTA 6 It's a Date! Walkthrough: The Courtyard Bar Date

    GTA 6 It's a Date! walkthrough: when the optional date appears, where the Courtyard Bar is, what to do there and why to play it before Making Gainz.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • It's a Date! is an optional Chapter 4 mission that appears on the map right after Heavy Lifting.
    • Take Jason and Lucia together to the Courtyard Bar in Stockyard, buy a drink, dance if you like and walk back to the street.
    • The reward is a relationship increase; do it before Making Gainz, which stops the couple being seen together in public.

    This GTA 6 It's a Date! walkthrough covers one of the shortest missions in the game: a drink for two at the Courtyard Bar. It has two objectives and cannot go wrong. The only thing worth planning is when you do it, because the next story mission makes dates much harder.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission It's a Date! (Chapter 4, optional)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia, together
    Given by Nobody; it is marked on the map
    Starts at Courtyard Bar, Stockyard, Vice City
    Requirement Heavy Lifting
    Reward Relationship increase

    You have been here before: the Courtyard Bar is where Lucia can meet Raul to start Crosstown Traffic in Chapter 3.

    Objectives

    1. Go to the Courtyard Bar.
    2. Buy a drink in the Courtyard Bar.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go together

    The date marker appears as soon as Heavy Lifting ends. Both leads have to be there, so bring your partner along rather than arriving alone. If the two are apart, see how to switch characters and meet up first.

    2. Buy a drink

    Walk into the bar area and buy a drink.

    3. Dance, if you want

    You can dance for a while. Both guides list it as optional.

    4. Walk back to the street

    Leave the bar area. The mission completes when you step back out onto the street.

    Why to do it now

    Making Gainz unlocks a couple of in-game days after Heavy Lifting. Both guides warn that once it is done, Jason and Lucia cannot be seen together in public until the VCIA Takedown, and can only spend time together in the Y Hotel room. That makes this date, and any other couple activity, far easier to fit in beforehand.

    Tip: treat It's a Date! as the first item on a short list. Anything that needs both characters in public, including partner goals from GTA 6 romance, is best done before you start Making Gainz.

    Where players fail

    There is nothing to fail. Neither guide lists a fail condition or a time limit.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None
    Relationship Increases (both guides)
    100% GTABase marks it as required; PowerPyx says skipping it has no consequence

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you complete the date in this GTA 6 It's a Date! walkthrough?

    Go to the Courtyard Bar in Stockyard with Jason and Lucia together and buy a drink. Dancing is optional. The mission completes when you leave the bar area and walk back out to the street.

    Is It's a Date! required in GTA 6?

    It is optional for the story. PowerPyx says skipping it has no consequence, while GTABase's page marks it as required for 100%, so play it if you are aiming for full completion.

    What is the reward for It's a Date!?

    A relationship increase between Jason and Lucia. There is no cash.

    When does It's a Date! unlock?

    Immediately after Heavy Lifting in Chapter 4. Nobody gives it to you; it is simply marked on the map.
