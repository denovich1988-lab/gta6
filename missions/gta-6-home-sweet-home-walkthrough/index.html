    GTA 6 Home Sweet Home Walkthrough: Escape the Manhunt

    GTA 6 Home Sweet Home walkthrough: fight the two intruders in the burning house as Lucia, get out in time and slip through the police search of Key Lento.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Home Sweet Home is played as Lucia and starts the moment Tourist Trap ends: two intruders are in Jason's house and it is on fire.
    • After the fight you escape a police search of Key Lento on foot, by car or by water.
    • VGC reports that reaching Andy's car earns a unique jacket; every route leads into Discharge Time.

    This GTA 6 Home Sweet Home walkthrough covers the second of Chapter 1's three closing missions: Lucia alone at Jason's house, two intruders, a fire and a police search of Key Lento to slip through. It is short and mostly about stealth. It starts automatically after Tourist Trap, so there is nothing to prepare.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Home Sweet Home (mission 21 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Playable character Lucia
    Starts at Jason's house, Key Lento
    Requirement Starts automatically after Tourist Trap
    Reward No cash; a unique jacket on one escape route (VGC)
    Next mission Discharge Time, the last mission of Chapter 1

    You cannot free roam between these missions. See the warning in our Tourist Trap walkthrough.

    Objectives

    1. Grab Jason's stash and escape the house.
    2. Escape the area.

    Step-by-step

    1. Enter the house

    Lucia has come back to collect some of Jason's things before the two leave town. Inside are two masked intruders who have wrecked the place, are after the stash and have started a fire.

    2. Fight the intruders

    Beat both of them.

    Tip: keep away from the flames. VGC warns that catching fire drains health very quickly, so pull the fight toward the parts of the room that are not burning.

    3. Get out

    Leave by the front door at once. PowerPyx says there is not enough time to reach the cash before the house explodes. VGC mentions grabbing the photos by the door on the way out, and that the blast throws Lucia off the balcony.

    4. Escape the search area

    Lucia wakes up with police on the way and a search area on the map. The guides describe three ways out:

    Route How Notes
    Andy's car Call Andy, then sneak through the neighbourhood to the car VGC: Andy takes Lucia to the hospital and gives her a unique jacket
    The main road Crouch through the dark to the road through the middle of Key Lento, steal a car or keep walking (PowerPyx) VGC says open roads are very hard, with a helicopter overhead
    The water Take a boat or jet ski from the docks by the houses VGC: stealing one alerts the police further

    Tip: stay crouched and stay off open ground. VGC notes that trailers and buildings can be cut through, though most have to be broken into first.

    5. The edge of the area

    As you near the edge of the search area a cutscene takes over and leads straight into Discharge Time. VGC says the other routes play a slightly different scene but end at the same place, the hospital.

    Where players fail

    • Standing in the fire. It is the quickest way to die in the house.
    • Lingering inside. Leave as soon as the second intruder is down.
    • Being seen by the police. PowerPyx lists being spotted as what ends the escape. For how detection works, see GTA 6 wanted level.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)
    Route reward A unique jacket from Andy if you reach the car (VGC). VGC does not say whether the other routes give it

    Next is Discharge Time, which closes Chapter 1; we will add its walkthrough to the GTA 6 mission list next.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you escape the police in the GTA 6 Home Sweet Home walkthrough?

    Stay crouched and out of sight, and head for the edge of the search area. You can sneak to Andy's car, steal a car on the main road through Key Lento, or take a boat or jet ski from the docks.

    Can you save Jason's stash in Home Sweet Home?

    The guides do not describe a way to keep it. PowerPyx says there is no time to reach the cash after the fight and you must leave by the front door before the house explodes.

    Who do you play as in Home Sweet Home?

    Lucia. Jason was wounded in Tourist Trap, and this mission begins as soon as she returns to his house.
