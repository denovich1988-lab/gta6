    GTA 6 Thrillbillies Walkthrough: Mud Club Dirt Bike Race

    GTA 6 Thrillbillies walkthrough: buy outfits at the Thrillbilly Mud Club, rent a Sanchez at Roostere's Rentals and race Billy's crew for up to $2,500.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Thrillbillies is an optional Chapter 2 mission at the Thrillbilly Mud Club that introduces Billy and his dirt bike crew.
    • Rent the Sanchez rather than the Manchez: it costs more but is faster, and the better finish pays the difference back.
    • First place pays $2,500 and third about $625; you do not need to win, only to avoid coming last.

    This GTA 6 Thrillbillies walkthrough explains how to get through the Mud Club race and start Billy's questline. It is one of the four jobs that appear after Coffee Break, and the safest of them: no police, no shooting, and a cash prize that depends on where you finish. PowerPyx lists it as Check Out the Mud Club, the first of Billy's Strangers and Freaks missions.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Thrillbillies (Chapter 2, optional)
    Playable characters Jason or Lucia
    Given by Billy
    Starts at Thrillbilly Mud Club, Kelly County
    Requirement Finish Coffee Break
    Reward Up to $2,500, by race position

    Objectives

    1. Buy a new outfit at the merch stall.
    2. Leave the store.
    3. Rent a vehicle at Roostere's Rentals.
    4. Go to the race organizer.
    5. Beat a member of Billy's crew.

    Step-by-step

    1. Buy outfits

    At the club entrance, go into the souvenir shop and buy clothes for both Jason and Lucia.

    2. Meet the crew

    Leaving the shop starts a scene with Billy and his riders: Ricky, Brittney and Wyman, the man from the motel. Billy invites you to race.

    3. Rent a bike

    Bike at Roostere's Rentals Notes
    Manchez Cheaper, slower
    Sanchez Costs more, faster; recommended by both guides

    4. Go to the start line

    Ride to the marked start.

    5. Race

    There are 13 other riders. You do not have to win, but do not come last.

    Finish Payout
    1st $2,500
    3rd About $625
    Lower places Less; the guides give no full table

    Tip: keep clear of other vehicles. Both guides warn that ramming them can throw you off the bike.

    6. After the race

    Billy says he will be in touch with more work.

    Where players fail

    • Renting the cheaper bike. The saving is smaller than the prize money you lose.
    • Fighting for position. Contact costs more time than it gains.
    • Finishing last. It is the one result both guides tell you to avoid.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash Up to $2,500 for first place
    Questline Leads to Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab
    100% GTABase marks the mission as counting towards 100%

    Neither guide lists the outfit or rental prices. The winnings count towards the $15,000 for Prepare for Vice City. Other side characters are in GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks; the main order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you unlock the GTA 6 Thrillbillies walkthrough mission?

    Finish Coffee Break in Chapter 2. Wyman mentions the Mud Club on the way to the motel kitchen, and the mission is marked on the map afterwards.

    Which bike should you rent in Thrillbillies?

    The Sanchez. PowerPyx and GTABase both say it is faster than the Manchez and that a higher finish earns back the extra rental cost.

    Do you have to win the Thrillbillies race?

    No. Both guides say winning is not required; just do not finish last of the 14 riders.

    What comes after Thrillbillies?

    Billy texts a few in-game hours later with Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, an armored truck job.
