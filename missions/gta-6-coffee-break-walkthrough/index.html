    GTA 6 Coffee Break Walkthrough: Unlock Character Switching

    GTA 6 Coffee Break walkthrough: buy Lucia a $2 coffee in the motel kitchen, open the partner menu to unlock character switching and pick up four new jobs.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Coffee Break follows Breakfast Run directly: walk to the motel kitchen and buy Lucia a coffee for $2.
    • Holding Right on the D-pad by the car opens the partner menu, which unlocks free switching between Jason and Lucia.
    • Four jobs appear on the map at once: Thrillbillies, Hit Avery Pawn, Hit Kingpin Bowling and Impounded Vehicle.

    This GTA 6 Coffee Break walkthrough is short, but the mission is the turning point of Chapter 2: it gives you both leads at once. After a $2 coffee, Jason and Lucia agree to raise money for a move to Vice City, and the game unlocks character switching and a set of jobs to fund it.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Coffee Break (Chapter 2)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Starts at Outside the Starlet Motel, Kelly County
    Requirement Finish Breakfast Run; it follows without a break
    Cost $2
    Reward Character switching and the partner menu

    Objectives

    1. Go to the motel kitchen with Lucia.
    2. Buy Lucia a coffee.
    3. Go to your car.
    4. Hold Right on the D-pad to interact with your partner.

    Step-by-step

    1. Walk to the kitchen

    The kitchen is straight ahead, in the southern corner of the motel grounds. On the way, a man called Wyman recommends the Mud Club for dirt racing. That is the lead for Thrillbillies.

    2. Buy the coffee

    Lucia sits at the table. Use the coffee machine and pay $2. In the scene that follows, the two decide to work towards something bigger so they can move to Vice City.

    3. Go back to the car

    The follow-up jobs are now marked on the map.

    4. Open the partner menu

    Action PS5 Xbox
    Open the partner menu Hold Right on the D-pad Hold Right on the D-pad
    Switch character R1 RB

    From the same menu you can give your partner commands when they are with you. Details and limits are in how to switch characters in GTA 6.

    The four jobs that open

    Job What it is Guide
    Thrillbillies A dirt bike race at the Thrillbilly Mud Club Thrillbillies walkthrough
    Hit Avery Pawn Rob the pawn shop in Port Gellhorn Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough
    Hit Kingpin Bowling Raid a gang-held bowling alley Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough
    Impounded Vehicle Take Lucia's car back from the police Impounded Vehicle walkthrough

    Both guides say the order is up to you.

    Tip: start with Impounded Vehicle if you want Lucia's car for the others, or with Thrillbillies for a low-risk payout of up to $2,500.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail state. The only thing people miss is the last objective: the mission does not move on until you hold Right on the D-pad by the car.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does the GTA 6 Coffee Break walkthrough unlock?

    Free switching between Jason and Lucia and the partner menu for giving your partner commands. GTABase adds a BuckMe upgrade to Silver.

    Where is the motel kitchen in Coffee Break?

    Straight ahead from where you start outside the Starlet Motel, in the southern corner of the property.

    Which job should you do first after Coffee Break?

    It does not matter. PowerPyx and GTABase both say the four jobs can be done in any order.

    How do you switch characters after Coffee Break?

    Hold Right on the D-pad to bring up the partner menu, then tap R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox.
