    GTA 6 Bricked Up Walkthrough: Take the Red Bag for $3,500

    GTA 6 Bricked Up walkthrough: park outside the red zone, take the red bag before leaving Raymond's room, escape Venture Apartments and collect $3,500.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Bricked Up is a late Chapter 3 mission from Boobie Ike that starts at the Bayside apartment after Hidden Charges.
    • The $3,500 payment and the Professional bonus depend on picking up the red bag before you leave the first room.
    • Park outside the red area around Venture Apartments in Rockridge, or your car is impounded during the mission.

    This GTA 6 Bricked Up walkthrough covers Boobie's pickup job at Venture Apartments, which turns into a police raid and a fight down through the building to a window. Boobie reminds Jason and Lucia that while they live in his apartment they work for him, and sends them to collect cocaine bricks owed by a gang called San4San. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Return to the Apartment.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Bricked Up (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Given by Boobie Ike
    Starts at Bayside apartment, Vice City
    Requirement Hidden Charges and the other remaining Chapter 3 main missions
    Reward $3,500, only if you take the red bag

    You begin the fight with pistols only, so bring body armor.

    Objectives

    1. Go to Venture Apartments.
    2. Park away from Venture Apartments.
    3. Ask for Raymond in the lobby.
    4. Escape Venture Apartments.
    5. Help Lucia.
    6. Get in a car.
    7. Pick up Jason.
    8. Lose the cops.
    9. Go to the Jack of Hearts.

    These are PowerPyx's nine lines; GTABase covers the same route in six stages.

    Step-by-step

    1. Back at the apartment

    Return to the Bayside apartment when Boobie gets in touch. He is asleep there, and sends you both to pick up the bricks.

    2. Drive to Rockridge and park away

    Venture Apartments is in Rockridge. Lucia suggests parking out of sight, and the map shows a red area around the building.

    Tip: leave the car outside the red area. You can park at the door, but both guides say the vehicle is impounded during the mission if you do. Getting it back means a trip like the one in Impounded Vehicle.

    Walk to the front entrance.

    3. Ask for Raymond

    In the lobby, turn right and talk to the man standing by the elevator. Upstairs, walk down the hallway and knock on the red door with a slot in the middle.

    4. Escape the raid

    Captain Méndez and his dirty cops raid the building.

    1. Take the red bag first. It is near the end of the table in the first room. These are Boobie's bricks: no bag, no $3,500 and no Professional bonus.
    2. Pick up the longarms that police drop. You start with pistols.
    3. Head back down the hallway towards the elevators. Enemies come from the side doors and from the larger room before the elevators.
    4. Take the stairs at the end of the hallway or the elevator. If the elevator stops, press X on PS5 or A on Xbox (per PowerPyx) to open the doors.
    5. The third floor has no lights. A police weapon with a flashlight on it makes enemies much easier to see.

    5. Out through the window

    Fight down through the third and second floors. At the end of the last hallway Raymond steps out and points you into the apartment on his left. Lucia climbs out of the window; interact with her to help her drop down.

    You switch to Lucia. Run to the parking lot to the south, take a car and drive back under the window to pick up Jason.

    6. Lose the cops

    A police helicopter is above you from the start, so this escape takes longer than most. Put distance between you and the building and watch for roadblocks. Our wanted level guide explains how search areas work.

    7. Go to the Jack of Hearts

    Boobie is waiting in his car in front of the Jack of Hearts. Stay out of the police search area on the way; you can change cars if you want.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

    • Walking out without the red bag. The payment and the bonus both depend on it.
    • Parking at the entrance. The car is impounded.
    • Rushing the dark third floor. Enemies wait around corners; use a weapon light.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash $3,500 with the red bag
    Professional bonus Carry the bricks out
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Bricked Up is another job for Boobie, whom you first drove around in Boobie Time. After it, PowerPyx's order has South Leonida Real Estate and then the chapter finale; see GTA 6 heists. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the red bag in the GTA 6 Bricked Up walkthrough?

    In the first room behind the red door, near the end of the table. Take it before you step into the hallway; it holds Boobie's bricks.

    How much does Bricked Up pay in GTA 6?

    $3,500, but only if you carry Boobie's bricks out. Both guides say the red bag also counts for the Professional bonus.

    Where should you park in Bricked Up?

    Outside the red area marked on the map around Venture Apartments. A car left inside it is impounded while you are in the building.

    How do you unlock Bricked Up in GTA 6?

    Finish Hidden Charges and the other remaining Chapter 3 main missions, then return to the Bayside apartment when Boobie gets in touch.
