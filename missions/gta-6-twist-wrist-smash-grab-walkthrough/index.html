    GTA 6 Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab Walkthrough: Armored Truck

    GTA 6 Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab walkthrough: ride with Billy's crew, chase the armored truck until it crashes, blow the rear doors and take the bag for $2,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab is Billy's second mission in Chapter 2: an armored truck robbery on dirt bikes.
    • Do not shoot the truck. Follow it until it crashes into a tree, then plant and detonate a charge on the rear doors.
    • It pays $2,000, and you can keep the Highwayman Sanchez by parking it in a garage and registering it with Moses.

    This GTA 6 Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab walkthrough covers Billy's armored truck job, the best-paid optional mission of Chapter 2 for the time it takes. The crew does the hard part. Your job is to stay on the bike, open the truck and carry the bag. PowerPyx lists it as Join Billy on a Job.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab (Chapter 2, optional)
    Playable characters Jason or Lucia
    Given by Billy
    Starts at A bench at the marker in Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Thrillbillies, then wait a few in-game hours for Billy's message
    Reward $2,000

    Objectives

    1. Get on the dirt bike.
    2. Follow Billy.
    3. Stop the armored van.
    4. Plant a bomb on the rear doors.
    5. Handle the security guard.
    6. Follow Billy.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Billy

    Use the bench at the marker. Billy turns up with a Highwayman Sanchez dirt bike for you and explains the plan.

    2. Follow him

    Ride behind Billy until a scene shows the armored truck. The rest of the crew joins in.

    3. Chase the truck

    Stay with the pack. The truck ends the chase itself by hitting a tree.

    Tip: both guides give the same two rules for this part: do not shoot the truck, and stay on the bike.

    4. Blow the doors

    Walk to the back of the truck.

    Step PS5 Xbox
    Plant the charge on the rear doors Triangle Y
    Detonate Right on the D-pad Right on the D-pad

    The button prompts are from PowerPyx.

    5. The guard

    A security guard climbs out and walks to the side. You can knock him out or leave him alone; neither guide reports a difference.

    6. Take the bag and go

    Pick up the bag behind the truck, get back on the bike and stay close to Billy. He leads you up a mountain to regroup with the crew, and a scene ends the mission.

    Where players fail

    • Shooting at the truck. It is not needed and both guides warn against it.
    • Falling off. Crashing during the chase or the climb costs you the group.
    • Losing Billy on the mountain. Keep him in sight on the way up.

    Optional goals and rewards

    The $2,000 goes towards Prepare for Vice City. More side characters: GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks. Main order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab walkthrough mission?

    Finish Thrillbillies and wait a few in-game hours for Billy's message. Then sit on the bench at the marker in Port Gellhorn and Billy arrives with a dirt bike.

    Should you shoot the armored truck in Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab?

    No. PowerPyx and GTABase both say not to shoot it; stay on the bike and follow until the truck hits a tree.

    How much does Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab pay?

    $2,000, according to both guides.

    Can you keep the dirt bike from Billy's mission?

    Yes. Both guides suggest parking the Highwayman Sanchez in a garage, such as the one in Port Gellhorn, and registering it at Moses's shop.
