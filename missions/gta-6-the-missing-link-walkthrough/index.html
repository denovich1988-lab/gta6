    GTA 6 The Missing Link Walkthrough: Get Monty's Pistol

    GTA 6 The Missing Link walkthrough: meet Andrea in Watson Bay, grab Monty's 1992 Pistol before you leave and keep him alive for the Professional bonus.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • The Missing Link is a Cartel Heist prep mission in Chapter 3; Lucia must start it alone at Sloppy Molly's in Watson Bay.
    • Monty's 1992 Pistol, a variant weapon, lies on the floor of the photo room and can only be picked up during the mission.
    • Lowering Monty all the way into the water kills him and loses the Professional bonus.

    This GTA 6 The Missing Link walkthrough covers the mission where Lucia and Andrea grab a cartel enforcer called Monty and Raul's crew questions him. It has a fistfight, a shootout, a drive to Vice City and an interrogation with a loader over a pool, plus a variant pistol that is easy to walk past. PowerPyx subtitles it Meet Andrea.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission The Missing Link (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Lucia, then Jason
    Given by Andrea
    Starts at Sloppy Molly's, Watson Bay (Lucia only, alone)
    Requirement Company Property
    Reward Monty's 1992 Pistol if you pick it up; Professional bonus for keeping Monty alive

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists 18 on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into seven stages:

    1. Meet Andrea in Watson Bay.
    2. Get to Monty.
    3. Knock out Monty.
    4. Fight your way out.
    5. Drive to Vice City.
    6. Interrogate Monty.
    7. Deal with Monty.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Andrea (Lucia)

    Switch to Lucia and go to Sloppy Molly's in Watson Bay without Jason. Andrea has arranged a meeting with Monty, a senior enforcer for La Polilla Negra, at a cockfight. Once Lucia has changed clothes, follow Andrea across the street into the warehouse.

    2. Get to Monty

    Wait inside while Andrea texts her contact. When prompted, join her at the north door, beside the bouncer in the white suit, and follow her to the back. Monty asks you into the back room and wants photos for the cartel's social media. Use the left stick to change pose and R2 (RT on Xbox, per PowerPyx) to take each picture.

    3. Knock out Monty

    After the photos Monty attacks. It is a melee fight: keep hitting until he is knocked out.

    4. Fight your way out

    You now shoot a path through the building so Andrea can get Monty out.

    Tip: kill the first enemy, then turn around and go back into the photo room. Monty's 1992 Pistol is on the floor. GTABase calls it a variant weapon you can only get this way, and PowerPyx tells you to take it before moving forward.

    Work through the building and check doorways and corners. In the large room where the cockfight was held, more enemies arrive in vehicles through the garage on the right.

    5. Drive to Vice City

    Lucia and Andrea put Monty in a car outside. Get in and drive to the marker in Vice City. Gang members follow, but you can ignore them: both guides say you lose them on the way out of Hamlet. About halfway there the game prompts you to switch to Jason.

    6. Interrogate Monty (Jason)

    1. Meet Lucia and Andrea at the front of the building.
    2. Pull Monty out of the trunk.
    3. Walk behind him with your gun out until he reaches the backyard.
    4. Knock him down when Raul tells you to.
    5. Use the loader to swing Monty over the alligator pool, then lower and raise him as Raul asks his questions.

    Tip: lower him close to the water, never into it. Fully lowering him kills him, and getting through every question with Monty alive is what earns the Professional bonus.

    7. Deal with Monty

    When Raul has finished, you choose.

    Choice Result
    Set Monty down on land Mission completes
    Drop him into the pool Mission completes

    Neither guide reports a different reward for either option.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

    • Bringing Jason to the start. The mission only begins for Lucia on her own.
    • Leaving the photo room behind. The pistol cannot be collected later.
    • Holding the loader down too long. Monty dies and the bonus is gone.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed
    Weapon Monty's 1992 Pistol, a variant pistol, from the photo room
    Professional bonus Finish Raul's questions without killing Monty
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    More variant pistols are listed in GTA 6 weapons. The other half of the practice for the bank job is Safe Word; the whole chain is in GTA 6 heists. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start The Missing Link in GTA 6?

    Finish Company Property, then go to Sloppy Molly's in Watson Bay as Lucia, on her own. Both guides say the mission only starts for Lucia.

    Where is Monty's 1992 Pistol in the GTA 6 The Missing Link walkthrough?

    On the floor of the back room where the photos were taken. Kill the first enemy after the fistfight, then go back in for it before you move on.

    Should you drop Monty into the pool in GTA 6?

    It is your choice at the end, and the mission completes either way. Just do not lower him fully into the water during the questions, or he dies early and you lose the Professional bonus.
