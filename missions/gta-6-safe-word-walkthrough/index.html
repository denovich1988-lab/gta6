    GTA 6 Safe Word Walkthrough: Steal the Practice Safe

    GTA 6 Safe Word walkthrough: blend into the shoot at Gil's mansion, find the remote in his desk, winch the safe out and practise safecracking for $1,500.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Safe Word is a Cartel Heist prep mission in Chapter 3, given by Nicole at Raul's compound after Company Property.
    • The remote that reveals the safe is in the left drawer of the desk in Gil's office at his mansion in Peacock Estates.
    • Opening the practice safe gives Gil's Kalinga .50 pistol and $1,500, and teaches the safecracking used in the heist.

    This GTA 6 Safe Word walkthrough covers the theft of a practice safe: a smaller model of the one in Sinfrontera National Bank, kept in the mansion of an adult-film producer named Gil. Jason bluffs his way through a film shoot, Lucia tears the safe out with a truck, and the mission ends with a safecracking tutorial worth doing properly. PowerPyx subtitles it Steal the Practice Safe.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Safe Word (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Given by Nicole
    Starts at Raul's compound, Stockyard
    Requirement Company Property
    Reward Gil's Kalinga .50 and $1,500, both from the practice safe

    Objectives

    1. Talk to Nicole and drive to Gil's mansion.
    2. Sneak onto the set and follow Gil's assistant.
    3. Play your part in the shoot.
    4. Find the safe in Gil's office.
    5. Open the window, attach the winch and pull the safe out.
    6. Deliver the safe to Felipe.
    7. Practise on the safe.

    PowerPyx shows 18 on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into seven.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive to Peacock Estates

    Speak to Nicole at the compound, then take the large truck parked outside. Gil's mansion is in Peacock Estates, at the very south edge of the city.

    2. Sneak onto the set (Jason)

    Crouch and go through the front gate. Move down the right side of the house and through the archway. A woman coming out of a door stops you, so blend in: follow Gil's assistant to the backyard, where a shoot is under way.

    3. Play your part

    After makeup, walk to the pool and pick up the skimmer. Then follow the prompts in order:

    1. Skim the pool until you are told to stop.
    2. Look at Samantha, on a lounger.
    3. Pick up the lotion and spread it on Sasha's back.
    4. When Rodney steps in, punch him a few times.
    5. Stop and let him hit you.
    6. Keep punching until he is knocked out.

    You are then asked whether to participate further in the shoot.

    Choice Result
    Participate Jason stays in the scene longer; both guides say Lucia will not be happy
    Do not participate Jason walks away and heads for the safe

    Tip: PowerPyx recommends not participating. If you care about the couple's relationship level, that is the safe pick; see GTA 6 romance.

    4. Find the safe

    Run around the house to the door you could not get through earlier. Inside, go through the first double doors, then the closed doors on the right side of the hallway. That is Gil's office. Open the drawer on the left side of the desk and use the remote. It reveals the safe.

    5. Pull the safe out

    Lucia backs the truck up to the house. Open the window and attach the winch to the safe. The game switches to Lucia: hold the accelerator (R2 on PS5, RT on Xbox, per PowerPyx) until the safe is pulled out of the house.

    6. Deliver the safe to Felipe

    Drive west to Felipe's garage with the safe dragging behind. Police chase you on the way. Swing the truck left and right so the safe swings into their cars.

    Tip: two things here give a Psycho criminal rating, according to both guides: hitting the cyclists Jason calls out, and hitting gravestones when the route turns into the cemetery. Keep the safe straight through both.

    Drive into the garage to drop it off.

    7. Practise safecracking

    Back at Raul's compound you can practise as many times as you like. The steps match the Cartel Heist at the end of the chapter:

    1. Drill. Hold the drill in position. Keep the right gauge steady and adjust as the left one rises. Do not let the light on the drill turn red.
    2. Cable. Guide the cable through the door without touching the walls, and hook the gold ring at the end.
    3. Hold the door. Hold both triggers and mash the button to keep the door secure while Nicole drills and enters the combination.

    The safe opens. Take Gil's Kalinga .50 and the $1,500 inside, then quit the practice to complete the mission.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

    • Standing up at the gate. Crouch on the way in.
    • Hitting cyclists or gravestones with the safe. It marks your criminal profile.
    • Quitting the practice before the safe opens. The pistol and the cash come only from a successful run.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash $1,500 from inside the practice safe
    Weapon Gil's Kalinga .50, a variant pistol
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the safe in the GTA 6 Safe Word walkthrough?

    In Gil's office in his mansion in Peacock Estates, at the far south of Vice City. Open the left drawer of the desk to find the remote that reveals it.

    Should you participate further in the shoot in Safe Word?

    It is optional. Choosing to participate keeps Jason in the scene longer, and both guides warn that Lucia will not be happy. Choosing not to sends Jason straight to the safe.

    What do you get for cracking the practice safe in GTA 6?

    Gil's Kalinga .50, a variant pistol, and $1,500 in cash from inside the safe.

    How do you avoid a Psycho rating in Safe Word?

    While dragging the safe, do not hit the cyclists Jason warns you about, and do not hit gravestones when you cut through the cemetery.
