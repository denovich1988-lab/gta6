    GTA 6 Impounded Vehicle Walkthrough: Get Lucia's Car Back

    GTA 6 Impounded Vehicle walkthrough: find the green car icon near the Port Gellhorn police station, drive through the fence and escape a two-star wanted level.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Impounded Vehicle is an optional Chapter 2 mission: Lucia's personal car is impounded automatically after Breakfast Run.
    • The car is a green icon near the police station in Port Gellhorn; driving through the fence brings two stars.
    • It doubles as a tutorial: later, the Scooter Bros app can return an impounded vehicle for a fee.

    This GTA 6 Impounded Vehicle walkthrough covers the quickest of the four jobs that follow Coffee Break: stealing Lucia's own car back from the police. It takes a couple of minutes and teaches a rule that matters for the rest of the game, which is what happens to a registered car you abandon at a crime scene.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Impounded Vehicle (Chapter 2, optional)
    Playable characters Lucia or Jason
    Given by Lucia
    Location Impound lot near the police station, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Coffee Break
    Reward Lucia's personal vehicle back

    Objectives

    1. Steal Lucia's personal vehicle from the impound.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find the green car icon

    Lucia's car, the one registered in Moses, is impounded automatically after the robbery spree in Breakfast Run. It appears as a green car icon in Port Gellhorn, next to the police station.

    2. Go through the fence

    Drive through the fence around the lot. Two officers see you, and you get a two-star wanted level.

    3. Swap cars and leave

    Jump into Lucia's car and get out of the search area. Off-road trails work well around Port Gellhorn; see how the wanted level works.

    Tip: arrive in a vehicle you do not mind losing. You leave it in the lot when you switch.

    How impounding works afterwards

    Both guides treat the mission as a tutorial and give the same rules.

    Rule Details
    What gets impounded A personal vehicle used in a crime and left in the police search area
    What does not Stolen, unregistered vehicles
    Getting it back the easy way The Scooter Bros app on your phone returns it instantly for a fee
    Premium tier $12,500; includes daily credits for free returns

    Neither guide states the standard fee.

    Why it matters: for robberies, a stolen car is the better getaway vehicle. Keep the registered one out of the search area.

    Where players fail

    • Lingering in the lot. The officers are already on you; switch and go.
    • Using a second registered car to break in. It can end up impounded in turn.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward Access to Lucia's personal vehicle again (PowerPyx)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as counting towards 100%

    The other three jobs from the same scene are Thrillbillies, Hit Avery Pawn and Hit Kingpin Bowling. Order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the car in the GTA 6 Impounded Vehicle walkthrough?

    At the impound lot near the police station in Port Gellhorn. It shows on the map as a green car icon.

    How do you get a car out of the impound in GTA 6?

    In this mission you drive through the fence and take it, which brings a two-star wanted level. Afterwards you can also pay a fee in the Scooter Bros app to have an impounded vehicle returned at once.

    Why does a car get impounded in GTA 6?

    Both guides say a personal vehicle used in a crime and left inside the police search area is eventually found and impounded.
