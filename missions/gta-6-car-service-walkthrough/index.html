    GTA 6 Car Service Walkthrough: Steal the Schafter V12

    GTA 6 Car Service walkthrough: pick up the silenced pistol, cross the chop shop roof in Salton, steal the Schafter V12 and deliver it for $3,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Car Service is a Lucia-only Chapter 3 mission from Raul: steal a Schafter V12 from a gang chop shop in Salton.
    • A nearby car trunk holds flash grenades and a silenced Klose K17; the quiet route is over the white van on the north side.
    • Delivering the car to Leonida Auto World pays $3,000 and unlocks Randy as a vehicle drop-off.

    This GTA 6 Car Service walkthrough covers Raul's test for Lucia: take one car out of a guarded chop shop and deliver it across the city. It can be done without a shot if you use the roof. PowerPyx subtitles it Steal The Car for Raul.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Car Service (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Lucia only
    Given by Raul, by message from a blocked number
    Starts at Salton, south Vice City
    Requirement Meet the Heders, then about a day's wait
    Reward $3,000; Randy unlocked as a vehicle drop-off

    Objectives

    1. Search the chop shop for the Schafter V12.
    2. Steal the Schafter V12.
    3. Escape the area.
    4. Deliver the car to Leonida Auto World.

    Step-by-step

    1. Take the gear

    Raul's contact has left equipment in the trunk of a car near the start: flash grenades and a silenced Klose K17 pistol. Taking them is optional, and both guides recommend the silenced pistol.

    2. Go over the roof

    Climb onto the white van on the north side of the chop shop and step across to the metal roof. Walk around the edge of the yard to see who is where.

    Who Where
    Two gang members Loading a van in the middle of the yard
    Two guards Left and right of the entry gates
    A dog Usually beside one of the gate guards

    If one of them is shot and the others notice, they open fire and more men arrive at the gates by car.

    Tip: leave the dog alone unless it attacks. Both guides say harming it hurts your criminal rating.

    3. Steal the car

    The Schafter V12 is parked in the middle of the covered area, next to a small room. Get in and drive out.

    4. Escape

    Leave before the police arrive. If they do see you, you have to lose them before the delivery marker works. See GTA 6 wanted level.

    5. Deliver to Leonida Auto World

    The dealership is in the far north-west corner of the city. Driving into the car park starts the closing scene, in which the dealer, Randy, offers to buy high-end vehicles from you.

    Where players fail

    • Going in through a gate. That walks you into both guards and the dog.
    • Firing an unsilenced weapon. Reinforcements arrive by car.
    • Driving to the dealership with police behind you. Lose them first.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $3,000
    Unlock Randy as a drop-off for vehicles with standard trackers and for cars on his request list
    Also Wanted areas in Port Gellhorn and Key Lento cleared (PowerPyx only)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Randy is the second way to sell cars, after Pay 'n' Spray opens in Welcome to Vice City. See GTA 6 cars and how to make money in GTA 6. PowerPyx lists First Impressions, the first meeting with Raul, as the mission this one unlocks; we will add it once a second guide confirms the details. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Car Service walkthrough mission?

    Finish Meet the Heders, then wait about a day. Raul messages Lucia from a blocked number and a marker appears in Salton, in the south of Vice City.

    Where is the Schafter V12 in Car Service?

    In the middle of the covered area of the chop shop, next to a small room.

    Should you shoot the dog in Car Service?

    No, unless it attacks you. Both guides warn that hurting it counts against your criminal rating.

    What does Car Service unlock?

    $3,000 and Randy at Leonida Auto World as a drop-off for vehicles. PowerPyx adds that the wanted areas in Port Gellhorn and Key Lento are cleared.
