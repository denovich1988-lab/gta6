    GTA 6 Ride or Die Walkthrough: Free Sneakers and DWNPLY's Car

    GTA 6 Ride or Die walkthrough: get the $1,580 DWNPLY sneakers free with a basketball, lift his keys, find the car by its horn and reach the Speedshop.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Ride or Die is a Jason-only Chapter 3 mission from Dre'Quan; it unlocks about a day after Credit Check.
    • The DWNPLY sneakers cost $1,580, but hitting the seller with a basketball makes him drop the bag for free.
    • Sound the horn with the stolen keys to find the car on the east side of the lot, then deliver it to the Stockyard Speedshop.

    This GTA 6 Ride or Die walkthrough covers Dre'Quan's revenge on the rapper DWNPLY: Jason poses as a fan, gets a pair of sneakers signed and walks off with the man's car keys. The only real decision is whether to pay $1,580 for the sneakers. You do not have to. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Meet Dre'Quan.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Ride or Die (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Dre'Quan
    Starts at The Jack of Hearts, Crosstown; go alone as Jason
    Requirement Credit Check, then about a day's wait for his message
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    1. Meet Dre'Quan at the Jack of Hearts.
    2. Go to the Eris store and get a pair of DWNPLY sneakers.
    3. Get into the VIP lounge.
    4. Hand DWNPLY the shoes and take his keys.
    5. Find and steal DWNPLY's car.
    6. Deliver it to the Speedshop.

    PowerPyx lists 13 on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into six.

    Step-by-step

    1. Wait for Dre'Quan

    Sit on the ledge outside the Jack of Hearts until Dre'Quan comes out. His plan is to steal DWNPLY's car.

    2. Get the sneakers

    Drive to the Eris store on the Downtown waterfront and go in with Dre'Quan. Upstairs, to the right of the DJ, a man with a bag offers a pair of DWNPLY sneakers for $1,580.

    Option What happens Cost
    Pay You get the sneakers $1,580
    Haggle He refuses and walks away Nothing yet
    Basketball Follow him outside, take a basketball from the stand on the right and throw it at him; he drops the bag Free

    Tip: both guides recommend the basketball. PowerPyx adds that simply walking into him also makes him drop the bag.

    Pick up the bag and go back in. Dre'Quan is waiting just inside the door.

    3. Get into the VIP lounge

    Follow Dre'Quan upstairs. When the bouncer stops you, choose Show Shoes. That gets you in without paying.

    4. Take the keys

    DWNPLY is sitting with a group to the left of the bar. Jason hands him the sneakers to sign. While he signs, hold the button to reach for his keys (Triangle on PS5, Y on Xbox, per PowerPyx). He stops to ask who the signature is for. Answer him, then keep holding.

    5. Find the car

    Run downstairs after Dre'Quan to the parking lot. Press the horn button on the keys and follow the sound to the east side, where the car sits in a lit VIP space. Get in and wait for Dre'Quan.

    6. Drive to the Speedshop

    As you leave the lot, a gang member jumps onto the hood. Swerve left and right to throw him off. DWNPLY pays the Cash City gang for protection, and they may give chase: get out of the red area on the map to lose them. Then drive to the Stockyard Speedshop to finish.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions. The places people get stuck:

    • Letting go when DWNPLY speaks. Answer his question, then hold the button again until Jason has the keys.
    • Searching the lot car by car. The horn tells you where the car is.
    • Fighting the pursuers. Leaving the red area is enough.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment None listed by either guide

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you get the sneakers for free in the GTA 6 Ride or Die walkthrough?

    Haggle with the seller so he walks off, follow him outside, take a basketball from the stand on the right and throw it at him. He drops the bag and you pick it up. That saves $1,580.

    Where is DWNPLY's car in Ride or Die?

    On the east side of the parking lot, in one of the lit VIP spaces. Use the horn button on the keys and follow the sound.

    How do you start Ride or Die?

    Finish Credit Check and wait about a day for a message from Dre'Quan. Then go alone as Jason to the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown and wait on the ledge outside.

    Does Ride or Die pay anything?

    No reward is listed by either guide. GTABase marks the mission as required for 100% completion.
