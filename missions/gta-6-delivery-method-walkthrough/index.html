    GTA 6 Delivery Method Walkthrough: TSS Office Hack and Fire Alarm

    GTA 6 Delivery Method walkthrough: get Jason into the airport as a food courier, hack both TSS computers, use the fire alarm and leave without being caught.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Delivery Method is a Jason-only Chapter 4 mission from Andrea that opens after Power Play and starts in the planning room in Crosstown.
    • You hack two computers in the Transport Security Services office at the airport; a fire alarm clears the building for the second one.
    • Take too long upstairs and staff come back and report you; getting out unseen earns a Professional bonus.

    This GTA 6 Delivery Method walkthrough covers Andrea's job for Jason: ride into the airport dressed as a food courier and put Lucia's details into the security system. It is short and has no combat, but the second half is on a hidden clock. Once the fire alarm is ringing, you have until the staff walk back in.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Delivery Method (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup)
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Andrea
    Starts at The planning room, Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Power Play
    Reward No cash; Professional bonus if you are not caught

    Why it matters: the hack is what lets Lucia pass airport security on the day of the heist, when she poses as a wine authenticator.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists eleven on-screen steps; GTABase folds them into six.

    1. Go to the security gate.
    2. Go to the TSS office.
    3. Talk to the receptionist.
    4. Hack the computer.
    5. Remove the flash drive.
    6. Cause an evacuation.
    7. Find the Airport Security Department.
    8. Find a computer with database access.
    9. Wait for the hack to complete.
    10. Remove the flash drive.
    11. Leave the area.

    Step-by-step

    1. Ride to the airport

    Andrea sends Jason out as a delivery rider. Ride the bike south to the security gate. When the guard waves you through, turn right and go into the Transport Security Services (TSS) office.

    2. Hack the reception computer

    1. Talk to the receptionist.
    2. As soon as she leaves, go behind her desk.
    3. Use the computer to plug in the flash drive.
    4. When the hack finishes, interact again to take the drive out.

    3. Set off the fire alarm

    The alarm is behind the reception desk. Pull it and wait while the staff file out. You cannot go upstairs until the building is empty.

    4. Hack the security database

    Step Where
    Go up Stairs to the second floor (the Airport Security Department)
    Find the computer Office at the back left of the room
    Plug in Use the computer to insert the drive
    Finish Wait for the hack, then remove the drive

    Tip: do not explore. Both guides say that if you take too long, someone returns to the building, catches you and reports you for trespassing.

    5. Leave the airport

    If you were quick, everyone is still standing at the muster point across the parking lot. Get back on the bike and ride out of the airport to finish the mission.

    Where players fail

    • Dawdling upstairs. The only fail-like outcome either guide describes is being found in the building when staff come back.
    • Forgetting the drive. Both hacks need a second interaction to remove the flash drive before the objective moves on.
    • Trying the stairs early. The upper floor only opens up once the evacuation is complete.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None
    Bonus Professional bonus for getting in and out without being caught
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Delivery Method sits beside Close Surveillance, Team Building and Fully Catered as setup for the VCIA Takedown. Jason's earlier undercover shift at the same airport is in Too Much Baggage. For the full order, see the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the second computer in the GTA 6 Delivery Method walkthrough?

    On the second floor of the TSS office, in the Airport Security Department. The computer with database access is in the office at the back left of the room.

    How do you get upstairs in Delivery Method?

    Pull the fire alarm behind the reception desk after the first hack. Everyone has to leave the building before you can go up the stairs.

    What is the reward for Delivery Method in GTA 6?

    No cash. PowerPyx lists the reward as none; both guides say finishing without being caught gives a Professional bonus.

    How do you start Delivery Method?

    Finish Power Play, then take Jason to the planning room in Crosstown, Vice City, and meet Andrea.
