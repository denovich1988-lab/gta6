    GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks: All Side Missions by Chapter

    Every GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks mission by chapter: who gives it, who can play it, which ones are missable and which strands count for the trophy.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated

    Read

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has more than 35 Strangers and Freaks missions from about 15 characters, opening chapter by chapter.
    • The Strangers and Freaks trophy needs 8 strands; Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed to count.
    • Three missions are flagged as missable: Get Shirley's Rum, Steal the Scooter and Race Against Yago.

    GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks are the optional side missions given by characters you meet around Leonida, and there are more than 35 of them spread over the first four chapters. This page lists every one that appears on both major lists, GTABase and PowerPyx, with the giver, who can play it and the chapter. Where the two disagree, we say so. The status is "partly confirmed" because neither list gives rewards, and a few details differ.

    How Strangers and Freaks work

    • Finding them. PowerPyx says they usually show as Optional Missions in the map's quest list or as purple side-activity markers; some are unmarked.
    • Strands. Each character has a chain of missions. After one, do something else until the character calls or texts; the next part can take in-game days and can cross into the next chapter.
    • Characters. Many can be played as either lead, but some are for Jason only or Lucia only.
    • After the story. Both lists say strands stay available in free roam afterwards, apart from the missable ones.
    • No replay. See can you replay missions in GTA 6.

    Which strands count for the trophy

    The Strangers and Freaks trophy asks for 8 strands.

    Counts Does not count Not labelled yet
    Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone, Eddie Acosta, Shirley Bret, Randy, Dunk

    PowerPyx adds Cal and Moses to the "does not count" side. Only four strands are confirmed as counting so far, which is fewer than the eight required, so this table is incomplete.

    Tip: PowerPyx suggests checking your trophy progress before and after the last mission of a strand to see whether it counted. More in the trophy guide.

    Missable missions

    Mission Why
    Get Shirley's Rum Chapter 1 only
    Steal the Scooter Chapter 1 only
    Race Against Yago Time-sensitive

    Chapter 1's free roam ends at Tourist Trap, so do Shirley's two before then. Both lists say missable missions are not required for 100% completion, which PowerPyx says counts 100 optional tasks.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many Strangers and Freaks missions are in GTA 6?

    Neither main list gives an official total. GTABase lists 35 entries and PowerPyx lists 41, from about 15 characters across Chapters 1 to 4.

    Which GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks missions are missable?

    PowerPyx flags Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter, which exist only in Chapter 1, and Race Against Yago, which is time-sensitive. Both lists say missable missions are not needed for 100%.

    Can you replay Strangers and Freaks missions in GTA 6?

    No. PowerPyx reports that neither story missions nor Strangers and Freaks missions can be replayed, so keep a manual save if you want to see one again.

    Which strands count for the Strangers and Freaks trophy?

    You need 8 strands. Both lists agree that Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count and that Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone, Eddie Acosta and Shirley do not. The remaining strands are not labelled yet.
