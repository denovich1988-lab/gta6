    GTA 6 Feng Shui Walkthrough: Furniture and the Two Games

    GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough: move the furniture with Cal, then play Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III. What you can skip and how the mission ends.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Feng Shui is the tenth mission in GTA 6: Jason and Cal get the house ready before Lucia comes home.
    • You move four pieces of furniture, then play two games on the console: Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III.
    • Neither game needs to be beaten; the mission moves on by itself and ends the next morning.

    This GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough covers the tenth mission, a quiet evening at Jason's house with Cal before Lucia is released. PowerPyx also lists it as "Meet Cal". There is nothing to fight and nothing to fail: you move furniture, play two games-within-the-game and wake up to a phone call.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Feng Shui (mission 10, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Meet Cal
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Jason's house in Key Lento
    Requirement Finish Island Dreams
    Reward None listed
    Next mission Coming Home, after a set of optional errands

    PowerPyx reports that a text message arrives after Island Dreams and a marker then appears on the house. Walk in to begin.

    Objectives

    1. Sit on the couch.
    2. Move the TV, the coffee table, the couch and the chair.
    3. Play Tenebrae Lux.
    4. Play Street Trash III.

    Step-by-step

    1. Get a beer and sit down

    Take a beer from the fridge and sit on the couch with Cal. He decides the place is not good enough for Lucia after her time inside and that the room needs rearranging.

    2. Move the furniture

    Help Cal carry four things, in this order: the TV, the coffee table, the couch and the chair.

    After the coffee table you can say it is fine or offer to move it again. PowerPyx tried both: if you move it again, Cal cannot make up his mind and has you shuffle it back and forth until it is where it started. Nothing changes.

    3. Play Tenebrae Lux

    Sit back down and the console comes on. The first game, Tenebrae Lux, is a slow black-and-white first-person game.

    Source What it reports
    PowerPyx Head for the locked door on the right and try to climb the hill. The game cannot be finished and ends by itself after a while
    VGC It has small chessboard-style puzzles where you clear the board. Finish the first two and Jason asks to play something else

    Either way, a few minutes of play is enough. Jason gets bored and the game switches.

    4. Play Street Trash III

    The second game is an arcade shooter; VGC describes it as a twin-stick shooter. PowerPyx notes an on-screen timer and says it cannot be won. Play until you are killed, or let yourself die straight away if you want to move on.

    5. The morning after

    A cutscene shows the two of them up through the night. In the morning Jason gets the call that Lucia is about to be released, which sets up Coming Home.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail state. The only time sink is trying to beat the two games:

    • Tenebrae Lux has no ending to reach in this mission.
    • Street Trash III always ends in a loss.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed in either guide
    Optional goals None listed
    Unlocks The console at the safe house, usable at any time (VGC)

    What to do before the next mission

    Feng Shui is the last mission before Lucia returns, and the game gives you a set of optional errands first: groceries, flowers, a necklace, a haircut and collecting her clothes. Both guides say these affect the relationship between the two leads, so they are worth doing. Our Coming Home walkthrough lists them with the one price the guides give.

    The full story order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you finish the games in the GTA 6 Feng Shui walkthrough?

    You do not have to. Tenebrae Lux ends after a short while and Street Trash III ends when you die or the timer runs out. PowerPyx says dying at once is the quickest way through.

    How do you start Feng Shui in GTA 6?

    Finish Island Dreams. A marker then appears at Jason's house, and the mission begins when you walk in.

    Does the coffee table choice matter in Feng Shui?

    No. If you agree to move it again, Cal has you shift it back and forth until it is where it started. The result is the same.

    Can you play the console again after Feng Shui?

    Yes. VGC notes that the JoyStation at the safe house can be used at any time.
