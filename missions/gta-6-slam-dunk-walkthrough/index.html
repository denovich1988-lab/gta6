    GTA 6 Slam Dunk Walkthrough: Dr. Bill and the Kolts

    GTA 6 Slam Dunk walkthrough: grab Dr. Bill with Dunk, escape the Kolts at the docks, win the airstrip shootout and collect $1,700 and a new fence.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Slam Dunk is the Chapter 2 mission that finally gets Jason his painkillers; it starts at Dunk's trailer after his second phone call.
    • It has two escapes and one big fight: reverse out of the Kolts' docks, then clear every Kolt at the airstrip south-west of Port Gellhorn.
    • Dunk sends $1,700 the next day, becomes a fence for stolen goods and marks two robbery locations.

    This GTA 6 Slam Dunk walkthrough covers the hardest fight of Chapter 2 so far: Lucia, Dunk and a retired doctor against the Kolts. It closes the painkiller storyline that began in Girl's Trip. PowerPyx subtitles it Find Painkillers.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Slam Dunk (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Dunk
    Starts at Dunk's trailer, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Other Chapter 2 missions done, and both of Dunk's calls in Painkiller Supplier
    Reward $1,700 via BuckMe the next day; Dunk becomes a fence

    Tip: both guides rely on Zombix for healing in the airstrip fight, so stock up at a convenience store before you go to the trailer.

    Objectives

    1. Follow Dunk.
    2. Go to the docks.
    3. Escape the compound and lose the Kolts.
    4. Pick up Dr. Bill.
    5. Go to the meeting spot.
    6. Defend yourself and find Dunk.
    7. Take Dunk to his trailer.

    Step-by-step

    1. Wait for Dr. Bill

    Follow Dunk to a spot behind a dumpster near the convenience store. Time skips to the afternoon and Dr. Bill arrives, pretending not to know Dunk. Follow him to his car, where Dunk pulls a gun and forces him inside.

    Bill explains that he has retired and can no longer write prescriptions, but that he used to supply the Kolts.

    2. Drive to the docks

    The Kolts let the car through the gate. Drive slowly behind the man walking ahead of you.

    3. Reverse out

    The gang grows suspicious because nobody expected Bill, and they start shooting. Reverse quickly, leave the compound and keep driving until you have lost them.

    4. Pick up Dr. Bill

    Bill phones Declan, his contact in the Kolts, then jumps out of the car and knocks himself out. Go back for him; he is put in the trunk automatically.

    5. The airstrip

    Drive to the meeting point at the airstrip south-west of Port Gellhorn. The Kolts shoot Bill at once and turn on you.

    6. Clear the area

    Kill every Kolt, including the ones inside the buildings. Stay in cover, move slowly and heal with Zombix when needed. Then enter the main building in the middle of the area, where Dunk grabs the pills in a short scene.

    7. Drive Dunk home

    Take Dunk back to his trailer. Lucia delivers the painkillers to Jason and you are placed outside the motel.

    Where players fail

    • Hesitating at the docks. Reverse the moment the shooting starts.
    • Rushing the airstrip. Enemies are inside buildings as well as outside; clear them from cover.
    • Arriving without healing items. This is the first long shootout of the chapter.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $1,700 from Dunk via BuckMe the next day
    Dunk as a fence Sells stolen goods for you from now on
    Robbery locations Two are marked when Dunk calls the next day
    Dr. Bill's body About $1,000 if you loot it (both guides)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Slam Dunk walkthrough?

    Finish the other Chapter 2 missions and wait for Dunk to call twice (the waiting mission Painkiller Supplier). Slam Dunk is then marked at Dunk's trailer in Port Gellhorn.

    Where is the meeting spot in Slam Dunk?

    At the airstrip south-west of Port Gellhorn. The Kolts open fire there; Dunk is in the main building in the middle of the area.

    What is the reward for Slam Dunk in GTA 6?

    $1,700 from Dunk through BuckMe the next day. Dunk also becomes a fence, and his next call marks two robbery locations. Looting Dr. Bill's body gives about $1,000 more.
