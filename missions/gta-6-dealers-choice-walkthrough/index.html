    GTA 6 Dealer's Choice Walkthrough: Cut or Pure?

    GTA 6 Dealer's Choice walkthrough: meet Dre'Quan in the studio, pick Cut or Pure product at Tyrone's, close the deal peacefully and handle the ambush.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Dealer's Choice is a Jason-only Chapter 3 mission from Dre'Quan that starts at the Jack of Hearts.
    • Tyrone offers Cut or Pure product. Buyers spot Cut, but you can offer half price and still walk away with the money.
    • Both guides say the choice affects this mission only; settling the deal without violence earns a Professional bonus.

    This GTA 6 Dealer's Choice walkthrough covers Jason's first job in Vice City: a pickup and a sale with Dre'Quan as backup. Boobie Ike calls it a favor in return for the apartment. The one decision, Cut or Pure, sounds important and is not, so pick the version of the scene you would rather watch. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Go See Dre'Quan.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Dealer's Choice (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Dre'Quan
    Starts at Jack of Hearts, Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Finish Welcome to Vice City
    Reward Disputed: see the FAQ above

    Objectives

    1. Drive to the pick-up location.
    2. Choose which product to take.
    3. Drive to the deal location.
    4. Take the money.
    5. Deal with the confrontation.
    6. Take Dre'Quan to Southside.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find Dre'Quan in the studio

    Go up the stairs you used in the last mission, through the door at the top and into the recording booth. Wait for Dre'Quan to finish. He asks which hook you prefer; any answer works. Follow him upstairs to Boobie, who explains the job.

    2. Pick up from Tyrone

    Get into Dre'Quan's car outside and drive to a nearby apartment. Follow him to the room on the second floor, where he talks with a dealer called Tyrone. You can interrupt if you do not want to wait.

    3. Cut or Pure

    Cut Pure
    Tyrone's pitch Hints at extra profit The straightforward option
    At the deal Buyers notice Buyers test it and pay
    Your way out Offer it at half price; they hand back the product and pay None needed
    Later effect None (both guides) None (both guides)

    4. The football detour

    Dre'Quan takes a different route back to the car through a large park. A short tutorial has you throw a football back to some kids.

    5. Make the deal

    Drive a short way south, close to the Jack of Hearts. Follow Dre'Quan to the group standing by the cars and hand over the bag when prompted. The buyer tests the product slowly; you can ask him to hurry. When he is done, take back the backpack, which now holds the money.

    Tip: both guides say a non-violent finish here earns a Professional bonus.

    6. The confrontation

    On the way back to the car a man stops you and insists the bag is his. He attacks no matter what you say. Knock him out with melee, then get in the car.

    7. Drive to Southside

    Head for the shop in Southside. A scene starts about halfway there and ends the mission. Back at the apartment, Cal tries and fails to get into the hard drive, and mentions that Brian once had a capable contact in Vice City.

    Where players fail

    • Drawing a gun on the man with the bag. Melee is enough and keeps the job quiet.
    • Expecting Cut to pay more. Neither guide reports extra money for it.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Bonus Professional, for settling the deal peacefully
    Choice Cut or Pure; this mission only
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Finishing Dealer's Choice opens several Chapter 3 missions at once, including Dubious Provenance, Mami Knows Best and, a day or two later, Meet the Heders. Tyrone has his own job for Jason in Here, Kitty Kitty. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Should you pick Cut or Pure in the GTA 6 Dealer's Choice walkthrough?

    Pure gives a smooth deal. Cut is noticed by the buyers, but offering it at half price still ends peacefully. PowerPyx and GTABase both say the choice has no effect later.

    Who can play Dealer's Choice?

    Only Jason. Go back to the Jack of Hearts as Jason after Welcome to Vice City.

    How much does Dealer's Choice pay?

    PowerPyx's summary lists $1,500 for selling Pure product, but its own walkthrough says you gain no money either way, and GTABase lists no cash reward. Treat the payout as unconfirmed.

    Can you avoid the fight at the end of Dealer's Choice?

    No. The man who claims the bag attacks whatever you answer. Both guides say to knock him out with melee and drive off.
