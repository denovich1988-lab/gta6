    GTA 6 Pipework Walkthrough: Fix Mandy's Sink (Mission 7)

    GTA 6 Pipework walkthrough: where the plunger is, how to unblock Mandy's sink, what happens afterwards and whether Jason can romance Mandy.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Pipework is the seventh mission in GTA 6: Jason goes to collect Mandy's rent and ends up fixing her sink.
    • The plunger is on the bathroom floor; use it on the kitchen sink with R3, then clear the pipe underneath.
    • Jason cannot romance Mandy, whatever you choose in the conversation.

    This GTA 6 Pipework walkthrough covers the seventh mission, a short indoor job where Jason has to unblock a tenant's sink before she will talk about rent. PowerPyx also lists it as "Collect Mandy's Rent". There is no combat and no way to fail.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Pipework (mission 7, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Collect Mandy's Rent
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Mandy's house in Key Lento
    Requirement Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch and Unwanted Attention (PowerPyx)
    Reward Macizo Cigar (PowerPyx); rent money (VGC)
    Next mission Hey Arnold!

    If the marker is missing, finish the four earlier missions first: Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch and Unwanted Attention.

    Objectives

    1. Follow Mandy.
    2. Pick up the plunger.
    3. Fix the sink.

    Step-by-step

    1. Talk to Mandy

    Drive to the house and talk to Mandy outside. She is one of Brian's tenants, and she will not pay until her sink is fixed. Follow her in. PowerPyx notes that you can let her keep talking or cut in and ask where the plunger is.

    2. Pick up the plunger

    The plunger is on the floor of the bathroom, across from the bedroom.

    3. Plunge the sink

    Go to the kitchen, interact with the sink and tap R3 to work the plunger.

    4. Clear the pipe

    The second half happens under the sink. The guides word it differently:

    Source What you do
    PowerPyx Press R2 (RT) to use a wrench on the pipe
    VGC Move under the sink and clear the pipes in a short minigame

    Follow the on-screen prompt. Mandy keeps flirting with Jason throughout.

    5. The conversation afterwards

    When the sink is fixed, Mandy makes a pass at Jason. You can interrupt or hear her out. PowerPyx reports the same result either way: she offers a cigar for the repair, Jason says no to the rest, and she throws the cigar box out into the street.

    6. Leave the house

    Head outside. PowerPyx says to pick up the cigar from the ground on your way out. VGC says you collect the money from Mandy and that Brian phones as you leave.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition. The two things players get stuck on are:

    • Not finding the plunger. It is in the bathroom, not the kitchen.
    • Walking past the cigar. If you want it, pick it up before you drive off.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward Macizo Cigar, if you pick it up (PowerPyx)
    Rent VGC says you collect the money; PowerPyx does not mention a payment
    Optional goals None listed

    Does the choice matter?

    Not for romance. VGC says plainly that Jason cannot romance Mandy however you handle the visit, and that the relationship with her is still intact when you leave. The dialogue choice only changes how much of her pitch you hear. Jason's story is with Lucia, who returns four missions later in Coming Home.

    After Pipework, wait a few minutes: PowerPyx reports that a call from Brian starts the next mission, Hey Arnold!. The full order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the plunger in the GTA 6 Pipework walkthrough?

    On the floor of Mandy's bathroom, across from the bedroom she leads you to.

    How do you fix the sink in Pipework?

    Interact with the kitchen sink and tap R3 to plunge it, then work on the pipe under the sink. PowerPyx gives the second prompt as R2 (RT on Xbox) with a wrench.

    Can you romance Mandy in GTA 6?

    No. VGC states that Jason cannot romance Mandy however you play the mission, and PowerPyx describes him turning her down.

    What is the reward for Pipework?

    PowerPyx lists a Macizo Cigar, which you can pick up outside after Mandy throws the box out. VGC says you collect the rent money from her.
