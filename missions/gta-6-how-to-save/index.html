    How to Save in GTA 6: Manual Saves, Autosave and Slots

    How to save in GTA 6: pause, open Game and choose Save Game. 15 manual slots, one autosave, when saving is locked and how to organise saves by chapter.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated

    Read

    Quick answer

    • To save in GTA 6, open the pause menu, go to Game and choose Save Game; you can do it anywhere in free roam.
    • There are 15 manual slots and a single autosave slot that is overwritten each time.
    • Saving is locked during missions, and KeenGamer reports it is also locked in the prologue and after any cheat is used.

    To save in GTA 6, pause the game, open the Game section and choose Save Game: you get 15 manual slots and can save anywhere in free roam. Because missions cannot be replayed, your saves are the only way back to an earlier point, so it pays to use the slots with a plan.

    Saving at a glance
    Menu path Pause > Game > Save Game
    Pause button Options (PS5), Menu (Xbox Series X|S)
    Manual slots 15
    Autosave One slot, overwritten each time
    Where Anywhere in free roam; no safehouse needed
    Locked During missions; also the prologue and after cheats (KeenGamer)

    How to save manually

    1. Make sure you are not in a mission.
    2. Press Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox.
    3. Go to Game.
    4. Choose Save Game.
    5. Pick an empty slot, or overwrite one you no longer need.
    6. Let the save finish before you quit.

    To load, use the same Game tab and choose Load Game.

    How autosave works

    The game keeps one autosave. KeenGamer says it updates after you finish missions and open-world events; all three guides agree it lives in its own slot and replaces itself every time. Beebom confirms that an autosave never overwrites a manual save.

    Why it matters: the autosave only ever holds your most recent moment. If you want to go back further than that, only a manual save will do. PowerPyx also warns that loading an older manual save replaces the autosave.

    When you cannot save

    Situation Source
    During any mission All three guides (PowerPyx: "usually greyed out")
    The whole prologue, until Jason reaches his house after the first job for Brian KeenGamer
    After using any cheat, even a screen filter, until you restart the game KeenGamer

    The cheat rule matches what cheat lists report: an active cheat switches off saving and trophies. Make a clean save first. Details: GTA 6 cheats.

    Do beds and safehouses save the game?

    No. KeenGamer says sleeping in the bed at Jason's house skips the night and restores health and stamina, but saving is done from the menu, wherever you are.

    How to organise 15 slots

    PowerPyx and KeenGamer suggest the same structure for the five-chapter story:

    Slots Use
    10 Start and end of each chapter
    5 Rotating free-roam saves and saves before big choices

    Tip: alternate at least two slots after every mission. If something goes wrong in one save, you lose a few minutes instead of hours.

    Saves worth making early

    1. After the prologue, as soon as saving unlocks.
    2. Before Lucia's release. Beebom gives the house repairs as an example of content that closes: see Jason's house projects.
    3. Before Tourist Trap, the point of no return in Chapter 1: see the Tourist Trap walkthrough.
    4. Before entering any cheat code.

    Cloud saves

    KeenGamer notes that PS5 saves back up to PlayStation Plus cloud storage and Xbox saves sync to the cloud automatically. That is console behaviour rather than a GTA 6 setting.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you save in GTA 6?

    Press Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox, open the Game section, choose Save Game and pick a slot. It works anywhere in free roam; you do not need to be at a safehouse.

    How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

    15 manual save slots, plus one autosave slot. Every guide we checked gives the same number.

    Does GTA 6 autosave?

    Yes, into one slot that is overwritten each time. KeenGamer says it triggers after missions and open-world events. It never overwrites your manual saves.

    Why can't I save in GTA 6?

    You are probably in a mission, where saving is greyed out. KeenGamer adds two more cases: the whole prologue, and any session in which a cheat has been used.
