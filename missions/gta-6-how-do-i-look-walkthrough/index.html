    GTA 6 How Do I Look? Walkthrough: Buy a New Outfit

    GTA 6 How Do I Look? walkthrough: go to Bargain World in the south of Port Gellhorn and buy Lucia a new top and pants or shorts to complete the mission.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • How Do I Look? is a quick Chapter 2 mission: Lucia buys a new outfit.
    • You need one top and one pair of pants or shorts; any items count.
    • Bargain World, in the south of Port Gellhorn, is the cheap store the mission points you to.

    This GTA 6 How Do I Look? walkthrough covers the second half of Lucia's makeover: after the haircut in New Look, she needs clothes. It is a shopping mission with a single objective and no way to fail, and it is a good moment to learn how clothing stores work.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission How Do I Look? (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Lucia (self-started)
    Location Bargain World, south Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Girl's Trip
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    1. Buy a new outfit: a top and pants.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go to Bargain World

    The store is in the south of Port Gellhorn. The mission description offers a choice between shopping around and getting cheap clothes here; both guides send you to Bargain World.

    2. Buy a top

    Browse the tops and buy one. There are plenty of options and none is required.

    3. Buy pants or shorts

    Add a pair of pants or shorts. With both pieces bought, the mission completes.

    Tip: the objective needs two separate items. A top alone will not finish it.

    If you are short of cash

    PowerPyx repeats the advice from New Look: mug someone or rob another store's register. If you have finished Moses, selling an untracked car is the lower-risk way to fund both errands.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition. The usual hold-up is buying only one of the two pieces.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None (PowerPyx)
    Prices Not listed by either guide
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    What you buy goes into Lucia's wardrobe; swapping outfits later is explained in how to change clothes and hair in GTA 6. While you wait for the next story call, see Painkiller Supplier. Chapter order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do you buy clothes in the GTA 6 How Do I Look walkthrough?

    At Bargain World, a clothing store in the south of Port Gellhorn. The mission text says you can also shop around.

    What do you need to buy to finish How Do I Look?

    A top and a pair of pants or shorts. PowerPyx and GTABase both say any choice works.

    When does How Do I Look? unlock?

    After Girl's Trip in Chapter 2, at the same time as New Look.
