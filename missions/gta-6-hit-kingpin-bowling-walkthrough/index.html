    GTA 6 Hit Kingpin Bowling Walkthrough: Let's Go Bowling

    GTA 6 Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough: what to buy first, where the Luxury Watch, the Gutterball shotgun and the safe are, and how to get the trophy.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Hit Kingpin Bowling is an optional Chapter 2 raid on a bowling alley in Port Gellhorn held by the Kolts gang.
    • Loot all three key valuables for the Let's Go Bowling trophy: the Luxury Watch, the Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun and the office safe.
    • Bring sticky bombs for the safe, body armor and five Zombix; you leave with a two-star wanted level.

    This GTA 6 Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough lists what to buy before the raid and where each of the three key valuables is. Unlike the pawn shop job, this one is a gunfight from the first step: the bowling alley belongs to the Kolts, and they do not hand anything over. It carries a bronze trophy, Let's Go Bowling.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Hit Kingpin Bowling (Chapter 2, optional)
    Playable characters Jason or Lucia
    Given by Lucia
    Starts at The bowling alley, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Coffee Break
    Reward Cash from fenced loot, a unique shotgun, a collectible watch, a trophy

    Preparation

    Both guides give the same shopping list.

    Prepare Where Why
    Call your partner Hold Right on the D-pad and ask to meet up A second gun
    Five Zombix Any convenience store Healing
    Body armor Ammu-Nation Refill it with the clerk
    Sticky bombs Ammu-Nation The only way into the safe

    Objectives

    1. Rob the compound.
    2. Flee the compound.

    Step-by-step

    1. Shoot your way in

    There is no quiet option in either guide. Enter and clear the Kolts room by room, with your partner covering.

    2. Collect the three key valuables

    Valuable Where
    Luxury Watch (a collectible) South-east room
    Gutterball Moreland 850 (unique shotgun) On the pool table in the north-east corner
    Safe Small office in the north-east corner; open it with a sticky bomb

    Tip: all three are needed for the trophy. Count them off before you walk out.

    3. Flee

    Leaving the building gives you a two-star wanted level. Take the nearest vehicle and get out of the search area. The trophy appears shortly after you have driven away.

    If you miss something

    The bowling alley has a long cooldown. Both guides say it becomes available again in Chapter 3, a few in-game weeks later, and that you can then pick up whatever you left; the trophy still unlocks.

    Where players fail

    • No sticky bombs. Without them the safe stays shut.
    • Going in alone and unarmored. The building is full of gang members.
    • Leaving after the safe. The watch and the shotgun are in other rooms.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details

    Frequently asked questions

    Where are the three valuables in the GTA 6 Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough?

    The Luxury Watch is in the south-east room, the Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun is on the pool table in the north-east corner, and the safe is in the small office in the north-east corner.

    How do you open the safe in Kingpin Bowling?

    Blow it open with a sticky bomb. Buy them at Ammu-Nation before you start.

    Is the Let's Go Bowling trophy missable?

    No. PowerPyx and GTABase say the bowling alley respawns in Chapter 3 after a few in-game weeks, so you can return for anything you missed.

    What wanted level do you get after Kingpin Bowling?

    Two stars when you leave the building.
