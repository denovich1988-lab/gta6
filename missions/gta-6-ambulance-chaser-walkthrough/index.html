    GTA 6 Ambulance Chaser Walkthrough: Burn the Evidence

    GTA 6 Ambulance Chaser walkthrough: find the barn east of the Starlet Motel, chase the stolen ambulance on the quad bike and burn it with the gas can.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Ambulance Chaser is an early Chapter 2 mission for Lucia: destroy the ambulance used in the hospital escape before the police find it.
    • Locals drive off in it; chase them on the quad bike from the barn until the ambulance hits a tree.
    • Pour the red gas can over it and ignite it, then leave: the explosion alerts the police.

    This GTA 6 Ambulance Chaser walkthrough covers Lucia's first job in Chapter 2: the ambulance from the hospital escape is hidden near the motel, somebody else has found it, and it has to burn. PowerPyx lists it as "Ambulance Chaser (Move the Ambulance)". It is a short chase with one decision at the end.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Ambulance Chaser (Chapter 2; VGC counts it as the second mission of the chapter)
    Also listed as Move the Ambulance
    Playable character Lucia
    Starts at Outside the Starlet Motel, Kelly County
    Requirement Finish What's in the Bag? and leave the motel room
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    1. Find the ambulance.
    2. Stop the ambulance.
    3. Destroy the ambulance.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go to the barn

    The ambulance is in a barn a short way east of the motel. Go round the back and up the stairs to the platform; VGC notes there is also a side door. Inside, a group of locals has found the ambulance, and they drive off in it.

    2. Take the quad bike

    A quad bike is parked inside the barn. Drop down, get on and follow.

    Tip: PowerPyx's advice is simply to stay close and not crash. VGC adds that you can shoot while riding and that aiming for the ambulance's tyres can end the chase sooner.

    3. The crash

    If you only follow, the ambulance eventually leaves the road and hits a tree further south.

    4. Deal with the thieves

    VGC describes a choice once the ambulance has stopped: kill and rob them, or let them run. Neither guide reports a consequence. Either way the ambulance still has to be destroyed.

    5. Burn it

    Step PS5 Xbox
    Pick up the red gas can behind the ambulance - -
    Pour fuel over the ambulance Hold R1 Hold RB
    Look at the fuel and ignite it Triangle Y

    The alternative from PowerPyx and GTABase: throw the can into the back and shoot it.

    6. Leave

    The explosion draws the police. Get out of the area straight away. If you pick up stars, see how the wanted level works.

    Where players fail

    • Crashing the quad. Falling too far behind is the main risk in the chase.
    • Standing next to the fire. Light it and back off.
    • Waiting around afterwards. The police are already on the way.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the ambulance in the GTA 6 Ambulance Chaser walkthrough?

    In a barn just east of the Starlet Motel. Take the stairs at the back of the barn; the mission marker appears the first time you leave the motel room.

    How do you destroy the ambulance in Ambulance Chaser?

    Pick up the red gas can behind the crashed ambulance, hold R1 (RB on Xbox) to pour fuel over it, then use the Ignite prompt with Triangle (Y). You can also throw the can in the back and shoot it.

    Can you shoot the ambulance during the chase?

    VGC says yes: you can fire from the quad bike and aim for the tyres to end the chase early. If you do not, the thieves crash into a tree on their own.
