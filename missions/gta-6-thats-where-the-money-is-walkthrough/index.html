    GTA 6 That's Where the Money Is Walkthrough: $80,000 Bank Vault

    GTA 6 That's Where the Money Is walkthrough: wait at Café Orquídea, tail the security van, blow the vault door, carry every bag and lose a 4-star wanted level.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • That's Where the Money Is… is an optional Chapter 4 job from Yasmani that opens after the VCIA Takedown and starts at Café Orquídea in Ocean Beach.
    • Taking every marked bill is worth about $80,000 once you sell the haul back to Yasmani.
    • Leaving the vault triggers an instant 4-star wanted level, and the bank has no back door.
    On this page 5

    This GTA 6 That's Where the Money Is walkthrough covers Yasmani's optional bank job, the first time the game lets you rob a vault on your own. The robbery itself is quick. The hard part is the drive away, because the police response starts at four stars and they open fire at once.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission That's Where the Money Is… (Chapter 4, optional)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia; either can start it
    Given by Yasmani
    Starts at Café Orquídea, Ocean Beach, Vice Beach
    Requirement VCIA Takedown
    Reward About $80,000; Security Van tips from Yasmani

    Tip: bring a fast car and park it where you can reach it quickly. You will need it twice: once to follow the van and once to get away.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists four on-screen steps; GTABase adds the café, the bills and the sale as stages of their own.

    1. Tail the security van.
    2. Tail the guard.
    3. Rob the vault.
    4. Lose the cops.

    Step-by-step

    1. Wait at the café

    Go into Café Orquídea and sit at the window seats. The mission begins properly when the security van drives past.

    2. Tail the security van

    Get to your vehicle and follow the van. Keep your distance so the driver does not notice you.

    3. Follow the guard into the bank

    The van stops outside a bank. Park close by and follow the guard in on foot.

    4. Rob the vault

    1. Put on a disguise and draw your gun.
    2. Go to the back of the room and plant explosives on the doorway there.
    3. Deal with the guards inside the vault.

    5. Take every marked bill

    The money is a pile on a cart in the corner of the vault.

    Problem Fix
    Your bag is full Hand the bag to your partner
    You want to carry more Switch control to your partner and keep loading

    Tip: do not leave any behind. Both guides put the full haul at about $80,000, and that figure assumes you took all of it.

    6. Lose the cops

    You get a 4-star wanted level automatically, and there is no back exit: you have to fight your way out of the front.

    Stage What both guides suggest
    Leaving Let your partner drive while you shoot at the cars chasing you
    On the road The highway helps if your car is fast, but expect frequent roadblocks
    Breaking away Get out of sight and pull into a parking lot off the main road
    Staying hidden Swap vehicles, take off the disguise and put on a hat and glasses from your inventory

    Our wanted level guide explains how stars and heat work.

    7. Sell the haul to Yasmani

    Once you are clear, go back to Yasmani and sell the marked bills. From now on he also sells Security Van tips.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a formal fail condition. The danger points are these:

    • Tailing too close. The driver can notice you.
    • The front door. With no back exit, you walk out into a 4-star response.
    • Roadblocks on the highway. Speed alone does not lose the police; breaking line of sight and changing cars does.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash About $80,000 for all marked bills
    Unlock Security Van tips from Yasmani
    Trophy Classic Blitz Play, if you have not earned it already (PowerPyx)
    100% GTABase lists it as optional but required for 100%

    This job opens during Tie Up Loose Ends, and it is worth finishing before Sunset ends free roam for a while. Yasmani's earlier work is in Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs. For other ways to earn, see how to make money in GTA 6; the full order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much money do you get in the GTA 6 That's Where the Money Is walkthrough?

    About $80,000 if you take all the marked bills from the cart in the vault and sell them to Yasmani. Both guides give the same figure.

    Where do you start That's Where the Money Is in GTA 6?

    At Café Orquídea in Ocean Beach. Go inside, sit at the seats by the window and wait for the security van to drive past.

    Is That's Where the Money Is missable?

    Neither guide calls it missable, but it opens just before Sunset, the Chapter 4 point of no return, so it is safest to do it during Tie Up Loose Ends.

    What does That's Where the Money Is unlock?

    Yasmani starts selling Security Van tips after the job. PowerPyx also lists the Classic Blitz Play trophy.
