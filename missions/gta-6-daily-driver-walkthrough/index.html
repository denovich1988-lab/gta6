    GTA 6 Daily Driver Walkthrough: Steal the '82 Crawler

    GTA 6 Daily Driver walkthrough: find the white '82 Canis Crawler by the old Traders' Union building, get past the guards and deliver it to Moses for $1,250.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Daily Driver is Moses's second job in Chapter 2: steal an '82 Canis Crawler, a white jeep, and bring it to his garage.
    • The car is guarded; sneak to it or shoot your way in, then drive off.
    • The reward is $1,250 plus $500 if the Crawler arrives in good condition, and Moses later gifts a garage in Port Gellhorn.

    This GTA 6 Daily Driver walkthrough shows where to find the '82 Crawler and how to collect the full $1,750. Moses wants the jeep taken back from clients who did not pay him. It is a short theft with armed company, and it is the best-paid job available at this point in Chapter 2. PowerPyx lists it as Steal the Crawler for Moses.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Daily Driver (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia, solo
    Given by Moses, by text message
    Starts at Search area near Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Moses
    Reward $1,250, plus $500 for good condition

    Objectives

    1. Search the area for the '82 Crawler.
    2. Deliver the '82 Crawler to Moses.

    Step-by-step

    1. Read the text

    A little while after the Moses mission, he sends a picture of the car. Opening the message marks the search area on your map.

    2. Find the jeep

    The '82 Canis Crawler is a white jeep parked in the middle of the search area, by the old Traders' Union building. VGC describes the place as a scrap yard with a fence around it.

    3. Get to the car

    Several people are standing around it and turn hostile if they see Lucia.

    Approach How Source
    Quiet Climb the fence and sneak to the jeep VGC
    Loud Shoot the guards first VGC, PowerPyx
    Fast Run straight to the car and drive away PowerPyx, GTABase

    Tip: the bonus depends on the car's condition, so a firefight around the jeep works against you. Sneaking in, or sprinting to the door, keeps the bodywork clean.

    4. Drive out

    VGC says to leave through the gate. Moses's garage in Port Gellhorn is close.

    5. Deliver it

    Park the Crawler in the garage to finish the mission. Drive carefully on the way: every dent counts against the $500.

    Where players fail

    • Shooting near the jeep. Stray bullets cost you the condition bonus.
    • Crashing on the short drive back. There is no timer in any of the guides, so take it slowly.
    • Fighting everyone. You only need the car.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment $1,250
    Bonus $500 for good condition
    Port Gellhorn Garage Gifted by Moses in a later text; stores vehicles
    Vice City Real Estate app Unlocks for buying more garages
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    VGC adds that Moses goes on to send locations of special cars to steal. The payout from this one job covers the $900 owed in Behind on Payments. More ways to earn: how to make money in GTA 6. Chapter order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the Crawler in the GTA 6 Daily Driver walkthrough?

    In the middle of the search area by the old Traders' Union building. It is a white jeep; VGC describes the spot as a fenced scrap yard.

    How much does Daily Driver pay in GTA 6?

    $1,250, plus a $500 bonus if the car is delivered in good condition, according to PowerPyx and GTABase.

    How do you unlock Daily Driver?

    Finish the mission Moses. Shortly afterwards he texts Lucia a picture of the car and the search area appears on the map.

    What does Daily Driver unlock?

    Soon after, Moses texts again and gifts the Port Gellhorn Garage, and the Vice City Real Estate app unlocks for buying more garages.
