    GTA 6 Girl's Trip Walkthrough: Find Painkillers for Jason

    GTA 6 Girl's Trip walkthrough: get pills from Vicki in Room 10, drive to Delights, track Dunk to the car wash, handle the Kolts and take Dunk home.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Girl's Trip is the longest early Chapter 2 mission: Lucia and Vicki search Port Gellhorn for a dealer called Dunk.
    • At the car wash, picking Defuse twice ends the stand-off with the Kolts without a fight and earns Professional reputation.
    • Do not destroy Dunk's car in the chase; follow him until he crashes, then drive him back to his trailer.

    This GTA 6 Girl's Trip walkthrough takes Lucia from a motel room to a strip club, a boatyard and a car wash in search of real painkillers for Jason. It introduces Vicki, the dealer Dunk and the Kolts gang, and it is the mission that opens up Port Gellhorn. PowerPyx subtitles it Find Painkillers.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Girl's Trip (Chapter 2); GTABase spells it Girls' Trip
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Vicki
    Starts at Search area at the Starlet Motel, Kelly County
    Requirement Finish Overnight Charges and Just Moved In
    Reward $50 returned by Vicki through BuckMe

    Objectives

    1. Ask around the motel about painkillers.
    2. Get the pills from Vicki in Room 10 and take them to Jason.
    3. Go back to Vicki's room.
    4. Drive Vicki's car to Delights.
    5. Look for Dunk: his trailer, the boatyard, the car wash.
    6. Deal with the Kolts.
    7. Follow Dunk and take him home.

    Step-by-step

    1. Ask Zach

    Zach is peering through the windows between your room and reception. He points you to Vicki in Room 10.

    2. Buy the pills

    Knock on Room 10. Vicki fetches pills from the bathroom and charges $50. VGC notes an optional bong while you wait.

    3. Back to Jason

    In your room Jason recognises the pills as Zombix, an ordinary over-the-counter product. They are useless to him.

    4. Confront Vicki

    Return to Room 10. Lucia gets rough with Vicki, who then offers to find the real thing through someone she knows.

    5. Delights

    Get in Vicki's car and drive west into Port Gellhorn. Park at Delights, the club where she works, and follow her inside. Her boss, Frankie Delight, hands over her pay. Go back to the car.

    6. Trailer, then boatyard

    Dunk is not at his trailer. Vicki suggests the boatyard, where his friends say he went to the car wash to sell.

    7. The car wash

    Inside, Dunk is in trouble with the Kolts. He slips out through the shutter and leaves you with them.

    Choice Result
    Defuse, twice in a row The Kolts leave without a fight; you gain Professional reputation (PowerPyx, GTABase)
    Provoke, or attack A fight

    Tip: VGC's guide describes the fight route only, so the calm ending is easy to miss. Take Defuse both times.

    8. Chase Dunk

    Lift the shutter and follow Dunk's car. You are not meant to stop him: he crashes by himself. PowerPyx and GTABase place the crash at a farm; VGC says he ends up in the far paddock beside the reception building at the gun range.

    9. Take Dunk home

    Vicki talks to the rangers while you put Dunk in the car and drive him to his trailer. He has no painkillers left but says his supplier, "Doctor Bill with the Pill", can get more.

    Where players fail

    • Destroying Dunk's car. VGC lists this as a mission failure. Follow; do not ram or shoot.
    • Picking a fight you do not need. Defuse saves health and ammo.
    • Losing the chase. Stay on the road behind him until the crash scene.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward $50 back from Vicki via BuckMe
    Reputation Professional, for defusing the Kolts
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Afterwards the chapter opens up: Moses, New Look, How Do I Look? and the waiting mission Painkiller Supplier all become available. GTABase suggests doing Moses first so you have your own car. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Girl's Trip walkthrough?

    Finish Overnight Charges and Just Moved In. A search area appears at the Starlet Motel; talk to Zach near reception, then knock on Room 10 to meet Vicki.

    Should you choose Defuse or Provoke with the Kolts?

    PowerPyx and GTABase recommend Defuse twice in a row: the Kolts leave and you earn Professional reputation. Provoking or attacking starts a fight.

    Do you get the $50 back from Vicki?

    Yes. PowerPyx and GTABase both list $50 returned through BuckMe as the mission reward.

    Is it Girl's Trip or Girls' Trip?

    PowerPyx and VGC write Girl's Trip; GTABase writes Girls' Trip. It is the same mission.
