    GTA 6 Clean Up After Donnie Walkthrough: Donnie's Drop

    GTA 6 Clean Up After Donnie walkthrough: find the empty package in Hamlet, clear the Kingfishers' shack, and deliver the cocaine to Donnie's trailer for $600.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read

    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Clean Up After Donnie is the first job after Lucia's release: Jason recovers a lost package from the Kingfishers in the Hamlet swamps.
    • The cocaine is inside a shack downriver, guarded by Kingfishers; more arrive by boat after you take it.
    • PowerPyx lists a $600 reward that arrives later in the mailbox, and the mission opens Donnie's trailer as a place to sell narcotics.

    This GTA 6 Clean Up After Donnie walkthrough covers Jason's first job after Lucia comes home: tracking a lost package into the Hamlet swamps, taking it back from the Kingfishers and dropping it at Donnie's trailer. PowerPyx also lists it as "Donnie's Drop". It is short, but it is the first real gunfight on the water and it opens an early way to earn money.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Clean Up After Donnie (mission 12 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Donnie's Drop
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Swamps south-west of Hamlet, Vice Dale County, after Donnie's call
    Requirement Finish Coming Home; Donnie calls the next morning
    Reward $600 (PowerPyx), sent later to the mailbox at Jason's house
    Next mission Buck Me

    Bring a full pistol or a long gun. You fight from a boat with very little cover.

    Objectives

    1. Search the area for the package.
    2. Examine the package.
    3. Search the shack for the cocaine.
    4. Deliver the cocaine to Donnie's trailer.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find the package

    Drive to the pink search area on the riverbank. PowerPyx says to ask the fisherman on the dock, who points to the package on the shore under a roof. VGC simply sends you to the water's edge. Either way, the package is there and it is empty.

    2. Examine it

    Inspect the package. The trail leads to a shack downriver that the Kingfishers gang is using.

    3. Take the boat to the shack

    A boat is tied up next to the package; VGC identifies it as a fan boat. Drive it south to the marked shack.

    4. Deal with the Kingfishers

    PowerPyx counts three gang members at the shack. VGC notes you can talk to them first, but they turn hostile soon after and the section cannot be finished without a fight.

    Tip: VGC warns that the fan boat gives you almost no protection. Start shooting early rather than waiting on the water for them to open fire.

    5. Grab the cocaine

    Go through the shack door and pick up the package. VGC says a sniper rifle is inside too, and that more Kingfishers then arrive by boat. Take them out before you leave.

    6. Deliver it to Donnie's trailer

    VGC has Jason phone Donnie, who tells him to leave the package in the old tool box behind his trailer. PowerPyx marks the trailer in Key Lento with a green fence icon. Drive the boat there and put the bag in the container to finish.

    Where players fail

    • Fighting from open water. The boat is poor cover. Close the distance or pick targets off quickly.
    • Leaving right after the pickup. VGC reports a second group arriving by boat once you have the package.
    • Expecting the cash straight away. PowerPyx says the $600 turns up later in the mailbox at Jason's house.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward $600 by mail (PowerPyx)
    Pickup Sniper rifle inside the shack (VGC)
    Unlocks Selling narcotics at Donnie's trailer; VGC calls it an early money-maker and says Donnie later sends more package locations

    For more ways to earn early on, see how to make money in GTA 6. Next, Lucia calls about a bank account: Buck Me. The full order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Clean Up After Donnie walkthrough mission?

    Donnie phones the morning after Coming Home. The mission marker appears in the swamps south-west of Hamlet, in Vice Dale County, and Jason starts it.

    Where is the cocaine in Clean Up After Donnie?

    In a shack a short boat ride downriver from the empty package. Take out the Kingfishers outside, then go through the door.

    What is the reward for Clean Up After Donnie?

    PowerPyx lists $600, delivered later to the mailbox at Jason's house. VGC adds that you can sell narcotics at Donnie's trailer from this point.

    Is Clean Up After Donnie also called Donnie's Drop?

    Yes. PowerPyx lists the mission as Clean Up After Donnie (Donnie's Drop). VGC uses only the first name.
