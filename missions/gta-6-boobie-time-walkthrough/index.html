    GTA 6 Boobie Time Walkthrough: Seashark Chase and Yasmani

    GTA 6 Boobie Time walkthrough: drive Boobie Ike on his pickups, chase the bag thieves down the river on a Seashark and unlock Yasmani as a fence.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Boobie Time is a Jason-only Chapter 3 mission from Boobie Ike that starts at the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown.
    • Thieves take the cash bag and Jason chases them down the river on a Seashark; ride over the bag to pick it up.
    • It unlocks Yasmani, a fence who pays more for jewelry and marked bills than the other drop-offs.

    This GTA 6 Boobie Time walkthrough covers a driving job for Boobie Ike that turns into a river chase, and ends by introducing the best fence for jewelry in the game. The unlock is the reason to do it early. PowerPyx subtitles it Talk to Boobie.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Boobie Time (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Boobie Ike
    Starts at Jack of Hearts, Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Part 1 of Dubious Provenance
    Reward Yasmani unlocked as a goods drop-off

    Objectives

    1. Get in Boobie's car.
    2. Go to the pick-up location.
    3. Go to the club.
    4. Retrieve the cash bag.
    5. Return to Boobie.
    6. Get in your car.
    7. Go to the smoke shop.
    8. Enter the smoke shop.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive Boobie

    Follow Boobie out of the club and take the driver's seat of his car.

    2. Two pickups

    • First stop: a few blocks south. Wait while Boobie talks to the men in the parking lot.
    • Second stop: a short drive south-west. Pull up, press L3 to honk and wait while he talks to the man on the stairs.

    3. The club by the river

    Drive south to the city centre and stop beside the building near the river.

    4. Chase the bag

    After the scene inside, thieves take the cash bag from the car. Jason follows on a Seashark automatically. Chase them down the river; you can shoot as you ride.

    Tip: you do not need to hit them at speed. Both guides say the thieves end up in a dead end and circle while shooting, which makes them much easier targets.

    When they are down, ride over the bag and press Triangle (Y on Xbox) to pick it up.

    5. Back to Boobie

    Ride back up the river, get off the Seashark and return to Boobie's car.

    6. Meet Yasmani

    Drive a short way across the river to the smoke shop and go inside with Boobie. He introduces Yasmani, and the mission ends.

    Where players fail

    • Wasting ammo during the chase. Wait for the dead end.
    • Missing the bag. It floats where the thieves went down; you have to ride over it and press the button.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Unlock Yasmani as a drop-off for goods
    Why it matters He pays more for jewelry and marked bills than the other drop-offs (both guides)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Yasmani gets in touch afterwards about three stores; see Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs. If you are holding jewelry from Chapter 2 jobs such as Hit Avery Pawn, this is the place to sell it. More in how to make money in GTA 6. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Boobie Time walkthrough mission?

    Finish the first part of Dubious Provenance, then go to the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown as Jason.

    How do you get the cash bag back in Boobie Time?

    Follow the thieves down the river on the Seashark. They stop in a dead end and circle, which makes them easier to hit. Then ride over the bag and press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox.

    What does Boobie Time unlock?

    Yasmani as a goods drop-off. PowerPyx and GTABase both say he pays more for jewelry and marked bills than other fences.

    What comes after Boobie Time?

    Yasmani contacts you about three store robberies, covered in our Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs walkthrough.
