    How to Play GTA 6: Beginner's Guide to Your First Hours

    How to play GTA 6 in the first hours: saving, the phone, talking to people with L2/LT, switching between Jason and Lucia, and keeping your cash safe.

    Quick answer

    • Save from the pause menu under Game, then Save Game; there are 15 manual slots.
    • Switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks in Chapter 2: hold Right on the D-pad and press R1/RB.

    To learn how to play GTA 6, start with five basics: the new controls, the phone, saving by hand, the hold L2/LT interaction and keeping your cash safe. These GTA 6 tips come from launch guides that agree with each other. Anything only one guide reports has been left out.

    Key controls at a glance

    The default layout differs from GTA 5. Running is on a click of the left stick, and Cross/A is jump and climb.

    Action PS5 Xbox Series X/S
    Run L3 (click left stick) LS (click left stick)
    Jump or climb Cross A
    Aim, or talk to a person (hold) L2 LT
    Holster weapon L1 LB
    Open the phone D-pad Up D-pad Up
    Quick navigation (hold) D-pad Down D-pad Down
    Switch character (from Chapter 2) Hold D-pad Right + R1 Hold D-pad Right + RB
    Map Touch pad View button

    If you prefer the old layout, Push Square notes that the Control Scheme setting can be changed from Default to Classic.

    How to talk to people

    GTA 6 lets you speak to almost anyone on the street. The game teaches this in the Prologue.

    1. Put your weapon away with L1/LB. With a gun out, L2/LT aims instead.
    2. Walk up to the person and face them.
    3. Hold L2/LT to focus on them.
    4. Pick a response. The usual set is Greet, Provoke, Taunt and Defuse. Some characters offer their own options.

    Greet is the safe choice with strangers. Defuse calms someone who is squaring up to you. Provoke and Taunt can start a fight, and a fight can bring police.

    The phone

    Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone. These are the apps you will use most early on:

    • BuckMe. Your bank. Its ATM Finder points you to the nearest ATM.
    • RydeMe. A ride service that works as fast travel.
    • Reminders. Important messages and contacts waiting for a reply.

    How to save in GTA 6

    Do not rely on the game to save for you.

    1. Open the pause menu.
    2. Go to Game.
    3. Pick a slot and choose Save Game.

    There are 15 manual save slots, and you load from the same menu. PC Gamer suggests keeping one save from the start of each chapter, which is a sensible habit in a long story.

    One warning: an active cheat disables saving until you quit and reload.

    How to switch characters in GTA 6

    You cannot switch at the start. In Chapter 1 you play as Jason in the open world, and Lucia is playable only in certain story missions. Free switching unlocks in Chapter 2.

    Once it is open, hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox.

    A few rules apply:

    • A greyed-out portrait means the other character is busy. Try again a little later.
    • Some missions belong to one character. The mission's map icon shows who it is for.

    Read more about the two leads on our GTA 6 characters page.

    Keep your cash safe

    Cash in your pocket is lost when you are wasted or busted. Early on you have no bank account, so do not carry more than you can afford to lose.

    The account opens during the Chapter 1 story mission "Buck Me". After that, deposit at an ATM before every risky job. For ways to earn, see how to make money in GTA 6.

    Getting around

    • Find the next mission. Open the map and use its index to list story missions, then set a waypoint.
    • Quick waypoints. Hold Down on the D-pad for a short list of nearby places.
    • Fast travel. Book a RydeMe from the phone. It needs the bank account, so it is not available in the very first missions. You can sit through the drive or pay an extra $100 to skip it.
    • Keep a car. Pay 'n' Spray locations let you store vehicles you want to keep and sell the ones you do not.

    Wanted level basics

    GTA 6 has six wanted stars, and police also track what they know about you: your face, your clothes and your vehicle. Getting out of sight is only half the job. Change your car and your outfit as well. The full system is explained in our GTA 6 wanted level guide.

    What to do first

    1. Play the story until the bank account opens, then deposit your cash.
    2. Make a manual save at the end of each session.
    3. Practise the hold L2/LT interaction on people in the street.
    4. Use the map index to learn where shops, ATMs and Pay 'n' Spray locations are.
    5. Push on into Chapter 2 to unlock character switching and the first steady income.

    Frequently asked questions

    How to play GTA 6 as a beginner?

    Follow the story until the bank account and the phone apps open up, save by hand often, deposit your cash at ATMs and learn the hold L2/LT interaction, which is how you talk to people.

    How to save in GTA 6?

    Open the pause menu, go to Game and choose Save Game. There are 15 manual save slots. Saving is disabled while a cheat is active.

    How to switch characters in GTA 6?

    Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. This works only from Chapter 2, and only when the other character is not greyed out.

    Is there fast travel in GTA 6?

    Yes. The RydeMe app on your phone books a ride to a destination, and you can pay an extra $100 to skip the journey.
