    GTA 6 Adventures in Babysitting Walkthrough: The Vault

    GTA 6 Adventures in Babysitting walkthrough: Jason's night guarding Andrés at The Vault, the drugs choice, the club shootout and the helicopter escape.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By

    Quick answer

    • Adventures in Babysitting is a Jason-only Chapter 4 mission from Andrés de León that opens some time after Making Gainz.
    • It starts as errands inside The Vault nightclub and turns into a shootout, a car chase and a helicopter defence.
    • Accepting or declining Andrés' drugs changes little: both guides say Jason has to take the drink he is handed afterwards.

    This GTA 6 Adventures in Babysitting walkthrough covers Jason's night as nightclub security for Andrés de León, which starts with fetching drinks at The Vault and ends with a rocket launcher in a helicopter. It is one of the longer missions in Chapter 4: PowerPyx counts 20 on-screen objectives. The first half cannot really go wrong. The second half is a run of fights where Jason's health is the thing to watch.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Adventures in Babysitting (Chapter 4), on-screen task "Work Security for Andrés"
    Playable character Jason only
    Given by Andrés de León
    Starts at Andrés' house, Starfish Island, Vice Beach
    Requirement Making Gainz, then a call to Jason some time later
    Reward None (PowerPyx)

    Tip: the mission moves from the club to a chase to a rooftop without a break in either guide's description, so it makes sense to arrive with full health. Enemies drop weapons you can pick up, so ammunition is less of a worry.

    Objectives

    The 20 objectives PowerPyx lists fall into four stages.

    Stage On-screen objectives
    Getting there Drive to The Vault; Escort Andrés
    Inside the club Order Andrés a drink from the bartender; Give Andrés his drink; Take a picture of Andrés; Talk to the group; Confirm the order with a bartender; Join Andrés' group; Talk to Donatello's security; Take a drink; Return to Andrés' table; Find Andrés in the bathroom; Talk to the dealer; Return to Andrés
    The attack Protect Andrés; Follow Paulo; Move Andrés; Get to the helipad
    The escape Put Andrés in the helicopter; Defend Andrés

    Step-by-step

    1. Pick up Andrés and drive to The Vault

    Go to the house on Starfish Island and follow Paulo around to the back to meet Andrés. He wants a night out, so drive to The Vault nightclub.

    2. Get him through the door and fetch a drink

    Follow Andrés in. A drunk man stops him at the entrance: step in so Andrés keeps moving. On the first dance floor he asks for a drink. Order it from the bartender on the left. He wants it without lime, but both guides say getting the order wrong does not matter. Bring it to him at the corner by the stairs, take the photo of him and his friends when he hands over his phone, and follow him upstairs.

    3. Run the booth upstairs

    1. Talk to the group of girls on the right and invite them to Andrés' booth.
    2. Confirm their order with the bartender nearby.
    3. Sit in the free seat on the couch.
    4. When asked, talk to Donatello's security at the other booth, then go back to Andrés.

    4. The drugs and the drink

    Andrés offers Jason drugs.

    Choice Result
    Accept No lasting difference (GTABase)
    Decline GTABase says Andrés spikes Jason's drink later anyway

    Whatever you pick, the next objective is "Take a drink", and both guides say you cannot refuse it.

    5. Find Andrés

    Follow Andrés to the DJ booth, the office and back. Push your way out of the DJ booth and look for him at the table. His friends point you to the bathroom on the far side of the floor. He is in the last stall.

    6. Collect his delivery

    Andrés sends Jason outside for his drugs. Take the main stairs down, leave by the door at the bottom, and walk to the car that pulls up at the end of the alley on the right. Then hurry back to Andrés at the bar in the south-east corner.

    7. Protect Andrés in the club

    Gunmen from FAP storm the club. PowerPyx describes it as an attempt to kidnap Andrés. Jason's vision is heavily distorted by what he was given, and it stays that way until you are out of the building.

    • Fight across the floor with Andrés and pick up weapons from fallen enemies.
    • At the bottom of the stairs, watch for a car full of gunmen, then go through the door on the right.
    • An attacker tackles Jason: mash the prompt to stop him being stabbed.

    Tip: civilians run through the firefight. Both guides advise not hitting them if you want to keep a good Criminal Profile rating.

    8. The car escape

    Outside, jump into the car with Andrés' security. Jason fires an LMG through the roof: destroy the vehicles chasing you. Both guides warn that he takes heavy damage up there, so keep an eye on his health.

    9. Reach the helipad

    Leave the car and follow Paulo up the escalator and through the building. At the train tracks, interact with Andrés to help him across, and keep clearing enemies. Take the elevator, follow Paulo onto the roof, clear the helipad and push Andrés into the helicopter.

    10. Defend the helicopter

    Weapon How to use it
    Assault rifle (unlimited ammo here) Aim for the enemy helicopters' tail rotors; switch sides of the cabin when prompted
    Rocket launcher Wait for the marker on a helicopter to turn red, then fire

    Clearing the helicopters completes the mission. Back at the house, Andrés learns that his son Rafa has been taken.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. The objectives are about keeping Andrés safe, and the danger points both guides flag are these.

    • The LMG section. Jason is exposed through the roof and takes damage quickly, so watch his health bar as well as the cars.
    • The tackle. Mash the prompt as soon as it appears.
    • The distorted vision. It does not clear until you leave the club, so check your targets before you fire.
    • The helicopters. Shooting pilots or gunners is slower than hitting tail rotors.

    The club errands, including the wrong drink order, have no penalty.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None (PowerPyx)
    Optional goals None listed by either guide
    100% GTABase marks the mission as mandatory and required

    What comes next

    PowerPyx follows this mission with a short separate entry, Rafa's Kidnapping, and then Fresh Evidence; GTABase has no separate page for the kidnapping entry. The two also place the mission differently: PowerPyx puts another short entry, Nate Will Be in Touch, between Dirty Work and this one, while GTABase lists them back to back. Since this mission only needs Making Gainz, it can appear on Jason's map earlier than its place in either list.

    For the hardware used at the end, see GTA 6 guns and planes and helicopters. The full story order is in our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Should you take the drugs in the GTA 6 Adventures in Babysitting walkthrough?

    It makes no lasting difference, according to GTABase. You can accept or decline, but Jason must take the drink Andrés hands him next, and his vision is distorted for the club fight either way.

    How do you start Adventures in Babysitting in GTA 6?

    Finish Making Gainz and wait for the call to Jason. Then go to Andrés' house on Starfish Island with Jason and follow Paulo around to the back.

    How do you shoot down the helicopters at the end?

    Both guides say to aim the unlimited-ammo assault rifle at the tail rotors, which is quicker than hitting pilots or gunners. With the rocket launcher, wait for the marker on a helicopter to turn red before firing.

    Is there a reward for Adventures in Babysitting?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. GTABase marks the mission as mandatory and required for 100% completion.
