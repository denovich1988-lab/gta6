    GTA 6 Sunset Walkthrough: Chapter 4 Point of No Return

    GTA 6 Sunset walkthrough: what to finish before the Chapter 4 point of no return, where to find Raul on the houseboat and how to survive the marina escape.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Sunset starts when you collect the note at the Y Hotel front desk; both guides call it the Chapter 4 point of no return.
    • Raul is in the small bathroom behind the second door on the left inside his houseboat at the marina.
    • The escape is a dock shootout and a boat chase with limited ammo; shoot down the helicopter early.

    This GTA 6 Sunset walkthrough covers the mission that ends free roam in Chapter 4: a visit to Raul's houseboat that turns into a running fight along the docks and out to sea. Read the checklist below before you pick up the note at the Y Hotel. Once you do, there is no going back to the open world for a long time.

    Read this first: the point of no return

    GTABase labels Sunset the Chapter 4 point of no return, and PowerPyx says the same: starting it carries the story into Chapter 5, and most of Chapter 5 has to be completed before the open world opens up again. The next mission, Starting From Zero, begins the moment this one ends.

    Before you collect the note:

    1. Finish the optional bank job That's Where the Money Is….
    2. Clear any side missions and activities you care about.
    3. Make a manual save in its own slot: how to save in GTA 6. Missions cannot be replayed.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Sunset (Chapter 4)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Starts at Front desk of the Y Hotel, Washington Beach, Vice Beach
    Requirement VCIA Takedown, then a wait: see Tie Up Loose Ends
    Reward None listed

    GTABase counts Valentina's phone call and the call back as the first steps of Sunset; PowerPyx treats them as separate entries and starts the mission at the note. The route is the same either way. GTABase adds that you can change clothes before you leave the hotel.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists six on-screen steps.

    1. Go to the marina.
    2. Go to Raul's houseboat.
    3. Look for Raul.
    4. Escape with your partner.
    5. Get in the boat.
    6. Defend the boat.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive to the marina

    Take a vehicle to the marina on the south side of Vice Beach. The note names it as the place where Raul's boat is moored.

    2. Board the houseboat

    Walk onto the dock. The game moves you closer to the houseboat, and you go inside. It has been turned over.

    3. Find Raul

    Step Where
    1 Second door on the left
    2 The small bathroom beyond it

    A scene follows.

    4. Fight along the docks

    Police surround the boat, and you come out into a high wanted level with officers on several sides.

    • Move from cover to cover toward the far end of the dock.
    • Switch between Jason and Lucia so each can cover the other.
    • Shoot down the helicopter early. Both guides say it makes the rest of the dock much easier.

    Tip: GTABase's warning is not to stand in the open. You are being flanked from the start.

    5. Get in the boat

    At the end of the dock, board the boat quickly.

    6. Defend the boat

    One of you drives while the other shoots. More police arrive on boats and in helicopters once you leave the marina.

    What to know Detail
    Ammo Limited
    When the assault rifle runs dry The game swaps you to the other character while your partner takes the wheel
    Resupply GTABase says there is a gun locker on board

    Get clear of the area to finish. GTABase describes one last beat, in which the pair set the autopilot, slip between barges and jump off so the police follow an empty boat; PowerPyx ends the mission once you are clear.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a formal fail condition. The danger points are these:

    • The docks. Standing still in the open under a high wanted level.
    • The helicopter. Leaving it in the air while you deal with the officers on foot.
    • The boat. Spending rifle ammo too freely before the second wave arrives.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Sunset the point of no return in the GTA 6 Sunset walkthrough?

    Yes. PowerPyx and GTABase both say starting Sunset moves the story into Chapter 5. PowerPyx adds that most Chapter 5 missions must be finished before the open world is available again.

    Where is Raul in the Sunset mission?

    On his houseboat at the marina on the south side of Vice Beach. Go through the second door on the left, then into the small bathroom.

    How do you start Sunset in GTA 6?

    After the VCIA Takedown, wait for time to pass or call Valentina back to speed it up, then pick up the note left at the Y Hotel front desk.

    What is the reward for Sunset?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. GTABase marks the mission as required for 100%.
