    GTA 6 Fully Catered Walkthrough: Steal the Catering Truck Unseen

    GTA 6 Fully Catered walkthrough: the rooftop route to the Plane & Simple catering trucks at the airport, which truck to take and where to deliver it.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 26, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Fully Catered is a Chapter 4 mission from Raul that either lead can start at the airport after Power Play.
    • The quiet route goes over the roof of the building beside the trucks; drop down on the left side of the middle truck and open it with the Slim Jim.
    • Deliver the truck to Rockridge, then bag fake cash in the planning room. There is no cash reward.

    This GTA 6 Fully Catered walkthrough covers Raul's request for an airport catering truck, the last piece of equipment the crew needs for the airport job. The truck yard is off-limits, and any worker who sees you will report you for trespassing. There is a route over the roof that avoids all of them.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Fully Catered (Chapter 4, VCIA Takedown setup)
    Playable character Jason or Lucia
    Given by Raul
    Starts at The marked spot at the airport (VCIA)
    Requirement Power Play
    You need The Slim Jim
    Reward None listed

    When it appears: both guides say the job shows up a little after Close Surveillance and Delivery Method, or straight after them if you do those first.

    Objectives

    1. Steal a Plane & Simple catering truck.
    2. Deliver the catering truck to the drop-off.
    3. Pick up the cash and put it in the bag.

    Step-by-step

    1. Reach the trucks over the roof

    1. Go to the marked area at the airport, but do not walk in through the yard.
    2. Circle around the right side of the building where the trucks are parked, staying out of sight.
    3. Climb the lower section of the building to get onto the roof.
    4. Walk across the roof to the row of catering trucks.
    5. Drop down on the left side of the middle truck. Both guides say workers are not normally standing there.

    2. Break in

    Step Detail
    Tool Slim Jim
    Target The middle truck
    Pace Do it quickly, before a worker walks round

    3. Deliver the truck

    Drive out of the airport and take the truck to the marked drop-off in Rockridge.

    4. Bag the fake cash

    The mission ends back in the planning room. While Raul talks, pick up bundles of fake cash from the pile and interact with the bags to pack them. Repeat a few times and the mission completes.

    Why it matters: GTABase notes that this fake cash is what the crew swaps for the real money during the VCIA Takedown.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide gives a hard fail condition. The risk is the trespassing report:

    • Walking through the yard. Workers there will spot you.
    • Dropping on the wrong side. The left side of the middle truck is the blind spot.
    • Fumbling the lock. The longer you stand at the door, the more likely someone comes around.

    What comes next: Heist Plans in Place

    PowerPyx lists a short entry after this mission that GTABase does not count.

    Stage What you see
    Before all setup is done "Heist Planning" on the map, with no marker of its own
    After the setup missions It becomes Heist Plans in Place: wait for a call
    The trigger Finish Dre'Quan's missions through Headliners, then play as Lucia until Nate phones the next morning

    Raul sends a message the morning after Fully Catered. If the call from Nate does not come, the usual reason is that Headliners is still open.

    With the truck delivered, the setup list for the airport job is nearly complete: see Team Building if Lucia has not done it yet. The full order is in the GTA 6 mission list, and the basics are in how to play GTA 6.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you steal the truck unseen in the GTA 6 Fully Catered walkthrough?

    Circle around the right side of the building next to the trucks, climb the lower section onto the roof, cross it, and drop down on the left side of the middle truck, where workers are usually absent.

    Where do you deliver the catering truck in Fully Catered?

    To the marked drop-off in Rockridge.

    What is Heist Plans in Place in GTA 6?

    A waiting entry that PowerPyx lists after Fully Catered. It has one objective, to wait for a call, and it turns into the VCIA Takedown once Headliners is done and Nate phones Lucia.

    Who can play Fully Catered?

    Either Jason or Lucia can start it. Both guides list both leads.
