    GTA 6 Company Property Walkthrough: Save Every Crate

    GTA 6 Company Property walkthrough: meet Brian at Skeeter's, load the crates, escape the airfield with Pete and protect the cargo for the Cartel Heist.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Company Property is a Cartel Heist prep mission in Chapter 3, given by Brian Heder at Skeeter's bar in the south of Hamlet.
    • The cargo Lucia saves in the truck chase decides how many explosive charges you get in Hidden Charges.
    • Keeping every cargo box earns a Professional bonus; the mission ends with Jason crash-landing Pete's plane.

    This GTA 6 Company Property walkthrough covers the weapons pickup that supplies the Cartel Heist with explosives. Brian calls in a favour from an old smuggling contact, Pilot Pete, and the handover at a country airfield is raided within minutes. Jason leaves by plane, Lucia by truck, and how much cargo she keeps matters two missions later. PowerPyx subtitles the mission Meet Brian.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Company Property (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia
    Given by Brian Heder
    Starts at Skeeter's bar, south side of Hamlet
    Requirement Security Expert
    Reward No cash; the cargo you save sets your explosive charges in Hidden Charges

    Bring body armor and a full rifle if you have them. The first half is a long fight on foot.

    Objectives

    1. Follow Brian.
    2. Drive to the airfield.
    3. Place the weapons crate in the truck.
    4. Pick up a weapons crate.
    5. Escape with Pete.
    6. Escape the area.
    7. Protect the cargo.
    8. Return to the planning room.
    9. Land the plane.

    These are the nine lines PowerPyx lists. GTABase groups the same mission into six stages.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Brian at Skeeter's

    Go to Skeeter's in the south of Hamlet and talk to Brian at the bar. Raul has asked him to return a favour: a weapons delivery flown in by Pilot Pete. Follow Brian out and get in his truck.

    2. Load the crates

    Drive to the airfield south of Hamlet and wait for Pete's plane. Pete hands over a crate; carry it to the back of Brian's truck. Then walk back, pick up a second crate from the ground and load that one too.

    3. Fight off the raid (Jason)

    IAA agents and police arrive. Lucia leaves with Brian in the truck and you stay as Jason.

    1. Follow Pete through the garage door on the right into the hangar.
    2. Opposite the door there is an assault rifle and health items. Take both.
    3. Deal with the agents who come in, then follow Pete out of the back door.
    4. Head south down the road and climb onto the roof Pete uses. Shoot the cars coming down the road from up there.

    4. Take off

    Follow Pete through the trees and across the runway to the second hangar. Watch the far side of the runway for more cars. Jason jumps into the plane automatically. Taxi out of the hangar, turn left onto the runway, hold full speed and pull up as early as you can so the cars cannot block you.

    5. Protect the cargo (Lucia)

    The game switches to Lucia in the back of Brian's truck with an LMG and unlimited ammo. Shoot pursuing vehicles before they ram the truck: every hit can knock a cargo box off.

    Tip: reload only when nothing is directly behind you. Both guides say that keeping all the cargo earns a Professional bonus, and each box lost costs one explosive charge in Hidden Charges.

    When Lucia gets into the car, follow the road objective until the game switches back to Jason.

    6. Crash-land the plane (Jason)

    The plane is too damaged to keep flying. Bring it down on the colourful field to the right of the river. Landing completes the mission.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. What costs you:

    • Skipping the rifle in the hangar. The fight to the second hangar is long.
    • Reloading the LMG at the wrong moment. A vehicle that reaches the truck knocks cargo off.
    • A slow take-off. Cars drive onto the runway; lift off early.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None
    Explosive charges Up to three in Hidden Charges, one fewer per cargo box lost
    Professional bonus Keep every cargo box
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Company Property opens Safe Word and, for Lucia, The Missing Link. For how the setup missions fit together, see GTA 6 heists. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where does the GTA 6 Company Property walkthrough start?

    At Skeeter's, a bar on the south side of Hamlet. Talk to Brian at the bar after finishing Security Expert, follow him outside and get in his truck.

    What happens if you lose cargo in Company Property?

    Each box knocked off Brian's truck means one fewer explosive charge in Hidden Charges, and fewer charges mean less time in The Cartel Heist.

    Where do you land the plane in Company Property?

    On the colourful field to the right of the river. The plane is too damaged to keep flying, so both guides say to bring it down there.

    Is Company Property required for 100% in GTA 6?

    Yes, according to GTABase, which lists it as a mandatory mission.
