    GTA 6 Dubious Provenance Walkthrough: The Halifax Choice

    GTA 6 Dubious Provenance walkthrough: give Halifax the statue or not, break into the antique store, trust him at the motel, win the chase and loot $2,500.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Dubious Provenance is a three-part Chapter 3 mission that starts at the bag in the Bayside apartment closet and moves to Capri.
    • Giving Halifax the statue earns a Crowex watch and opens parts 2 and 3; refuse and never call him, and those parts are lost.
    • Part 3 ends at a storage container with about $2,500 in jewelry and cash and a '32 Hustler to keep or sell.

    This GTA 6 Dubious Provenance walkthrough covers all three parts of the statue mission and the three choices in it. Jason and Lucia take the statue to an antique store in Capri to learn what it is. Only the first part is needed for the story, but the later parts hold the money and a long chase. PowerPyx subtitles it Visit the Antique Store.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Dubious Provenance (Chapter 3), in three parts
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia; either can start it
    Starts at The bag in the closet of the Bayside apartment
    Then Capri, Mariana County, on the far west of the map
    Requirement Finish Dealer's Choice
    Reward Crowex watch (part 1); about $2,500 and a '32 Hustler (part 3)

    The three choices at a glance

    Choice Option that keeps the mission going The other option
    Give Halifax the statue? Accept: you get his watch and part 2 Decline: call him later, or lose parts 2 and 3 at the end of Chapter 3
    Trust Halifax at the motel? Trust: part 3 opens The mission stops short of part 3
    Shoot or spare Halifax? Free choice Free choice

    Objectives

    • Part 1: go to the antique store; optionally explore Capri together; optionally call Halifax about the statue.
    • Part 2: get into Evelyn Andrews Antiques; get inside through the window; let Jason in; find the statue; go to the motel; search for Halifax in the motel.
    • Part 3: go to Maryam's house; chase Halifax; approach Halifax; call Jason.

    Step-by-step

    Part 1: the antique store

    1. Interact with the bag in the apartment closet.
    2. Drive west to Capri, or take a RydeMe to save time (fast travel guide).
    3. Try the front door of the store. You go upstairs automatically.
    4. The owner, Evelyn Andrews, will not touch the statue. A British dealer, Benedict Halifax, offers to appraise it off the books and leaves his watch as collateral.
    5. Accept or decline. If you accept, the watch can be worn from a wardrobe under Accessories, then Watches.

    An optional objective asks you to explore Capri with your partner. Visiting any shop or leaving town clears it.

    Part 2: the break-in

    Halifax never calls back. Return to the store and wait until Evelyn leaves.

    1. You can force the door, or go in quietly: take the stairs round the back, jump to the lower roof and climb through the window above the air conditioner.
    2. Walk to the door and let Jason in.
    3. Check the drawer of the desk in the north corner. It points to the motel next door.
    4. At the motel, a resident sends you to an upstairs room on the west side. Interact with the door.
    5. After the scene, decide whether to trust Halifax.

    Part 3: the chase

    After another silence, Evelyn calls. Talk to her at the store; she gives an address in the north-west of town.

    1. Halifax runs for a boat. Jason follows on a Seashark. Get close and hold Square (X on Xbox) to board.
    2. He crashes and runs. Swim to shore and keep after him.
    3. He takes a golf cart. Grab the bike in the yard.
    4. He pepper-sprays Jason, and the game switches to Lucia as Halifax runs into her car. Get out, use Focus (L3 + R3) and shoot him in the leg.
    5. Walk up to him, take out Lucia's phone and call Jason.
    6. When Jason arrives you get the statue back. Shoot or spare Halifax.

    Tip: before you leave, empty the storage container. Both guides put it at about $2,500 in jewelry and cash, and the '32 Hustler parked there is yours to keep or sell.

    Where players fail

    • Declining and forgetting. If you refuse Halifax and never phone him, the statue plot closes with the chapter.
    • Choosing not to trust him. That ends the mission before the chase and the loot.
    • Losing Halifax in the chase. Each vehicle swap is scripted; keep moving and take the vehicle the guides name at each stage.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Frequently asked questions

    Should you give Halifax the statue in the GTA 6 Dubious Provenance walkthrough?

    Yes, if you want the whole mission. Accepting gives you his Crowex watch and leads to parts 2 and 3. If you decline you can still call him later, but if you never do, the rest disappears at the end of Chapter 3.

    Do you have to trust Halifax at the motel?

    PowerPyx and GTABase both say trusting him is what lets you play part 3, so choose it if you want the chase and the loot.

    Should you shoot or spare Halifax?

    Either. Both guides call it a free choice and report no consequence.

    What do you get for finishing Dubious Provenance?

    The Crowex watch in part 1, then about $2,500 in jewelry and cash from the storage container in part 3, plus a '32 Hustler parked there.
