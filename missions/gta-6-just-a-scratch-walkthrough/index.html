    GTA 6 Just a Scratch Walkthrough: Fix Jason's Car for Free

    GTA 6 Just a Scratch walkthrough: take Jason's Ganado to Coastal Auto Care, get the free engine repair and respray, and learn how car repairs work.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Just a Scratch is the fifth mission in GTA 6 and one of the shortest: a tutorial for the repair shop.
    • Drive Jason's '70 Ganado to Coastal Auto Care, where the repair is free during this mission.
    • Later repairs cost money, and you can phone the shop for a paid tow.

    This GTA 6 Just a Scratch walkthrough covers the fifth mission, a two-minute stop at the repair shop that teaches you how fixing and respraying a car works. PowerPyx also lists it as "Fix Your Car". The repair costs nothing this once, so take it.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Just a Scratch (mission 5, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Fix Your Car
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, driving Jason's own car
    Requirement Finish Gone Fishing
    Reward Free engine repair and respray
    Next mission Unwanted Attention

    You need Jason's personal car, a '70 Ganado, which is kept at his house. If you wrecked it, PowerPyx's fix is to save manually (Pause Menu > Game > Save/Load Game) and load that save: you return at the house with the car outside.

    Objectives

    1. Talk to Joey.
    2. Restore the engine and get a respray.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive the Ganado to Coastal Auto Care

    Follow the marker to the shop. Joey is standing outside the garage. Talk to him (PowerPyx gives the prompt as hold Up and press X on PS5, A on Xbox), then drive into the garage.

    2. Choose Full Engine

    In the shop menu, pick Full Engine. It is free during this mission.

    3. Respray the car

    Open Cosmetics > Respray and choose any colour. The colour has no effect on the mission.

    4. Leave the shop

    Drive out. VGC notes that a short cutscene plays, and from then on this is where you repair and change cars.

    Where players fail

    There is no real way to fail. The only snags reported are:

    • Arriving in the wrong car. The mission is about Jason's Ganado, not a stolen vehicle.
    • Spending money on upgrades. VGC says you probably cannot afford mods yet anyway. PowerPyx warns that the Ganado is replaced later and upgrades do not carry over.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward Free engine repair and respray
    Optional goals None listed in either guide
    Unlocks Coastal Auto Care for repairs, resprays and mods

    What the mission teaches

    • Repairs cost money after this. The free fix is a one-off.
    • You can call for a tow. VGC says you can phone Coastal Auto Care from anywhere. It costs money but is quicker than driving a damaged car back.
    • A respray is more than cosmetic. Police remember your vehicle, so a new colour helps after a chase. The next mission, Unwanted Attention, makes you do exactly that, and our wanted level guide explains the system.
    • Personal vehicles come later. PowerPyx reports that the Ganado is your personal car for the rest of Chapter 1. You can drive stolen cars, but registering a new personal vehicle opens up in Chapter 2.

    For the cars you can expect to find, see GTA 6 cars. The full story order is in the GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where does the GTA 6 Just a Scratch walkthrough start?

    At Jason's house in Key Lento. Get into his own car, a '70 Ganado, and drive to the Coastal Auto Care marker.

    Is the repair free in Just a Scratch?

    Yes. Both PowerPyx and VGC say the engine repair is free during this mission. PowerPyx lists the respray as free too. Later visits cost money.

    What if Jason's car is destroyed before Just a Scratch?

    PowerPyx's fix is to make a manual save from the pause menu and load it. You come back at the house with the car parked outside.

    Can you upgrade Jason's car in Just a Scratch?

    The mod options are there, but you probably cannot afford them yet. PowerPyx also warns that this car is replaced later in the story and its upgrades are lost.
