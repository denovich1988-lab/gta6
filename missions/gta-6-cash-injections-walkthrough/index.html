    GTA 6 Cash Injections Walkthrough: Nate's Synthol Deal

    GTA 6 Cash Injections walkthrough: call Nate, survive the Synthol deal in the alley, chase his stolen car to the motel, then lose the cops with Lucia.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Cash Injections is a Lucia-only Chapter 4 mission from Nate that starts at the Muscle Beach gym on Ocean Beach.
    • A Synthol deal in a back alley goes wrong: Lucia protects Nate, chases his stolen car to a motel and clears the gunmen there.
    • The thief cannot be stopped by shooting during the chase, and the mission ends at an ice cream shop in northern Vice Beach.

    This GTA 6 Cash Injections walkthrough covers the mission where Lucia backs Nate up on a Synthol sale that turns into a shootout, a car chase and a motel gunfight. After the easy errand of Amateur Hour, this is a proper combat mission, so stock up on ammo before you call Nate.

    Everything below is described the same way by PowerPyx and GTABase unless we name one of them.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Cash Injections (Chapter 4); PowerPyx subtitles it Hang Out With Nate
    Playable character Lucia only
    Given by Nate
    Starts at The Muscle Beach gym, Ocean Beach, Vice City
    Requirement Amateur Hour, then a phone call with Nate
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)

    How it unlocks: after Amateur Hour, Nate texts Lucia. Call him yourself, or wait about a day and he calls her. PowerPyx counts the call as a separate entry named Call Nate, with no reward and no map marker of its own. Once the call is done, the meeting at the gym is marked on the map.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists ten on-screen steps.

    1. Follow Nate.
    2. Get in Nate's car.
    3. Reach the deal location.
    4. Open Nate's trunk.
    5. Protect Nate.
    6. Get in the car.
    7. Chase down Nate's car.
    8. Retrieve Nate's car.
    9. Lose the cops, or leave the area.
    10. Go to the ice cream parlor.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Nate and drive to the deal

    Meet Nate at the Muscle Beach gym and follow him off the beach to his car. Drive a few blocks north to a back alley and wait for the deal to begin.

    2. Open the trunk

    When Nate asks, open the trunk and take out the bag. This is where the deal falls apart: the buyers turn on the two of you.

    3. Knock the gun away and protect Nate

    A prompt appears to knock the gun out of the hand of the man standing next to Lucia. Press it as soon as you see it. PowerPyx gives the button as Square on PlayStation and X on Xbox.

    One of the men then jumps into Nate's car and drives away with it. Ignore him for now. Nate is still in the alley with the rest of them, and the objective is to protect him, so deal with the enemies around him first.

    4. Chase Nate's car

    When the alley is clear, get into the car that was left behind and go after the thief.

    Tip: do not waste time shooting. Both guides say the driver cannot be stopped that way in this section. All the game asks for is that you stay on his tail until he stops by himself.

    5. Clear the motel

    The thief pulls into an alley by a motel, and more gunmen get out to meet you. Nate's car is in the parking area, and you need to clear the enemies to reach it.

    Where they come from What to do
    The alley and parking area Take cover as you arrive and work through them first
    The motel's upper floor Check above you before moving up
    Doors along the sides Expect new enemies to step out as you advance

    Both guides single out the upper floor and the side doors, which is a fair sign that this is where players get hit from. For the guns worth bringing, see GTA 6 weapons.

    6. Get away

    Get into Nate's car and leave the area at once. If the police spot you, you have to lose them before the mission moves on. Our guide to the GTA 6 wanted level explains how to drop the stars.

    7. Ice cream

    With the heat gone, Nate offers to buy Lucia an ice cream. Drive to the shop in the northern part of Vice Beach to finish the mission. GTABase calls it an ice cream shop, and PowerPyx's objective calls it an ice cream parlor.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide spells out fail conditions, so we will not invent any. From what they do describe, these are the points to watch:

    • Missing the disarm prompt. Both guides tell you to press it when it appears.
    • Chasing the car too early. The objective after the trunk is Protect Nate, and the car only matters once the alley is clear.
    • Shooting during the chase. It does nothing, and it takes your attention off the road.
    • Rushing the motel. Enemies on the upper floor and at the side doors appear after the first group.
    • Lingering afterwards. Leave straight away. A wanted level has to be lost before you can finish.

    GTA 6 has no mission replay menu. If you want to be able to try this again, make a manual save before meeting Nate: see how to save in GTA 6.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details

    Neither guide lists optional objectives.

    What comes next

    PowerPyx lists Dirty Work as the next mission (PowerPyx subtitles it Deal with Raina). Lucia's undercover work at Ajax Gym began in Making Gainz, and it all leads to the airport job described in GTA 6 heists. For the whole story in order, see our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you get Nate's car back in the GTA 6 Cash Injections walkthrough?

    Take the car left in the alley and follow the thief without trying to shoot him. He stops in a motel alley. Clear the gunmen, including those on the upper floor and at the side doors, then get into Nate's car in the parking area.

    How do you start Cash Injections in GTA 6?

    Finish Amateur Hour and wait for Nate's text. Call him as Lucia, or wait about a day for him to call, then meet him at the Muscle Beach gym on Ocean Beach.

    Can you shoot the driver during the chase in Cash Injections?

    It does not help. PowerPyx and GTABase both say the thief cannot be stopped by shooting in this section, so concentrate on keeping up.

    Is there a reward for Cash Injections?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. The point of the mission is Nate's trust: he says he will put in a good word for Lucia with Kordell.
