    GTA 6 Child's Play Walkthrough: Rescue Rafa and Chase Gustavo

    GTA 6 Child's Play walkthrough: find Rafa at the quarry with Lucia, hold off the kidnappers as Jason, then chase Gustavo by car and bike. Reward: $30,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 25, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By

    Quick answer

    • Child's Play is a Chapter 4 mission from Andrés that starts at Megamundo HQ in Downtown and switches between Lucia and Jason.
    • Lucia sneaks in to free Rafa at a quarry south of VCIA while Jason protects Andrés, then both chase Gustavo.
    • The reward is $30,000, and the timer shown on the map does not make the mission missable.

    This GTA 6 Child's Play walkthrough covers the rescue of Rafa, Andrés's kidnapped son, from a quarry south of the airport. It is one of the longer action missions in Chapter 4: a stealth section with Lucia, a defence with Jason, an escort with a child in your arms, and a chase by car and then by bike.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Child's Play (Chapter 4)
    Playable characters Lucia and Jason, switching during the mission
    Given by Andrés de León
    Starts at Megamundo HQ, Downtown, Vice City
    Requirement Fresh Evidence
    Reward $30,000

    Three things to know first:

    • The timer is not real. Both guides say the map makes the mission look timed, but it is not. The game forces it once every other Jason and Lucia mission before Power Play is finished, and you can start it sooner without changing the story.
    • How it appears. PowerPyx describes a short waiting step after Adventures in Babysitting: once Jason's missions for Dre'Quan are done, a phone call about the kidnapping comes in and Child's Play is marked on the map. PowerPyx says the caller is the lawyer Abe Gonzalez; GTABase says Andrés calls for help.
    • Finish optional jobs. Power Play comes next in the story, and the optional Bad Driver! is no longer available once Power Play starts.

    Tip: bring body armour and a full rifle. Most of the mission is open combat, and part of it is fought from a moving vehicle.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists 14 on-screen steps.

    1. Go to the quarry.
    2. Drive to the deal.
    3. Use binoculars to find Rafa.
    4. Rescue Rafa.
    5. Protect Andrés.
    6. Switch to Lucia.
    7. Escape with Rafa.
    8. Give Rafa to Gustavo.
    9. Chase down Gustavo.
    10. Protect Andrés.
    11. Get in a vehicle.
    12. Rescue Rafa.
    13. Chase down Gustavo.
    14. Deal with Gustavo.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Andrés and drive to the quarry

    Go to Megamundo HQ as either character. After the opening scene, drive to the quarry south of VCIA and continue on to the meeting point. Lucia gets out of the car before the deal.

    2. Find Rafa with the binoculars (Lucia)

    As Lucia, look at the building ahead. Rafa is in the room on the upper level, below a row of red lights. Before you move, sweep the area with the binoculars and tag the guards you can see.

    3. Sneak in and free Rafa

    Lucia has a silenced pistol for this part. Both guides give the same route:

    1. Head right, along the buildings.
    2. Climb the ladder at the metal structures, then take the stairs up.
    3. Follow the walkway to the door on the left.
    4. Deal with the last guard inside the room, then go to Rafa.

    GTABase adds a detail for the walkway: let two guards walk past, then take out the two at the corner.

    4. Protect Andrés (Jason)

    Control moves to Jason at the exchange. Shoot the kidnappers in front of you, stay near the cars and watch the right side, where more enemies come out of a building. Switch to Lucia when the game asks you to.

    5. Escape with Rafa (Lucia)

    Lucia now carries Rafa, and both guides warn that her aim is worse while she does.

    1. Take him to the ladder and let him climb down on his own. Cover him from above.
    2. Pick him up again on the lower platform and carry him down.
    3. At ground level Lucia is grabbed. Mash the prompt to break free, then shoot the man holding Rafa.
    4. Rafa gets on a forklift and drives it forward. Follow him and keep the path clear.
    5. When the car arrives, pick Rafa up and carry him to Gustavo at the door.

    6. Chase Gustavo by car

    Gustavo drives off with Rafa instead of bringing him back. Get in a car and go after him. Jason drives, so switch to Lucia to shoot. The chase ends when your car flips. Climb out and protect Andrés again. How to switch characters in GTA 6 has the controls if you have not used a mid-mission switch for a while.

    7. Continue by bike

    Take a bike nearby and pick the chase back up. Lucia rides on the back and can shoot.

    Threat What the guides say
    Large truck PowerPyx: shooting as Lucia helps against the enemies on it
    Enemy bikes PowerPyx: they close in from both sides
    Helicopter Both: when it appears, shoot the pilot or the gunmen

    8. Pull Rafa out of the SUV

    Andrés, in another vehicle, forces Gustavo off the road. The SUV stops on train tracks. Run to it, cut Rafa's seatbelt and pull him out.

    9. Deal with Gustavo

    Gustavo runs. Follow him up to the highway, where he tries to steal a car and fails. Deal with him to finish the mission. PowerPyx's story summary says he dies here.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions, so we do not claim any. Two objectives are literally "Protect Andrés", so keep him covered in both of those fights. The spots that ask the most of you:

    • The stealth approach. Tag the guards first and stay on the route to the right.
    • Carrying Rafa. Lucia's aim drops, so clear what you can from the ladder while he climbs down.
    • The grab at ground level. Break free first, then shoot the man who has Rafa.
    • The bike chase. Deal with the helicopter as soon as it shows up.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash $30,000 (both guides)
    Trophy Family Matters, according to PowerPyx; it is story-related in our GTA 6 trophies list
    Optional goals None listed by either guide
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    PowerPyx also names the kidnappers and the site (a group it calls FAP, at a cement factory), describes Gustavo as the family's head of security who was working with them, and says Abe Gonzalez reports afterwards that the new identities are ready. Those story details come from one guide.

    The mission is number 91 in GTABase's list and 95 in PowerPyx's, which counts the waiting step about the kidnapping as a mission of its own. PowerPyx places one optional, missable job for Lucia before the next story mission, This is a Free Call From…, and both sites continue the story with Power Play. The full order is in our GTA 6 mission list, and GTA 6 heists shows how these jobs lead to the VCIA Takedown.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Rafa in the GTA 6 Child's Play walkthrough?

    At the quarry south of VCIA. Look through Lucia's binoculars at the building ahead: Rafa is in the room on the upper level, under a row of red lights.

    Is Child's Play timed or missable in GTA 6?

    No. PowerPyx and GTABase both say the map suggests a time limit, but the game simply makes you play the mission once every other Jason and Lucia mission before Power Play is done. Starting it earlier changes nothing in the story.

    What is the reward for Child's Play?

    $30,000 in both guides. PowerPyx also lists the Family Matters trophy or achievement for finishing it, and GTABase marks the mission as required for 100% completion.

    Who do you play as in Child's Play?

    Both leads. Either Jason or Lucia can start it at Megamundo HQ, and the game switches between them during the rescue and the chase.
