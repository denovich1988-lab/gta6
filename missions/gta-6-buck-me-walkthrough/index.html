    GTA 6 Buck Me Walkthrough: Set Up the BuckMe Bank App

    GTA 6 Buck Me walkthrough: visit the bank in Key Lento, get the BuckMe app, move the $100 welcome gift into Family Savings and learn the banking rules.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Buck Me is a short Chapter 1 mission with no combat: Jason and Lucia are refused a normal account and get the BuckMe app instead.
    • PowerPyx reports a $100 welcome gift in checking, which you move into Family Savings to finish the mission.
    • Money in Family Savings cannot be lost and cannot be withdrawn; pocket cash is lost when you die.

    This GTA 6 Buck Me walkthrough covers the mission that gives Jason and Lucia a bank account: a visit to the bank in Key Lento, a refusal, and the BuckMe app that handles your money for the rest of the game. PowerPyx lists it as "Buck Me (Set Up Bank Account)". There is no combat and no driving challenge, but the banking rules it introduces matter for every job afterwards.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Buck Me (mission 13 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Set Up Bank Account
    Playable character Jason; Lucia joins at the bank
    Starts at Fleeca bank, Key Lento (PowerPyx), after Lucia's call
    Requirement About half a day after Coming Home
    Reward BuckMe app; $100 welcome gift (PowerPyx)
    Next mission Down to Earth

    VGC places Buck Me before Clean Up After Donnie; PowerPyx places it after. You can treat the two as a pair.

    Objectives

    1. Ask about setting up a bank account.
    2. Speak to the manager.
    3. Open BuckMe.
    4. Move money into the Family Savings account.

    Step-by-step

    1. Talk to the teller

    Go into the bank and speak to the teller behind the counter. She sends you to the manager.

    2. Follow the manager

    PowerPyx says he sits at the table in the right-hand corner. Follow him into his office, where Lucia joins the conversation.

    3. The refusal

    The two of them cannot open a normal account; PowerPyx says the manager checks their credit and it is too poor. VGC adds a dialogue choice when he asks about savings: say you have nothing, or say you keep cash at the house. He offers the BuckMe app instead.

    4. Open BuckMe at home

    A cutscene takes you to Jason's house and a tutorial explains the phone. Open the phone and choose the purple "B" icon. PowerPyx says $100 has been placed in checking as a welcome gift.

    5. Move the money

    In the app, open Checking Account from the top menu and choose Buck Yourself to move the $100 into Family Savings. VGC ends the mission with the pair watching TV and going to bed.

    BuckMe rules worth knowing

    These come from PowerPyx's notes on the app:

    Rule What it means
    Family Savings is locked Money moved there can never be withdrawn, but it cannot be lost either
    Savings goals Each goal unlocks perks that lower prices, and raises your checking limit
    Checking limit When checking is full, incoming payments wait until you spend or move money
    Purchases over $1,000 Paid through BuckMe, because vendors take pocket cash only up to $999
    Deposits Use the app's ATM Finder to set a destination, then deposit at any ATM
    Dying You lose your pocket cash; a smaller part of checking is lost too

    Tip: deposit pocket cash before anything risky. It is the only money you lose in full.

    Where players fail

    There is no fail state here. The usual mistake comes later: carrying cash into a mission and losing it.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward BuckMe app and a $100 welcome gift (PowerPyx)
    Long-term goal "Save for the Future" savings goals, which PowerPyx describes as optional for the story

    More on earning and keeping cash: how to make money in GTA 6. Next up is Brian's job at the marina, Down to Earth.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you complete the GTA 6 Buck Me walkthrough mission?

    Talk to the teller, follow the manager to his office, then at home open the BuckMe app on the phone and move money from checking into Family Savings.

    Where is the bank in Buck Me?

    PowerPyx gives it as the Fleeca branch in Key Lento, Mariana County. Lucia calls about half a day after Coming Home to meet there.

    Can you withdraw money from Family Savings in GTA 6?

    PowerPyx says no: money in Family Savings can never be withdrawn, but it also cannot be lost. Checking can be withdrawn at an ATM.
