    GTA 6 Clear Skies Walkthrough: Escort, Airstrip and Rescue

    GTA 6 Clear Skies walkthrough: take off from the water, escort Pilot Pete's plane, survive the Final Chapter ambush at the airstrip and get Donnie out alive.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Clear Skies is a flying and shooting mission: escort a plane to a swamp airstrip, then rescue Donnie from the Final Chapter biker gang.
    • Two things send you back to a checkpoint: wrecking the seaplane on landing and letting Donnie die.
    • It runs straight into a short scene at Jason's house, which PowerPyx lists separately as Building a Life.

    This GTA 6 Clear Skies walkthrough covers Donnie's job in the air: an escort flight over the ocean, a deal at a swamp airstrip that goes wrong, a rescue through several buildings and a motorbike escape. PowerPyx lists it as "Clear Skies (Join Donnie On A Job)". It is the biggest gunfight of Chapter 1 so far.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Clear Skies (mission 16 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Also listed as Join Donnie On A Job
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at The seaplane in Southwind, Mariana County, in the afternoon
    Requirement A call after Down to Earth
    Reward None listed

    Stock up on ammo and armor at a gun shop first. The airstrip fight comes in several waves.

    Objectives

    1. Talk to Brian.
    2. Taxi the plane into the water and take off.
    3. Fly to the intercept point.
    4. Catch up to Pilot Pete's plane and escort it to the landing strip.
    5. Fly to the next airstrip.
    6. Follow Donnie and observe the deal.
    7. Find and rescue Donnie.
    8. Escape with Donnie.
    9. Hold off the Final Chapter.
    10. Grab a bike, catch up with Donnie and get in the plane.

    Step-by-step

    1. Brian, then the plane

    Donnie is at the seaplane and sends you to Brian at the tiki bar up the street. Come back and taxi the plane.

    Tip: PowerPyx recommends rolling the plane off the side of the road into the water and taking off from there. Taking off from the road works but is riskier.

    2. The escort

    Fly to the intercept point, marked over the ocean in the south-east corner of the map. Pilot Pete's plane appears flying low. Catch up and hold position directly above it all the way to the swamp airstrip.

    3. Land at the second airstrip

    Once Pete is down, fly on to the next marker in the north-west of the swamps and land. VGC lists destroying the plane here as a fail state, so take the approach slowly.

    4. The deal

    Taxi, then follow Donnie on foot to meet his contact. The Final Chapter biker gang arrives, the deal collapses, they drag Donnie away and open fire.

    5. Clear the airstrip

    Fight from cover. VGC warns that the bikers try to flank you, so deal with them quickly rather than sitting still.

    Tip: PowerPyx points out that the bikers leave a row of different motorcycles here, which you can scan with the WAiNK app once the area is quiet.

    6. Rescue Donnie

    Head for the next building up the strip; VGC says to go left and break through a door. Rooms along the way have gang members inside. Donnie is tied to a chair. Untie him.

    7. Protect him on the way out

    More enemies appear as Donnie leads you back to the runway, and VGC shows a second "rescue Donnie" moment in a hangar. Shoot whoever is attacking him first. If Donnie dies you go back to the checkpoint.

    8. Hold the line, then ride

    Donnie boards the seaplane and a large group arrives. Hold them off until he is away, then take one of the bikes and follow. PowerPyx says enemies keep spawning during the ride and you can ignore them.

    9. Meet the plane

    The meeting point is on the water east of Watson Bay. Get into the plane to end the chase.

    10. Back at the house

    The game moves straight to Jason's house. VGC includes a short scene here in which you help Lucia build a coffee table with a quick button prompt; PowerPyx lists the scene as its own entry, Building a Life.

    Where players fail

    • Crashing the landing. The plane has to survive.
    • Losing Donnie. Prioritise the enemies closest to him, twice.
    • Stopping to fight on the bike. Keep moving.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)
    Optional Scan the bikers' motorcycles with WAiNK

    With this, Day Release and Building a Life done, Brian's next job opens: Swamped.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Clear Skies walkthrough mission?

    After Down to Earth a call opens it. Go to the seaplane in Southwind in the afternoon; at other times PowerPyx says you can hold X (A on Xbox) to wait. Donnie sends you to Brian at the tiki bar.

    How do you escort Pilot Pete's plane in Clear Skies?

    Fly to the intercept point over the ocean, catch up with the low-flying plane and stay directly above it until it lands at the swamp airstrip. PowerPyx says flying above it hides its radar signature.

    Where is Donnie in Clear Skies?

    In a building further up the airstrip, tied to a chair, with Final Chapter members inside. Clear the room, untie him, then protect him on the way back to the plane.
