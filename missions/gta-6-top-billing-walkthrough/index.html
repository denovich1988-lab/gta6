    GTA 6 Top Billing Walkthrough: Find Roxy at the House Party

    GTA 6 Top Billing walkthrough: follow Roxy from the Jack of Hearts, let the dog out to move Jacki, knock out Kaamal and cut the power as Jason.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Top Billing is a Chapter 3 mission from Dre'Quan that starts at the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown after Car Service.
    • At the house party, let the dog out through the kitchen door to move Jacki away from the door to Roxy.
    • Jason cuts the power at the green box on the sidewalk corner. The mission pays no cash.

    This GTA 6 Top Billing walkthrough covers the Real Dimez' headline night, which falls apart when the rapper DWNPLY takes over their set. There is no combat beyond one punch. The mission is a short drive, a food stop and a small puzzle at a house party. PowerPyx subtitles it Watch the Real Dimez.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Top Billing (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Mostly Lucia; Jason for one step
    Given by Dre'Quan
    Starts at The Jack of Hearts, Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Car Service
    Reward No cash

    Objectives

    1. Go backstage.
    2. Find the Dimez.
    3. Follow Roxy.
    4. Stop at Peewees and order.
    5. Go to the house party and find Roxy.
    6. Lure Jacki away from the door.
    7. Deal with Kaamal.
    8. Cut the power.
    9. Follow Roxy out.

    PowerPyx splits these into 14 on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into seven. The order is the same.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go backstage

    You are taken into the club automatically. Dre'Quan is backstage, down the hallway to the right of the stairs.

    2. Find the Dimez

    After the scene you play Lucia. Ask the host at the front door: the Dimez have been thrown out. Bae-Luxe and Roxy are arguing in the parking lot.

    3. Follow Roxy

    Roxy drives off. Get into the nearby car with the others and follow her until a passing train cuts you off.

    4. Stop at Peewees

    Dre'Quan suggests getting food. Go into Peewees and order for the group at the counter. When they decide to go after Roxy, return to Bae-Luxe's car.

    5. Reach the house party

    The party is in the south of the city, near VCIA. Lucia goes in alone through the back door. A woman called Jacki is leaning on the door that leads to Roxy and will not move.

    6. Let the dog out

    Go to the kitchen on the other side. Jacki's dog is pawing at a door there. Open it so the dog goes out into the yard, and Jacki follows. Use the door she was blocking.

    7. Deal with Kaamal

    Roxy is talking to a man named Kaamal. When he stands up, hit him. One knockout is enough to persuade Roxy to leave.

    8. Cut the power (Jason)

    The game switches to Jason outside. Open the green power box on the sidewalk corner, look at its left side and flip the switch. The house goes dark.

    9. Follow Roxy

    Back as Lucia, follow Roxy out of the house to finish.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions, and the mission has no timer either of them mentions. The usual hold-ups:

    • Trying to talk Jacki out of the way. She only moves for the dog.
    • Looking for the switch on the front of the power box. It is on the left side.
    • Expecting the chase to succeed. The train always stops it; the story continues at Peewees.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you get past Jacki in the GTA 6 Top Billing walkthrough?

    Go to the kitchen side of the house, where her dog is scratching at a door. Open that door so the dog runs into the yard, and Jacki leaves her post.

    Where is the power box in Top Billing?

    On the sidewalk corner outside the house. It is green. As Jason, open it, look at its left side and use the switch.

    Does Top Billing pay any money?

    No. PowerPyx lists the reward as none, and GTABase lists one unlock without naming it.

    How do you start Top Billing?

    Finish Car Service. Dre'Quan then invites Jason and Lucia to see the Real Dimez at the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown.
