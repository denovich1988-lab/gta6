    GTA 6 Swamped Walkthrough: Kingfishers Camp and Lori

    GTA 6 Swamped walkthrough: ride with Brian to the Kingfishers camp, survive the ambush, chase Lori's kidnappers by helicopter and jump onto the boat.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Swamped sends Jason and Brian to the Kingfishers camp to make peace; it turns into an ambush and a rescue.
    • The chase has two parts: shooting from a helicopter, then jumping from your boat onto the one carrying Lori.
    • The mission fails if the Kingfishers get away with Lori or if you shoot her by accident.

    This GTA 6 Swamped walkthrough covers Brian's attempt to settle things with the Kingfishers: the drive to their camp, the ambush, a helicopter pursuit over the swamp and a jump between boats to rescue Lori. PowerPyx lists it as "Swamped (Meet Brian)". This page describes a major story event in the step-by-step section, so skip to the objectives if you want to avoid it.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Swamped (mission 17 in our list, Chapter 1)
    Playable character Jason
    Starts at Brian's marina, Key Lento
    Requirement Finish Day Release, Clear Skies and Building a Life
    Reward None listed
    Next mission Don Gone, which starts immediately

    Arrive with armor and plenty of ammo. Once you get into Brian's truck there is no chance to shop.

    Objectives

    1. Go to the Kingfishers Camp.
    2. Escape with Brian and get to Lori.
    3. Get into the helicopter.
    4. Rescue Lori.

    Step-by-step

    1. Ride to the camp

    Meet Brian and get in his truck. The camp is in the north-west of Mariana County and PowerPyx calls the drive a long one. Lori covers the meeting from a distance with a rifle (VGC).

    2. Walk in with Brian

    A Kingfisher meets you at the entrance. Follow Brian on foot through the camp to the far end, where he talks to a man named Doug beside a pig pen.

    3. The pig pen

    Inspect the pen when prompted (L2 + Triangle on PS5, LT + Y on Xbox). PowerPyx describes what Jason finds: Donnie's body. The Kingfishers have already killed him, and the meeting is a trap.

    4. Fight out

    Shoot your way through the camp and stay near Brian, who shows as a green dot on the minimap. VGC says you do not have to kill everyone, but getting out without thinning the gang is hard. Lori is grabbed during the fight.

    5. Helicopter

    A helicopter sits at the edge of the camp, on the left according to PowerPyx. Get in and go after the Kingfishers carrying Lori.

    Tip: when the game prompts you to destroy the truck, do it. VGC says that removes a large group of Kingfishers at once.

    Keep shooting the enemies on the ground. PowerPyx notes that one of them always moves Lori to another boat, and VGC says killing her kidnapper during this stretch can end the chase early.

    6. Boat chase

    If they reach the water, the pursuit continues by boat. There are two ways to finish it:

    Method How
    Jump across Pull alongside and hold Square (PS5) or X (Xbox), then tap the same button in the quick-time event (PowerPyx)
    Shoot the driver VGC says shooting the Kingfisher steering the boat also works

    Be quick. PowerPyx warns that enemy fire can destroy your engine and force a checkpoint restart.

    7. Afterwards

    The rest is cutscenes. Back home you can tell Lucia about Brian's retirement plans.

    Tip: do tell her. PowerPyx says the conversation improves the relationship and skipping it loses the boost.

    Where players fail

    • Shooting near Lori. Hitting her fails the mission (VGC). Aim at the driver or the men away from her.
    • Hanging back in the boat. The jump prompt needs you right alongside.
    • Letting them leave. If the Kingfishers escape with Lori, the mission fails.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Reward None listed (PowerPyx)
    Optional The conversation with Lucia at home, for a relationship boost

    Brian's next request follows straight away: Don Gone. The Kingfishers first appear in Clean Up After Donnie.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Swamped walkthrough mission?

    Finish Day Release, Clear Skies and Building a Life. The marker then appears at Brian's marina in Key Lento; PowerPyx says to enter the back room.

    How do you jump onto Lori's boat in Swamped?

    Drive close alongside it and hold Square on PS5 (X on Xbox). PowerPyx warns that the prompt does not appear if you are too far away, then you tap the same button in a short quick-time event.

    What makes you fail Swamped?

    VGC lists two fail states: the Kingfishers escaping with Lori, and shooting Lori. PowerPyx adds that enemy fire can destroy your boat's engine, which sends you back to a checkpoint.
