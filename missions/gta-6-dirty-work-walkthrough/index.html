    GTA 6 Dirty Work Walkthrough: Raina and the Motel Photos

    GTA 6 Dirty Work walkthrough: how to start Andrea's plan, where Lucia should stand at the Velvet Palm Motel, Jason's part in the room and the photos of Raina.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated

    Read

    Quick answer

    • Dirty Work is a Chapter 4 mission from Andrea that opens once both Too Much Baggage and Cash Injections are done.
    • Jason poses as Raina's client at the Velvet Palm Motel in Tequesta while Lucia photographs them through the window.
    • It ends the next day when Lucia talks to Nate by the pool at the gym; PowerPyx lists no reward.

    This GTA 6 Dirty Work walkthrough covers the mission where Jason and Lucia set Raina up at a motel so Lucia can move further into Kordell Grant's crew. It is short and has no combat: one drive, a button-mashing scene for Jason, a few photos for Lucia and a chat at the gym the next day. The only thing to get right is where Lucia stands.

    Why Raina is the target

    By this point Lucia has two of Kordell's bodybuilders on her side, Cisco and Nate, but Raina will not warm to her. Both PowerPyx and GTABase describe the same plan from Andrea: Jason books Raina, who works as a dominatrix, and Lucia photographs the two of them together. PowerPyx adds the purpose, which is to create distrust and weaken Raina's place in the crew.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Dirty Work (Chapter 4), on-screen task "Deal with Raina"
    Playable character Jason and Lucia; the game switches between them
    Given by Andrea
    Starts at The planning room, Stockyard, Vice City
    Requirement Too Much Baggage and Cash Injections both completed
    Reward None (PowerPyx)

    Either character can start it. Because the mission needs both of them, it sits where Jason's airport thread and Lucia's gym thread meet. If you are missing the marker, check that you have finished both requirements, not just one.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists seven on-screen steps.

    1. Go to Velvet Palm Motel.
    2. Choose a vantage point.
    3. Take a photo of Raina.
    4. Continue taking photographs or leave.
    5. Regroup with Lucia.
    6. Meet Nate at the pool.
    7. Speak to Nate.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Andrea in the planning room

    Go to the planning room with Jason or Lucia. After Andrea explains the plan, the mission begins.

    2. Drive to the Velvet Palm Motel

    Get in a vehicle and drive to the Velvet Palm Motel in Tequesta. Neither guide names a required car, so use whatever you arrived in.

    3. Pick Lucia's vantage point

    Control moves to Lucia, and the game asks you to choose where to shoot from. Both guides give the same answer: use the containers Jason points out. From there Lucia looks straight into the motel windows.

    Tip: the photos only work if Jason and Raina are both in view through the window, so a clear line into the room is what matters when you settle on the spot.

    4. Jason's appointment

    The game switches to Jason inside the room, where Raina starts ordering him around. Press the prompt repeatedly to resist her; PowerPyx gives it as Square on PlayStation or X on Xbox. Keep going until Jason gets the window open.

    5. Take the photos

    Back as Lucia, photograph Raina through the open window while Jason is visible with her. The objective then changes to "Continue taking photographs or leave". A few shots are enough, and both guides say Lucia leaves by herself afterwards.

    6. Regroup in the alley

    Switch to Jason, drive around the building and meet Lucia in the alley.

    7. Talk to Nate at the pool

    The story skips to the next day with Lucia at the gym. Go upstairs and outside to the pool, and talk to Nate, who is sitting on one of the loungers. The mission ends with that conversation.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition, and nothing here is timed in their descriptions. The two places to slow down are the vantage point (take the containers instead of experimenting) and Jason's scene, where you need to keep pressing the prompt until the window opens, not just once.

    There are no choices with lasting effects in either walkthrough, so you do not need a manual save for this one. If you like keeping one per mission anyway, see how to save in GTA 6.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None (PowerPyx)
    Optional goals None listed by either guide
    100% GTABase marks the mission as mandatory and required

    What comes next

    The two guides number this stretch differently. PowerPyx follows Dirty Work with a short separate entry, Nate Will Be in Touch, subtitled Help Kordell's Crew, and then the Jason-only mission Adventures in Babysitting. GTABase has no page for the Nate entry and lists Adventures in Babysitting straight after Dirty Work. PowerPyx also notes that missions in this part of Chapter 4 can show up in a slightly different order, so follow the markers on your own map.

    For the gym missions that lead here, see Making Gainz and Amateur Hour. The whole airport setup is outlined in GTA 6 heists, and the story order is in our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do you take the photos in the GTA 6 Dirty Work walkthrough?

    From the containers Jason points out at the Velvet Palm Motel in Tequesta. Both guides say they give Lucia a straight view into the motel windows.

    How do you start Dirty Work in GTA 6?

    Finish Too Much Baggage and Cash Injections, then go to the planning room with Jason or Lucia to meet Andrea. Either character can start it.

    Do you have to keep taking photos of Raina?

    No. A few shots while Jason is visible in the window are enough. Both guides say Lucia then leaves on her own, so there is nothing to trigger.

    Is there a reward for Dirty Work?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. GTABase marks the mission as mandatory and required for 100% completion.
