    GTA 6 Security Expert Walkthrough: Get Nicole Out Quietly

    GTA 6 Security Expert walkthrough: drive to the house in Waning Sands, follow Jason past the guard, carry Nicole to the trunk and reach the planning room.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Security Expert is a Cartel Heist prep mission in Chapter 3 that starts by itself when Sinfrontera National Bank ends.
    • Nicole is in the north-east bedroom of a guarded house in Waning Sands; stay right behind Jason to avoid the guards.
    • Put her in the trunk and drive to the planning room in Stockyard. The mission pays no cash.

    This GTA 6 Security Expert walkthrough covers a stealth pick-up: Jason and Lucia take the bank's security specialist, Nicole Park, out of a house where the cartel's men are guarding her. Done properly, no shot is fired.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Security Expert (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Lucia and Jason
    Given by Raul
    Starts at Waning Sands, Leonard County
    Requirement Sinfrontera National Bank; starts automatically after it
    Reward No cash

    Objectives

    1. Find Nicole: drive to the house and park.
    2. Sneak into the house.
    3. Pick up Nicole and escape with her.
    4. Escape the area.
    5. Take Nicole to the planning room.

    PowerPyx shows seven on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into four.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive past, then park

    In the neighbourhood, head south to the marked house. Jason tells you to drive past slowly. Do that, then park nearby.

    2. Get in through the back

    Continue on foot behind Jason, who leads you around to the back. Wait there until a guard comes out of the back door and walks away. Then go inside while Jason keeps watch.

    3. Find the bedroom

    Cross the hallway to the bedrooms. The one you want is in the north-east corner, with its door closed. Open it.

    4. Carry Nicole out

    After the scene Nicole is unconscious. Pick her up and follow Jason out of the back door, along the side of the house, to the front.

    Tip: both guides give the same advice for this stretch: stay right on Jason's heels and the guards do not notice you.

    5. Trunk, then leave

    At your car, open the trunk and put Nicole inside. Leave the area quickly to get clear of the guards.

    6. Drive to the planning room

    Drive to Raul's planning room in Stockyard. Arriving completes the mission. In the scene that follows, Raul tells Nicole he knows she has been stealing from La Polilla Negra, and she agrees to join the crew.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. Where the stealth breaks down:

    • Going in before the guard has left the back door. Wait for him to walk off.
    • Falling behind Jason on the way out. His route is the safe one.
    • Lingering after closing the trunk. Drive away at once.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Payment None
    Story Nicole joins Raul's crew for the Cartel Heist
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    With Nicole on board, the remaining prep opens up. PowerPyx lists Company Property next, then Safe Word, which Nicole herself gives you. See GTA 6 heists for how the heist chains work. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Nicole in the GTA 6 Security Expert walkthrough?

    In a guarded house in Waning Sands, Leonard County. Inside, cross the hallway to the bedrooms and open the closed door of the room in the north-east corner.

    How do you avoid the guards in Security Expert?

    Follow Jason closely. Wait at the back of the house until the guard comes out of the back door and walks off, then go in. On the way out, stay right behind Jason along the side of the house.

    How do you start Security Expert in GTA 6?

    You do not start it yourself. It begins automatically after Sinfrontera National Bank and moves you to Waning Sands.
