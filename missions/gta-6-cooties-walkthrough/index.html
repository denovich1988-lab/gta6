    GTA 6 Cooties Walkthrough: Shoot the Music Video

    GTA 6 Cooties walkthrough: wait for Dre'Quan's call after Ride or Die, film the music video as Lucia, escape DWNPLY's crew and unlock Bae-Luxe's Bat.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 23, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By

    Quick answer

    • Cooties is a Chapter 3 main mission from Dre'Quan that unlocks a couple of in-game days after Ride or Die.
    • You play Lucia through a mostly scripted music video shoot in Crosstown, then drive DWNPLY's car to the studio.
    • PowerPyx lists the reward as Bae-Luxe's Bat, a variant melee weapon; GTABase shows one unnamed unlock.

    This GTA 6 Cooties walkthrough covers Dre'Quan's music video shoot: a short, mostly scripted mission where Lucia helps the Dimez film the video for their song "Cooties" using DWNPLY's stolen car. There is one fistfight, some set dressing with a bat and a spray can, and a car chase at the end. PowerPyx subtitles it Shoot the Music Video.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Cooties (Chapter 3)
    Playable character Lucia
    Given by Dre'Quan Priest
    Starts at Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Ride or Die, then a wait of a couple of in-game days
    Reward Bae-Luxe's Bat variant weapon (PowerPyx)

    If the marker has not appeared yet, play other missions until Dre'Quan calls.

    Objectives

    1. Meet Dre'Quan in Crosstown.
    2. Shoot the music video.
    3. Escape with DWNPLY's car.
    4. Drive to the studio.

    PowerPyx lists the last three as on-screen objectives; GTABase adds the meeting as a first stage.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Dre'Quan

    A couple of in-game days after Ride or Die, Dre'Quan calls. The Dimez want to film the video for "Cooties" with the car you took from DWNPLY. Go to the marker in Crosstown.

    2. Shoot the video

    Most of the shoot plays out by itself. The parts you control, in order:

    1. Driving shots. Hold the accelerator (R2 on PS5, RT on Xbox, per PowerPyx) to keep the car moving and use the left stick to steer it around the circle.
    2. The street scene. When the car stops after the drive through the city, get out. Pull the man off Roxy, then fight the second man who comes at you.
    3. Wrecking the car. After the girls film themselves smashing the car, Lucia is handed a bat. You can take a few swings yourself.
    4. Spray paint. Shake the can (X on PS5, A on Xbox), then spray the side of the car with R2 (RT).

    3. Escape DWNPLY's crew

    DWNPLY and his friends turn up. Drive away and keep going until every chasing car has dropped off and you are outside the red area on the map.

    Tip: you do not have to fight. Both guides describe the goal as getting clear: lose the cars and leave the red area.

    4. Drop the car at the studio

    Drive to the studio. Arriving completes the mission.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions, and the shoot itself is hard to get wrong. The one place to lose time is the chase: turning back into the red area keeps DWNPLY's crew on you.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed
    Weapon Bae-Luxe's Bat, a variant melee weapon (PowerPyx; GTABase lists one unnamed unlock)
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Cooties follows on from Ride or Die, where the car was taken; the Dimez first appear in Top Billing. It is also one of the two missions that must be done before Karim calls you to Hidden Charges. Other variant weapons are in GTA 6 weapons. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you unlock Cooties in GTA 6?

    Finish Ride or Die, then wait a couple of in-game days. Dre'Quan calls about the video shoot and a marker appears in Crosstown.

    Who do you play in the GTA 6 Cooties walkthrough?

    Lucia. PowerPyx says either lead can start the mission, but the shoot itself is played as Lucia.

    What is the reward for Cooties in GTA 6?

    PowerPyx lists Bae-Luxe's Bat, a variant weapon. GTABase shows one unlock without naming it. Neither guide lists cash.
