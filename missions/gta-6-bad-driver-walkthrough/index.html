    GTA 6 Bad Driver! Walkthrough: Kill Felipe or Spare Him?

    GTA 6 Bad Driver! walkthrough: find Felipe at the Millport bar, stop him in the woods and choose between $20,000 from Andrea and Felipe's gift vehicle.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Bad Driver! is an optional, missable Chapter 4 mission from Andrea that opens after Party Hard and disappears once you start Power Play.
    • You find Felipe at a bar in Millport, chase him toward the woods and decide what happens to him.
    • Killing Felipe pays $20,000 from Andrea; sparing him unlocks Collect Felipe's Gift, where you pick up a vehicle he leaves for you.

    This GTA 6 Bad Driver! walkthrough covers a short optional mission with a real choice: Andrea wants Felipe dealt with for running out on the Cartel Heist, and you decide whether he lives. It is also missable, so the first thing to know is the deadline.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Bad Driver! (Chapter 4, optional)
    Playable character Jason or Lucia (PowerPyx)
    Given by Andrea
    Starts at The marked bar in Millport, Lummox County
    Requirement Party Hard
    Missable Yes: gone for good once you start Power Play
    Reward $20,000, or the follow-up mission Collect Felipe's Gift

    Felipe ran out on The Cartel Heist. Andrea, who gave you The Missing Link, has now heard he is in Millport.

    Tip: make a manual save before you walk into the bar. Missions cannot be replayed, so a save is the only way to see the other outcome. See can you replay missions in GTA 6.

    Objectives

    1. Look for Felipe.
    2. Chase after Felipe.
    3. Deal with Felipe.

    Step-by-step

    1. Find Felipe

    Drive to the bar in Millport, go inside and walk toward the back. Felipe comes out of the restroom, sees you and runs.

    2. Chase him

    He goes out of the back of the bar toward the woods.

    1. Shoot him in the legs or the back to slow him down.
    2. Run up and hit him. He gives up.

    Tip: aim low. A leg shot stops him without ending the mission's choice for you.

    3. Deal with Felipe

    Hold L2 (LT on Xbox, per PowerPyx) to focus on him and ask what happened. You can keep questioning him, spare him through the dialogue options, or shoot him at any point in the conversation.

    Kill or spare Felipe?

    Choice How What you get
    Kill Felipe Shoot him at any time during the conversation $20,000 from Andrea
    Spare Felipe Choose to let him go in the dialogue Felipe contacts you later and unlocks the optional mission Collect Felipe's Gift, where you collect the vehicle he left for you

    Both guides describe the outcomes the same way. Neither names the vehicle or says what it is worth, so we cannot tell you which reward is bigger. What is certain: $20,000 is paid straight away, while the spare path adds one more optional mission to play.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition. The real risk is missing the mission altogether: it has to be done after Party Hard and before you start Power Play, a later mission in the VCIA Takedown chain.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash $20,000 if you kill Felipe
    Unlock Collect Felipe's Gift if you spare him
    100% The guides disagree: GTABase marks it as required, PowerPyx says it is not required for completion

    Because of that disagreement, play it if you want 100%: it is short, and skipping it means missing either reward. For other ways to earn at this stage, see how to make money in GTA 6. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Should you kill or spare Felipe in the GTA 6 Bad Driver! walkthrough?

    Kill him for $20,000 from Andrea, or spare him through the dialogue to unlock the optional mission Collect Felipe's Gift, where you collect a vehicle he leaves you. Neither guide names the vehicle, so the cash is the known quantity.

    Is Bad Driver! missable in GTA 6?

    Yes. Both PowerPyx and GTABase say it becomes permanently unavailable once you start the Chapter 4 mission Power Play.

    Where is Felipe in Bad Driver!?

    At the marked bar in Millport, Lummox County. Walk toward the back and he comes out of the restroom, then runs out of the back door toward the woods.

    How do you stop Felipe from running away?

    Shoot him in the legs or back to slow him down, then run up and hit him so he gives up.
