    GTA 6 Breakfast Run Walkthrough: The Store Robbery Spree

    GTA 6 Breakfast Run walkthrough: wait for Jason's call, rob Hank's Waffles, the liquor store and the thrift store, then lose a two-star wanted level.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    2 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Breakfast Run starts about half a day after Slam Dunk, when Jason calls and the motel icon turns pink.
    • Jason and Lucia rob a diner, a liquor store and a thrift store back to back; each robbery has a one-minute timer.
    • It ends with a two-star wanted level, unlocks the Trust trophy and makes Jason playable again.

    This GTA 6 Breakfast Run walkthrough covers the mission where Jason gets back on his feet and the couple go straight back to work. It is a chain of quick store robberies that teaches the hold-up system you will use for money for the rest of the game. PowerPyx subtitles it Check on Jason.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Breakfast Run (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia, with Jason alongside
    Given by Jason and Lucia
    Starts at Starlet Motel, Kelly County (pink icon)
    Requirement Finish Slam Dunk, then wait for Jason's call
    Reward Trust trophy; Jason playable again

    Objectives

    1. Go to Hank's Waffles.
    2. Rob the diner.
    3. Rob the liquor store.
    4. Rob the thrift store.
    5. Get in the car.
    6. Lose the cops.

    Step-by-step

    1. Take Jason's call

    The call comes roughly half a day after Slam Dunk. Both guides say finishing other open missions helps bring it on. Go back to the motel room; a scene shows time passing until Jason has healed, and the two decide to rob some stores.

    2. Drive to Hank's Waffles

    Follow the marker to the waffle house.

    3. Rob the diner

    Step What to do
    Threaten Aim your gun at the staff and use the threaten prompt (Square on PS5, X on Xbox, per PowerPyx)
    Collect They open the register and put cash on the counter; pick it up
    Leave A one-minute timer runs in the bottom-right corner; walk out before it ends

    When you leave, the game moves you straight to the next store.

    4. Liquor store and thrift store

    You appear inside the liquor store. Aim at the clerk, take the money and get in the car, which takes you to the thrift store. Repeat.

    Jason and Lucia then hit a gas station and another liquor store by themselves.

    5. Lose the cops

    Back at the car you have a two-star wanted level. Both guides recommend the small off-road trails as the easiest way out. See how the wanted level works.

    6. Back at the motel

    A scene returns you to the Starlet Motel for the night. In the morning Vicki comes for her laptop, and the story runs directly into Coffee Break.

    Where players fail

    • Letting the timer run down. Threaten, grab the cash and go; do not browse.
    • Staying on main roads with two stars. Take the dirt trails.
    • Expecting free roam afterwards. Coffee Break starts immediately.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Trophy Trust (both guides)
    Jason Playable again
    Personal vehicles One each for Jason and Lucia (PowerPyx)
    Take Care of Jason The background mission Take Care of Jason completes after this one
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Neither guide lists how much the robberies pay. Hold-ups as a way to earn are covered in how to make money in GTA 6. Chapter order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Breakfast Run walkthrough?

    Finish Slam Dunk and wait for Jason's call, which takes about half a day of in-game time. Then return to the room at the Starlet Motel; its icon turns pink.

    How do you rob the stores in Breakfast Run?

    Aim your gun at the staff and use the threaten prompt (Square on PS5, X on Xbox, per PowerPyx). They put the cash on the counter; grab it and leave before the one-minute timer runs out.

    What does Breakfast Run unlock in GTA 6?

    The Trust trophy or achievement, and Jason as a playable character again. PowerPyx adds that Jason and Lucia each have a personal vehicle afterwards.
