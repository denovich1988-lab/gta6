    GTA 6 Making Gainz Walkthrough: Nate, Workouts and Steroids

    GTA 6 Making Gainz walkthrough: what to do before you start, how Lucia gets close to Nate at Ajax Gym, and what the steroids choice changes for good.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Making Gainz is a Lucia-only Chapter 4 mission from Raul that opens a couple of in-game days after Heavy Lifting.
    • After it, Jason and Lucia cannot be seen together in public until the VCIA Takedown, so finish couple activities first.
    • Nate offers Lucia steroids during the workout montage: Decline is the default, and accepting changes her physique for the rest of the game.

    This GTA 6 Making Gainz walkthrough covers the mission where Lucia joins Ajax Gym for real and trains her way into Kordell Grant's crew. The workouts are simple. What needs thought are two things with lasting effects: a restriction that starts when the mission ends, and a choice about steroids in the middle of it.

    Read this first: the couple restriction

    Both PowerPyx and GTABase give the same warning. Once Making Gainz is done, Jason and Lucia cannot be seen together in public until the VCIA Takedown. They can only be together in the Y Hotel room, which makes raising their relationship much harder.

    Before you start:

    • Play It's a Date! if you have not.
    • Do any couple activities you care about. GTA 6 romance explains what counts as time together.
    • Or do what GTABase suggests and play the next story missions back to back, so the restricted stretch is short.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Making Gainz (Chapter 4)
    Playable character Lucia; Jason for one short drive
    Given by Raul Bautista
    Starts at The planning room, Stockyard, Vice City
    Requirement Heavy Lifting, then Raul's message a couple of in-game days later
    Reward None listed

    Objectives

    PowerPyx lists nine on-screen steps.

    1. Locate Nate in the workout area.
    2. Get Nate's attention.
    3. Use the bench press.
    4. Follow Nate.
    5. Use the exercise bike.
    6. Leave the gym.
    7. Meet Jason.
    8. Go to the planning room.
    9. Enter the planning room.

    Step-by-step

    1. Go to the planning room alone

    Take Lucia to the planning room without Jason. After the opening scene you are taken to Ajax Gym, the place you scouted in Heavy Lifting.

    2. Find Nate

    Go upstairs to the main workout area. Nate is near the weight benches. Talk to him, more than once, until he takes notice of Lucia.

    3. Train with Nate

    Order Exercise Where
    1 Bench press The bench Nate is standing behind
    2 Squat rack Follow Nate to it
    3 Deadlift Follow Nate to the outdoor area
    4 Exercise bike The free bike on the far side of the pool, after Nate leaves with Kordell

    4. The montage and the steroids choice

    A long montage follows: Lucia trains with Nate over several weeks, cut with scenes of Jason and Dre'Quan. Keep doing the workouts as they come up.

    During it, Nate offers Lucia steroids.

    Choice Result
    Decline (the default) Lucia's body stays as it is
    Accept (you have to select it) Changes Lucia's physique for the rest of the game

    Tip: you cannot replay missions, and neither guide mentions a way to undo this. If you want to see both, make a manual save before the mission. See how to save in GTA 6.

    5. Leave the gym and meet Jason

    After the montage, Nate shows Lucia the Trauma Floor, Kordell's private workout area at the gym. Leave, and meet Jason around the north side of the building.

    6. Drive to the planning room (Jason)

    Control switches to Jason. Drive to the planning room and use the intercom to go in. After the scenes with Raul, PowerPyx's walkthrough ends here.

    The Trauma Floor: one mission or two?

    The guides split what happens next differently. GTABase's Making Gainz page continues with a final stage back at the gym, while PowerPyx lists a separate entry straight after, Blood Sweat and Gear, subtitled Work Out. GTABase describes the stage like this:

    1. As Lucia, change into workout clothes at the locker on the right of the Trauma Floor and talk to Nate.
    2. Train next to the bodybuilders in order: near Raina, then near Nate and Francisco, then the deadlift on the stage where Kordell is.
    3. Answer Kordell however you like when he comes over, talk to the rest of the crew, and leave the gym.

    We have only been able to check this part against one walkthrough, so treat the details as GTABase's. Either way, follow the on-screen objectives: if the game sends Lucia back to Ajax Gym after the planning room, this is what it wants.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists a fail condition. The mistakes here are strategic: starting the mission before you have finished couple activities, and picking the steroids option without meaning to. The game protects you from the second, because Decline is the default.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed (PowerPyx)
    Unlocks Access to the Trauma Floor at Ajax Gym
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    If the gym has given you a taste for it, the fitness category in GTA 6 challenges lists the workout goals. From here the story splits, with Lucia working on Kordell's crew and Jason scoping out the airport. The whole chain is outlined in GTA 6 heists, and the order is in our GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    Should you take the steroids in the GTA 6 Making Gainz walkthrough?

    It is your call, but it is permanent. Both guides say Decline is the default and that accepting changes Lucia's physique for the rest of the game. Neither lists any other effect.

    What should you do before starting Making Gainz?

    Finish anything that needs Jason and Lucia together in public, such as the optional date It's a Date!. After this mission they can only be together in the Y Hotel room until the VCIA Takedown.

    How do you start Making Gainz in GTA 6?

    Finish Heavy Lifting and wait a couple of in-game days for Raul to contact Lucia. Then go to the planning room in Stockyard with Lucia alone.

    Is there a reward for Making Gainz?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. The mission gets Lucia into Kordell Grant's circle and access to the Trauma Floor at Ajax Gym.
