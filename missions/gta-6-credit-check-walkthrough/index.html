    GTA 6 Credit Check Walkthrough: Rob Three Fleeca Banks

    GTA 6 Credit Check walkthrough: meet Raul's crew, take the Dye Pack Jammer, rob three Fleeca banks and split up to lose the police for $6,000.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 22, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Credit Check is a Chapter 3 mission from Raul: meet his crew in Stockyard, then rob three Fleeca banks as practice.
    • The Dye Pack Jammer from Karim's desk lets you steal marked bills from banks and Payday Loans from now on.
    • The reward is $6,000; PowerPyx adds that bank jobs and Payday Loans appear on the map as robbery targets.

    This GTA 6 Credit Check walkthrough covers the mission that introduces Raul's crew and teaches bank robbery in three steps: a simple branch, a branch with a safe, and a large branch where the alarm goes off. What you learn here is used again in free roam. PowerPyx subtitles it Meet Raul's Crew.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Credit Check (Chapter 3)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia together
    Given by Raul
    Starts at Near the Vice Vinyl mod shop, Crosstown; use the intercom on the wall
    Requirement Crosstown Traffic
    Reward $6,000 and the Dye Pack Jammer

    Raul's crew: Felipe drives, Karim handles tech, Andrea handles comms and disguises.

    Objectives

    1. Meet the crew: talk to Andrea, choose masks, talk to Karim, take the Dye Pack Jammer.
    2. Rob the Fleeca in Washington Beach.
    3. Rob the second Fleeca, with your partner waiting in the car.
    4. Rob the final, largest Fleeca.
    5. Lose the cops and regroup at the apartment.

    PowerPyx lists 25 on-screen lines; GTABase groups them into these five stages.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet the crew

    After the scene with Raul, go upstairs to Andrea and pick one of the two pairs of masks on the table beside her. Then cross the room to Karim and take the Dye Pack Jammer from the desk. Stick bombs are on the desk next to him; take some if you have room.

    Leave the building. Felipe has a car ready outside.

    2. Bank one: Washington Beach

    Park outside. Karim cuts the alarm. Before you go in, open the inventory and equip the mask.

    1. Threaten the guards first.
    2. Go behind the counter and empty the four marked cash drawers.
    3. Leave and drive off.

    Tip: if a guard draws, knock the gun away or shoot his knees. Both guides say that keeps a Professional rating.

    3. Bank two: your partner waits

    Karim marks the next branch. Drive around the police search area left by the first robbery. Here the game teaches you to tell your partner to wait in the car.

    1. Go in alone, mask on.
    2. Open the back door with the Snap Gun, or force it; the alarm is off.
    3. Threaten the teller for the safe code and take the cash from the drawer.
    4. Plant an explosive on the inner door, step aside and set it off.
    5. Open the small safe with the teller's code, or with another charge.
    6. Run back to the car.

    4. Bank three: the big one

    The largest branch is further south. Park facing the road so you can leave in a straight line, and put the mask back on. If one character's bag is full, switch to the other.

    There are more guards here and they draw sooner. Threaten them quickly, then take the marked bills from all four drawers.

    5. Escape and regroup

    The alarm sounds and security opens fire from the back. Fight your way out. Do not go for the safe.

    Outside, use the split-up command so your partner leaves separately and the police are divided. Lose them, then head for the Bayside apartment. After a short scene, answer the door to finish.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists formal fail conditions. These are the costly mistakes:

    • Walking in without the mask. Re-equip it at every bank; it can come off between robberies.
    • Driving through the first bank's search area. Go around it on the way to bank two.
    • Trying the safe in bank three. The alarm is already on and the police are coming.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details

    Item Dye Pack Jammer: marked bills can now be stolen from banks and Payday Loans

    This is the point where robbery becomes a steady income; see how to make money in GTA 6. The crew's real target comes next in Sinfrontera National Bank, the first step of the Cartel Heist covered in GTA 6 heists. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the GTA 6 Credit Check walkthrough mission?

    Finish Crosstown Traffic. Raul calls you to meet the crew: go with both Jason and Lucia to the marker near the Vice Vinyl mod shop in Crosstown and use the intercom on the wall.

    What does the Dye Pack Jammer do in GTA 6?

    It lets you steal marked bills from banks and Payday Loans stores. You pick it up from the desk next to Karim during Credit Check.

    Should you open the safe in the third bank in Credit Check?

    No. The alarm goes off in the third bank and security comes from the back. Both guides say to skip the safe and leave.

    What is the reward for Credit Check?

    $6,000 plus the Dye Pack Jammer. GTABase marks the mission as required for 100% completion.
