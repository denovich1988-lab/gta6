    GTA 6 Heavy Lifting Walkthrough: Ajax Gym, Nate and the Tail

    GTA 6 Heavy Lifting walkthrough: bring Raul the hard drive, go undercover at Ajax Gym, trap Nate in the tanning bed and tail the bodybuilders unseen.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    4 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Heavy Lifting is a Chapter 4 mission from Raul that starts when you collect the hard drive from the storage locker after Lawyer Up.
    • Jason and Lucia go undercover at Ajax Gym, trap Nate in a tanning bed to read his phone, then tail Kordell Grant's crew to the Grand Majestic Aparthotel.
    • During the tail, keep the arrow over the bodybuilders white; it fills yellow when you get too close.

    This GTA 6 Heavy Lifting walkthrough covers Raul's first Chapter 4 job: delivering the hard drive, then going undercover in a bodybuilders' gym to find out what a rival crew is planning. It is long, with about two dozen on-screen objectives, but there is no shooting. The parts that trip players up are the timed bag search and the night-time tail. PowerPyx subtitles it Bring Raul the Hard Drive.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Heavy Lifting (Chapter 4)
    Playable characters Jason and Lucia; the game switches between them
    Given by Raul Bautista
    Starts at The storage building in Crosstown, Vice City
    Requirement Lawyer Up, then Raul's message
    Reward None listed

    Jason and Lucia rented a storage unit at the end of Caught Slipping; this mission sends you to a storage locker to fetch the hard drive.

    Objectives

    PowerPyx shows 24 on-screen steps; GTABase groups them into eight stages, which is the easier way to follow the mission.

    1. Collect the hard drive.
    2. Meet Raul on the houseboat.
    3. Head to Ajax Gym.
    4. Search Raina's bag.
    5. Work out undercover.
    6. Trap Nate.
    7. Tail the bodybuilders.
    8. Go up to the roof.

    Step-by-step

    1. Collect the hard drive

    Go into the storage building, take the elevator up and take the drive from your locker. This starts the mission.

    2. Meet Raul on the houseboat

    Drive to Leonard County, where Raul's houseboat is docked. Board it and take the stairs on the right to the upper deck, where Karim is waiting.

    Raul lays out the plan: he wants to rob a rival crew led by Kordell Grant, a bodybuilder he once worked with, and he needs to know what their next job is. GTABase adds that Karim later works out who the drive belonged to: Giorgio Gallo, heir to the Bilgeco shipping company.

    3. Take the boat to Ajax Gym

    You are put on a boat automatically. Steer south to Ajax Gym and pull up at the dock beside it. Lucia goes in first; follow as Jason.

    Talk to the receptionist, the man in the blue tank top, then follow J-Wang on his tour of the gym.

    4. Search Raina's bag (Lucia)

    Back at reception the game switches to Lucia. Use the marked locker to change into gym clothes, then search Raina's backpack in the locker to the left.

    Tip: both guides say to do this quickly. Go straight from your locker to hers rather than looking around the changing room first.

    5. Work out undercover

    Walk upstairs to the main gym.

    Character Exercise Input (PowerPyx)
    Jason Treadmill Tap X (A on Xbox) repeatedly to keep pace
    Lucia Leg extension L2 + R2 (LT + RT)

    When Kordell's people leave, head to the tanning salon area as Jason.

    6. Trap Nate

    Jason distracts the employee. As Lucia, slip past into the tanning rooms.

    1. Nate is in the second room on the right.
    2. Pick up the lifting strap from the floor.
    3. Use it on the tanning bed to lock Nate inside.
    4. Search his bag, to the right.
    5. On his phone, open the WhatUp app, read the messages and exit.
    6. Put the phone away and use the tanning bed again to let him out.

    Once Nate has gone, leave the gym. You can change back into your normal clothes first. Jason is waiting outside by the water.

    7. Tail the bodybuilders

    The scene cuts to night, with you in a car behind Kordell's crew. An arrow sits above their vehicle:

    Arrow Meaning
    White You are at a safe distance
    Filling yellow You are too close; drop back

    It is a long drive. Tip: GTABase suggests easing off the accelerator and letting another car slot in between you whenever the marker starts to turn.

    8. Follow them to the roof

    They stop at the Grand Majestic Aparthotel. Go to the entrance and follow them inside, watch them get into an elevator, then walk up as Jason calls another one. Ride it to the top floor and follow the hallway out onto the roof. The mission ends there.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists exact fail conditions, but the mission has two obvious pressure points: the bag search, which both guides tell you to do fast, and the tail, where the yellow arrow is your warning. The rule for the tail is distance first, speed second.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash None listed (PowerPyx)
    Unlocks It's a Date! right away; Making Gainz a couple of in-game days later
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    Heavy Lifting is the start of the long run-up to the chapter's airport job, explained in GTA 6 heists. Before you take Making Gainz, read the warning at the top of that page: it limits what Jason and Lucia can do together for a long stretch. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you start the mission in this GTA 6 Heavy Lifting walkthrough?

    Finish Lawyer Up and wait for Raul to ask for the hard drive. Go to the storage building in Crosstown, take the elevator up and collect the drive from the locker.

    How do you trap Nate in Heavy Lifting?

    As Lucia, slip past the tanning salon employee while Jason distracts him. Nate is in the second room on the right. Pick up the lifting strap from the floor and use it on the tanning bed, then search his bag and read the WhatUp messages on his phone.

    How do you tail the bodybuilders without being spotted?

    Stay far enough back that the arrow above their car stays white. If it starts turning yellow, ease off the accelerator and let another car get between you.

    What is the reward for Heavy Lifting in GTA 6?

    PowerPyx lists no reward. The mission sets up the VCIA Takedown story and unlocks the optional date It's a Date! and, a couple of days later, Making Gainz.
