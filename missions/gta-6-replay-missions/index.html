    Can You Replay Missions in GTA 6? No, Here Is the Fix

    You cannot replay missions in GTA 6: there is no replay menu, chapter select or medals. How to use the 15 manual save slots so you can still go back.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has no mission replay, no chapter select and no gold medals, for story missions or Strangers and Freaks.
    • The workaround is the 15 manual save slots: keep a save at the start and end of each of the five chapters.
    • Some optional missions disappear as the story moves on, so a spare save is the only way back to them.

    No, you cannot replay missions in GTA 6: there is no replay menu, no chapter select and no medal system. That is a real change from GTA 5, where any finished mission could be restarted from the pause menu. The only way to see a mission again is a manual save from before it, so it is worth setting up your saves properly from the first hour.

    The short answer

    Feature GTA 5 GTA 6
    Replay a story mission from the menu Yes No
    Gold medal objectives Yes No
    Chapter select No chapters No
    Replay Strangers and Freaks Yes No (PowerPyx)
    Manual save slots - 15
    New Game Plus No Not at launch (Beebom)

    Why there is no replay

    Rockstar has not explained it. Insider Gaming, citing an IGN report, gives the likely reason: choices in missions change what happens afterwards, and random events in the open world depend on those earlier choices, so restarting a single mission in isolation would not fit the state of your game.

    You can see this in the missions themselves. In Coming Home and Day Release, small choices feed the relationship between the two leads.

    The workaround: plan your 15 saves

    PowerPyx and KeenGamer recommend the same layout. The story has five chapters:

    Slots Use
    1 to 10 One save at the start and one at the end of each chapter
    11 to 15 Rotating saves for free roam, or before a mission you may want again

    How to make a save is covered step by step in how to save in GTA 6. The short version: pause, open Game, choose Save Game. You cannot save in the middle of a mission, so do it before you walk into the marker.

    Tip: the most important single save in the early game is the one before Tourist Trap, the point of no return for Chapter 1.

    What you can miss for good

    • Optional missions tied to a chapter. PowerPyx says some become unavailable as the story advances, with big changes between chapters.
    • Jason's house repairs. They close when Lucia is released: see Jason's house projects.
    • Time-limited stranger missions. A few are flagged as missable in Strangers and Freaks.
    • One-off pickups, such as the pistol in the trunk in Don Gone.

    What you cannot miss

    After the credits you return to free roam. Collectibles, challenges and most of the 100% checklist stay available, and PowerPyx notes that missable optional missions are not needed for 100%.

    If you already passed a mission you wanted to replay

    1. Check your save list for an older manual save. The autosave will not help: there is one autosave slot and it is overwritten constantly.
    2. If there is none, the only route is a new game.
    3. From now on, alternate two or three slots after every mission so you always have a recent fallback.

    Will replay be added later?

    Nobody knows. Beebom says it is unclear whether Rockstar will add replay or a New Game Plus in an update. We will change this page if that happens. For how long a second run would take, see how long GTA 6 is.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

    No. Beebom, Insider Gaming and PowerPyx all report that GTA 6 has no mission replay feature. You can only play a mission again by loading a manual save made before it.

    Does GTA 6 have gold medals?

    No. Beebom and PowerPyx both say the gold medal objectives from GTA 5 are gone, so there is no score to improve by replaying.

    Does GTA 6 have New Game Plus?

    Not at launch, according to Beebom. Rockstar has not said whether one will be added.
