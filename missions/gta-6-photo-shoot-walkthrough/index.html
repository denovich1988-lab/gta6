    GTA 6 Photo/Shoot Walkthrough: Helicopter Chase and Rescue

    GTA 6 Photo/Shoot walkthrough: photograph the Dimez from the helicopter, chase Dre'Quan's kidnappers by air and bike, and win the fight under the highway.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Photo/Shoot is a Chapter 4 mission from Dre'Quan that starts at a building in Tequesta after Party Hard.
    • Lucia photographs the Dimez in a helicopter, then Jason flies and Lucia rides a bike after the car that takes Dre'Quan.
    • It pays $6,000 and two weapon unlocks; you cannot shoot the kidnappers' car, only the cars that join after the second jump.

    1. Before you start

    This GTA 6 Photo/Shoot walkthrough covers Dre'Quan's promo shoot for the Dimez, which lasts about a minute before he is kidnapped and the mission becomes a chase across the city. The title is the plan: first you shoot photos, then you shoot people. PowerPyx subtitles it Shoot a Promo.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Photo/Shoot (Chapter 4)
    Playable characters Lucia (camera, bike) and Jason (helicopter)
    Given by Dre'Quan Priest
    Starts at The marked building in Tequesta, Vice City
    Requirement Party Hard
    Reward $6,000 and two weapon unlocks

    The mission continues the feud from Chapter 3. The gang that takes Dre'Quan is Cash City, the crew DWNPLY pays for protection in Ride or Die.

    Tip: the last stage is a street fight against several waves, so arrive with body armor and full ammunition. See GTA 6 weapons for where to buy both.

    Objectives

    GTABase lists four; PowerPyx leaves out the first.

    1. Meet Dre'Quan in Tequesta.
    2. Photograph the Dimez.
    3. Chase after Dre'Quan's car.
    4. Rescue Dre'Quan.

    Step-by-step

    1. Meet Dre'Quan

    Go to the building in Tequesta. Follow Dre'Quan into the elevator and out onto the roof, where the helicopter is waiting.

    2. Photograph the Dimez (Lucia)

    When the helicopter takes off you control Lucia. Per PowerPyx:

    Action Input on PS5 (Xbox)
    Bring up the phone camera Up on the D-pad
    Take a photo R2 (RT)

    Photograph Bae-Luxe and Roxy as they pose inside the helicopter.

    3. The kidnapping

    Dre'Quan has gone down to the lobby to meet a club promoter PowerPyx names as KD. Cash City ambush him there and drive him away.

    4. Chase the car (Jason, then Lucia)

    • Jason flies the helicopter after the car.
    • When the car drives into a building, the game has you switch to Lucia, who follows on a bike at street level.
    • You cannot shoot the kidnappers' car in this section. Both guides say so; just keep up.
    • After the second jump, more cars join the chase. These can be shot, and GTABase's tip is to deal with them before they ram you or open fire.

    5. Rescue Dre'Quan

    The chase ends under the highway. Walk up to the gang members and fight your way through to Dre'Quan.

    Where they come from What to do
    Cars pulling up from the right Watch that side first when the fight starts
    Garage doors on the left Expect a second group once the first is down
    The alley ahead Clear it and reach the car at the far end

    Reaching the car at the end of the alley completes the mission. In the closing scene, per PowerPyx, KD promises Dre'Quan a meeting with another club promoter, Royce Jones.

    Where players fail

    Neither guide lists fail conditions. The two places to be careful are the bike section, where shooting at the lead car achieves nothing and costs you speed, and the fight under the highway, where reinforcements arrive from both sides.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Cash $6,000 (both guides)
    Unlocks Two; PowerPyx names Roxy's W-10 SMG variant and Bae-Luxe's W-10 variant
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    The Dimez also gave Lucia a weapon variant in Cooties, so this is the second mission where helping the label pays in gear. Dre'Quan's story carries on in a Jason-only mission called Watch It, which we will cover once a second guide's walkthrough can be checked against GTABase's. Full order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you take the photos in this GTA 6 Photo/Shoot walkthrough?

    When the helicopter takes off you control Lucia. Bring up the phone camera with Up on the D-pad and take pictures with R2 (RT on Xbox) while the Dimez pose, per PowerPyx.

    Can you shoot the car that kidnaps Dre'Quan?

    No. Both guides say you cannot shoot the kidnappers' car during the chase. After the second jump more cars join in, and those can be shot.

    What is the reward for Photo/Shoot in GTA 6?

    $6,000 and two unlocks. PowerPyx names them as Roxy's W-10 SMG variant and Bae-Luxe's W-10 variant.

    How do you start Photo/Shoot?

    Finish Party Hard. Dre'Quan then gets in touch about a promo shoot for the Dimez; go to the marked building in Tequesta, Vice City.
