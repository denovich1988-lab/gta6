    GTA 6 Moses Walkthrough: Register Your First Car

    GTA 6 Moses walkthrough: bring an untracked car to the Port Gellhorn garage, scan vehicles with WAiNK, use the Slim Jim and register Lucia's personal vehicle.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 21, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Moses is the Chapter 2 mission that teaches car theft: scanning for trackers with WAiNK and opening doors with the Slim Jim.
    • Moses only accepts a car with no tracker that was not stolen next to his garage; the main road through Port Gellhorn is a safe place to find one.
    • You leave with the Slim Jim, the Scooter Bros delivery app and a registered personal vehicle for Lucia.

    This GTA 6 Moses walkthrough covers the mission that gives Lucia her own car and turns stolen vehicles into income. Moses runs a garage in Port Gellhorn. He shows you how to tell which cars are safe to take, hands you a tool for quiet break-ins and registers your first vehicle. PowerPyx lists the mission as Take a Vehicle to Moses.

    Before you start

    Detail Info
    Mission Moses (Chapter 2)
    Playable character Lucia, solo
    Given by Moses
    Starts at Moses's garage, Port Gellhorn
    Requirement Finish Girl's Trip
    Rewards Slim Jim, Scooter Bros app, a registered personal vehicle

    Objectives

    1. Bring a car to Moses.
    2. Follow Moses and scan the vehicles with WAiNK.
    3. Pick up the Slim Jim and break into the Montagne XL.
    4. Follow Moses back inside.
    5. Steal a suitable vehicle (only if yours was rejected).
    6. Register your new personal vehicle.

    Step-by-step

    1. Drive into the garage

    Any car starts the mission. Whether Moses keeps it depends on step 5.

    2. Scan the three cars in the yard

    Moses leads you behind the garage, where three vehicles are parked. Bring up the WAiNK app and scan each one. PowerPyx gives the quick input: hold Up on the D-pad, then hold R1 (RB on Xbox). The result appears in the bottom-right corner; VGC adds that the scan also shows what garages will pay for the car.

    Scan result What it means
    No tracker Safe to steal, sell and register. Usually cheap cars
    Standard tracker Police are told where the car was stolen. Usually mid-range cars
    Advanced tracker Police are called if you drive too slowly. Usually supercars; needs the Key Cloner from Chapter 3

    3. Slim Jim

    Pick up the Slim Jim beside Moses and use it on the Montagne XL.

    Step PS5 Xbox
    Start at the car door Triangle Y
    Find the spot Tilt the right stick until the bar turns from white to pink Same

    The Slim Jim opens a locked car without an alarm. VGC notes it does not work on keyless cars, mostly high-end models.

    4. Back to the garage

    Moses offers to register a stolen car for you.

    5. If your car is rejected

    Moses turns away tracked cars and cars taken too close to his shop. Go to the big main road through the middle of Port Gellhorn, scan until you find one with no tracker, and drive it back.

    Tip: scan before you steal. It costs nothing and saves a wasted trip.

    6. Respray and register

    Choose a new colour, then select Register. The car becomes Lucia's personal vehicle.

    Where players fail

    • Bringing a tracked car. You will be sent out again.
    • Stealing on Moses's street. He refuses cars taken near the garage.
    • Rushing the Slim Jim. Move the stick slowly and stop when the bar turns pink.

    Optional goals and rewards

    Item Details
    Slim Jim Quiet break-ins on locked cars
    Scooter Bros app Delivers your registered vehicle to you near a road
    Personal vehicle The first registration is free
    Selling cars Moses buys untracked stolen cars; the garage shuts for a minute or two after each sale
    100% GTABase marks the mission as required

    To register more cars you need more garage slots or have to sell a registered one. Selling to Moses is the fastest income at this stage of Chapter 2; more methods are in how to make money in GTA 6. Moses's next job is Daily Driver. Chapter order: GTA 6 mission list.

    Frequently asked questions

    What car should you bring in the GTA 6 Moses walkthrough?

    Any car without a tracker. If yours has one, Moses sends you to fetch another; PowerPyx and GTABase suggest the main road through the middle of Port Gellhorn.

    How do you use the Slim Jim in GTA 6?

    Press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox at the car door, then tilt the right stick until the bar turns from white to pink. It opens the car without setting off the alarm.

    What does the Moses mission unlock?

    The Slim Jim, the Scooter Bros app that delivers your registered car, a free registered vehicle for Lucia, and the option to sell untracked stolen cars to Moses.

    Can Moses register cars with trackers?

    No. Cars with an advanced tracker need the Key Cloner, which PowerPyx and GTABase say comes in Chapter 3.
