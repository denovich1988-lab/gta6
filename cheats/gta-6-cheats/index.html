    GTA 6 has 61 cheats, but none of them work until you finish the story. They come in five groups: player, combat, vehicle spawns, world and screen filters. Each one also has its own unlock task, such as shooting-range medals, races or collectibles. Button codes are confirmed for 21 cheats so far; the rest are listed below with their unlock tasks, and we add each code as soon as two independent sources agree on it. Rockstar has not released an official list, so this page is marked partly confirmed.

    How to enter cheats in GTA 6

    1. Finish the main story. Launch guides report 91 story missions; the Cheats section appears under the Progress tab after the credits.
    2. Complete the unlock task for the cheat you want.
    3. Save your game. Cheats turn saving off.
    4. In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to open the phone.
    5. Hold L1 + R1 on PS5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S. Guides differ on the wording: some say to hold L1 (LB) first and then press R1 (RB).
    6. Enter the code on the D-pad.

    All codes use only the D-pad, so they are the same on PS5 and Xbox.

    Code patterns

    • Vehicle spawn codes start with Left, Up, Right, Down.
    • The three ammo cheats share the opening Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up.
    • The screen filter codes start with Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left.

    Checked on November 20: still 18 confirmed codes

    We compared seven code lists again the day after launch. The 18 codes above match on every list that includes them, and none of the lists contradicts another.

    • Drunk. A code for the Drunk cheat now appears on two sites, but one of them says the input comes from a single outlet's guide and is not widely corroborated. That is one source, not two, so it stays off the table until an independent guide or Rockstar confirms it.
    • Raise Wanted Level. Its code is on four lists. Three other guides still mark it as not found, so treat it as the least certain of the 18.
    • Cycle Weather. The code is agreed. The unlock task is not: guides say either finishing the story or revealing the whole map.
    • Everything else. No code has been published for the other 42 cheats.

    Second check, later on November 20

    We ran the comparison again against three more lists: GTABase, Beebom (updated November 19) and Red Bull. All three show the same 18 codes, input for input, and all three still mark the other 43 cheats as "to be announced", including Drunk. Nothing was added to or removed from the tables above.

    Two details from that check:

    • Cycle Weather. All three lists give "finish the story" as the unlock task, which is now the more widely reported version.
    • Story length. The lists disagree on how many missions stand between you and the Cheats menu: Red Bull says 91, Beebom says 118. The condition itself, finishing the main story, is the same everywhere.

    Beebom alone says a cheat can be switched off by entering its code again. One source is not enough, so treat that as unconfirmed.

    Checked on November 21: no new codes

    Two days after launch the count has not moved. GTABase's list, read again today, still shows 61 cheats with 18 codes and marks the other 43 as "to be announced". Its 18 codes are the same as ours, input for input, and so are the unlock tasks.

    Beebom's list, updated on November 20 and read again in a second check today, agrees: 61 cheats, the same 18 codes, 43 still to be announced.

    Two details from GTABase are worth knowing, although we have not yet seen a second guide state them:

    • Entering the code of a cheat you have not unlocked does nothing.
    • Saving stays off until you quit, so progress made after a cheat is lost when you leave; loading the earlier save restores trophies.

    • GTABase and KeenGamer still give no code for Drunk. GTABase shows 18 codes; KeenGamer shows 17 and marks Raise Wanted Level as undiscovered.

Two lists also repeat the rule that saving stays off until you quit: GTABase and KeenGamer both say to save first and quit without saving to get trophies back.

    What cheats switch off

    • Saving is disabled while a cheat is active.
    • Trophies and achievements cannot be earned in that session.
    • Cheats are for story mode free roam only.

    Tips for unlocking cheats faster

    1. Many unlock tasks count progress you make during the story, so play shooting ranges, races and workouts as you go.
    2. Do challenge groups in pairs: five challenges unlock one vehicle, all of them unlock a second.
    3. Unlock tasks that need your partner (hours together, store robberies, activities) are easiest to finish in free roam after the credits.

    How we check this page

    Frequently asked questions

    Does GTA 6 have cheat codes?

    Yes, according to early guides published the day before launch: 61 cheats, entered with the D-pad through the in-game phone. Rockstar has not published an official list.

    What is the GTA 6 money cheat?

    Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right gives $250,000. It is reported to unlock after you finish the main story.

    Can you use cheats from the start of GTA 6?

    No. The Cheats menu is reported to appear only after the main story is finished, and many cheats need an extra task, such as shooting-range medals or workouts.

    How many cheats are in GTA 6?

    61: 10 player cheats, 11 combat cheats, 21 vehicle spawns, 3 world cheats and 16 screen filters.

    Do cheats disable trophies and achievements in GTA 6?

    Yes. Reports say an active cheat disables saving and blocks trophies and achievements for that session, so save first.
