    GTA 6 Safe Codes: Every Known Code and How to Get Yours

    Every GTA 6 safe and keypad code confirmed by two guides, from Brian's lockup (7700) to the jewelry store safes, plus how to get a random safe code.

    Status

    Quick answer

    • Story mission safes have fixed codes, such as 7700 for Brian's lockup and 1680, 6815 and 0104 for Yasmani's jewelry jobs.
    • Safes in free-roam store and bank robberies are randomised, so no list can give you that code.
    • To get a random code, threaten an employee and choose the Safe Code prompt, or look for a written note; a sticky bomb opens any safe but raises your criminal profile.

    GTA 6 safe codes come in two kinds: fixed codes for story mission safes and keypads, which this page lists, and random codes for safes you crack in free-roam robberies, which you have to get in the game. Every fixed code below appears in at least two independent guides; each row links to the walkthrough where you use it. After the table you will find how to get a random code, what to do when a code fails and when to use a sticky bomb instead.

    Facts here are confirmed by Rockstar or by at least two independent launch guides.

    Codes that are not confirmed yet

    These turn up in one guide, or guides disagree on where they go. Try them, but do not rely on them:

    • 6823, 6969 and 0300 for the three Kolt & Associates hideouts in Port Gellhorn (Brooks Boatworks, La Cascara Motel and Kingpin Bowling). The Click says all three come from one launch-day playthrough, where a wall map upstairs at Brooks Boatworks revealed them. The guides behind our Hit Kingpin Bowling walkthrough open that safe with a sticky bomb instead, so bring one either way.
    • 44017, credited to a trailer safe at Cottonmouth Lake by one guide and to a Key Lento safe by another.
    • 6499 at Avery Pawn in Hit Avery Pawn: PowerPyx got this code but warns it may be randomised. See the Hit Avery Pawn walkthrough.
    • The Pelican Valley codes (0072, 0212 and the password CLEAVES), listed by one guide so far.

    Why most safe codes are random

    Safes in store and bank robberies you start in free roam get a new combination each time, guides agree, so robbing the same shop twice can give two codes. That is why no online list can give you the code for a convenience store or a bank. The codes in the table above are fixed because they belong to missions and keypads.

    How to get a random safe code

    1. Pick a target. Start with a small, low-security store.
    2. Take control. Walk in with a gun drawn, aim at the cashier and press Circle (B on Xbox) to threaten them.
    3. Ask for the code. Find the safe, usually in a back office, then threaten the nearest employee again and select the Safe Code prompt. It only appears when an employee is close enough.
    4. Or search for it. If nobody is near, look around the office for sticky notes, notebooks or papers with the digits written on them.
    5. Enter it and get out. Use the on-screen keypad, take the cash and valuables and leave through a back exit.

    Why it matters: safes hold far more than the till, and the valuables inside, such as jewelry and collectible cards, sell to fences. Where the money goes next is covered in how to make money in GTA 6.

    No code? Use a sticky bomb

    A sticky bomb from Ammu-Nation blows a safe open without a code. Guides warn that it raises your criminal profile and pulls the police in, so it is the last resort. Where to buy one is in GTA 6 guns.

    When a code does not work

    • Check leading zeros. 0420, 0104 and 0420 start with 0.
    • Check the place. 0420 opens the lab door in Lab Fees; 7700 is the lockup, not a safe.
    • It may be randomised. If it is a free-roam robbery safe, get the code from staff or a note instead.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the safe codes in GTA 6?

    Fixed codes confirmed by two guides include 0420 and 7700 (Lab Fees), 2404 (First Impressions garage), 2008 (Delights safe in Afternoon Delight), 1680, 6815 and 0104 (Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs).

    How do you get safe codes in GTA 6?

    During a robbery, threaten the nearest employee and select the Safe Code prompt, or search the room for a note with the digits. Mission safes have fixed codes.

    Are GTA 6 safe codes random?

    Codes for safes in free-roam robberies are randomised and can change each time you rob the same place. Mission and keypad codes are the same in every playthrough.

    Can you open a safe without the code in GTA 6?

    Yes. A sticky bomb from Ammu-Nation blows it open, but guides warn it raises your criminal profile and draws police attention.
