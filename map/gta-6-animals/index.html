    GTA 6 Animals: Wildlife Confirmed in Leonida

    GTA 6 animals confirmed so far: more than 170 species in Leonida, including alligators, flamingos, manatees, sharks, deer and over 60 kinds of birds.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    1 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar says GTA 6 has more than 170 animal species.
    • That includes over 60 birds and more than 30 land mammals.
    • Alligators, flamingos, pelicans and manatees appear in a Rockstar game for the first time.
    On this page 3
    1. Animals confirmed in official footage and previews
    2. Where to look
    3. What is not confirmed

    GTA 6 has more than 170 animal species, the most in any Grand Theft Auto. Rockstar shared the number in a Game Informer cover story in September 2026: over 60 birds, more than 30 land mammals, plus fish, reptiles and marine life.

    Animals confirmed in official footage and previews

    Group Species seen
    Reptiles Alligators, green iguanas, sea turtles
    Land mammals Deer, wild boar, bobcats, big cats, dogs
    Marine life Sharks, dolphins, manatees
    Wetland birds Flamingos, pelicans, spoonbills, cranes, storks, ducks
    Other birds Eagles, woodpeckers, pigeons, seagulls

    Alligators, flamingos, pelicans and manatees are new to Rockstar's games.

    Where to look

    • Grassrivers: the swamp region is alligator and wading-bird country.
    • Leonida Keys and the coast: pelicans, gulls, dolphins and sharks.
    • Mount Kalaga National Park: deer and forest predators.
    • Vice City: pigeons, gulls, pets, and the occasional alligator where it should not be.

    See the GTA 6 map guide for the regions.

    What is not confirmed

    • A full official species list. Rockstar has given the total, not the names.
    • Hunting, selling or collecting animals.
    • How dangerous predators are to the player.

    We will build a species-by-species list with locations from the game.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many animals are in GTA 6?

    More than 170 species, according to details Rockstar gave to Game Informer in September 2026.

    Are there alligators in GTA 6?

    Yes. Alligators appear in official trailers, in swamps and in town.

    Can you hunt in GTA 6?

    Hunting has not been confirmed. We will update this page once it is checked in the game.
