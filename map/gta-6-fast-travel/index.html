    GTA 6 Fast Travel: How RydeMe and Taxis Work

    How to fast travel in GTA 6: call a taxi or use the RydeMe app on your phone, set a destination and pay $100 to skip the ride. Controls and unlock points.

    Status
    Confirmed
    Updated
    November 20, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 fast travel works through rides: order one on the phone, set a destination and pay $100 to skip straight there.
    • The RydeMe app is the main method; PowerPyx also describes calling Paradise Taxi from your contacts early in Chapter 1.
    • The skip fee is $100 on top of the normal fare, so it is a convenience rather than a free teleport.

    GTA 6 fast travel is done by ordering a ride on your phone and paying to skip it: there is no map teleport, but a $100 skip gets you anywhere a car can reach. With a map about twice the size of GTA 5's, you will use it a lot. Here is how each method works, what it costs and when it opens up.

    Fast travel at a glance

    Method How Available
    RydeMe app Phone > RydeMe > choose a destination With the bank account (VGC) or from Chapter 2 (PowerPyx)
    Paradise Taxi Phone > Contacts > All Contacts > Paradise Taxi After Gone Fishing (PowerPyx)
    Skip the ride Talk to the driver and pay $100 on top of the fare

    How to use RydeMe

    1. Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone.
    2. Open the RydeMe app.
    3. Choose where to go. VGC lists three options: your current safehouse, your map marker, or New Destination, which opens the map so you can pick a spot.
    4. Wait for the car. It shows as a white car icon on the minimap, so check there if you cannot see it.
    5. Get in.
    6. Hold L2 (LT on Xbox) to talk to the driver and press Triangle (Y) to pay $100 and skip to the destination. Or do nothing and ride along.

    VGC mentions a middle option as well: paying to speed the ride up without skipping it entirely.

    How to call a taxi without the app

    PowerPyx describes an older-style route that works before RydeMe:

    1. Open the phone and go to Contacts > All Contacts > Paradise Taxi.
    2. Wait for the line to answer and for the taxi to arrive.
    3. Set a marker on the map. PowerPyx says the marker can be placed on parts of the map you have not uncovered yet.
    4. Get in, hold L2 / LT to speak to the driver, and press Triangle / Y to skip for $100.

    PowerPyx adds that the same number still works later if RydeMe has no driver available. VGC, for its part, calls RydeMe the only traditional fast travel, so treat the phone call as a fallback.

    What it costs

    Charge Amount
    Fare Not listed by any guide; PowerPyx says it rises with distance on RydeMe
    Skip $100, regardless of distance, on top of the fare

    Tip: early in Chapter 1, $100 is real money. Once the BuckMe account is open and jobs pay better, it stops mattering. Ideas for getting there: how to make money in GTA 6.

    When it unlocks

    The two launch guides do not agree:

    • PowerPyx: taxis by phone from the end of Gone Fishing; the RydeMe app from Chapter 2.
    • VGC: RydeMe unlocks with Buck Me, because the app needs a bank account.

    What both agree on is that you cannot use RydeMe in the first few missions. If the app is missing from your phone, call the taxi number instead.

    Limits

    • The ride is cancelled if the car cannot reach you (VGC). Walk to a road and order again.
    • No guide describes fast travel during a mission or with a wanted level. Assume you need to be in free roam and clear of the police.
    • Buses and trains are not documented as fast travel in the guides we checked.

    Tips

    1. Set a map marker first, then open RydeMe: "map marker" is one tap.
    2. After swapping characters, VGC notes the lead you left may take a RydeMe of their own. See how to switch characters.
    3. Use rides to open up the map early; the full region guide is in GTA 6 map.

    New to the game? The other basics are in our beginner's guide.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you fast travel in GTA 6?

    Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone, order a ride with the RydeMe app, set a destination, get in and pay $100 to skip the journey. You can also sit through the ride.

    How much does fast travel cost in GTA 6?

    Skipping a ride costs $100 on top of the fare. PowerPyx says the RydeMe fare itself rises with distance; no guide lists exact fares.

    When does fast travel unlock in GTA 6?

    Guides differ. PowerPyx says you can call Paradise Taxi after Gone Fishing and that RydeMe arrives in Chapter 2. VGC says RydeMe unlocks with the Buck Me mission, because it needs a bank account.
