    GTA 6 Collectibles: Every Type, How Many, and What They Unlock

    All GTA 6 collectibles in one place: 480 collectible cards, 125 cocaine flamingos, 34 watches, WAiNK scans and more, with counts and what each unlocks.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 has 18 collectible card sets with 480 cards, 125 cocaine flamingos and 34 luxury watches, plus WAiNK scans, underwater discoveries and meditation spots.
    • You do not need everything for 100%: for example 15 of 18 card sets, 100 of 125 flamingos and 100 WAiNK scans are enough.
    • Several collectibles unlock cheats, including the Inflatable Flamingo and the two time-of-day cheats from watches.

    GTA 6 collectibles come in more than a dozen types, led by 480 collectible cards, 125 cocaine flamingos and 34 luxury watches. This hub lists every type, how many there are, what each one unlocks and where to check your progress. It does not list individual locations. Counts are given only where at least two launch guides agree; those guides are still being filled in, so this page is marked partly confirmed.

    What to know:

    • Cards lie around the world, indoors and outdoors. Outdoors they are often in yellow boxes.
    • Colour variants of a card count as separate cards for the set.
    • You can sell cards at pawn shops. A sold card still counts toward its set.
    • 3 complete sets earn the Don't Touch My Deck trophy. 15 complete sets are needed for 100%.
    • All 24 Vice City Flamingos cards are the reported unlock task for the Bubblegum screen filter cheat.

    GTA 6 cocaine flamingos

    Cocaine flamingos are pink flamingo figures with a drug package inside. There are 125. You destroy them; you do not pick them up.

    • The ones that count have a black and white beak. Ordinary lawn flamingos do not count.
    • 50 destroyed is the reported unlock task for the Inflatable Flamingo spawn cheat.
    • 100 destroyed counts toward 100% completion. No trophy is tied to them.

    GTA 6 watches and other styles

    The Styles category has 79 entries: magazine styles for Jason, magazine styles for Lucia, magazine tattoos and 34 luxury watches. You need 70 of the 79 for 100%.

    Watches matter for cheats. Finding 10 collectible watches is the reported unlock task for Move Time Forward, and 20 for Pause Time of Day.

    WAiNK scans

    WAiNK is the phone scan app. Guides agree on 31 landmarks, 35 curiosities and 23 sculptures. No Flusher tags, which are spray-paint markings on the ground, also count, but guides do not agree on how many there are. 100 scans earn the You WAiNKer! trophy and tick the 100% requirement.

    One cheat, the Powdered Sugar filter, is reported to need all space debris scanned with WAiNK. Launch guides have not yet listed how many pieces there are.

    Where to see your progress

    Open the menu (Touch Pad on PS5) and go to Progress.

    1. Progress > Collections holds collectible cards, WAiNK locations, styles and an Exploration group with underwater discoveries, magazines, meditations and flamingos.
    2. Some sub-categories only show up after you find the first item of that type.
    3. Progress > Stats > 100% Completion shows the full checklist. It appears only after you finish the story.

    Tips

    1. Do not chase full sets of everything. The 100% checklist leaves spare items in most categories.
    2. Sell duplicate and finished cards at pawn shops. It costs you no progress and feeds the Pixel Art unlock.
    3. Check the beak before you spend ammo on a flamingo.
    4. Collectibles pair well with GTA 6 challenges: several exploration challenges are about cards, scans and underwater finds.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many collectibles are in GTA 6?

    There is no single official number. The main collectible types that launch guides agree on are 480 collectible cards in 18 sets, 125 cocaine flamingos, 79 styles (34 of them luxury watches), 33 underwater discoveries, 18 meditation spots and at least 89 WAiNK scan locations.

    Do you need all GTA 6 collectibles for 100% completion?

    No. The 100% checklist asks for part of most types: 15 of 18 card sets, 100 of 125 cocaine flamingos, 100 WAiNK locations, 70 of 79 styles and 15 of 18 meditation spots. All 33 underwater discoveries are needed.

    What do cocaine flamingos unlock in GTA 6?

    Destroying 50 cocaine flamingos is the reported unlock task for the Inflatable Flamingo spawn cheat. Destroying 100 of the 125 counts toward 100% completion.

    Can you sell collectible cards in GTA 6?

    Yes. Cards can be sold at pawn shops, and guides agree that a sold card still counts toward its set.
