    GTA 6 Map vs GTA 5: How Big Is Leonida?

    GTA 6 map vs GTA 5: Rockstar says Leonida is about twice the size of the GTA 5 map and three times Red Dead Redemption 2. No official area figure exists.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 19, 2026
    Read
    3 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • Rockstar developers say the GTA 6 map is about twice the size of the GTA 5 map.
    • They also say it is about three times the size of the Red Dead Redemption 2 map.
    • Rockstar has not published the map size in square miles or square kilometres.

    GTA 6 map vs GTA 5: Leonida is about twice the size of the GTA 5 map, according to Rockstar's own developers. Rockstar has not published an area in square miles or square kilometres, so every exact number you see online is an estimate. This page lists what Rockstar has actually said and what is still unmeasured.

    GTA 6 map vs GTA 5 at a glance

    All of these comparisons come from Rockstar staff in interviews before launch. None of them is an official measurement.

    Comparison What Rockstar said Where it was said
    GTA 6 map vs GTA 5 map About twice the size Rob Nelson, head of development, to YouTuber TGG; repeated in Edge magazine's cover story
    GTA 6 map vs Red Dead Redemption 2 map About three times the size Rob Nelson to TGG
    GTA 6 landmass vs GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 together Nearly the same as all three combined Edge magazine cover story
    Vice City vs Los Santos About twice the size Rob Nelson to TGG
    Vice City with suburbs vs Los Santos with suburbs About eleven times larger Rob Nelson to TGG

    The TGG comparisons were reported by VGC in August 2026. The Edge comparison was reported by Forbes and others in October 2026.

    How big is the GTA 6 map in square miles?

    Nobody outside Rockstar can say yet. Rockstar has given ratios, not an area.

    Sites that print a figure are doing their own sums. They take a number for GTA 5 and multiply it. The trouble is that published sizes for the GTA 5 map already disagree, depending on whether the sea is counted and how the land was measured. That is why estimates for Leonida range from about 125 square kilometres to more than 300. We do not print any of them as fact.

    On launch day we found no measurement taken inside the released game that two reputable sources agree on. When careful in-game measurements appear, we will add them here with the method used.

    What "twice the size" means in practice

    • Los Santos and Blaine County fit inside Leonida twice. GTA 5 had one city and one large rural county. GTA 6 has Vice City plus five more named regions.
    • The city is the big change. Vice City alone is about twice the size of Los Santos. With its suburbs it is about eleven times the size of Los Santos and its surroundings, by Rockstar's count.
    • The streets are tighter. Rockstar North co-studio head Aaron Garbut said the roads in GTA 6 are significantly narrower than in GTA 5, as reported by Bloomberg's Jason Schreier. Narrower roads leave room for more buildings, so the city is denser as well as larger.
    • There are more interiors. Garbut told Edge that GTA 6 has far more interiors than any earlier Rockstar game.

    For what each region is, see the GTA 6 map and all confirmed regions. The city has its own page: Vice City.

    Is bigger the whole story?

    No. Size says little about how long the game is or how much there is to do. For that, see how long GTA 6 takes to beat. Smaller towns such as Port Gellhorn show how the space outside Vice City is used.

    What is not confirmed

    • An exact area. No official figure in square miles or square kilometres.
    • Land versus water. Rockstar has not said how its comparisons treat the sea, swamps and islands.
    • Drive or flight times across the map. Early numbers from players vary with vehicle and route, and we have not found two reliable tests that match.

    Frequently asked questions

    How big is the GTA 6 map vs GTA 5?

    About twice the size, according to Rockstar's head of development Rob Nelson. Rockstar has not given the figure in square miles or kilometres.

    How big is the GTA 6 map in square miles?

    There is no official number. Figures such as 120 to 130 square miles are estimates worked out from Rockstar's comparisons, and published estimates disagree with each other.

    Is Vice City bigger than Los Santos?

    Yes. Rockstar says Vice City alone is about twice the size of Los Santos, and far larger again once its suburbs are counted.

    Is the GTA 6 map bigger than Red Dead Redemption 2?

    Yes. Rockstar puts it at about three times the size of the Red Dead Redemption 2 map.
