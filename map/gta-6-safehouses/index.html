    GTA 6 Safehouses: Every Home by Chapter and What It Does

    All GTA 6 safehouses in story order: Jason's house in Key Lento, the Starlet Motel, the Bayside apartment and Room 701 at the Y Hotel, and when you lose each.

    Status
    Partly confirmed
    Updated
    November 24, 2026
    Read
    5 min
    By
    GTA6Box team

    Quick answer

    • GTA 6 gives you one safehouse per chapter rather than a list to buy: Jason's house, the Starlet Motel, the Bayside apartment and Room 701 at the Y Hotel.
    • Each home is lost for good when its chapter ends, so finish anything tied to it before the chapter finale.
    • Safehouses hold your wardrobe and a bed; saving is done from the pause menu anywhere, not at home.

    GTA 6 safehouses work differently from earlier games: instead of buying properties, Jason and Lucia are given one home per chapter, and each one is taken away when the chapter ends. This page lists every home confirmed so far, in story order, with where it is, how you get it, what it is for and the exact mission that closes it.

    Everything here comes from walkthroughs by at least two guides that agree. Chapter 5 is not included yet.

    All safehouses at a glance

    Chapter Safehouse Where You get it in You lose it in
    1 Jason's house Key Lento, Leonida Keys The prologue Home Sweet Home
    2 Starlet Motel room Kelly County Discharge Time Checking Out
    3 Bayside apartment Bayside, Vice City Welcome to Vice City Ocean Beach
    4 Room 701, Y Hotel Vice Beach side of Vice City Ocean Beach Not covered yet

    There is also one place that is storage rather than a home: the unit at Big House Storage, from Caught Slipping.

    What a safehouse does

    • Wardrobe. Everything you buy or pick up is kept in the wardrobe at your current safehouse, and that is where you build outfits. See how to change clothes in GTA 6.
    • Bed. At Jason's house, sleeping skips the night and restores health and stamina, according to KeenGamer.
    • Fast travel target. The RydeMe app can take you to your current safehouse; see GTA 6 fast travel.
    • Mission starts. Several missions begin at home, including chapter finales.

    What it does not do is save your game. Saving is in the pause menu under Game and works anywhere in free roam; see how to save in GTA 6.

    Chapter 1: Jason's house, Key Lento

    Jason lives rent-free on a property owned by Brian Heder in the Keys, in return for helping with shakedowns. It is the only home with its own side content: four repair jobs, covered in Jason's house projects, which close when Lucia is released. Lucia moves in after Coming Home.

    How you lose it: in Home Sweet Home, Lucia finds intruders in the house, a fire starts and the house explodes. PowerPyx says there is not enough time to reach the cash stash before it goes.

    Tip: finish the house projects and anything else you want in the Keys before Tourist Trap, because the last three missions of Chapter 1 run without a break.

    Chapter 2: the Starlet Motel, Kelly County

    Chapter 1 ends at a motel, and Chapter 2 opens there with What's in the Bag?. Jason is stuck in bed at first, so Lucia stocks the room in Just Moved In. The motel's coffee room is also where the chapter's money goal, Prepare for Vice City, begins.

    How you lose it: Checking Out, the chapter finale, starts at the bathroom door in your room once you have $15,000 and every other Chapter 2 main mission done. PowerPyx and GTABase both say starting it removes the motel as your home for good.

    Tip: do not touch the bathroom door until you have finished the Chapter 2 side content you care about.

    Chapter 3: the Bayside apartment, Vice City

    In Welcome to Vice City, Boobie Ike lets the couple stay in an apartment he owns near the Jack of Hearts. To get in, park in the covered spot, walk round to the front, climb to the second floor and take the first door in front of you. Both guides note that the door opposite the parking spots works as a shortcut afterwards.

    The apartment comes with strings. Boobie treats the stay as a debt, which is how Dealer's Choice and Bricked Up start; more on him in Boobie Ike. It is also burgled: Caught Slipping triggers at the apartment door after Boobie Time and First Impressions.

    How you lose it: after The Cartel Heist, returning to the apartment starts Ocean Beach, and the pair pack up and leave for good.

    Big House Storage: not a home, but yours

    At the end of Caught Slipping, Jason buys a unit at Big House Storage, south of the apartment, as a safer place to keep their things after the burglary. You return to a storage locker to collect that drive at the start of Heavy Lifting in Chapter 4.

    Chapter 4: Room 701 at the Y Hotel

    Valentina puts the couple in a suite at the Y Hotel as part of her money-laundering operation. Ride the elevator to the 7th floor, turn left and walk to the far end of the hallway. Both guides say new outfits become available through the suite's wardrobe from this chapter on.

    The hotel adds two conveniences:

    • Santino, the concierge, gives the mission Company Cars, which hands each lead a free car.
    • The hotel's car service works as a free taxi from the front of the building, per both guides.

    Why it matters: from Making Gainz until the VCIA Takedown, this room is the only place Jason and Lucia can be together, so it becomes the base for anything that needs both of them.

    Checklist before every chapter finale

    1. Make a manual save in a slot you will keep.
    2. Finish missable and optional missions for that chapter; the mission list marks the points of no return.
    3. Finish anything tied to the home itself, such as Jason's house projects in Chapter 1.
    4. Bank your cash before the finale, since carried cash is lost when you are wasted or busted.

    What is not confirmed

    • Chapter 5. We have not yet checked two walkthroughs for the final chapter, so we do not say whether the Y Hotel stays your home.
    • Buying property. No two guides we have read confirm purchasable safehouses in story mode.
    • Garages and vehicle storage at each home. Details differ between guides and will be added when they agree.

    For the districts around the two city homes, see our Vice City guide; for the full region list, see the GTA 6 map.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many GTA 6 safehouses are there?

    The walkthroughs we have checked show four homes in the first four chapters, one per chapter: Jason's house in Key Lento, the Starlet Motel in Kelly County, the Bayside apartment in Vice City and Room 701 at the Y Hotel. Chapter 5 is not covered yet.

    Can you buy safehouses in GTA 6?

    None of the story homes is bought: each is provided by the story. We have not found two guides that confirm purchasable homes in the single-player game, so we do not list any.

    Do you need a safehouse to save in GTA 6?

    No. You save from the pause menu under Game, anywhere in free roam. Sleeping in the bed at Jason's house skips the night and restores health and stamina but does not save.

    Can you go back to an old safehouse in GTA 6?

    No. Jason's house is destroyed at the end of Chapter 1, the Starlet Motel stops being your home when Checking Out begins, and the pair clear out the Bayside apartment at the start of Chapter 4.
