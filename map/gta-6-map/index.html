    GTA 6 Map: All Confirmed Regions of Leonida

    ConfirmedUpdated November 21, 20262 min readChecked against 5 sources

    Short answer: Where GTA 6 is set and every region Rockstar has confirmed: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

    Key facts

    • GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida.
    • Six regions are officially named: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.
    • Rockstar has not published an official map image or a map size.

    GTA 6 is set in Leonida, a fictional state modelled on Florida. Rockstar's official site names six regions. Here is what each one is.

    Interactive GTA 6 map

    Click a region (or pick it from the list) to see what it is and jump to our guides for that part of Leonida.

    Where each region is

    • Mount Kalaga National Park runs along the northern border of the state.
    • Port Gellhorn sits on the west coast.
    • Ambrosia is inland, next to Lake Leonida.
    • Grassrivers covers the swampy south-west.
    • Vice City is on the south-east coast.
    • Leonida Keys stretch south of Vice City.

    These positions are what Rockstar's descriptions and several mapping guides agree on. Exact borders and distances will only be clear once a full in-game map is documented, so our map above is a schematic, not a to-scale copy.

    Confirmed GTA 6 locations

    Region What it is
    Vice City The main city: beaches, nightlife and neon, inspired by Miami
    Leonida Keys A chain of tropical islands off the mainland, inspired by the Florida Keys
    Grassrivers Swamp and wetland wilderness, inspired by the Everglades
    Port Gellhorn A run-down coastal town on Leonida's forgotten coast
    Ambrosia An industrial heartland town built around a sugar refinery
    Mount Kalaga National Park Forested hills and backcountry in the north of the state

    Vice City

    Vice City returns for the first time since 2002's GTA: Vice City, now as a modern city. Expect the dense urban centre of the map here, along with the beach districts shown in the trailers.

    Leonida Keys

    The Keys are where Jason Duval is based at the start of the story, working for local drug runners.

    Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga

    These regions make up the rest of the state: wetlands, a faded coastal town, an industrial town and a national park. They give the map its countryside, in the way Blaine County did for GTA 5.

    What is not confirmed

    • Map size. No official figure exists.
    • A full map image. Maps shared online before launch are fan-made from trailers and screenshots.
    • Interiors and exact landmarks. We will document these on separate pages once verified in the game.

    We will add location guides for collectibles, shops and activities to this section after release.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where does GTA 6 take place?

    GTA 6 takes place in the fictional US state of Leonida, which includes Vice City and the surrounding regions. It is inspired by Florida.

    What cities are in GTA 6?

    Vice City is the main city. Rockstar has also named the towns and areas of Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia, plus the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park.

    How big is the GTA 6 map?

    Rockstar has not given an official size. Comparisons with GTA 5 that you see online are fan estimates.

    Is there an official GTA 6 map?

    Rockstar has described the regions on its website but has not released a full official map image before launch.
