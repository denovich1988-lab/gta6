    Port Gellhorn in GTA 6: Location Guide

    Port Gellhorn in GTA 6 is a faded coastal town on Leonida's forgotten coast. Where it is, what Rockstar has shown and what to do there.

    Quick answer

    • Port Gellhorn is one of the six regions of Leonida named by Rockstar.
    • It is a run-down coastal town away from Vice City.
    • A race circuit with the Gellhorn name appears in gameplay footage and cheat unlocks.

    Port Gellhorn is a faded coastal town in GTA 6, one of six regions Rockstar names on its official site. It sits on what the site calls Leonida's forgotten coast, far in mood from the neon of Vice City.

    What Rockstar has shown

    • A town of cheap motels, closed attractions and strip malls.
    • A working-class, down-on-its-luck feel compared with the tourist coast.

    Racing at Gellhorn

    Official gameplay footage shows wheel-to-wheel racing on a purpose-built circuit. Reported cheat unlocks name Gellhorn Raceway as one of three race venues where you need ten first-place finishes to unlock the Repair Vehicle cheat, alongside Thrillbilly Mud Club and Boone Speedway.     Frequently asked questions

    Where is Port Gellhorn in GTA 6?

    On the coast of Leonida, away from Vice City. Rockstar calls the area Leonida's forgotten coast.

    Is there a race track in Port Gellhorn?

    A circuit called Gellhorn Raceway is named in reported cheat unlocks, and track racing appears in the Extended Look.

    What is Port Gellhorn based on?

    Rockstar has not said. Leonida is modelled on Florida, and the town resembles its quieter Gulf coast.
