    Vice City in GTA 6: Districts and What to Expect

    Partly confirmedUpdated November 19, 20261 min readChecked against 3 sources

    Short answer: Vice City returns in GTA 6 as the main city of Leonida. What Rockstar has confirmed about its beaches, nightlife, businesses and place on the map.

    Key facts

    • Vice City is the largest city in GTA 6 and is modelled on Miami.
    • It returns for the first time since GTA: Vice City in 2002, now set in the present day.
    • Ocean Beach is confirmed by name through the pre-order bonus garage.
    On this page 4
    1. What is confirmed
    2. Vice City and the rest of Leonida
    3. Things to do in the city
    4. Coming to this page

    Vice City is the heart of GTA 6. It is the biggest city in the state of Leonida and Rockstar's modern take on Miami: beaches, high-rises, nightlife and money.

    What is confirmed

    • It is the main urban area of the map, with the densest streets and crowds.
    • It is set today, with phones, social media and modern cars. The 1980s version belongs to the 2002 game.
    • Ocean Beach exists by name. The pre-order bonus garage, Shore Court, is described as being near Ocean Beach.
    • Local businesses matter to the story. Boobie Ike, a Vice City figure, has interests in property, a strip club and a recording studio, and co-runs the label Only Raw Records. See GTA 6 characters.

    Vice City and the rest of Leonida

    Vice City is one of six named regions. The others are the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The full overview is in our GTA 6 map guide.

    Things to do in the city

    Official footage shows gyms, shops you can rob, beach activities and street life. Radio matters here too: Vice City FM is among the reported stations. See GTA 6 radio stations.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Vice City in GTA 6?

    Yes. Vice City is the main city of the state of Leonida, where GTA 6 takes place.

    What city is GTA 6 based on?

    Vice City is modelled on Miami, and the wider state of Leonida on Florida.

    Is GTA 6 set in the 1980s?

    No. It is set in the present day. The 1980s setting belonged to 2002's GTA: Vice City.
