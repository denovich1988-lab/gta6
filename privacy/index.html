    Privacy Policy

    Updated: November 19, 2026

    You can use GTA6Touch without an account. We don't run advertising. We measure visits with Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager.

    Data we process

    • Server and security logs. Our hosting and security providers record technical data when pages are requested (IP address, time, page, browser type). We use it only to deliver and protect the site, and logs are kept for a short period.
    • Analytics. Google Analytics tells us which pages are read and from which country, in aggregate. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it stores analytics cookies only after you press Accept in the cookie notice; advertising storage is always off.
    • Email. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.
    • Search. The site search runs in your browser; what you type is not sent to us.

    Service providers

    Cloudflare (delivery and security), Google Ireland Ltd. (analytics), Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting in the EU) and GitHub (source files; receives no visitor data).

    Cookies

    We set no advertising cookies. Analytics cookies follow the choice you make in the cookie notice; to change it, clear this site's data in your browser and choose again. Our security provider may set strictly necessary cookies to tell people from bots.

    Your rights

    Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to processing. Email contact@gta6touch.com.

    Changes

    If we add advertising or new tools, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
